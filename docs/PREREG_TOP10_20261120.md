## N1 — new-perp funding spikes (#37, Hyperliquid)

- **Universe:** every perp in Hyperliquid's current `meta` whose first funding
  record is on or after 2024-01-01. That first record is the listing time L.
  **Survivorship:** delisted coins aren't in `meta`, so the worst listings may
  be missing. Stated, not fixable with this API.
- **Trade:** from L+1h, short $1,000 of the perp, hedged by $1,000 long spot,
  for 336 hours (14 days) or until the data ends. Income is funding:
  Σ hourly rate × $1,000, positive rate paid to shorts. Costs: perp taker 0.045%
  per side and spot taker 0.07% per side (assumed), $2.30 per trade. The hedge
  is assumed perfect, which is **optimistic**: many new coins have no spot
  market to hedge with.
- **Baseline (amended 2026-11-20, before any data was fetched):** the same
  14-day short-funding carry, over the same hours, averaged over a fixed sample:
  the **20 coins with the largest open interest** in Hyperliquid's current
  `metaAndAssetCtxs`, among those listed at least 180 days before L. Chosen
  without looking at funding. Reason: paging funding history for every
  established coin would take about 2 hours at the API's rate limit.
- **Pass:** mean net carry > 0 with t > 2 **and** above the baseline (Welch
  t > 2), in **both** halves of the listings by date.
- **Also reported:** the share of listings that rose ≥ 50% at any point in the
  14 days. That would liquidate a 2× short leg even when hedged, if margin
  isn't topped up.

## N2 — utilisation-threshold arbitrage (#38, DefiLlama lend/borrow history)

- **Pools:** Aave v3, all chains, supply ≥ $10M at the time, symbols USDC,
  USDT, DAI, WETH, WBTC.
- **Daily:** utilisation u = borrow ÷ supply, and supply `apyBase`.
- **Event:** day t where u rises above its own 90th percentile of the prior 90
  days (no look-ahead).
- **Trade:** move $10,000 into that pool for days t+1..t+7.
- **Benchmark:** that day's median supply `apyBase` across all tracked pools of
  the same symbol. Excess $ = Σ (pool apy − benchmark)/365 × $10,000.
- **Cost:** $10 in and $10 out (assumed gas, $20 per event).
- **Pass:** mean net $ per event > 0 with t > 2, in both halves by date, and
  positive on at least half the pools with ≥ 5 events.
- **Fallback, only if** utilisation history is unavailable: event = `apyBase`
  above 2× its prior-30-day median, from `/chart/{pool}`; same trade and rule.

## N3 — liquidity incentive mispricing (#39, DefiLlama pool history)

- **Pools:** two-token DEX pools (`exposure = multi`), TVL ≥ $1M on the entry
  day, with an `il7d` series.
- **Incentivised:** first day with `apyReward` ≥ 20%. **Baseline:** pools with
  `apyReward` < 1%, entered in the same calendar month.
- **Hold 30 days.** Return = Σ (apyBase + apyReward × 0.99)/365, assuming
  rewards are sold daily with 1% slippage, minus impermanent loss from
  `il7d` (Σ il7d/7 per day).
- **Pass:** incentivised mean > 0 with t > 2 **and** above the baseline (Welch
  t > 2), in both halves.

## N4 — multi-outcome probability arbitrage (#40, Polymarket)

- **Events:** closed `negRisk` events with ≥ 3 markets, ending 2024-01-01 ..
  2026-10-31, where exactly one market resolved YES (so the outcomes cover
  everything). Hourly price history for every YES token.
- **Opportunity:** an hour where all outcomes have a price and the sum of YES
  prices S ≤ 0.97 (buy every YES, $1 payout), or S ≥ 1.03 (buy every NO).
  Edge = |1 − S|.
- These are traded or mid prices, not executable asks with size, so this is an
  **upper bound**.
- **Pass (screen only):** opportunities with ≥ 3% edge lasting ≥ 2 consecutive
  hours in ≥ 10% of events. A pass means it's worth building a live order-book
  scanner; a fail means even the upper bound is empty.

## N5 — liquid staking redemption discounts (#41, stETH)

- **Data:** daily stETH and ETH prices (DefiLlama coins API).
- **Window:** from 2023-04-12, when withdrawals opened. Before that no
  redemption was possible.
- **Event:** a run of days with stETH/ETH ≤ 0.997, counted once per run, at
  that run's lowest ratio.
- **Gain on $10,000:** (1/ratio − 1) − 0.1% swap − 0.2% gas. The ~5-day
  withdrawal queue is assumed; its time cost is ignored (small).
- **Pass:** ≥ $500 a year per $10,000 (5%) in **both** halves of the
  post-April-2023 period.

## N6 — vault allocation inefficiencies (#42, DefiLlama)

- **Pools:** USDC and USDT vaults (Yearn, Morpho, Beefy and the like) and
  lending pools, on Ethereum, Arbitrum and Base, from DefiLlama's current pool
  list (survivorship noted).
- **Each day:** best lending `apyBase` for the same asset and chain against the
  vault's APY.
- **Switching strategy:** hold yesterday's best pool; each switch costs $20 on
  Ethereum, $2 on L2 (assumed), on $10,000.
- **Pass:** switching beats holding the vault by > 1% a year net, in both
  halves, for at least half the vaults.
