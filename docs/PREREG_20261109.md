# Pre-registration, 2026-11-09 — risk size, ES sensitivities, two-account portfolio

Committed before any of these runs. Results are appended below as a dated
section; definitions above that line do not change.

Common setup unless stated: LTF sweep `LTFSweepConfig()` defaults, one trade per
day, `XFARules()` (rulebook 20261107), `Policy.LOCK_FIRST`, one unbroken chain.
NQ trades on MNQ at $1.24, ES on MES at $1.24 (same as docs/ES_PREREG.md).
Windows: SEEN 2016-01-01..2026-10-28, OLD 2010-07-01..2015-12-31.
Coin flip: same trade sizes, random sign on gross P&L, commission kept, seed 1.

Command for all three: `uv run python scripts/jx_ideas_20261109.py`

## Test 1 — risk per trade (account policy, not a strategy parameter)

GO_LIVE uses $900 a trade. Levels: **$500, $700, $900, $1,200, $1,500**.
Datasets: NQ SEEN, NQ OLD, ES SEEN, ES OLD. For each, report recycling net, money
spent, pass rate, longest stretch without a payout, and the coin-flip median
(100 flips).

The Combine's 50-micro cap (`recycle.py`) clips large positions, so above some
level extra risk stops adding size. That is part of what is being measured.

**Rule:** recommend replacing $900 with level X only if X's recycling net is
higher than $900's in **all four** datasets. If more than one level qualifies,
pick the one with the highest NQ SEEN net. Otherwise $900 stays.

## Test 2 — ES sensitivities (exploratory; cannot overturn the ES verdict)

The ES test (docs/ES_PREREG.md) is closed: does not confirm. These two runs only
say how much of that result comes from fees and from NQ-sized settings.

- **2a** commission $0.74 per MES round trip instead of $1.24.
- **2b** point settings scaled to ES. Factor k = median over SEEN of (daily ES
  RTH close ÷ daily NQ RTH close), rounded to 2 decimals, computed in the script.
  Scaled: `stop_buffer_points`, `min_fvg_size`, `min_risk_points`,
  `max_risk_points` (each × k). `min_penetration` stays 0.25, which is one ES
  tick, as it is one NQ tick. Everything else unchanged. $1.24 commission.

Each reports the same figures as the ES test, including where it sits against
its own coin flips (200 flips). Labelled post-hoc in every write-up.

## Test 3 — NQ and ES accounts side by side (descriptive)

One NQ chain and one ES chain, $900 each, SEEN window, run independently.
Report: combined net, the deepest the combined cash position goes (capital
needed), the share of calendar months with at least one payout (NQ alone vs
combined), and the correlation of the two chains' monthly cash flows.

No decision rides on this. ES did not confirm, so a second account on ES is not
recommended whatever this shows. It is here to measure whether a second market
smooths cash flow, for if a later market does confirm.

## Results (2026-11-09, `scripts/jx_ideas_20261109.py`, output `docs/jx_runs/ideas_20261109.txt`)

**Test 1, risk per trade — verdict: $900 stays.** $1,200 and $1,500 earn more
in 3 of 4 datasets but not ES 2010–2015, so neither passes the rule.

| recycling net | $500 | $700 | **$900** | $1,200 | $1,500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NQ 2016–2026 | $15,800 | $31,137 | **$44,359** | $73,960 | $69,240 |
| NQ coin-flip median | −$209 | $8,962 | $18,811 | $32,187 | $36,930 |
| NQ 2010–2015 | −$3,553 | −$3,315 | −$2,505 | −$2,400 | −$2,490 |
| ES 2016–2026 | $4,789 | $14,073 | $20,338 | $33,782 | $38,174 |
| ES 2010–2015 | −$1,071 | $1,778 | **$2,478** | $847 | $656 |

Bigger risk earns more mostly because the account structure pays more, not
because of the signal: the coin-flip median rises almost as fast. On NQ
2016–2026 the margin over a coin flip is $25.5K at $900, $41.8K at $1,200 and
$32.3K at $1,500. Above about $1,200 the 50-micro Combine cap starts clipping
positions.

**Test 2, ES sensitivities (post-hoc, does not change the ES verdict):**

| ES 2016–2026 | PF | recycling net | coin-flip 95th pct | beats flips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as registered | 0.977 | $20,338 | $24,180 | 86% |
| 2a: $0.74 commission | 1.020 | $27,587 | $31,335 | 90% |
| 2b: settings × 0.31 | 1.001 | $31,672 | $23,998 | **100%** |

2b is the notable one. Scaling the point settings to ES prices takes ES from
86% to 100% of flips. It was run after the ES failure, so it is a hypothesis.
It is tested cleanly on YM and RTY in docs/PREREG_YM_RTY.md.

**Test 3, NQ + ES side by side (descriptive):** combined net $64,697. The
deepest month-end cash position goes from −$3,520 (NQ alone) to −$4,444. Months
with at least one payout go from 20% to 32%. The two chains' monthly cash flows
correlate at 0.14, so a second market does smooth cash flow.
