# PREREG: Morpho Blue liquidations on Base, as a less-crowded venue than Aave (candidate #100, "more venues" lever) — written 2026-11-21 before any Morpho data

Question: Jx asked whether liquidation income can be raised or scaled. The measured Aave pools are dominated by same-block backruns (64–78%; Chainlink SVR on Arbitrum/Base) and by a few repeat winners. Morpho Blue does not use SVR (its markets use their own oracles) and its liquidations are public. Is Morpho Blue on Base a venue where bots outside the top 3 still earn real money, in both halves of 24 months? Same bars as `PREREG_LIQUIDATIONS_20261120.md`.

## Stage 0: desk check (web, third-party unless stated)
- Morpho Blue liquidations are permissionless (anyone calls `liquidate`); incentive and close rules are in Morpho's docs (to be confirmed by the fetch below; formula not yet read). Morpho maintains an open-source "backstop" liquidation bot (`morpho-org/morpho-blue-liquidation-bot`) that anyone can run, which means many competitors run similar logic.
- A recent wave on Base (7–13 Nov 2026, about USD 4.0M USDC repaid against cbXRP, per CryptoSlate) shows activity exists; who won is unknown. Morpho deposits reportedly passed USD 3B in 2026 (single research site, unverified).
- Malaysia/legal: unchanged from the Aave test (unverified, no live use without Jx).
- Five-part filter: repeatable (events every month: to be measured), long-lived (since 2024: third-party), one setup (same `liquidate` call on every market), capped downside (flash loan plus revert-on-loss, Morpho flash loans carry no fee: third-party), scales by adding copies (more chains/markets). Not yet measured: competition.

## Stage 1: pilot (cheapest slice, KILL-ONLY)
Window: last 3 months (2026-08-01 .. 2026-10-31), Morpho Blue singleton on Base, all `Liquidate` logs, same price/gas methods as the Aave test. KILL (drop the venue, skip stage 2) only if pilot gross bonus < USD 3,750 (a venue paying under USD 1,250 a month cannot leave USD 500 a month to bots outside the top 3 when the top 3 hold at most 60%). Anything else proceeds to stage 2. Stage 2 rules are not changed by pilot data.

## Stage 2: full test
Window: 24 months Nov 2024 – Oct 2026 (H1 = months 0–11, H2 = 12–23), venue = Morpho Blue singleton (0xBBBBBbbBBb9cC5e90e3b3Af64bdAF62C37EEFFCb) on Base, all markets pooled. A venue passes only if in BOTH halves: (a) top-3 winners hold < 60% of gross bonus value; (b) a liquidation in every month; (c) net bonus (gross − gas) won outside the top 3 averages >= USD 500/month. Winner = tx `from`, top 3 chosen per half by gross bonus, 20-liquidation minimum per half, as in `liquidations_20261120.py` (the same `venue_stats` code is reused). Bonus = seized collateral value − repaid loan value at DefiLlama daily prices; events with missing price or bonus/collateral outside [−2%, +30%] excluded and counted. Gas only; Morpho flash loans assumed free (stated, not verified); slippage, bribes and failed attempts unobserved (income is an upper bound). Receipt cap 40,000 txs (sampled as in addendum 3 of the Aave test if exceeded).
Descriptive, no pass/fail: top-3/top-10 and repeat-winner shares by bonus size; gas share of bonus by size; median and 75th-percentile net per active non-top-3 bot per month and the share of months above USD 200 / 1,000; month table (lumpiness); median bonus as % of collateral (plausibility).
Data: `eth_getLogs` on the singleton, topic0 `Liquidate(bytes32,address,address,uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256,uint256)`; market tokens from `idToMarketParams(id)`; decimals by `decimals()`; receipts for gas/winner; DefiLlama prices. Resumable Actions job, per-chain files `data/public/liq_morphobase*`. Other chains (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon) and other lenders (Compound v3, Euler, Fluid, Kamino) are NOT measured in this round.
If the venue passes: the registered claim is only that its non-top-3 income clears the bars, NOT that a new bot would win a share; a replay/shadow arm would follow in a separate pre-registration. If it fails: drop the Morpho lever.
Output: `scripts/liq_morpho_20261121.py` → `docs/jx_runs/liq_morpho_20261121.txt` (run twice, identical), FIGURES_LEDGER rows, CANDIDATES #100 update.

## Results (2026-11-22; raw `docs/jx_runs/liq_morpho_20261121.txt`, re-run identical; command `uv run python -I scripts/liq_morpho_20261121.py full`; stage-1 pilot `..._pilot.txt`)
Morpho Blue on Base, 24 months Nov 2024 – Oct 2026: 35,192 `Liquidate` events (97.6% with valid prices, receipts missing 0.3%), gross bonus USD 54.1M (H1 6.6M, H2 47.5M).
**VENUE PASS = True** under the registered rules: (a) top-3 winners hold 14.9% (H1) and 3.7% (H2) of bonus value (< 60%); (b) liquidations in all 12 months of both halves; (c) net bonus won outside the top 3: USD 459K a month (H1, 458 bots) and USD 3.79M a month (H2, 5,518 bots), pooled over all those bots, gas only (flash-loan fee assumed zero, slippage/bribes/failed attempts unobserved).
Descriptive: extremely lumpy: the top 5 months (2026-02 20.5M, 2026-06 9.0M, 2025-11 8.2M, 2026-01 6.6M, 2025-10 3.9M) hold 89.1% of the bonus and 12 of 24 months paid under USD 200K. Since March 2026 Morpho Base paid USD 12.1M versus USD 2.3M for the five Aave pools. Concentration by the `liquidator` field is much higher than by tx sender: top 10 hold 48–52% and addresses with >= 50 wins hold 81–86% of the value in every size bucket. Median active non-top-3 bot (zeros included) made under USD 200 in 23 of 24 months and over USD 1,000 in 1; among bots that won that month, under 200 / 200–1,000 / over 1,000 in 16 / 4 / 4 months. Median winner gas is 0.01–0.55% of the bonus. Median bonus 10.0% of collateral value (some price noise: the 90th percentile exceeds Morpho's reported 15% cap).
What the pass does NOT show: that a new bot would win a share (speed/competition untested). Next: replay (same-block-as-oracle-update share) and a Morpho shadow arm, each pre-registered separately.
