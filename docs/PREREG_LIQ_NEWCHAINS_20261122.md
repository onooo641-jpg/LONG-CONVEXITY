# PREREG: Aave-style liquidations on newer, smaller chains (candidate #100, "more venues" lever) — written 2026-11-22 before any data

Question (Jx: "find every lever"): the measured Aave pools are crowded and mostly same-block backruns. Do newer, smaller chains where Aave v3 or its forks run (and where oracle auctions and professional bots may be thinner) offer a venue that passes the same bars as `PREREG_LIQUIDATIONS_20261120.md` and is less same-block?
Chains (candidates, no assumption about which contracts exist: the scan is topic-only and discovers every contract emitting the Aave `LiquidationCall` event): Sonic, Mantle, Linea, Celo, Ink, Soneium, zkSync Era. A chain with no free public endpoint that serves topic-only logs over the window is DROPPED and reported as unmeasured (probe job `liq_diag4`). Not covered: BNB Chain (no free data, needs the Etherscan key), Solana lenders, Compound/Euler/Fluid (different decoders), Ethereum mainnet (no free deep history, known crowded).
Stage 0 (desk, done now): protocol rules as for Aave (permissionless `liquidationCall`); Chainlink SVR covers Base, Arbitrum, BNB, Ethereum only (third-party; the other chains' oracle arrangements are unread); Malaysia/legal unchanged (unverified); five-part filter as for Aave.
Stage 1 pilot (KILL-ONLY): last 3 months (2026-08-01..2026-10-31) per chain, same price and gas methods. A venue (chain, contract) is dropped if its pilot gross bonus < USD 3,750; a chain with no venue above that line is dropped. Others proceed to stage 2. Stage 2 rules are not changed by pilot data.
Stage 2 (full): 24 months Nov 2024 – Oct 2026, H1 = months 0–11, H2 = 12–23. A VENUE passes only if in BOTH halves (a) top-3 winners hold < 60% of gross bonus, (b) a liquidation in every month, (c) net bonus (gross − gas) won outside the top 3 averages >= USD 500 a month; 20-event minimum per half; winner = tx `from`; same `venue_stats` code. Overall the lever "passes" if at least 1 venue on these chains passes AND its replay (same-block share; separate pre-registration) leaves a reachable share >= 10% by count and value; a pass of (a)–(c) alone only shows money exists, not that a new bot can win it.
Costs: gas only (flash-loan fees, slippage, bribes unobserved). Venues that existed for less than 24 months (Sonic from 2025, Mantle, Linea, Celo, Ink, Soneium 2025) cannot meet "a liquidation in every month" for months before launch: those months count as failures for (b) UNLESS the venue's first event is later than the month and I state so; the rule is fixed as: (b) is evaluated over the months from the venue's first logged liquidation to Oct 2026 (>= 6 months required), and (a), (c) over the available months of each half (averages over the months present). Stated now because a launch date is not a failure of competition.
Data: `eth_getLogs` topic-only for `LiquidationCall(address,address,address,uint256,uint256,address,bool)`; receipts for gas/winner; DefiLlama prices (chain keys: sonic, mantle, linea, celo, ink, soneium, era); decimals via `decimals()` with DefiLlama fallback. Resumable Actions jobs, per-chain files `data/public/liq_<chain>*`.
Output: `scripts/liq_newchains_20261122.py` → `docs/jx_runs/liq_newchains_20261122.txt` (run twice, identical), ledger rows, CANDIDATES #100 update.
