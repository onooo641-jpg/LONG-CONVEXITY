「沒領走」那一欄是沒有領出來的餘額。它不算收入 —— 帳戶還會死，死了就沒了。$100/單那一行整年連一次 funded 都沒拿到。但這一年不能拿來挑倉位。$200 和 $250 在這一年是小賺，放到 11 年卻是實實在在的虧損（下一節那張 11 年的表）—— 倉位太小的問題是月費一直在走，而 $3,000 的目標好幾年都到不了。“Left behind” is balance never withdrawn. It is not income: the account can still die and take it. The $100 row never reached a funded account at all. Do not pick a size from this year, though. $200 and $250 show a small profit here and a real loss over eleven (the eleven-year table in the next section): too small a position keeps paying the subscription while the $3,000 target stays out of reach for years at a time.

要不要先鎖住再領Take it early, or lock it first

XFA 的虧損上限追着收盤最高餘額走，而餘額一旦到 $2,000，上限就永久鎖在 $0 —— 之後帳戶殺不死，只會被掏空。出金會把餘額拉下來，但不會把那個最高點拉下來，所以太早領錢等於一直不買那張保險。An XFA's loss limit follows the highest end-of-day balance, and once the balance reaches $2,000 the limit locks at $0 for good: from then on the account cannot be killed, only emptied. A payout lowers the balance but not the high-water mark, so taking money early means never buying that insurance.

Eleven years say A wins at every size, by around $3,505. The reason is not subtle: accounts die before they lock often enough that waiting for the insurance costs more than the insurance saves. Across the windows, B is ahead in only 42% of them. Taking it early beats timing it — though this is still one path and not a law.

最壞的一年是 −1,726 —— 而且它有底：一年最多就是付掉月費、重置和啟用費。這是整個結構最重要的一個性質 —— 上檔沒有上限，下檔被手續費封住。打亂順序的中位數比真實的高（+2,372 對 +1,560），因為它把連續的虧損打散了。The worst year is −1,726, and it has a floor: a year can cost no more than its subscriptions, resets and activations. That is the most important property of the structure — the upside is open and the downside is capped by fees. 這份東西是什麼、不是什麼What this is and is not

  • 這是回測，一張單都沒有真的送出去過。成交模型偏向它（限價單碰到就算成交），資料源是 Databento 不是 TopstepX。季度報告那份的每一條保留都還在。A backtest; no order here was ever sent. The fill model favours it, and the data is Databento rather than TopstepX's feed. Every caveat in the quarterly report still applies.
  • 規則是 2026-09-14 查的，而規則會變。出金門檻、上限、分成、MLL 鎖死的條件，全部寫在 backtest/recycle.py 的 XFARules 裡，改一個地方就會全部重算。The rules were read on 2026-09-14 and rules change. Payout thresholds, caps, the split and the MLL lock all live in XFARules in backtest/recycle.py; edit there and every figure recomputes.
  • 這整套只在 Express Funded 這一層成立。官方書面答覆寫得很清楚：Live Funded 不能透過 TopstepX API。而拿到 Live Funded 的時候「所有 Express Funded 帳戶都會關閉」。所以這個模型假設你一直留在 XFA —— 我沒有找到強制升級的規則，但如果有，這裡的跑道就比算出來的短。All of this lives at the Express Funded layer. Topstep's written answer is explicit that Live Funded Accounts cannot use the TopstepX API, and receiving a Live Funded Account closes every Express Funded one. So the model assumes you stay on XFAs indefinitely. I could not find a rule forcing the upgrade, but if one exists the runway is shorter than this.
  • 出金不是瞬間到帳，也沒有算稅。模型在符合資格的那一天就記入現金。Payouts are neither instant nor taxed here. The model books the cash on the day it becomes eligible.
  • 百分位的有效樣本只有 11。118 個視窗是重疊的，背後只有 11 個年份。「1% 最壞情況」是外插，不是量出來的 —— 要看真的發生過什麼，看上面的逐年表。The percentiles rest on 11 observations. The 118 windows overlap heavily and come from 11 years. A “1% worst case” from that is an extrapolation. For what actually happened, read the year-by-year table above.
