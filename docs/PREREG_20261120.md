# Pre-registration, 2026-11-20 — futures timing patterns and Topstep cost structure

Committed before any of these runs. Results go in a dated section at the end;
nothing above it changes. CANDIDATES rows 29–35.

## Part A — futures timing patterns (own account, micro contracts, not Topstep)

These would be traded in a normal futures account, not inside Topstep:
Topstep requires being flat each evening, and most of these hold overnight.

### Data and costs

- 1-minute bars, Databento continuous `.v.0`: NQ (processed file), ES, YM, RTY
  (`data/raw/`). RTY starts 2017-07.
- RTH open = open of the 09:30 ET bar; RTH close = close of the last bar before
  16:00 ET. Days with either missing are dropped.
- **Contract rolls.** `.v.0` is not back-adjusted and carries no contract ID,
  so a return spanning a roll includes the calendar-spread jump. Every return
  whose window touches the **10 trading days before a quarterly expiry** (third
  Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) is dropped, for events and baselines alike.
- Round-trip cost in points: 2 ticks of slippage plus $1.24 commission ÷ the
  micro's $-per-point. NQ 1.12 (MNQ $2/pt), ES 0.748 (MES $5/pt), YM 4.48 (MYM
  $0.50/pt, tick 1.0), RTY 0.448 (M2K $5/pt, tick 0.1).
- Windows: **OLD** 2010-07-01..2015-12-31 and **SEEN** 2016-01-01..2026-10-28.

### Pass rule (each hypothesis)

In **both** windows, and on **at least 3 of 4 markets** in SEEN and **2 of 3**
in OLD (RTY has no OLD data):
(a) the event's mean net return is above zero with t > 2, and
(b) it beats its baseline (Welch t > 2), where a baseline exists.

### Hypotheses

- **H1 overnight drift (#29).** Long from RTH close to the next RTH open.
  Baseline: the same days' RTH open → close.
- **H2 turn of month (#30).** Long from the RTH close of the second-to-last
  trading day of a month to the RTH close of the third trading day of the
  next. Baseline: every other 4-trading-day close-to-close window that doesn't
  overlap a turn-of-month window.
- **H3 pre-FOMC drift (#31).** Long from the 14:00 ET bar's open on the trading
  day before an FOMC statement to the 13:59 bar's close on statement day.
  Baseline: the same 14:00 → 13:59 window on every other day. Dates: OLD from
  `docs/tier1_calendar_2010_2015.csv` (Fed sourced); SEEN uses **2024-01 to
  2026-10 only**, statement days as listed by third-party calendars on
  2026-11-20. The official Fed page wasn't read, and 2016–2023 is not covered.
  So SEEN for H3 means 2024–2026.
- **H4 roll week: not testable** with this data, because the roll exclusion
  removes the very window. Recorded, not run.
- **H5 gap fade (#32).** If the RTH open is at least 0.25% away from the prior
  RTH close, trade toward the prior close at the open: short a gap up, long a
  gap down. Exit at the prior close (a limit, touch fill) or at the RTH close.
  No baseline; rule (a) only.
- **H6 day of week (#33).** Long RTH open → close, one test per weekday.
  Baseline: the other four weekdays. Five tests, so the bar is **t > 2.6** in
  place of 2 for both (a) and (b).

Size is reported as $ per year for one micro, so a pass that is too small to
matter is visible.

Command: `uv run python scripts/patterns_20261120.py`

## Part B — Topstep cost structure (in `backtest/recycle.py`)

Rules read on 2026-11-20 from Topstep's pages (help.topstep.com Back2Funded and
EFA articles, topstep.com/express-funded-account-rules, the MLL article):
- Back2Funded reactivates a lost Express Funded Account up to **2 times**, at the
  same size and payout policy, for $599 (50K).
- The reactivated account starts like a new one: balance, P&L and winning days
  at zero, and an MLL that trails and locks at $0.
- The decision window is 7 days on one page and 30 days on another. That
  doesn't matter here; the model reactivates immediately.

- **P1 Back2Funded always (#34).** On a funded bust, reactivate if the XFA has
  reactivations left (max 2), paying $599. Otherwise go back to a Combine.
- **P2 Back2Funded once (#35).** The same, at most 1 reactivation per XFA.
- Baseline: never reactivate (the current plan). NQ, $900, LOCK_FIRST, rulebook
  20261107.
- **Pass:** recycling net higher than the baseline in **both** NQ 2016–2026 and
  NQ 2010–2015, **and** the deepest cash dip no worse by more than $500.

- **P3 doubled payout caps (descriptive).** The limited-time offer for new
  Combines with a daily loss limit added at checkout doubles the caps: 50K
  $4,000, 100K $6,000, 150K $10,000 (Standard). It is still open on Topstep's
  page, with no end date given. Re-run the account-size table with the doubled
  caps. No decision rides on it, because the offer may not be available at
  purchase.

Not run, and why:
- **Billing-cycle timing:** the model bills in whole months and has no
  calendar position inside a month, so it can't answer this.
- **Firm-by-firm comparison:** needs other firms' rule pages read in full,
  which needs the person's approval for those sites.

Command: `uv run python scripts/topstep_costs_20261120.py`
