Credits and pack rules
Buying credits, free packs, refunds and the Ultra 50K Elite.
October 18, 2026
Table of contents
Credits are bought in fixed packages from the Add credits panel.
Credits have no cash value and can't be transferred or redeemed for cash.
The Pack Pass gives a free Ignition Pack for every 1,800 credits you spend. Giveaway and Discord keys open free packs.
Opened packs are final. If something goes wrong during an opening, contact Support with the time of the opening.
Packs are available to account holders aged 18 or over, where our services are available.
Ultra 50K Elite
Ultra 50K Elite works like the 50K Elite with these differences: payout cap up to $4,000 per payout (instead of $2,000); the $1,200 daily loss limit is always on; it cannot be reset; in evaluation the trailing floor stops at $50,100. It isn't sold on the dashboard; you unlock the right to buy it with the Ultra 50K Elite Access card.
