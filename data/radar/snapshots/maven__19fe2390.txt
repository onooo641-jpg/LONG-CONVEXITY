5 Risk calculations activity and account controls
5.1 Balance means the virtual account amount after closed results and booked adjustments. Equity includes open-position profit or loss. Initial balance means the starting virtual balance for the relevant account or phase. Highest equity includes unrealised gains. Floating loss is the positive amount, if any, by which current balance exceeds current equity.
5.2 For an equity-based daily limit, take the higher of balance and equity at 00:00 UTC and multiply by one minus the applicable daily percentage. Equity must not fall below that floor during the trading day. Static maximum drawdown uses initial balance multiplied by one minus the maximum-loss percentage. Trailing maximum drawdown uses highest running equity multiplied by one minus the trailing percentage. A trailing floor moves upwards with new equity highs and does not move down merely because equity falls.
5.3 Both daily and maximum loss limits apply independently where specified. Falling below an applicable floor breaches the account. Instant Funding and Mini have an additional aggregate floating-loss limit of 1% of current balance: a floating loss greater than 1% breaches the account. This is an account-wide open-position test, not a separate allowance for each trade. Equality is the threshold shown in the FAQ, rather than a breach under this rule.
5.4 The M2 Account Saver activates when balance minus equity reaches 2% of current balance. It closes all open positions. On a first trigger, whether in an evaluation phase or in the Simulated Funded Account, the reward split in the Simulated Funded Account becomes 50% for the remainder of that account, including where a 90% add-on was purchased. A second trigger closes positions and permanently deactivates the account. The normal daily and maximum drawdown limits remain separate and can still be breached.
5.5 M2 applies in each evaluation phase and the Simulated Funded Account stage of 2 Step OMO. Its trigger counter carries over from one phase to the next, including the transition to the Simulated Funded Account. Triggers in an earlier phase count toward deactivation in a later phase. For BNPL, M2 applies only in the Simulated Funded Account stage. Within a Simulated Funded Account, the first-trigger reduction to a 50% split remains effective for the remainder of that account and does not reset after a payout.
5.6 Unless a product expressly states otherwise, at least one executed trading activity is required in every 30 calendar days. Mini has no inactivity rule and instead has its 24-hour term. OMO evaluations must be completed within 180 days across both phases combined. Exceeding 30 days without trading activity, or the OMO 180-day limit, terminates the account. Weekend and overnight holding are permitted, subject to market availability and the applicable loss and news rules.

10.4 Standard and OMO minimum requests are $20 of eligible profit before the reward split. Instant and Mini require profit of at least 3% of initial balance before the split. A consistency denominator is not a substitute for actual withdrawable profit.
10.5 For BNPL and Instant, consistency equals the largest profitable day divided by the total profit of all trading days in the payout cycle, including losing days, multiplied by 100. The result must be 20% or less, or 30% with the Consistency Score +10% add-on. Total profit is the sum of every day’s result, so losing days are subtracted from it. For example, days of +$1,000, +$500 and −$300 give a total profit of $1,200; the best day is $1,000, so consistency is 83%, above the 20% limit. The calculation resets after each payout. For Mini, consistency equals the largest profitable trade divided by the total profit of all trades during its 24-hour term, including losing trades, multiplied by 100. The result must be 15% or less, or 25% with the Consistency Score +10% add-on. Total profit is the sum of every trade’s result, so losing trades are subtracted from it. For example, trades of +$300, +$200 and −$100 give a total profit of $400; the best trade is $300, so consistency is 75%, above the 15% limit. Losses still affect account balance, equity, drawdown and actual profit available for withdrawal.
10.6 Standard 2 Step requires three qualifying profitable days per phase and three new days per payout cycle. OMO requires four per phase and four new days per payout cycle. Each qualifying day must earn at least 0.5% of initial balance; consecutive days are not required. Calculate daily profits at 00:00 UTC for the preceding UTC day. Allocate profit to the day generated, rather than the trade-opening day.
10.7 For Standard 1 Step, Standard 2 Step, Standard 3 Step, BNPL and Instant Funding Simulated Funded Accounts, a maximum of $10,000 in profit, before the reward split, may be withdrawn across two payouts or within any rolling 30-calendar-day period. Profit above this cap is removed and cannot be carried forward or withdrawn in a later period.
10.8 OMO instead caps its first reward request at 6% and its second at 8% of initial balance, before the split. Any profit above that cap on either of those two payouts is voided at payout, is not carried over, and the account is reset to its initial balance. From the third reward onward it has no product withdrawal cap. Mini is not subject to the trader-wide $10,000 cap.
10.9 A withdrawal reduces balance. When the payout is approved, the drawdown limits and the highest equity used for maximum drawdown are reset, so the withdrawal does not breach the account. BNPL is the exception and does not reset: before the first withdrawal its maximum loss limit trails highest equity at 8%; after the first withdrawal its maximum-loss floor is fixed at the initial account balance and replaces the trailing floor. The 4% daily loss limit and M2 continue to apply. A requested amount must satisfy all applicable conditions; for BNPL, which does not reset, that includes its remaining risk limits. The account balance is reduced by the full gross eligible profit withdrawn, before the reward split. For example, a $1,000 gross withdrawal at an 80% reward split reduces account balance by $1,000 and pays the trader $800.
