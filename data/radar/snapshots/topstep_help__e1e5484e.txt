Overview
The Trading Combine® is a simulated account where you prove your trading ability without risking your own capital. Follow 1 rule, hit 2 objectives, and you earn an Express Funded Account® (XFA) .
Rule
Do not let your account balance hit or go below the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL)
Objectives
Reach and maintain your Profit Target
Meet the Consistency Target

Parameters in Depth
Profit Target
The net profit you need to make in order to pass
You can't hit it in 1 day. The Consistency Target applies.
Example: $50K account, $3,000 Profit Target. Earn $3,000 and your balance is $53,000.
Maximum Loss Limit
The lowest your account balance can go. It cannot be adjusted.
It trails up with your end-of-day balance, never down. Once it reaches your starting balance, it locks permanently.
Touch it at any point, even on unrealized P&L, and your account is liquidated for the rest of the trading day.
Your account is ineligible for funding until you Reset . You can still practice trade.
Example: $50K account, $50,000 balance, $48,000 MLL. Make $500 and the MLL moves to $48,500.
Consistency Target
Your best single day must stay below 55% of your Profit Target. Stay below the Best Day Recommendation in the table above.
Go over and your Profit Target increases. You need to earn more to pass.
Losses do not reset your best day.
Your best day locks in at 3:10 PM CT.
Example: $50K account, $3,000 Profit Target. A $2,200 best day raises it to $4,000.
See Consistency at Topstep for the full rules.
Maximum Position Size
The maximum number of contracts you can hold open at one time, per account
Minis and micros share the same limit at a 10:1 ratio. 1 mini = 10 micros.
Example: $50K account, 5 mini limit. Hold 2 minis and you have room for 30 micros.
Hint: You're never required to trade the maximum. Fewer contracts is always allowed.
Position limits can tighten temporarily during high volatility and Tier 1 News Events. See Risk Adjustments: High Risk/High Volatility .
⚠️ Please note: The Micro to Mini ratio functionality is available for the Trading Combine and Express Funded Account. It is not currently available for the Live Funded Account® (LFA).
Daily Loss Limit
Optional. Add it at checkout when you purchase a Trading Combine.
It's fixed at purchase and can't be changed later. It carries into your XFA.
Hit it and open positions are flattened, pending orders are canceled, and no new trades are allowed until 5 PM CT.
It is not a rule violation. Your account stays eligible for funding.
No Daily Loss Limit at purchase? You can set a Personal Daily Loss Limit in Risk Settings. It can be adjusted or removed.
See Daily Loss Limit for details.
Trading Hours and Products
Topstep is a day trading program. No swing trading. Futures only.
The trading day runs from 5:00 PM CT to 3:10 PM CT the next calendar day. All positions must be closed by 3:10 PM CT.
Avoid opening new positions after 3:08 PM CT. Risk Managers begin flattening then.
Friday closes at 3:10 PM CT and reopens Sunday at 5:00 PM CT.
Some products close earlier. See When and What Products Can I Trade?
FAQs
Can I have more than one Trading Combine?
Yes. There's no limit to the number of Trading Combines you can have.
If you have multiple accounts, double-check which one you're trading on before you start. Topstep isn't responsible for trades made on the wrong account.
⚠️ Single Profile Policy:
All Trading Combines, Express Funded Accounts, and Live Funded Accounts must be under 1 Topstep profile.
Multiple profiles are a Terms of Use violation and can result in account closure or suspension.
If you've accidentally created a second profile, contact Trader Support .
To update your name or email, follow these steps instead .
Do I need to purchase a data subscription?
No. Level 1 (Top of Book) live data is included in your Topstep membership at no extra cost. Level 2 (Depth of Market) is available as an upgrade for DOM or Ladder traders. See Level 1 and Level 2 Market Data for details.
Can I pass the Trading Combine in one day?
No. Big spike days don't build funded traders, consistency does. You can pass in as few as two days, but keep your best day below 55% of your Profit Target.
What happens after I pass?
What happens after I pass?
When a 50K, 100K, or 150K Trading Combine® (account prefix: 50KTC, 100KTC, or 150KTC) passes, the account closes and the subscription is canceled. You'll receive a confirmation email and can activate your Express Funded Account® directly from your Dashboard. See step-by-step activation instructions here .
​
​ Please note: The status update can take up to 30 minutes. If it still shows Active after 30 minutes, verify your Consistency Target has been met.
👉 A few things to keep in mind:
Passed on a Friday? You can pay the Activation Fee immediately, but your XFA won't be available to trade until markets reopen at 5 PM CT Sunday.
Trading day hours: The trading day runs from 5:00 PM CT through 3:10 PM CT the next calendar day. Trades placed after 5:00 PM CT (e.g., 6:30 PM CT Tuesday) count toward Wednesday's activity.
Profits don't transfer. Money made in the Trading Combine does not carry over to your Express Funded Account.
Can I change my account size?
No. Account sizes can't be changed after purchase. If your purchase was made within the last 28 days and your account has had no trading activity, contact Trader Support to review your account for a refund so you can purchase the correct size. After 28 days or if any trading has occurred, refunds are not available.
Can I use automated trading strategies?
Yes, with conditions. Topstep won't help set up or troubleshoot automated strategies, and no exceptions are made for errant trades or malfunctions. Test on your Practice Account first and review Prohibited Conduct and Prohibited Trading Strategies before going live. You can also use a trade copier to duplicate trades across multiple accounts. See What is a Trade Copier?
