Account inactivity rule
How often you need to trade to keep CFD and Futures accounts active, and when the inactivity period starts.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 17 November 2026
Short answer
Place at least one trade within your account's inactivity period. If you don't, the account is treated as inactive and breached.
Inactivity periods
Account
Trade at least once every
CFD: all accounts
30 days
Futures: evaluation accounts
10 calendar days
Futures: funded accounts upgraded on or after 10 October 2026
10 calendar days
Futures: funded accounts upgraded before 10 October 2026
7 calendar days
When does the clock start?
CFD: the inactivity period counts from the moment the account is created, even though your evaluation itself only starts with your first trade. If you buy an account, don't leave it untouched for more than 30 days.
Tip
Keep an eye on your account activity in the dashboard, especially on funded Futures accounts, where the period is much shorter.
