Payout caps and buffers for E8 Signature explained
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Payout caps and buffers are part of the payout requirements on the performance stage of E8 Signature.
Payout buffer:
​
Before you can start requesting a payout, you need to leave a buffer in your account of the same size as your EOD Dynamic Drawdown. The buffer is in place to protect traders from violating EOD by requesting full profit from the account, and no matter how many payouts you request, the buffer will always stay there.
​

On E8 Signature Futures, there is a maximum number of payouts per account: 4. This is the same rule as on E8 Zero, and this determines how many payouts you can request in total from one account. After you request 4 payouts, no matter the remaining buffer or how much you requested in total payouts, the cycle is closed, the account is deactivated, and you move on with the free Challenge of the same size.
