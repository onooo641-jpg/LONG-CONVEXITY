How do I pay for a new challenge?
November 24, 2025
To purchase a challenge, you'll first need to create an account through the Blueberry Futures portal :
Sign up on the Blueberry Futures website and confirm your email .
Once verified, log in to your portal dashboard .
From the dashboard, you can browse available challenge types ( Ascent– EOD or Accelerated– Trailing ) and select your desired account size .
Proceed to checkout using one of the available payment methods:
Credit/Debit Card
Apply any discount or affiliate code , if you have one, and complete your payment .
Your challenge account will be activated shortly after the payment is confirmed, and you’ll be able to view all progress directly within the portal and email.
