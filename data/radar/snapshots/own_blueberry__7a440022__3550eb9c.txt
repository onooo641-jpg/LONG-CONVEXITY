💔 How to Cancel Your Subscription
💔 How to Cancel Your Subscription
To stop your recurring billing with Blueberry Futures, follow these four simple steps in your dashboard.
November 24, 2025
Table of contents
Step-by-Step Cancellation Process
Log In to Your Account: Access your Blueberry Futures trader dashboard.
Navigate to ' Account ': Find the ' Subscriptions ' tab on the Superior menu.
Select 'Cancel Subscription': Click the option to manage your current plan details.
Confirm Cancellation: A final prompt will appear asking you to confirm your decision. Click 'Yes' to finalize the cancellation.
Important Considerations
Account Access: Your trading account remains active and accessible until the end of the current paid billing cycle.
Challenge Reset: Canceled Evaluation are immediately ineligible for any future resets , even if the cancellation is future-dated.
Payouts: Ensure all pending payouts have been processed before your access ends.
Note: If you are experiencing difficulties or would like to provide feedback, please contact our support team before canceling.
