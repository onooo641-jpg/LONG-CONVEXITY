How Fast Are Alpha Futures Payouts? Performance Fee Speed & Rules (2026)
Alpha Futures processes a performance fee request (what many traders call a payout) in 48 hours or less, then pays it within 3 business days via ACH (US bank accounts), wire transfer, SWIFT, Wise, or Rise, in USD.
If your account needs extra verification, payment can take up to 14 business days. The minimum request is $200 on Zero, $500 on Standard and $1,000 on Advanced, and on all three you can request after every 5 winning trading days of $200 or more.
For the full payout rules by plan, see Alpha Futures payout rules .

How fast does Alpha Futures actually pay?
There are two stages, and it helps to keep them separate:
Processing: an approved request is processed in 48 hours or less.
Payment: funds are then sent within 3 business days via ACH (US bank accounts), wire transfer, SWIFT, Wise, or Rise, in USD. If your account requires additional verification checks, this can take up to 14 business days.
So in a clean case, the realistic end-to-end timeline is a few business days, not weeks. The slow cases are almost always verification, which you can avoid by keeping your KYC details accurate and matching.

Eligibility depends on your plan. From the official Payout Policy:

The minimum request is $200 on Zero, $500 on Standard and $1,000 on Advanced. Full mechanics are in our performance fee rules explainer.
How much can you withdraw?
You may request up to 50% of the profit in your account per request.
The rest of the profit stays on the account for drawdown or future withdrawals.
On Zero accounts there are hard caps: $1,500 max on a 50K and $2,500 max on a 100K.
One critical point: withdrawals count toward your Maximum Loss Limit. If you withdraw down to your MLL, the account closes permanently. The firm's own guidance is to pay yourself steadily as you profit rather than emptying the account in one go.
What performance split do you get?
It is the same on every plan:
Advanced and Zero: a flat 90% split at all times.
Standard: also a flat 90% from the start, with no tiered system.
The wider trading community often calls this a "profit split," but Alpha Futures uses the term performance split.
What can slow a performance fee down?
Speed is rarely about the transfer itself. The real delays come from:
Verification: additional KYC checks can extend payment to up to 14 business days.
Consistency: on Zero and Standard Qualified Accounts, a single day over 40% of total profit blocks the request until the rule is satisfied.
News violations: trades executed within 2 minutes of a high-impact news event have their profit voided, and the request for that window is denied.
For the full list of avoidable delays, see why performance fee requests get delayed.
A note on comparing "fastest payout" firms
"Fastest payout" depends heavily on the payment rail and the firm, and figures change often. We only publish competitor rules we can verify on that firm's official documentation, so rather than repeat unverified speed claims, the honest advice is to check each firm's own performance fee policy. What we can state with certainty is the Alpha Futures process above: 48 hours or less to process, paid within 3 business days in a clean case.
FAQs
How fast does Alpha Futures pay out?
Requests are processed in 48 hours or less, then paid within 3 business days via ACH (US bank accounts), wire transfer, SWIFT, Wise, or Rise, in USD. Extra verification can extend payment to up to 14 business days.
What is the minimum performance fee request at Alpha Futures?
$200 on Zero, $500 on Standard and $1,000 on Advanced.
How often can I request a performance fee?
On Advanced, Zero and Standard accounts, after every 5 winning trading days of $200 or more (they need not be consecutive), up to 4 times a month.
What is the Alpha Futures performance split?
Advanced, Zero and Standard all pay a flat 90% of each request from the start, with no tiered system.
Why was my performance fee (payout) delayed?
Usually verification or a consistency-rule block, not the transfer. Accurate KYC and satisfying the 40% consistency rule (where it applies) keep requests moving.
Can I withdraw 100% of my profit?
No. Each request can be up to 50% of the profit in your account, within your plan's maximum; the rest stays on the account for drawdown or future withdrawals. Withdrawing down to your Maximum Loss Limit closes the account.
