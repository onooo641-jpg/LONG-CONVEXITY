Skills cost 2 points, then 4, 6 or 8; keystones cost 16. A full path costs 36–38 points and level 50 gives 49.
Unlocking costs points once. Activating applies the skill to an account you choose, then it rests for its cooldown (7 to 90 days).
2 activations a day, 3 from Prestige IV and 4 from Prestige VI, refilled at 00:00 UTC.
Upgrade Boost and Step Up need the account at least $1 in profit.
Resetting the tree refunds all points and costs 10,000 credits.
Skills only change an account when you activate them. Live accounts are never affected.
