News trading
Allowed, no restrictions (Evaluation and SIM Funded)
Hedging
Not allowed. Long and short at the same time on the same instrument or on correlated instruments of the same group (example: ES and NQ), in one account or across your own accounts. Mini vs micro and different expiries also count
◦ 1st time: soft breach (warning). Hedged positions are closed
◦ 2nd time on the same account: hard breach (account closed)
◦ 1 soft breach in the Evaluation and 1 for the whole life of the SIM Funded account

Payout Policy
BrightPlus
Payout frequency: daily (1 payout per trading day)
First payout: 24h after the first trade, once the balance is above the buffer
Buffer (start + MLL + $100): $26,100 / $52,100 / $103,100 / $154,600
Withdraw anything above the buffer, up to the payout cap
No winning days needed
Max Loss Limit locks at start + $100 when the buffer is reached
After each approved payout, fresh closed profit of 1% of starting balance is required before the next request:
25K: $250
50K: $500
100K: $1000
150K: $1500
BrightCore
Payout frequency: every 5 winning days (3 with the 3 Day Payout Cycle addon)
Winning day: $100 / $200 / $300 / $300 net profit (25K / 50K / 100K / 150K)
Max Loss Limit locks at start + $100 at the first approved payout
Each payout request is limited to the lowest of the payout cap, 50% of current account profit, and the amount that leaves starting balance + $100. The 2x Payout Cap addon does not change the latter two limits.
Both plans
