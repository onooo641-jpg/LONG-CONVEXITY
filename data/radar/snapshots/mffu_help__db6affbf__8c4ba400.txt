Payout Requirements – Step by Step
Your buffer must be fully cleared before you can submit any payout request. The buffer equals MLL + $100 or $4,500
For your first payout: you must have at least $1,500 in net profit above the buffer.
For all subsequent payouts: you must have at least $1,500 in net profit since your last approved payout.
You must have traded on at least 2 trading days in the current cycle.
The 50% consistency rule applies: your single largest profit day cannot represent more than 50% of your total profits in the cycle. This calculation resets after each approved payout.
Maximum payout per cycle is $4,500. You keep 80% of what you withdraw.

Account Rules
How the EOD Trailing Drawdown Works
The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End of Day (EOD) trailing drawdown. It does not move intraday. It only adjusts after the market closes each session, based on where your account closed.
The MLL trails upward as your account equity reaches new end-of-day highs.
It never moves down.
The MLL locks permanently once it reaches $100 above the starting balance. From that point, it does not trail further.
How the Soft Daily Loss Limit Works
The Soft DLL ($2,500 on the Builder 150K) is a soft pause, not a breach. If your intraday losses reach the Soft DLL, trading stops for the remainder of that trading session. Your account remains active, and you resume the next trading session as normal.
It applies during both the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages. The only rule that can close your account is breaching the MLL.
News Trading
T1 news trading is allowed during the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages. You may open and hold positions through any scheduled T1 news event without limitation.
