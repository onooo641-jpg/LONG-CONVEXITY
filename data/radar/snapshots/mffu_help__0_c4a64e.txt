What is MyFundedClub?
MyFundedClub is the official loyalty program of MyFundedFutures, designed to reward traders for their engagement and achievements. Members can earn points by referring friends, participating in the community, and reaching key milestones throughout their trading journey.
These points can then be redeemed for valuable rewards, including evaluation discounts, exclusive merchandise, and card packs, making every step of the experience more rewarding.
Is There a Cost to Join?
Not at all. MyFundedClub is completely free to join, and there is no cost to participate or earn points.
​
Who Can Join MyFundedClub?
MyFundedClub is open to eligible traders who meet a few simple requirements. To participate, you must:
Be at least 18 years of age.
Register as an individual trader.
Accept the MyFundedClub Terms and Conditions during the enrollment process.
Please note that membership is not available to:
Companies
Trading groups
Signal-selling businesses or operations
Important: Reward packs and points are only issued after the Terms and Conditions have been reviewed and accepted.
In addition, participants must reside in a location where MyFundedFutures and the loyalty program are legally permitted to operate and may not be employees, contractors, or immediate family members of MyFundedFutures team members.
To ensure a fair and rewarding experience for everyone, each person is limited to one membership account. Duplicate memberships may be voided, and MyFundedFutures reserves the right to apply reasonable limits based on factors such as email address, household, payment method, or device.
These measures help protect the integrity of the program and ensure rewards are distributed fairly among members.
Earning Points
How do I Earn Points?
MyFundedClub rewards members for a variety of activities that support their trading journey and engagement with the community. Points can be earned by completing qualifying actions, including:
Referrals – Share your unique referral link and earn points when a new trader signs up through your link and completes a qualifying purchase within 30 days.
Community Participation – Get involved with the MyFundedFutures community by joining and following official channels, such as Discord and other supported platforms.
Trading Milestones – Earn points for reaching specific achievements throughout your trading journey, such as completing qualifying trading days, maintaining compliance with account risk parameters, or successfully progressing through an evaluation stage. Points are awarded for predefined milestones only and are never based on trade size, trading frequency, profitability, or risk-taking behavior. Participation in the loyalty program does not alter your account's trading rules or requirements.
Re-Engagement Activities – If an account closes, you can continue earning points by getting back into the action through qualifying activities, such as purchasing a reset or starting a new evaluation.
For the latest list of eligible point-earning activities and their current point values, visit www.myfundedfutures.com/loyalty . As MyFunded Club evolves, available activities and point values may be updated to provide members with new ways to earn and engage.
How Do Referrals Work?
As a MyFunded Club member, you can earn points by referring friends to MyFundedFutures. You'll receive a unique referral link that you can share with others. Rewards depend on whether the person you refer is new to MFFU or already a customer. Referral points are awarded when a new MyFundedFutures customer enrolls through your link and completes a qualifying purchase within 30 days of signing up.
​
Referring a new customer
If you refer a friend who is new to MyFundedFutures, once they complete any purchase:
You receive 2,000 points
Your friend receives 500 points
Do we need to complete KYC to earn referral points?
No. KYC verification is not required at any point to earn or receive Refer a Friend points.
To keep the referral program fair and rewarding for everyone, please follow these important guidelines:
Share Responsibly – Only send your referral link to individuals who have agreed to receive it. Unsolicited or spam-like promotion is not permitted.
No Self-Referrals – Referrals involving accounts controlled by the same individual or household are not eligible for rewards and may be voided.
Qualifying Purchases Must Remain Valid – If a referred purchase is later refunded, cancelled, or subject to a chargeback, any associated referral points will be removed from your account.
Represent MyFundedFutures Accurately – When discussing MyFundedFutures, always be truthful and transparent. Do not guarantee profits, funded account approvals, trading success, or specific tax outcomes. Misleading claims can result in the loss of referral points and may lead to suspension from the program.
Referral Reward Limits May Apply – To maintain program integrity, MyFundedFutures may place reasonable limits on the number of referral rewards that can be earned during a specific period.
By following these guidelines, you can confidently share MyFundedFutures with others while earning points and helping the community grow.
Where Can I See My Points Balance?
You can view your current MyFundedClub points balance at any time through your MyFundedFutures account dashboard . Simply navigate to the MyFunded Club section to see your available points, track your activity, and monitor your progress toward rewards.
Do My Points Expire?
Not as long as you remain active in the program. Your points will only expire after 24 consecutive months without any point-earning activity or reward redemptions . Any qualifying activity or redemption resets this inactivity period, helping you keep your balance active.
To ensure you have ample time to use your points, MyFundedFutures will provide notice by email and through your dashboard at least 60 days before any points are scheduled to expire .
Can Points Ever Be Removed from My Balance?
In limited circumstances, points may be adjusted or removed to maintain the accuracy and integrity of the program. This may occur when:
A purchase associated with the points is refunded, cancelled, or charged back .
Points were credited in error .
A qualifying community action is later reversed, such as immediately unfollowing a channel after receiving points.
Points were earned through fraudulent activity, automation, abuse, or other forms of manipulation .
If points are removed after rewards have already been redeemed, your account may show a negative points balance . In that situation, any future points you earn will first be applied toward restoring your balance before additional points become available for redemption.
Redeeming Points
What Can I Redeem My Points For?
As you earn points through MyFundedClub, you can redeem them for a variety of rewards designed to enhance your MyFundedFutures experience.
Current reward categories include:
Evaluation Discounts – Use points to save on new evaluations, resets, and other eligible MyFundedFutures products.
Official Merchandise – Redeem points for exclusive MyFundedFutures apparel and gear, available while supplies last.
BOGO Offers – From time to time, members may have the opportunity to redeem points for special buy-one-get-one evaluation promotions.
Card Packs – Unlock mystery-style reward packs that contain guaranteed prizes and benefits. Details on how Card Packs work can be found below.
The most current reward catalog, including available items and point costs, can always be found at www.myfundedfutures.com/loyalty .
How Do Card Packs Work?
Card Packs offer a fun and rewarding way to redeem your points while still providing transparency about what you'll receive.
Before redeeming, each Card Pack clearly displays:
The required point cost.
The guaranteed reward categories included in the pack.
Any applicable ranges or outcomes for randomized rewards.
For example, a Card Pack may guarantee:
One discount reward
One free account reward
One bonus prize
While the categories are guaranteed, the specific reward or tier within each category remains a surprise until the pack is opened.
Most importantly, every Card Pack delivers all advertised reward categories . There are no empty packs or losing outcomes.
Please note:
Card Pack rewards are single-use .
Rewards are non-transferable .
Unless otherwise stated, rewards expire 30 days after issuance .
Be sure to redeem and use your rewards before they expire.
Can I Combine a Reward Discount With a Promo Code?
Generally, no. Reward-based discounts cannot be combined with promotional codes or other offers unless the specific promotion explicitly states that stacking is permitted.
In addition, reward discounts:
Apply only to the eligible evaluation or reset purchase price.
Do not apply to other fees or charges.
Are typically single-use unless otherwise specified.
Are There Limits on Redemptions?
Certain rewards may be subject to redemption limits within a given period to ensure fair access for all members.
Examples may include:
A maximum number of merchandise redemptions per month.
A limit on evaluation discount redemptions during a calendar month.
Special limits tied to promotional rewards or limited-time offers.
Any active redemption limits will be displayed at www.myfundedfutures.com/loyalty .
What Should I Know About Merchandise Redemptions?
MyFundedFutures merchandise is offered on a first-come, first-served basis and is available only while inventory lasts.
When redeeming merchandise:
You'll provide shipping information through our designated fulfillment partner.
Shipping charges, customs duties, taxes, or other import fees are your responsibility unless otherwise stated.
Shipping details must be confirmed within 30 days of redemption.
Failure to complete the shipping process within the required timeframe may result in forfeiture of the reward.
Due to the nature of reward redemptions, merchandise orders are generally considered final, except in cases involving verified product defects.
Do Discounted Evaluations or Resets Have Different Rules?
No. Using a MyFunded Club reward does not change the terms, requirements, or functionality of your account.
Any evaluation or reset purchased using a points-based discount operates exactly the same as a standard purchase, including:
The same risk parameters
The same profit targets
The same trading rules
The same payout eligibility requirements
Rewards simply provide additional value to members. They do not alter the trading experience or create special account conditions.
Points, Your Account & the Rules
Do Points Have Cash Value?
No. MyFunded Club points are a loyalty reward and do not have any cash value. Points cannot be redeemed for cash or cryptocurrency and may not be sold, gifted, assigned, or transferred to another person, account, or membership.
Points are intended solely for use within the MyFunded Club rewards program.
Does MyFundedClub Change Any of My Trading Rules?
No. MyFunded Club operates independently from your trading accounts and does not affect any aspect of your evaluation, funded, or live trading experience.
Earning or redeeming points will not impact:
Drawdown limits
Profit targets
Payout eligibility
Risk parameters
Trading permissions
Any other account rules
All trading account terms continue to be governed exclusively by your applicable Trader Agreement and program rules.
What Happens to My Points if I Breach an Account or Fail an Evaluation?
In most cases, nothing happens to your points. Your MyFunded Club membership and points balance are tied to you as a member, not to any individual trading account. A failed evaluation or account breach does not affect the points you've already earned.
In fact, members may have opportunities to earn additional points by re-engaging with the platform through qualifying activities, such as purchasing a reset or starting a new evaluation.
There is one important exception:
If your account is suspended, banned, or terminated due to a violation of MyFundedFutures' Terms of Service or MyFunded Club program rules, any unredeemed points may be forfeited.
Is There a Limit to the Rewards I Can Receive?
Yes. To ensure the program remains sustainable and fair for all participants, the total combined value of rewards and promotional benefits received from MyFundedFutures is subject to an annual limit.
This includes:
MyFunded Club points and rewards
Discounts
Promotional offers
Contest prizes
Other eligible MyFundedFutures incentives
The maximum combined value a member may receive is $2,000 USD during any rolling 12-month period . If additional rewards would cause a member to exceed this limit, MyFundedFutures may reduce, withhold, or defer those benefits until they become eligible under a future period.
Can I Leave the Program?
Absolutely. Participation in MyFundedClub is entirely voluntary, and you may opt out at any time by contacting [email protected] .
Before leaving the program, please note the following:
Any unredeemed points will be forfeited upon cancellation.
Forfeited points cannot be restored.
If you choose to rejoin the program in the future, previously forfeited points will not be reinstated.
For that reason, members may wish to redeem any available rewards before requesting to leave the program.
Changes, Support & Important Program Details
Can MyFundedClub Change Over Time?
Yes. As MyFundedClub continues to evolve, MyFundedFutures may periodically update eligible earning activities, point values, available rewards, redemption options, or other program features to enhance the member experience.
Any changes are applied prospectively , meaning they affect future participation and do not retroactively reduce the value of points that have already been earned. In general:
Points already in your balance will not be reduced due to program updates.
Previously earned points will not have their redemption value retroactively diminished.
Changes to earning opportunities and reward offerings will apply going forward.
The only exceptions may involve situations involving fraud, abuse, misuse of the program, or a complete discontinuation of MyFunded Club.
If the program is ever discontinued, members will receive at least 30 days' advance notice and will have that entire notice period to redeem any remaining eligible points before the program ends.
