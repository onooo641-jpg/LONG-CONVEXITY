What are the pros and cons of being a prop trader?
Pros include access to capital, training, and potential high profits. Cons include risk of losses, performance pressure, and profit-sharing with the firm.
Do prop firms really pay out?
Yes, reputable prop firms do pay traders a share of their profits based on agreed-upon terms and performance.
What is a prop trading firm?
A prop trading firm is a financial company that provides capital to traders (prop traders) to trade the firm's money in financial markets, aiming to generate profits for both the trader and the firm.
Do you charge any other fees other than the initial assessment cost?
We do not have any hidden fees.
All relevant trading commissions depending on the price feed selected, spread or swaps are communicated via our FAQ section, accessible at all times before completion of the purchase.
You are able to customize any of the plans offered at the checkout if you wish - based on each account's available customization such as the extra cost based on the add-on for an account.
The purchase fee covers the challenge and verification.
Your purchase fee gets reimbursed to you with your first payout on request for 1,2 and 3 Phase plans.
What is the leverage?
Our Challenge accounts have the following leverage 30:1 (FX* &  Gold) Indices 10:1, Oil 5:1, Stocks 2:1, other commodities 10:1
We do not offer excessive leverage as this can cause traders to breach their accounts.
You can also customize the leverage at the checkout and increase it to a generous 50:1 ( FX & Gold )
