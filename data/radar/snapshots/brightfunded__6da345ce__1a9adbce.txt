Is there a minimum Reward?
1-Step Daily and 2-Step Bright: there is no minimum payout. On 1-Step Daily, 1% of your closed balance stays in the account as a buffer.
Instant: the minimum payout is 1% of your account size, and a 3% buffer must be built before your first payout.
OLD plans: there is no minimum. You can request any amount.
What are the extra requirements on Instant?
Instant accounts have a few extra conditions to request a payout, including the 15% consistency rule, a minimum number of profitable days and a profit buffer. See How do payouts work on Instant? for the full list.
How can I request my payout?
You can easily request your payout from the Payout Section in the BrightFunded Dashboard . Close all your trades and pending orders first.
Once your payment has been requested, it will take a maximum of 1 day to be processed by our finance team. It can take a little longer to be reflected in your wallet or bank, depending on the broker and your bank institution.
15% Evaluation Reward
A 15% bonus of your evaluation profits that is added to the starting balance of your next Funded Account once the conditions are met. It applies to 1-Step Daily, 2-Step Bright and the OLD plans. It does not apply to Instant , because Instant has no evaluation phase.
How it works (step by step)
Reach at least 10% growth on your Funded Account(s).
You may combine growth across multiple funded accounts (e.g., 5% on the first + 5% on the second = 10%).
Receive your payout for that performance.
You are issued a new Funded Account whose initial balance includes the 15% bonus calculated from your evaluation profits.
Example (illustrative)
Account size: $100,000
Suppose you earned $13,000 in total during the evaluation.
Bonus: 15% of $13,000 = $1,950 .
After you hit 10% growth on your funded account(s) and receive your payout, your new Funded Account starts at $101,950 .
Can I withdraw the bonus?
Yes. You can withdraw it with your next payout , whether you keep trading or simply wait to cash it out according to your profit split. (You need to place a single trade to activate the payout cycle.)
For the full explanation, see 15% Evaluation Reward .
