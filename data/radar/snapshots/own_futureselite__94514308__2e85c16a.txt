Max Loss Limit
The drawdown is End of Day: based on your closed balance, unchanged after a losing day and raised only after a profitable day. Once your equity reaches your starting balance plus the Max Loss Limit, the limit locks at your starting balance.

Keep the account active
Place at least one trade every 7 calendar days. Instant accounts bought after 23 November 2025 are breached if no trade is placed for 7 days.
