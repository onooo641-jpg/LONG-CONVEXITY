Alpha Futures is proud to offer a flexible Live Trader Program and the Opportunity to work beside our Professional Trading Floor, Alpha Prime.
Unlike an Evaluation or Qualified Account, the Traders called to LIVE are trading real live capital and markets.
All of our live traders enjoy:
Daily Payouts
No Consistency Rule
No news trading restrictions
No maximum withdrawal request
EOD Style Drawdown
Scaling Daily Loss Limit (30% of account)
The Alpha Futures Risk and Alpha Prime Teams constantly watch trader performance to determine if a trader is ready for live capital.
A trader may be reviewed or called to live immediately if they have met one or more of the following conditions:
5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account
A significant amount of lifetime performance fees
Outstanding performance in a Qualified Account
Former member of the Generic Live Program or Alpha Prime Program
Transition to Live
When a trader is called to live, all Qualified Accounts are closed
Traders receive one live account per eligible Qualified Account
Eligible Qualified Account = a Qualified Account that has reached at least one payout.

From here, it is the Trader's choice how they would like to continue:
1.) Alpha Futures Live Program
Traders elevate into the live markets with their eligible account(s) at an 80% profit split , with daily, uncapped withdrawals on any of their gains above starting live balance.
​
​ 2.) Alpha Prime Program
This is best for traders that are looking to develop into true professionals and build a career out of trading, not just a hobby. Alpha Futures' mission is to find talented traders, and retain them. We call our most impressive and most consistent traders up to live funds to trade alongside our team at Alpha Prime.
The professional traders at the Alpha Prime Trading Floor in London have been in the Traditional Prop space for more than 30 years, and are eager to bridge the gap between retail prop firms (sim props) and Traditional..
As mentioned, all Qualified Accounts are closed at their current balance when a trader is called to live. 50% of the existing Qualified Account(s) sim profit (max $75,000) will be split into a 12 MONTHLY BONUS as long as the Trader remains a part of the Alpha Prime Program. Monthly Bonus is unlocked in any month the Trader takes a withdrawal of their starting drawdown balance or larger. Traders may elect to receive the monthly bonus at an 80% split, or add 100% of the value as balance to their live account.
What are the benefits of the Alpha Prime program for traders?
Monthly Bonus
Weekly Calls with Alpha Prime Team for strategy development, refinement, risk management, and market analysis. Alpha Prime is ACTIVELY invested in your success.
Developing automated strategies with our in house Quantitative Team
Opportunities to visit the Alpha Prime trading floor in London to continue to learn from 7+ figure Professional Traders in person.
Scaling Opportunities: performance based increase in fund allocation and/or bonuses
Traders that excel in the Alpha Prime Program will be considered to increase their fund allocation, and even full time employment.
If an individual selects to go with the Alpha Prime Program, they will receive all of these resources above and a 60% profit split on all profits withdrawn from their live account(s). Both firm and trader deeply invest in the trader's success and enable the trader to truly grow into a professional.
​
Traders in the Prime program also may have the opportunity to opt for a singular static account up to $10,000 as well if the Alpha Prime team sees it as a better fit.
​
Traders that decide to go with the Alpha Futures Live Program will be sacrificing any Qualified Account sim profits, bonuses, as well as forgoing any of the Alpha Prime resources.
Traders that believe that they are not ready for live markets yet may elect to take a final payout of 50% of their Eligible Live Drawdown balance. This will result in a minimum 3 month cool down before they may return to Alpha simulated products.
