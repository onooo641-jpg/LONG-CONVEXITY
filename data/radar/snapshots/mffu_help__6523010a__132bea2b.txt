Quantower Download and Connection Guide

Quantower is a versatile and intuitive trading platform that provides both novice and experienced traders with advanced tools for market analysis and seamless execution across various instruments.
Let's Get Started!
Click on the Downloads button from your MFFU dashboard and select Quantower to download then follow the installation process.
Once the setup is complete:
Launch Quantower
On the Quantower dashboard, click dxFeed to set up connections.
In the connections window, ensure you select MyFundedFutures . Enter your credentials, which MyFundedFutures will send to you via email, and then click Connect to proceed.
When you are successfully connected to your MFFU trading account via Quantower, you will see the connection status. You are now online and ready to trade!
Get Funded to Trade on Quantower
How to trade using Quantower:
To view your Account Information , click Acc from your dashboard.
To see your Working Orders , click Ord . To check your Positions , click Pos from your dashboard.
For Order Entry , click OE from your dashboard and ensure you choose the instrument you would like to trade from the Symbol tab.
To open the Charts , click Cht from your dashboard and search for the instrument you wish to trade using the search bar.
