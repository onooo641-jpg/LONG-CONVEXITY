Which trading platforms you can use on evaluations, Pro, S2F, S2L and live accounts, including Mac options.
Updated today
Copy for LLM
Evaluations, Pro Accounts, S2F and S2L evaluations all use the same platforms, connected through Rithmic.
Simulated accounts
ArcTrader (recommended): our main platform, fully supported by DayTraders. Works in your browser at arctrader.com/platform. Sign in with your Rithmic credentials. The free ArcTrader Crypto Demo signs in with Crypto instead.
Onyx : license included; works in a browser, as a Mac desktop app and on mobile.
Quantower : license included; sign in to the DayTraders.com server with your Rithmic credentials.
rTrader Pro : Rithmic's order and position tool, and the record of your trade history.
Other Rithmic platforms : ATAS, Sierra Chart, MotiveWave, Bookmap, Jigsaw, VolFix, Finamark and WealthCharts. Some need their own license or subscription from the provider.
To connect, sign your Rithmic agreements with the credentials on your Dashboard, choose Non-Professional , and select the DayTraders.com system (not Rithmic Paper Trading).
Live accounts
Onyx is offered and the free version of Quantower is available. Other platforms need a paid license.
NinjaTrader is not officially supported. Some traders connect it through Rithmic, but we can't help with setup or connection issues.
Mac users
ArcTrader and Onyx work on Mac in the browser. Rithmic isn't fully compatible with macOS; to use rTrader Pro or other Windows platforms, run Windows with Parallels or VirtualBox, or use the Rithmic Trader web app. These options aren't officially supported.
ArcTrader is fully supported by DayTraders. For other third-party platforms we offer limited support. For platform issues, contact the platform's support.
