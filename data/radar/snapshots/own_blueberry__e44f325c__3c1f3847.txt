How much can I earn as an affiliate?
May 5, 2025
You’ll earn based on the number of referrals you generate:
Tier 1: 15% (0–20 referrals)
Tier 2: 18% (20–100 referrals)
Tier 3: 20% (100+ referrals)
Master Affiliate: Earn an additional 5% override on sub-affiliates you refer.
You can also earn monthly bonus rewards of up to $250 + a free 50K evaluation based on your monthly conversions.
We currently have no caps on commission earnings for our affiliate partners.
