What is leverage, and what leverage is offered during the evaluation and for Qualified Analyst accounts?
July 22, 2026
Leverage is a financial tool that allows traders to control a larger position size with a smaller amount of capital. It magnifies both potential profits and potential losses.
Please trade responsibly and be aware of the risks that leverage poses to your trading performance. Although no capital is at risk on our evaluation or Qualified Analyst accounts, leverage can have a significant impact on your trading strategy.
