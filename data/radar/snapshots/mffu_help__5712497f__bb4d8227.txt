Inactivity Rule - One-Time Payment Model
This is MyFundedFutures' policy on the inactivity rule for new customers.
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Effective August 25, 2026, MyFundedFutures will transition to a one-time payment model for all new evaluation purchases. If you're a current subscriber, nothing changes: you'll continue as a valued legacy customer under your existing price, plan, billing cycle, and terms as outlined in your current subscription plan.
This update applies only to purchases made on or after August 25, 2026 — and for those new evaluations, there is no renewal cycle since the one-time payment model replaces recurring billing entirely. Resets remain unaffected and continue to work the same way regardless of purchase date.
Inactivity Rule Changes (Effective August 25th)
New accounts purchased under the one-time payment model are subject to a new inactivity policy:
If a trader does not place a trade for 7 consecutive calendar days , the account could be closed. This includes simulated funded accounts.
An inactivity breach on a Sim Funded account results in the forfeiture of all profits.
Because there is no monthly billing to pause or cancel under this new payment model, staying active within this 7-day window is required to keep a one-time payment evaluation account in good standing.
Vacations
Traders taking a vacation should notify the customer support team if they will not be trading for more than 7 days.
