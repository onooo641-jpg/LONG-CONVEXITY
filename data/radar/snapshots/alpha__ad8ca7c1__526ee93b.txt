How Do Funded Futures Accounts Work? (2026 Guide)
By the Alpha Futures Team · Updated November 22, 2026
A funded futures account is a simulated trading account you earn by passing a prop firm's evaluation, not a brokerage account holding your money.
After you pass, you keep trading under the firm's rules, such as a maximum loss limit and contract limits. When you meet its conditions, you can request a performance fee, which is a share of your simulated profit. Breach the main loss rule and the account is closed.
At Alpha Futures this is called a Qualified Account: on Zero, Standard and Advanced you can request a performance fee after 5 winning days of $200 or more and receive 90% of each approved request.

FAQs
How do funded futures accounts work?
You pass a prop firm's evaluation on a simulated account, then trade a funded (at Alpha Futures, "Qualified") account under the same kind of rules and can request performance fees on simulated profit. It is not a brokerage account with your money in it, and performance fees are not guaranteed.
Is a Qualified Account a live brokerage account?
No. It is a simulated account with a nominal size label. Performance fees may be monetary rewards based on performance under published policy. That is different from trading live margin at a broker.
How is this different from a live futures account?
Live accounts use broker margin and your capital (or credit). Prop Qualified Accounts use simulated funds and program rules. Different product, different risk.
Does Alpha Futures offer skip-eval access without an evaluation?
Yes. Direct skips the evaluation: you start on a simulated Qualified Account for a one-time fee.
Who is the best futures prop firm?
It depends on your trading style. Compare the type of loss limit, consistency rules, contract limits, news rules, performance split and total cost. Alpha Futures offers one-step evaluations on simulated accounts, an end-of-day trailing Maximum Loss Limit and a flat 90% performance split. Do you need $25,000 to day trade futures?
Not for a futures prop evaluation. You pay a monthly evaluation fee and trade simulated funds. Read Do You Need $25K to Day Trade Futures? .
Is $100 enough to trade futures?
$100 is not enough for live futures margin. Evaluations start at $89/month on Zero $25K. See Cheapest Futures Prop Firm (2026) .
Which futures prop firms have no consistency rule?
Alpha Futures Zero evaluations have no consistency rule on the evaluation stage. Standard evaluations use 50% consistency and Advanced evaluations use 40% until you pass. Advanced Qualified Accounts have no consistency rule; Zero and Standard Qualified Accounts use 40% between performance fee requests, and Direct uses 20% for performance fee eligibility. Premium is discontinued. See Futures Prop Firms With No Consistency Rule (2026) .
