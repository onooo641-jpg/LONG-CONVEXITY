What Is The CFD Prime (Swing) Reward Policy?
On CFD Prime (Swing) you can receive a reward of up to 80% of the notional profit generated in your Earning Account, with a minimum reward of $100 per request.
When Can I Request A Reward?
Your first reward can be requested 14 days after you receive your Earning Account.
After that, you can request every 14 days for amounts above $100.
Reward requests are processed daily, Monday to Friday, within 24 hours on business days.
Does A Payout Affect My Maximum Drawdown?
Yes. Your Maximum Drawdown limit doesn't move down after a payout, and there's no cap on how much you can request. If your balance reaches the limit after a payout, the account is breached, so it's your responsibility to leave enough buffer when you choose how much to request.
Trailing accounts: once your limit has locked at your starting balance, requesting your full profit brings your balance down to the limit and breaches the account. Example: your balance is $110,000 and your limit is locked at $100,000. Requesting your full $10,000 profit drops your balance to $100,000 and breaches the account. Requesting $6,000 leaves your balance at $104,000, with a $4,000 buffer.
Static accounts: a payout alone can't breach your account, because you can only withdraw profit and your limit is below your starting balance. Your buffer still gets smaller after every payout. Example: your balance is $110,000 and your limit is $90,000. After a $10,000 payout, your balance is $100,000 and you have a $10,000 buffer.
For how each drawdown type works, see Maximum Drawdown in CFD Prime Rules.
Trading Requirements Before A Reward
Accounts created after 6 March 2026: at least 3 profitable trading days, each gaining at least 0.5% of the initial balance.
Accounts created before 6 March 2026: at least 5 trading days, regardless of outcome. If most of the profit was made on the first day, you must keep trading with the same approach or the reward may be declined by the Risk Team.
Accounts created after 5 July 2026 (CFD 1-Phase $100K/$200K): the 50% Consistency Rule must also be met. [Latest]
Transfer Timeframes
Crypto: within the day.
International wire / SWIFT: 5–7 business days.
SEPA transfer: 1–3 business days.
Can I Withdraw Anything I Make During The Challenge?
No. Trading in the Challenge takes place on a demo account with simulated funds, so there is nothing to withdraw — the Challenge only proves your trading against the targets and rules.
You can request rewards only after you pass and move to the Earning Phase.
Can I Get A Refund After I Move To The Earning Phase?
Yes, once you reach the Earning Phase your Challenge fee can be refunded, based on when the account was purchased.
Accounts purchased before 29 April 2026: refunded after your 1st payout is approved.
Accounts purchased on or after 29 April 2026: refunded after your 3rd payout.
The refund is tied to successful performance in the Earning Phase — if the payout milestone isn’t met, the refund isn’t issued.
Are There Any Reward Fees?
To cover transaction/gas fees, a 1% fee is deducted from the trader receiving the payout. It is calculated on your final reward share amount, not on the total profit made in the account.
In What Currency Can I Receive My Reward?
Accounts are supported in Euro, US Dollar (for bank transfers) and Cryptocurrency, and rewards can be paid in a range of currencies.
You choose your preferred method and currency when submitting the request.
If you pick a currency other than EUR or USD, the amount is converted at the exchange rate on the day of the transaction.
What Withdrawal Methods Are Available, And Can I Receive Rewards In Crypto?
We currently support bank wire transfers and cryptocurrency for reward withdrawals, with more methods coming.
For bank wires you’ll be asked to upload documentation showing the bank name, branch, account number and account holder’s name.
