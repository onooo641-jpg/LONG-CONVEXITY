Prime funded stage and payouts
Funded target, buffer, consistency and caps.
October 18, 2026
Table of contents
When you can request
You've reached the funded profit target: $250 (25K), $500 (50K), $900 (100K) or $1,350 (150K).
Your balance is above the payout buffer: $1,100 above start on 25K ($26,100), $2,100 on 50K ($52,100). Prime 100K and 150K funded accounts have a buffer of $3,100 and $4,600.
Your request is at least the minimum: $500, $500, $900 or $1,100.
On 25K, you have at least 3 trading days. Other sizes have no minimum days.
Buffer
The buffer is a profit cushion that is never withdrawable. Everything above it is. Example, 50K: balance $53,000 minus $52,100 = $900 withdrawable, above the $500 minimum.
Consistency
No single trading day can be more than 40% of the amount you request. If one day is above 40%, keep trading until its share drops below 40%.
