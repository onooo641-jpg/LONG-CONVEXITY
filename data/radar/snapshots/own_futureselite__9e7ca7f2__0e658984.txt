What you agree to when trading live.

Table of contents
Attach a stop order to every open position, without exception.
Close all positions at least 15 minutes before the market closes, or they are liquidated automatically.
Place your first trade within 15 calendar days of your credentials being sent and the account being created; if no trade is placed in that time, the live account is lost and closed permanently.
Keep your phone and email up to date and stay reachable while trading live.
FuturesElite may close trades, pause activity or terminate an account at any time if risk, compliance or operational concerns arise.
Holding long and short in the same instrument at once, in one account or across accounts you control, means an immediate permanent ban, all accounts closed and locked Reserve forfeited.
Commissions and fees are deducted automatically. Regulatory and exchange fees post during overnight settlement, around 2:00 AM CT; if they push the balance below the account's loss limit, the live account may be closed.
If you lose a live account, you can buy a new evaluation after 1 month.
Eligibility
You can't hold a live account if you have felony convictions or financial crimes on record, have been banned or sanctioned by regulators, owe money to a clearing firm or broker, or have used fraudulent payment methods or filed chargebacks.
Costs
Your own live market data subscription for every exchange you trade.
Commissions and exchange fees on every trade, deducted automatically.
Any platform licence fees.
Bank or broker transfer fees on withdrawals.
No hidden administrative fees.
