Frequently Asked Questions
What is Instant Funding?
Instant Funding gives you direct access to a Simulated Funded Account from day one . No evaluation, no challenges, and no waiting to qualify.
You can start trading immediately and work toward your first payout.
What are the Drawdown Rules for the Instant Funding Account?
The Instant Funding has two loss limits:
Daily Loss Limit: 2% (Equity-Based)
The daily drawdown resets based on the higher of your equity or balance at 00:00 UTC .
Your account must not exceed a 2% loss within a trading day.
Example: You have a $10,000 account , open a trade, and leave it running overnight. At 00:00 UTC , your equity is $10,300 , while your balance is $10,000 . Your maximum daily drawdown is 2% of $10,300 ($206) . Your account will breach the daily drawdown if your equity falls below $10,094 that day.
Maximum Drawdown: 3% (Trailing)
Your maximum drawdown moves up with your highest running equity , including unrealized profit from open trades.
Example: With a $10,000 simulated funded account and a 3% trailing drawdown , the initial drawdown limit is $9,700 . If your running equity reaches $11,000 , the trailing limit moves up to $10,670 . Falling below $10,670 would breach the drawdown limit.
Note: The trailing drawdown is based on running equity , not just closed trades.
Exceeding the limit will result in a breach of the Simulated Funded Account .
What is the 1% Max Risk rule in Instant Funding?
The 1% Maximum Risk Rule is based only on your current floating loss relative to your current account balance. It is not a trailing drawdown rule and is not calculated from your highest floating profit or equity.
Formula: Floating Loss = Balance − Equity
Example: $10,000 Account
Balance Equity Floating Loss Status
$10,000 $9,970 $30 (0.3%) ✅ No breach
$10,000 $9,900 $100 (1%) ⚠️ 1% threshold reached
$10,000 $9,899 $101 (1.01%) ❌ Breach
For a $10,000 account, the maximum permitted floating loss is $100.

How Is the 20% Consistency Score Calculated?
Your largest winning day must not exceed 20% of your total profit in the current payout cycle. Total profit counts all trading days, including losing days, so a losing day lowers the total and raises your score. The calculation resets after each payout.
Formula: Consistency Score = (Best Winning Day ÷ Total Profit) × 100%
