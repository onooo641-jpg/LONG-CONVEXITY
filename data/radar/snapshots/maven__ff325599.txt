1. Trading & Account Review (1–3 Business Days)
You must meet both the profit target and the minimum profitable-day requirement for each evaluation phase. Reaching the profit target alone does not qualify you to pass. After you complete the final phase and satisfy all requirements, your trades will automatically close, and your account will be disabled and placed under review.
2. Receive Your Challenge Certificate
Our team will review your trading history. Once approved, you’ll receive a congratulatory email and your Challenge Certificate.
3. Complete KYC & Receive Your Simulated Funded Account
You’ll receive a KYC email with instructions to submit the required documents. Once your KYC is successfully verified, typically within 1 business day , you’ll receive your Simulated Funded Account.
What are the Drawdown Rules for the Standard 2 Step Challenge and Simulated Funded Account?
The Standard 2 Step Challenge and Simulated Funded Account use the same loss limits: 4% daily loss and 8% static maximum drawdown.
Daily Loss Limit: 4% (Equity-Based)
The daily drawdown resets based on the higher of your equity or balance at 00:00 UTC .
Your account must not exceed a 4% loss within a trading day.
Example: You have a $10,000 account , open a trade, and leave it running overnight. At 00:00 UTC , your equity is $10,300 , while your balance is $10,000 . Your maximum daily drawdown is 4% of $10,300 ($412) . Your account will breach the daily drawdown if your equity falls below $9,888 that day.
Maximum Drawdown: 8% (Static)
Your maximum drawdown is 8% of the initial account balance and remains fixed.
The drawdown limit does not increase when your account equity or balance grows.
Example: With a $10,000 account , your maximum drawdown is $800 , so your equity must not fall below $9,200 .
Exceeding either limit will result in a breach of the Challenge or Simulated Funded Account.
Is there a minimum number of profitable days required for the Standard 2 Step Account?
You need at least 3 profitable days in each evaluation phase and 3 new profitable days for each funded payout cycle. Each qualifying day must earn at least 0.5% of the initial account balance. The days do not need to be consecutive.
Example for a $10,000 account:
Days 1, 3, and 4 count as the 3 required profitable days. Day 2 does not qualify because the profit is below 0.5%.

Is there an inactivity rule in the Standard 2 Step Account?
Accounts must have at least one trading activity within every 30 days to remain active.
Is there a minimum holding time for each trade?
At least 50% of your trades must be held for at least 60 seconds (1 minute). Trades held for exactly 60 seconds qualify.
Is News Trading allowed in the Standard 2 Step Account?
The Red Folder news restrictions in the General FAQ apply to this Challenge and Simulated Funded Account, including pending orders, stop-losses, take-profits and automatic profit-target closures.
Example: If a Red Folder news release is scheduled for 2:00 PM , you cannot open or close a trade between 1:58 PM and 2:02 PM .
What is the reward split for a Standard 2 Step Simulated Funded Account?
The standard reward split is 80%. With the applicable add-on, the reward split increases to 90%.
Note : Add-ons must be selected at checkout when purchasing the account. They cannot be added after the account has been purchased.
What is the Minimum Withdrawal for a Standard 2 Step Simulated Funded Account?
The minimum withdrawal request is $20 in profit before the reward split is applied. The amount you receive is your applicable share of that profit.
How Often Can I Request a Payout for a Standard 2 Step Simulated Funded Account?
Your first payout is available 10 business days after your first trade on the Simulated Funded Account. Each subsequent 10-business-day period starts with your first trade after the previous payout.
