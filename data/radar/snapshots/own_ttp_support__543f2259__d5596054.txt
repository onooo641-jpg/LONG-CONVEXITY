What Is The CFD Instant Reward Policy?
On CFD Instant you can receive a reward of up to 80% of the notional profit you generate in the Earning Phase. The key payout terms are:
• Minimum: payouts must be above $200.
• Maximum withdrawal: the lower of 50% of your total realised profit or the cap for your account size:

• Consistency: your biggest profitable day must be within the Consistency Rule for your account size: 30% for $5,000, $10,000 and $20,000; 25% for $50,000; and 20% for $100,000.
• Frequency: your first reward can be requested 14 days after you receive your account, then every 14 days for any amount above $200.
• Second and later payouts: you must have generated profit above $0 since your last request. Any positive profit qualifies, even $0.01.
Does The Drawdown Reset After A Payout?
No. Your drawdown level stays where it is after a payout.
​
Your balance goes down by the amount you withdraw, so you have less room before the drawdown level.
Your payout is capped at the lower of 50% of your realised profit or the cap for your account size, so a payout never takes your balance below the drawdown level.
Example ($5,000 account, 6% = $300):
​
• Your balance is $5,600, and your drawdown level is locked at $5,000.
• You withdraw the maximum of $300 (50% of your $600 profit).
• Your balance becomes $5,300, and your drawdown level stays at $5,000.
​
For how the drawdown trails and locks, see Maximum Drawdown in CFD Instant Rules.
How Are Rewards Paid?
Rewards are paid by bank wire or cryptocurrency.
