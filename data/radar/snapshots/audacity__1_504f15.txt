Trading Rules and Prohibited Practices
You must comply with the trading rules published in our Knowledge Center at https://audacity.capital/knowledge-center/ , as we may update them from time to time. Prohibited Practices. You must not use any of the following strategies or practices on any account:
(a) High-frequency trading (HFT): using Expert Advisors (EAs), bots or algorithms to open and close large volumes of trades within fractions of a second;
(b) Tick scalping: opening and closing trades within a very short window (2 minutes or below) to capture small price fluctuations;
(c) Hedging or group hedging: placing opposing buy and sell positions on the same instrument, whether within one account or across multiple accounts;
(d) Averaging down or martingale: increasing your position size after a loss, on the same losing idea, to recover previous losses;
(e) Dollar cost averaging (DCA): repeatedly adding to a losing position as price moves further against your original entry;
(f) Third-party Expert Advisors: running an EA, bot or algorithm that you did not personally build;
(g) Third-party account management: allowing anyone else to trade your account on your behalf;
(h) Copy trading: mirroring or duplicating another trader's trades onto your account;
(i) Grid trading: placing a pre-set series of buy and sell orders at fixed price intervals;
(j) One-sided bets during news: placing orders ahead of news releases purely to profit from the volatility spike;
(k) Exploiting system vulnerabilities: trading in a way that takes advantage of technical flaws, errors, bugs or pricing anomalies;
(l) Uncritical directional trading: repeatedly placing trades in one direction without any real market assessment;
(m) Automated multi-trade activity: using automated tools to open many positions simultaneously; and
(n) Lot size abuse: opening positions with a lot size that is disproportionately large relative to your account size.
(o) Weekend Gap Trading Rule: Traders are prohibited from intentionally exploiting price gaps that occur between the market close on Friday and the market reopening on Sunday or Monday.
This list is not exhaustive. Further detail and examples are set out in our Knowledge Center at https://audacity.capital/knowledge-center/trading-guidelines/#what-are-the-prohibited-strategies? .

Ability Challenge (2-Step): No Consistency Rule applies unless otherwise specified in the applicable program rules.
Ability One (1-Step): A maximum Consistency Score of 50% applies during the Funded Stage. This requirement does not apply during the evaluation stage.
FTP Instant Funding: A maximum Consistency Score of 35% applies.
The Consistency Score is calculated using the following formula:
Consistency % = (Best Trading Day Profit ÷ Total Net Profit) × 100
A Trader must have a positive Total Net Profit to satisfy the applicable Consistency Rule. The calculation period and treatment of trading costs shall be governed by the applicable program-specific rules published in our Knowledge Center.
Where a Trader has not satisfied the applicable Consistency Rule, the Trader shall not be eligible to receive the affected payout until the requirement is met, unless a different consequence is expressly provided under the applicable program rules.
