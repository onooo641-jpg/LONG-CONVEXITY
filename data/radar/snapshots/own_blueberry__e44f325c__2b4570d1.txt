How can I access support as an affiliate
September 25, 2026
Affiliate support is available by email at [email protected] . Our partnerships team is happy to help with anything related to your affiliate account. 📩
​ What we can help with
Tracking issues — questions about your referral link, attribution, or a referral that didn't register
Payout questions — the $200 minimum, the weekly Riseworks cycle, or a withdrawal that hasn't arrived
Commissions — how tiers and rates work, or a commission that looks incorrect (including voided or refunded referrals)
Marketing resources — banners, landing pages, and custom discount codes for your audience
Getting started — setting up your account or finding your referral link
​ Help us help you faster
​
When you email, include as much detail as you can so we can resolve things in one go:
The email address linked to your affiliate account
Your referral link or affiliate ID
Any relevant order numbers, dates, or amounts
Screenshots of the issue, if you have them
​
​
Our support team is here for you. You can reach us through:
Live chat — available directly on our website and dashboard
Email — [email protected]
