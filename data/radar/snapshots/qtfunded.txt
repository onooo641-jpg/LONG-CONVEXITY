Questions, Answered Plainly.
How do I get started with QT Funded?
Getting started is easy! Simply sign up as a new client using the button in the top right-hand corner of our website and choose the evaluation that best suits your trading style.
Want to learn a little more before getting started?
Check out our easy-to-understand FAQ here .
How quickly can I pass a challenge with QT Funded?
With our QT ONE account, you can pass in just one day — and if you’re really skilled, potentially in just one trade!
Our other evaluation types have a minimum number of required trading days. These are clearly displayed on your dashboard, so you can easily track your progress and see exactly where you are on your journey.
What rules do you enforce?
We want the trading experience at QT Funded to be as clear and transparent as possible.
Our risk management system automatically monitors trading activity and informs traders of any rule breaches during their journey with QT Funded. If a violation occurs, we aim to let you know immediately and will reach out via email to explain what happened and, importantly, how you can avoid it in the future.
We’re here to help, so if anything isn’t clear, you’re always welcome to open a support ticket for further clarification. You can also review our rules and FAQ page before trying again.
Our goal is simple: clear rules, clear communication, and no unnecessary surprises.
I’ve passed! What’s next?
Firstly, congratulations!
Once you’ve completed all the requirements to pass your challenge, simply complete the identity check on your dashboard and keep an eye on your emails.
Once our team has verified your results, you’ll receive a contract to sign. As soon as that’s completed, your account will be released and you’ll be ready to go.
The whole process shouldn’t take longer than 2 business days, and most clients receive their account within 24 hours.
How do payouts work?
Once your trading cycle is complete and you’ve met the requirements to withdraw, simply head to the home page and click Request Payout.
Enter your preferred crypto wallet details, submit your request, and our team will get to work processing your withdrawal. We aim to approve payouts within 1 business day.
As long as you’ve met the requirements, you can request a withdrawal every 4 trading days with QT ONE, while QT TWO and Instant allow withdrawals once every two weeks (14 days).
Simple, straightforward, and easy to track from your dashboard.
