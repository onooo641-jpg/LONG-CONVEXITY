“Show me the money.” That’s what skeptical traders ask on Reddit and Telegram when evaluating prop firms. Fair enough—you’ve seen the horror stories of firms that delay, deny, or disappear when withdrawal time comes. Traders want undeniable payout proof before risking their time and capital. At CTI, we don’t just talk about paying traders; we provide the verifiable payout proof you need, and we’ve built our entire reputation on it.
How Our Payout Pipeline Works
We operate a straightforward 4-step payout process: profit qualification, internal transfer to your Dashboard Wallet within two business days, withdrawal request processing within one business day, and final transfer to your bank or crypto wallet.
We process withdrawal requests within 24 hours, faster than most prop firms. We offer multiple payout methods, including bank transfers and cryptocurrency wallets.
Our Requirements:
– Minimum 10 active trading days.
– Minimum $100 profit (or 2% of account balance).
– All withdrawals must be to accounts under your name (no third-party transfers).
– KYC verification completed before your first payout.

Many say this is a bold claim, but it’s true: with our Instant Funding program , you can receive your first payout in as little as five days .
There’s no evaluation phase, waiting period, or fixed monthly cycle.
When you’re ready to trade, you’re ready to earn — and if you trade profitably from day one, you can request your first withdrawal just five days later.

Is 5 Days to Payout Really Possible?
Yes — and it has been done.
We’ve had traders use Instant Funding, log five profitable days in a row, and receive their payout within 24 hours of their request.
But understand this is the fastest possible timeline. It requires immediate KYC completion, consistent profitable trading from day one, and selecting crypto as your withdrawal method .
It’s not typical, but it is achievable.
What’s a More Typical Timeline?
Most traders don’t trade profitably five days in a row right out of the gate. Some skip days, take losses, or hit their 2% profit threshold a bit slower.
In these more common cases, the first payout usually happens within 7 to 14 calendar days , still among the fastest in the industry.
Also, keep in mind: weekends don’t count as trading days , and our team processes withdrawal approvals on business days.
If your fifth profitable day falls on a Friday and you request your payout that evening, your transaction may not process until Monday.
That’s why we always emphasize: 5 days is the earliest possible, but not the average.
What About 1-Step and 2-Step Challenge Accounts?
If you’re on one of our challenge models, the timeline is a little longer, but still faster than most firms.
However, you’ll need at least 7 Profitable Trading Days to request your first payout. Like Instant Funding, payouts are typically processed within 24 hours once approved.
Again, this is a great deal faster than many firms that enforce 30-day holding periods. We’re proud to offer one of the fastest and most flexible payout systems in prop trading, with real traders backing that up every week.

These aren’t fictional success stories or carefully crafted marketing case studies – they’re real CTI traders who’ve shared their complete journeys, including the challenges, setbacks, and ultimately, the results that made it all worthwhile.

Background: Night shift supermarket warehouse worker balancing trading with family life
Strategy: Gold scalping on 1-minute charts using support/resistance levels
Timeline: Started with $10k account, hit the 10% profit target to scale to $40k
Key Achievement: 12% profit in just 3 days through disciplined risk management
Rees’s story stands out because of his complete transparency about both wins and losses.
After a serious car accident in 2020 left him unemployed during recovery, he used the time to master scalping techniques.
His approach focuses on 30-minute trades during high-volatility sessions, keeping losses below 2% per trade.

Case Study #3: Emmanuel – The Comeback Portfolio Manager
Background: Swing trader managing $200,000 across multiple CTI accounts as portfolio manager
Strategy: Multi-day setups with 2-2.5R risk-to-reward ratio, strict entry criteria
Key Achievement: Lost initial $50k account due to overtrading, then rebuilt with discipline.
After losing his first $50k funded account to overtrading and forced trades, Emmanuel spent two months analyzing every failed trade, discovering that 60% didn’t meet his strict criteria.
Rather than giving up, he purchased two direct-funded $50k accounts and passed both through disciplined execution.
Now spending just 30-60 minutes daily on charts, he maintains a low 3.16% drawdown while managing six-figure capital.
His business mindset treats losses as “cost of goods sold,” demonstrating the mental framework needed for sustainable payout success.
