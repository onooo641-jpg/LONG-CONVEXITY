Daily Loss Limit for S2L evaluations and live accounts, and what happens when you hit it.
Updated over a month ago
Copy for LLM
S2L evaluations and live accounts have a Daily Loss Limit (DLL) : the most you can lose in one trading day. It's separate from the max drawdown.
Plan
Daily Loss Limit
Core
$1,000
Edge
$1,750
Ultra
$3,250
If you hit it
Open positions are closed and the account is Paused until the next trading day starts (6:00 PM ET). It isn't failed.
The DLL is measured from your balance at the start of the day. It doesn't trail intraday.
On a live account, the Risk Team may change or remove the DLL as part of scaling.
