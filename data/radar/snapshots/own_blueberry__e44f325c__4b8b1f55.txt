Do you offer giveaways for affiliates to host?
Affiliate Giveaways
September 25, 2026
Yes , we offer giveaway opportunities to help affiliates engage and grow their audience, including free evaluation account prizes for top performers. 🎁
​
What this looks like
Prizes to run with your audience — free evaluation accounts you can give away to your community as a promotion, competition, or reward.
Custom giveaways — tailored campaigns built around your audience and goals, arranged directly with our partnerships team.
Rewards for top performers — high-performing affiliates can unlock additional giveaway opportunities as part of their partnership.
How to participate
Giveaways are arranged on a case-by-case basis, so the best way to get started is to reach out. Email us at [email protected] and let us know:
Your affiliate account and referral link or ID
Your audience — where you'd run the giveaway (e.g. Discord, YouTube, X) and its rough size
What you have in mind — the type of prize, timing, and how you'd like to structure it
Our team will walk you through the available options and help set up something that works for your audience.

Live chat — available directly on our website and dashboard
