Is Blueberry Futures a Broker?
May 5, 2025
We are an educational and evaluation platform that helps traders learn and demonstrate their skills in futures trading. While we are part of the Blueberry family of brands , which includes the regulated brokerage Blueberry. Blueberry Futures itself does not take client deposits or hold client funds .
Our role is to evaluate traders in a simulated environment and, if successful, provide them with access to funded trading opportunities, including the potential to manage live capital through our proprietary funding pathway.
