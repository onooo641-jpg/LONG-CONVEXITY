Top Advice for Traders
Patience and Discipline: It’s crucial to be patient and disciplined, especially when dealing with losses.
Take Breaks: Regular breaks help maintain a clear mindset and avoid burnout.
Continuous Learning: Utilize online resources, trading communities, and mentorship to enhance your knowledge and strategies.
Proper Risk Management: Limit your risk per trade and avoid over-leveraging to ensure long-term success.
Avoid Overtrading: Focus on quality trades rather than the quantity to prevent unnecessary losses.
Mindset Shift: Learn to accept that not trading is sometimes the best decision. Feeling guilty for not working or not having a position is a common reflex but should be managed.
Journey to Trading
Uger’s interest in trading started during his years in London, working on an equity derivatives desk. This exposure to the market sparked his curiosity, although he initially pursued a career in the mining industry as an IT engineer and manager. The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 served as a catalyst for Uger to transition into full-time trading. The demanding work conditions and the need to spend more time with his family pushed him to explore trading as a viable career option.
Challenges and Breakthroughs
In his early trading days, Uger faced significant challenges, including substantial losses due to poor money management and over-leveraging. Taking a break and seeking mentorship were pivotal in his journey. He learned to focus on technical analysis and liquidity, moving away from relying solely on economic indicators.
Path to Profitability
By January 2022, Uger had gained a deeper understanding of the market, leading to consistent profitability from spring 2022 onwards. He refined his trading strategies, emphasizing technical analysis and careful money management. He learned to be selective with trades and to avoid overtrading.
Current Approach and Future Plans
Uger primarily trades the German index (DAX) and employs a multi-time frame approach, focusing on price action and liquidity. His future goals include continuing to grow his trading capital, leveraging the CTI scaling plan, and potentially engaging in social activities such as mentoring and sharing his experiences with other traders.
Conclusion
Uger’s story is a testament to the power of perseverance, continuous learning, and disciplined trading. His transition from engineering to a successful trading career offers valuable insights for aspiring traders seeking financial freedom and a balanced life.
