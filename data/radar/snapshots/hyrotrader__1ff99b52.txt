5. Attribution — how a transaction is attributed to you
5.1 Attribution: a New Customer's first purchase, then the Client Link

A transaction by a Customer who is neither a New Customer nor anyone's Linked Client is attributed to no Partner.
5.2 Conditions of a Qualifying Transaction
A transaction is a "Qualifying Transaction" in relation to you only if all of the following are satisfied:
(a) a Customer has placed a Refundable Challenge Deposit with the Provider, has paid a Challenge Fee or an Instant Fee that is commissionable under §6.9, or has paid for another item that is commissionable under §6.9;
(b) the transaction is either (i) the first paid purchase of a New Customer, made with a valid Referral Code issued to you, in force under its Code Terms at the moment of checkout and accepted by the Platform, or (ii) a transaction of your Linked Client that earns Commission under your Deal (§6.11);

(d) the Customer is not you, and the transaction is not a prohibited transaction under §12;
(e) the Customer is not in a Prohibited Jurisdiction and is not otherwise ineligible under GTC §12; and
(f) any hold period applicable to that transaction under §8.3 has expired without a Clawback Event occurring; where no hold period applies to the transaction, this condition is satisfied when the amount is received and cleared.
Conditions (a) to (e) determine whether Commission is earned. Condition (f) determines when it becomes payable.
5.3 The code decides only for a New Customer
For a New Customer, the valid Referral Code applied at checkout decides attribution and displaces any cookie, Tracked Link, click, session or other tracking signal. For a Linked Client, the Client Link decides attribution and no code, click or signal changes it. Another Partner's Referral Code cannot be applied to a Linked Client's transaction.

7. The multi-level structure
7.1 Three levels
The Affiliate Programme has three levels. Where a Qualifying Transaction is attributed under §5 to a Partner, Commission is payable not only to that Partner (Level 1) but also to the Partner directly above that Partner in the Network chain (Level 2) and to the Partner two places above that Partner in the Network chain (Level 3), at the rates set out in Schedule 1. The Level 2 rate is the higher of the two, because the Partner directly above you is your affiliate manager, who recruits, supports and carries the relationship with the Partners they bring in.
7.2 What the Level 2 and Level 3 rates are calculated on
The Level 2 and Level 3 rates are calculated on the same Commissionable Amount as the Level 1 Commission. They are not a share of, a deduction from, or a percentage of the Level 1 Partner’s Commission.
A Level 2 or Level 3 Commission neither reduces nor increases the Commission payable to the Partner below it. Each level’s Commission is a separate obligation of the Provider, calculated independently on the same Commissionable Amount. A worked example is set out in Schedule 1.
7.3 How the Network is formed
A Partner becomes your Level 2 Sub-Partner where that Partner applied your Partner Referral Code, or was otherwise recorded by the Provider as introduced by you, at the time that Partner’s own application to the Affiliate Programme was approved. That Partner’s own Level 2 Sub-Partners are your Level 3 Sub-Partners. The chain stops there. The Network relationship is fixed at the point the Sub-Partner’s application is approved and does not afterwards change. It is not re-attributed, does not lapse through inactivity, and is not transferable. It ends only when the Sub-Partner’s participation or your own participation ends (§7.6).
7.4 Limits
(a) The structure extends to three levels. No Commission is payable at Level 4 or beyond, except where the Provider has agreed further levels with a specific Partner in writing and recorded them in the Partner Area. A Partner cannot create further levels by recruiting.
(b) A Partner may not appear more than once in the same chain, and circular, reciprocal or looped Network structures generate no Commission at any level.
(c) You may not recruit as a Sub-Partner any person who is you, who is a person connected with you within the meaning of §12.2, or who is acting on your behalf or under your control, and no Commission is payable at any level in respect of such a person.
(d) Recruiting Sub-Partners is not itself a commercial activity for which any payment, entry fee, subscription, pack, position or other consideration may be charged to, or required of, any person. The Affiliate Programme is a referral programme for the Provider's own service. You must not present, operate or market it as an income opportunity, a business opportunity, a network-marketing scheme or an investment (§10.7, §10.8).
7.5 The structure may be varied or withdrawn
The Provider may vary the number of levels, the Level 2 and Level 3 rates, the method by which the Network is formed, and any other aspect of the multi-level structure, or may withdraw the multi-level structure entirely, in each case on notice under §20. A variation takes effect only for Qualifying Transactions completed on or after its effective date and does not affect Commission already earned.
7.6 Effect of a Network member leaving
Where a Sub-Partner's participation ends, no further Commission accrues to you in respect of that Sub-Partner's transactions. Where your own participation ends, no further Commission accrues to you in respect of your Network, and your Sub-Partners are not re-attributed to you or to your upline except as the Provider determines acting reasonably.

9. Reversal, clawback and repayment
9.1 Clawback Events
Commission attributable to a transaction is reversed, and if already paid becomes repayable, where any of the following occurs (" Clawback Event "):
(a) the amount received in respect of the transaction is returned, refunded or reversed in whole or in part, including on a cancellation before Activation under GTC §8.1, on the exercise of a statutory right of withdrawal under GTC §8.2, or on rejection of an amendment under GTC §19.4 — but excluding a return of a Deposit falling within §6.6 ;
(b) a chargeback, payment reversal, dispute or clawback is initiated with a card issuer, payment provider or payment scheme in respect of the transaction, whether or not it is ultimately upheld;
(c) the Customer's Account or Challenge is closed, terminated or forfeited by the Provider for a material breach of the GTC, for a Prohibited Trading Practice, for failure to complete KYC, for a deliberate provision of false identity information, for fraud, or on sanctions grounds (GTC §20.2);
(d) the transaction is determined under §5, §12 or §16 to be a self-referral, a prohibited transaction, fraudulent, artificial, incentivised in breach of §10.5, or otherwise non-genuine;
(e) the Referral Code applied was not valid under §4.4; or
(f) the Commission was calculated or paid in error, including by reason of a system, attribution or arithmetic fault.
9.2 Effect
On a Clawback Event the Commission concerned is reversed at every level of the Network, including the Level 2 and Level 3 Commission calculated on the same Commissionable Amount. Where a return is partial, the reversal is proportionate.
9.3 Recovery
A reversed amount is deducted from your balance and, where the balance is insufficient, from Commission that becomes payable to you afterwards. Where the amount is not recovered in that way within ninety (90) calendar days, or where your participation has ended, it is a debt immediately due and repayable by you to the Provider on demand.
9.4 Chargeback costs
Where a chargeback relates to a transaction on which you were paid Commission, the Provider may in addition recover from you the chargeback fees charged by the payment processor and the reasonable administrative costs it incurs, in accordance with GTC §9.3(c).
9.5 Set-off across your Accounts
Where you also hold an Account as a Customer, the Provider may set off an amount owed by you under this §9 against any amount payable to you as a Partner, and may set off an amount owed by you as a Customer against Commission payable to you. Nothing in this §9.5 permits set-off against a Refundable Challenge Deposit that falls to be returned to you as a Customer under GTC §8, which is a debt payable in full (GTC §21.1).
9.6 No reversal after two years The Provider will not reverse Commission more than twenty-four (24) months after it was paid , except where the Clawback Event is a chargeback or payment reversal initiated within that period, or where the Commission was obtained by your fraud, dishonesty or breach of §12, in which case no time limit applies.
