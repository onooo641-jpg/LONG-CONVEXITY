Affiliate program
How the Blue Guardian affiliate program works for CFD and Futures: joining, commission levels, discount codes, payouts, tracking and rules.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 13 November 2026
Short answer
Join the Blue Guardian Affiliate Program for free and earn 10% to 15% commission on every evaluation bought through your link or code. You can withdraw once you have $200 in commission, and payouts are made on the 15th and 30th of each month. Paid advertising isn't allowed.
How to join
Apply at blueguardian.com/affiliates . It's free, and you don't need to be a trader. Once approved, you'll get an affiliate dashboard where you can manage your links, track referrals and request payouts.

Commission levels are the same across CFD and Futures. You earn commission on evaluation fees and reset fees paid by your referrals, based on what Blue Guardian actually collects. Affiliates on a custom or individually negotiated deal keep the terms in their own agreement instead.
Your discount code
Every affiliate gets their own discount code. It always matches the discount Blue Guardian is currently running, and it works on new products too.
Payouts
Request a payout from your affiliate dashboard once you have at least $200 in commission.
Payouts are processed on the 15th and the 30th of each month (or the last day of the month if there's no 30th). If that date falls on a weekend, it moves to the next business day.
Only one payout request can be pending at a time. You keep earning while it's pending.
You'll need to complete KYC verification before your first affiliate payout.
Tracking
Your dashboard shows referrals, sales, earnings and campaign performance in real time. You can create multiple tracking links to measure different campaigns. Referrals are usually confirmed within 24–48 hours of purchase.
30-day cookie: when someone clicks your link, they're tracked to you for 30 days. If their browser already has an active cookie from another affiliate, that affiliate gets the commission.
Where you can promote
Use unpaid channels: your website or blog, email lists, organic social media (Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok and others), podcasts and similar. Everything you publish must describe Blue Guardian accurately.
What's not allowed
Paid advertising of any kind: Google Ads, PPC, Facebook Ads, native ads, paid endorsements.
Self-referrals , or referring people in your own household.
Spam, misleading claims or illegal marketing.
Breaking these rules can lead to removal from the program and loss of any pending commission. We may also audit your promotional activity and ask for details.
