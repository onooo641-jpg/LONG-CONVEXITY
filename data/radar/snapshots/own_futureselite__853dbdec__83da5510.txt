What are Elite Packs?
What are Elite Packs?
Packs, cards and rarities.
October 13, 2026
Table of contents
An Elite Pack holds three cards, bought with credits in your dashboard. You open it yourself and keep everything inside. Cards make real changes to accounts you trade: a free reset, the consistency rule removed, a minimum trading day taken off, extra drawdown room, a higher payout cap, a better split, a checkout discount, or a free challenge.
Packs and rarities
Five packs: Ignition, Breakout, Market Maker, Whale and Oracle.
Seven rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic and Exotic, plus the one Primordial card.
Drop rates are per card and shown on each pack's shop page before you buy.
The Whale Pack never contains a Common card. Every Oracle Pack card is Rare or better.
Exotic cards (Immortal Evaluation, Lifetime Split, Founder's Key, The Golden Streak) appear only in Whale and Oracle Packs.
The Primordial card, the Oracle Account, enters the pool only once you've discovered every other card.
