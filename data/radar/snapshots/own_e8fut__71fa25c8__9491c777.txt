What is E8 Markets?
E8 Markets is a simulated trading platform that provides users with simulated capital and an environment to test their skills without risking personal capital. Users have access to several analytical tools and data feedback to help them identify weaknesses and improve their discipline. Once a user proves their proficiency during a training stage (Simfi™ Challenge account ), they can advance to SimFi™ Performance account , where they earn payouts based on their trading performance.

Is E8 regulated?
We do not act as a broker or fund. We do not take customer deposits, and we do not manage users' money or trade on anyone's behalf. Users have access only to the simulated trading environment and demo accounts. We are a company registered in the United States with a focus on simulated finance.
﻿​﻿
﻿Because E8 does not operate as a broker or financial instrument provider, the regulatory frameworks that apply to licensed brokers and financial institutions do not apply to our business model. We operate under FTC advertising and consumer protection standards as an educational simulation platform.
