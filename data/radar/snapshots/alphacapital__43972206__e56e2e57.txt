What happens if a rule is broken on a Qualified Analyst account?
July 6, 2026
If a rule is breached on a Qualified Analyst account, please contact us at [email protected] . Depending on the rule broken, there will be different outcomes. For example:
If a prohibited strategy is used, the Qualified Analyst account may be terminated, with the potential for a ban on services.
If the maximum daily loss or maximum total loss is breached, the account will be closed.
