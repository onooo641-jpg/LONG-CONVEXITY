Express Live is an invite only account for traders moved to Live. Trading is simulated. Payouts are real money.
Express Live or Real Live?
You choose when you are moved to Live.
Who can get it?
Only traders invited by our Risk team. It can't be bought.

Max loss
3% of your starting balance, based on end of day equity. It moves up with your highest end of day equity and locks at starting balance + $100.
If your equity touches it, the account is closed.
What is not required
No daily loss limit
No consistency rule
No profit buffer
Profitable days
8 per payout cycle. They don't need to be in a row.

Other accounts
While on Express Live you can't buy challenges or Instant accounts, and you can't also be on Real Live. Express Live accounts are sent 2 at a time.
Demo accounts you already hold follow your transition terms.
Moving to Real Live later
Possible with Risk team approval. It isn't automatic.
If you hit the max loss
The account is closed. A 30 day cooldown starts, then the Risk team decides if you can buy new challenges.
Not allowed
Account sharing or third party trading
Hedging across accounts
Exploiting platform errors
Fraud or market manipulation
Going over your contract limits
News rules, if any, are shared before activation.
Is it a real brokerage account?
No. Trades are simulated. Payouts are real money paid by FuturesElite.
Express Live is a simulated trading program. The balance is simulated capital, not a deposit. Payouts follow these rules and the terms you accept before activation.
