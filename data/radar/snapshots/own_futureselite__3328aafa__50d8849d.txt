How FuturesElite works
From your first challenge to live capital, step by step.
October 13, 2026
Table of contents
Every FuturesElite account follows the same path. You buy a challenge, pass one evaluation phase, verify your identity, trade a simulated funded account and request payouts. Consistent traders are invited to a live account with real capital.
The five stages
Buy a challenge. You can buy as many challenges as you like and there is no time limit to pass.
Pass the evaluation. One phase. Reach the profit target without hitting the loss limits, and meet your account's minimum days and consistency rule.
Verify and sign. Complete KYC with Veriff in your dashboard, then sign the funded trader contract.
Trade Sim Funded. Request payouts once your account's payout conditions are met.
Go live. The Risk Team invites traders to a live account. The usual path is your 4th consecutive payout.
Instant accounts skip the evaluation and start at the Sim Funded stage.
Good to know
Trading happens on simulated accounts until you go live.
Your dashboard shows the exact rules for each account you own: how much you can still lose, the daily loss limit, what is left to the target and your consistency share.
If something goes wrong technically during a challenge (an outage or a data feed problem), contact Support straight away.
