Futures bundle discounts
How Futures bundle discounts work when you buy several accounts in one order, including the Zero 5-account bundle.
Applies to: Futures Updated 15 November 2026
Short answer
Buy several Futures accounts in one order and every account in the order gets a bigger discount. The discount is applied automatically at checkout. Zero has its own bundle: buy 5 Zero accounts in one order and get 5% off the total.
Discount tiers
Accounts in your order
Discount on every account
1
25% off
2
30% off
3
35% off
4
40% off
5
40% off, plus the 5th account free
Reserve: the 5th account is 50% off instead of free
How it works
Each time you add another account, all accounts in the order move up to the new tier. For example, with 3 accounts in your order, each one is 35% off, not just the third.
Zero bundle
The tiers above don't apply to Zero. Instead, buy 5 Zero accounts in one order and get 5% off the order total . The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
You can hold up to 5 funded Zero accounts at a time, of any size. See Zero account rules .
