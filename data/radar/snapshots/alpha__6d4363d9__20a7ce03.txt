Alpha Futures Plans Compared: Zero vs Standard vs Advanced vs Direct (2026 Update)
By the Alpha Futures Team · Updated September 28, 2026
Updated September 28, 2026: The active lineup is Zero, Standard, Advanced, and Direct. Premium was discontinued in July 2026. Confirm live prices and rules on the pricing page before checkout.
All Alpha Futures accounts use simulated funds. Zero, Standard, and Advanced start with a one-step evaluation; Direct starts on a Qualified Account without an evaluation. Each plan has different consistency and risk rules, so compare the stage you are buying as well as the headline account size.
Active Alpha Futures plans

Standard $129/month for 50K 50% 40% between performance fee requests

These are entry prices displayed on the Alpha Futures pricing page when this article was updated. Other sizes cost more. Check the live plan card and checkout for the current fee and limits.
How to choose
Zero: simplest evaluation consistency
Zero has no evaluation consistency rule, so one strong session may meet the profit target if all other rules are satisfied. Zero has a Daily Loss Guard and a 40% consistency rule on the Qualified Account. See the Zero plan .
Standard: balanced evaluation and qualified rules
Standard has a 50% evaluation consistency rule and 40% Qualified consistency. There is no activation fee. Compare sizes, Maximum Loss Limits, and performance fee caps on the Standard plan .
Advanced: no qualified consistency rule
Advanced has a 40% evaluation consistency rule and no consistency rule once Qualified. It has no preset Daily Loss Guard and a higher performance fee cap than the lower-priced plans. See the Advanced plan .
Direct: skip the evaluation
Direct begins with a Qualified Account. It has a 20% consistency rule for performance fee eligibility and its own profit targets, loss limits, and payout caps. It is a different purchase path from an evaluation; review the Direct plan before choosing.
Frequently asked questions
Which Alpha Futures plans are currently available?
Zero, Standard, Advanced, and Direct are shown on the current pricing page. Premium was discontinued in July 2026.
Which plan has no evaluation consistency rule?
Zero has no evaluation consistency rule. Direct has no evaluation stage. Standard evaluations use 50%, and Advanced evaluations use 40%.
Which Qualified Account has no consistency rule?
Advanced has no Qualified consistency rule. Zero and Standard use 40% between performance fee requests, and Direct uses 20% for performance fee eligibility.
Are these live brokerage accounts?
No. Evaluation and Qualified Accounts use simulated funds; eligible performance fees are based on simulated results. Outcomes are not guaranteed.
Read each plan card before purchasing.
