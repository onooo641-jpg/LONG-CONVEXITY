A Reset allows you to return your trading account to the default settings. This includes starting Account Balance, Maximum Loss Limit, and Trading Days.
Evaluation Reset
Traders may purchase a Reset at any point during their Evaluation subscription. You can purchase Evaluation Resets directly on your dashboard.
Evaluation Reset Fees start for as low as $79 and differ based on account size. Resets DO NOT change or affect your monthly rebill date.
