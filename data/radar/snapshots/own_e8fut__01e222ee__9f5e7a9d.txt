General questions
How is the commission calculated?
The commission is calculated from the final price, which is paid by the user. If the user applies your code for the first purchase ever and pays $1000, you will receive $200 as a commission.
​
How do I obtain a commission from referred clients?
To obtain the commission, your referred user must use your own custom discount code at checkout and validate it.
What is considered the first sale, and the Second and subsequent sales?
The first sale is considered the first purchase ever from the client.
​
Second and subsequent sale is considered as any purchase from a client who has already bought an account in the past.
Why did I not receive an affiliate commission?
If you did not receive the commission from the client's purchase, it is because the user did not use your custom discount code .
