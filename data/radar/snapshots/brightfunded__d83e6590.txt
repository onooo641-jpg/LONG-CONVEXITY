Which one fits you?
Choose Futures if...
You like trading CME futures such as ES, NQ or CL.
You want no daily loss limit, with the Max Loss Limit as your only loss rule.
You close your trades inside the session and are fine with the automatic close at 4:45 PM ET.
You want to use NinjaTrader, Tradovate or TradingView.
​
Choose CFD if...
You want to trade forex, indices, commodities or crypto.
You want to hold positions overnight or over the weekend.
You prefer MT5, cTrader or DXTrade.
You want a choice between a one-step and a two-step evaluation.
Frequently asked questions
Can I have both?
Yes. Futures and CFD are separate programs, and each one has its own accounts, rules and platforms.
​
Do the rules of one program apply to the other?
No. Each program has its own rules. Always check the rules of the program of your account.
​
