Finding your affiliate dashboard
Where Affiliates is in the menu and what each part of the page shows.
October 24, 2026
Table of contents
Once your application is approved, we unlock the Affiliates page on your FuturesElite account. Until then, it doesn't appear in the menu.
How to open it
Sign in at app.futureselite.com .
Click your name in the top-right corner.
Choose Affiliates .
What's on the page
Copy affiliate link : your personal link. Traders who sign up through it become your affiliated users, and their purchases count toward your sales.
Totals : currently affiliated users, total sales and your total available payout.
Claim : request your available payout. Each request appears under Requests with its date, amount and status.
Monthly stats : new affiliated users, sales and number of orders for each month.
Advanced stats : charts grouped by day, week, month or all time. You can filter by sub-affiliate or pick a date range.
Orders, Accounts, Payouts and Commissions : the detail behind your numbers.
Good to know
Don't see Affiliates in the menu? Your account hasn't been unlocked yet. It appears only after our team approves your application.
Already approved and still can't see it, or have a question about a payout request? Contact Support at [email protected] or through live chat.
