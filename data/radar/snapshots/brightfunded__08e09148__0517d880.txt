To become a funded trader at BrightFunded, you'll need to successfully complete our Evaluation Process. Depending on the plan you choose, this consists of either one or two stages .
2-Step Plans (Bright & Classic)
Stage 1: Challenge
This stage gives you the chance to showcase your trading skills in a simulated environment. There is no time limit — you trade at your own pace while meeting the defined profit target and respecting our risk management rules.

Once you've successfully completed Stage 1, you move on to the Verification stage.
Stage 2: Verification
The Verification stage is designed to confirm the consistency of your performance. The profit target is more relaxed compared to Stage 1, but disciplined risk management is still required. Like Stage 1, there is no time limit.

There is no time limit. Simply meet the profit target while respecting the risk management rules and you're ready to move forward.
After Passing the Evaluation
Once you've completed your evaluation stage(s) and we've reviewed your trading, you're set to become a BrightFunded Trader. To finalize your funded account agreement, we'll require a valid ID document (passport, national ID card, or driving license) to complete your identification process.
Important Notes
BrightFunded does not offer services to individuals under 18 years of age, those listed on sanction lists, persons with criminal records related to financial crime or terrorism, or individuals previously excluded due to a breach of the BrightFunded Account Agreement. If any of these conditions are detected after an agreement has been concluded, we reserve the right to terminate it.
We don't evaluate you based on past track records — what matters is your ability to consistently meet our trading objectives during the evaluation.
💡 15% Profit Share from your evaluation phases — profits generated during Stage 1 and Stage 2 are not lost. Upon reaching 10% growth on your funded account and requesting your first payout, 15% of your evaluation profits will be credited directly to your funded account.
