S2F 20% consistency rule per payout cycle, with an example.
Updated over a month ago
Copy for LLM
S2F accounts use a 20% consistency rule per payout cycle: your best day can't be more than 20% of the profit made since your last payout.
How it's calculated
Largest single-day profit ÷ profit this cycle = consistency %
Example: $5,000 made this cycle, best day $1,200. 1,200 ÷ 5,000 = 24%, over 20%. Once cycle profit reaches $6,000, you're back within the rule.
If you're over the limit
Consistency is checked at the end of every trading day and resets after each payout. A day that ends over 20% isn't a Qualifying Day, and you can't request a payout until you're back within the rule. Keep trading to bring it down, and check your numbers with the Consistency Calculator .
