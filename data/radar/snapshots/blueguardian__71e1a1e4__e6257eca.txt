Futures instruments, codes and costs
Every Futures contract you can trade, with codes, tick sizes, tick values, cost per side, market hours in New York time and month codes.
Applies to: Futures Updated 13 November 2026
Short answer
Below are all the Futures contracts you can trade, with their codes, tick sizes, tick values and total cost per side. At Blue Guardian you can trade from 6:00 PM to 4:10 PM New York time , within each market's own hours.
Market hours
All times are New York time.
Most contracts (equity indices, currencies, metals, energy, interest rates, crypto): Sunday–Friday, 6:00 PM–5:00 PM, with a daily break from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
Livestock (GF, HE, LE): Monday–Friday, 9:30 AM–2:05 PM.
Grains (ZC, ZS, ZM, ZL, ZW): Sunday–Friday, 8:00 PM–8:45 AM, and Monday–Friday, 9:30 AM–2:20 PM.
