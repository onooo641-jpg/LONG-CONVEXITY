Level 1 and Level 2 Market Data
Level 1 is free. Level 2 costs more. Here's the breakdown.
June 18, 2026
Table of contents
The Basics
Topstep covers Level 1 market data (Top of Book) for all 4 exchanges on Trading Combine® and Express Funded Account® (XFA) accounts at no cost. Level 2 (Depth of Market) is available as an upgrade for $38/month.
Level 1 vs. Level 2 Data

Level 2 (Depth of Market)

The CME requires payment for all market data on simulated accounts. Topstep covers Level 1 for all 4 exchanges at no cost to you.
What Is CME Agreement and why do I need to sign it?
The CME Market Data Agreement is a legally binding document that lets you access real-time exchange data. As part of signing, you must declare whether you're a professional or non-professional user.
⚠️ Topstep Traders must always select non-professional status.
How to Sign It
The non-professional CME Agreement appears automatically the first time you log into TopstepX™. Just follow the prompts.
Troubleshooting
If you're having trouble getting the agreement to load or submit:
Reset your password from the dashboard
Clear your browser cookies and cache, or try Incognito/Private mode
Make sure all profile fields are filled in — including a 5-digit ZIP code. Clear any pre-populated fields and retype them manually
Try completing the agreement on a mobile device — many Traders have better results there
How to Purchase Level 2 Data
Go to the Accounts tab in your Topstep Dashboard.
Click Add Ons .
Select Depth of Market Bundle .
👉 Important: You'll be charged immediately and Rebilled on the 28th of each month. Data is not pro-rated — you pay the full amount regardless of when you sign up.
FAQs
Why is Level 2 Data not free?
The CME charges for all market data on simulated accounts. Topstep covers Level 1 for everyone. Level 2 requires an upgrade at $38/month.
Can I cancel Level 2 Data?
Yes, cancel anytime from your Billing page. Cancellation takes effect immediately — you lose access right away. Cancel on the last day you want access.
Is Level 2 Data pro-rated?
No. You'll be charged the full $38 regardless of when you sign up. Billing renews on the 28th of each month.
Can I get a refund for Level 2 Data?
No. Once billing is processed, refunds are not issued. Cancel by the 27th to avoid the next charge. You'll automatically move back to Level 1 after cancellation.
Do I need to pay for data in an Express Funded Account®?
Level 1 is free for all simulated accounts including the XFA. Level 2 requires an upgrade — you can do it anytime from your dashboard.
If I already have Level 2 Data, does it carry over to new accounts?
Yes. An active Level 2 subscription applies to all Trading Combines® under the same username.
