Inactivity Rule on Qualified Accounts and Evaluation Accounts
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
November 5, 2025
Trading platforms archive trading data after 10 inactive trading days. To keep your account active you must place a trade at least once every 10 trading days (does not matter size of trade).
