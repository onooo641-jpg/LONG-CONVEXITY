What is LucidMaxx
LucidMaxx is our exclusive eval-to-live daily payout plan reserved for our most consistent and profitable traders.
This plan is not available to the general public. It can only be purchased by traders who have earned LucidMaxx status through demonstrated performance.
LucidMaxx is built to offer instant live capital with daily, uncapped payout potential.
Achieving LucidMaxx Status
LucidMaxx status is reserved for traders who demonstrate exceptional consistency and profitability.
Our quantitative risk team uses defined performance criteria to identify traders who qualify . Once a trader meets the required benchmarks:
They will be notified directly via email
They will be granted access to purchase the LucidMaxx evaluation
LucidMaxx status is earned, not requested.
LucidMaxx Benefits
LucidMaxx traders receive the following advantages:
Daily payouts
No payout caps
Up to 5 accounts
No DLL (No Daily Loss Limit)
End-of-day drawdown
Instant live capital
Live Payout Structure
LucidMaxx live accounts allow:
Uncapped daily payouts
No payout windows
Daily payout requests
There are no profit minimums once live. Traders may request payouts daily.
LucidMaxx FAQs
Can I buy a LucidMaxx account directly?
No. LucidMaxx can only be accessed by traders who have been invited by the Lucid risk team based on their performance history. There is no public purchase option.
How long does it take to earn LucidMaxx status?
There is no set timeline. The risk team evaluates traders based on their overall track record, not a fixed number of payouts or days.
Does LucidMaxx use real capital?
Yes. LucidMaxx accounts trade live capital from the moment the evaluation is passed.
What happens if I breach a LucidMaxx account?
Please see the LucidMaxx cooldown and re-evaluation policy page.
What is the profit split on LucidMaxx?
90/10. You keep 90% from the first payout.
How many LucidMaxx accounts can I hold?
Up to 5 simultaneous accounts.
Can I purchase/trade LucidMaxx accounts whilst I have an active Live account?
You may trade and reset your existing LucidMaxx accounts alongside your Live account, however, you may not purchase new LucidMaxx accounts whilst you hold an active Live account.
