Each payout cycle starts counting from your first trade of that cycle.
1-Step Daily: Withdrawable = (Balance − Starting balance − 1% buffer) × Profit split. Only one payout can be approved per trading day. Each payout takes all the profit above the 1% buffer, and any new profit made later that day can be requested the next trading day.
Instant: Withdrawable = (Closed balance − 103% of starting balance) × Profit split. Each payout takes everything above the 3% buffer, and your balance goes back to 103% of your starting balance.
Once your payment has been requested, our finance team processes it in a maximum of 1 day . It can take a little longer to show in your wallet or bank, depending on the broker and your bank institution.
