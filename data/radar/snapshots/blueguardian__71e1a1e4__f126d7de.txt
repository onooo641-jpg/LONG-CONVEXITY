Trading hours and the trading day
The 5 PM New York daily reset (rollover) for CFD and Futures, and Futures trading hours including the automatic 4:10 PM close.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 13 November 2026
Short answer
For both CFD and Futures, the trading day resets at 5:00 PM New York time (rollover). Futures can be traded from 6:00 PM to 4:10 PM, and any Futures trades still open at 4:10 PM are closed automatically. This does not count as a breach.
The Futures trading day (New York time) Replay
Markets open at 6 PM, open positions are closed automatically at 4:10 PM, and the trading day resets at 5 PM New York time, following US daylight saving. What happens when you hit the daily loss limit Replay
Reaching the daily loss limit closes your open trades and locks the account for the rest of the trading day. It isn't a failed account: trading unlocks at the 5 PM New York reset.
Daily reset (rollover): both products
A trading day starts at 5:00 PM New York time and ends at 5:00 PM the next day. The reset affects:
Daily profit and loss, including daily loss limits
Open trades carried over into the new day
Minimum trading day and winning day counts
Rule compliance tracking
If a Futures Reserve account is locked after hitting its optional daily loss limit (a soft breach), trading unlocks at the next reset.
Good to know
Trades opened after 5:00 PM count towards the next trading day.
Profit closed after 5:00 PM counts towards the new trading day.
Trades left open past the reset carry into the next day's calculations.
