What is 1-Step Daily?
One evaluation phase, no minimum days, payouts any day. Rules, drawdown and payouts at a glance.
Updated this week
Table of contents
1-Step Daily is the fastest way to a funded account at BrightFunded. You pass one evaluation phase, with no minimum number of trading days, no time limit and no consistency rule. Once you are funded, you can request a payout any day.
How it works
Evaluation. Reach the 10% profit target without breaching the daily loss or the max loss.
Verification. When you pass, complete your KYC from the Verification section of your dashboard. Once it is approved, your account goes through a Security Check by our Risk Team.
Funded account. After the check, your Trader Contract is sent automatically and your Funded Account is activated as soon as you sign it.
Payouts. Request a payout any day from your first funded trading day. After each payout you receive a new account.

How your two loss limits work
Daily loss (3%). It is calculated on the higher value between your balance and equity at the start of the day. On a $100,000 account you start the day with $100,000, so your equity cannot drop below $97,000 that day. It resets every day at the rollover, between 11:30 PM and 11:59 PM CET.
Max loss (6% trailing). The limit follows your highest equity in real time, including floating profit. It starts at $94,000 on a $100,000 account and moves up as your equity grows. When your equity reaches $106,000, the limit locks at $100,000 and stops moving.
How payouts work
You can request a payout any day from your first funded trading day. There is no minimum and no maximum amount. A 1% profit buffer stays in the account. On a $100,000 account at 80% with a balance of $103,000, the profit above the buffer is $2,000 and you receive $1,600.
Add-ons
You can add the 90% Payout Split, the 100% Account Fee Refund and Swap-Free when you buy the account.
