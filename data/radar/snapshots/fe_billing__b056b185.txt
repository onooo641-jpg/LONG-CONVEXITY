Elite Credits
Elite Credits
Reinvest payouts with extra buying power.
October 23, 2026
Payouts land as Elite Credits. You can withdraw them through Rise (no limit) or crypto ($500 per request), or use them to buy challenges. Any leftover balance stays withdrawable through Rise. Withdrawals have a 3% processing fee.
Elite Credits are worth $1.10 when used at checkout and $1.00 when cashed out, before the 3% processing fee. When you use credits to buy challenges, you reinvest instantly without waiting for an external transfer.
