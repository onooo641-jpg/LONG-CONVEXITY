1. Overview
MyFunded Futures, LLC, together with its affiliates ("MFFU," "we," "us," or "our"), wants you to be familiar with how we collect, use, and disclose information from and about you through cookies and similar technologies, because we respect your concerns about privacy and want to maintain your trust. We and our service providers and partners collect information by automated means when you visit, access, or use our website located at https://www.myfundedfutures.com/ , together with our related mobile sites, applications, electronic newsletters, and widgets that link to this Cookie Policy (collectively, the "Services").
We are committed to responsible data practices and handle visitor data with the care described in this Policy. We do not use cookies to collect more personal information than necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.
This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which is available at https://www.myfundedfutures.com/privacy-policy , and which describes more fully how we collect, use, and disclose personal information, your rights and choices, and how to contact us.
2. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?
Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. We and our service providers and partners use cookies and other tracking technologies, including web beacons, pixels, web server logs, software development kits (SDKs), JavaScript, and similar technologies (collectively, "Cookies"), to operate, secure, and improve the Services, to remember your preferences, to analyze how the Services are used, and to deliver and measure content and advertising.
The information we obtain through the use of Cookies may include information about your computer, device, and browser, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system and device characteristics, language preferences, mobile device identifier, referring URLs, and actions taken by you on the Services (such as the content you viewed, whether you have used the Services before, and the dates and times you access the Services). If you access the Services from a mobile or other device, we may collect a unique device identifier, geolocation data obtained with your consent where required by applicable law, and details about your mobile carrier.
First-party Cookies are set by MFFU. Third-party Cookies are set by parties other than MFFU, such as analytics and advertising partners, and are governed by those parties' own privacy and cookie notices. Cookies may be session Cookies (which expire when you close your browser) or persistent Cookies (which remain on your device until they expire or you delete them).
3. Categories of Cookies We Use
We use the following categories of Cookies on the Services. Depending on your jurisdiction and your choices, some of these Cookies may not be active during your visit.
Strictly Necessary Cookies. These Cookies are required to operate the Services and to provide features you have requested, such as login sessions, account security, evaluation dashboard access, page navigation, network management, and form submissions. The Services cannot function properly without these Cookies. These Cookies are always active and do not require your consent.
Functionality Cookies. These Cookies allow the Services to remember choices you make (such as your language, region, and content preferences) and to provide enhanced, more personalized features. These Cookies are placed subject to your consent where required by applicable law.
Analytics and Performance Cookies. These Cookies collect information about how visitors use the Services, including which pages are visited most often and whether users encounter errors, so that we can measure and improve the performance of the Services. This information is generally aggregated. Analytics providers we use include Google Analytics and HubSpot. See Section 6 for provider-specific opt-out options. These Cookies are placed subject to your consent where required by applicable law.
Advertising and Targeting Cookies. These Cookies are used by us and our advertising partners to deliver content and advertising that may be more relevant to you, including advertising relating to our simulated trading evaluations, to limit the number of times you see an advertisement, to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and to support cross-context behavioral advertising on and off the Services. These Cookies are placed subject to your consent where required by applicable law.
Social Networking Cookies. These Cookies enable you to share content from the Services through social networks and may be set by those social networks to track your activity across websites. They are governed by the relevant social network's own privacy and cookie notices. These Cookies are placed subject to your consent where required by applicable law.

We and our service providers and partners use Cookies to:

5. Third-Party Cookies
We use third-party service providers and advertising partners that may set cookies on our website in connection with analytics, marketing, advertising, and customer relationship management. The identity of these third parties may change over time as we engage new service providers, and this Policy will be updated to reflect material changes. For information about our current third-party partners' data practices, please refer to their respective privacy policies, which are accessible through the cookie consent tool on our website.
Third-party cookies set on our website may be used to support the following activities:
Anonymous visitor tracking. Prior to a visitor submitting any form on our website, a third party may assign a persistent, anonymous identifier to track browsing behavior across visits. This data is not linked to any personal information at this stage.
Contact identification and retroactive association. When a visitor submits a form or otherwise provides their contact information (such as when registering for or purchasing an evaluation), the applicable third party may associate that visitor's prior anonymous browsing history with their contact record. At this point, previously anonymous browsing data may become associated with an identifiable individual.
Marketing personalization and campaign tracking. Third parties may use cookies to track interactions with our email communications (such as opens and clicks), to personalize content on return visits, and to measure campaign performance, including tracking which pages a known contact visits after engaging with our communications.
Remarketing and behavioral advertising. We and our advertising partners may use cookies to serve advertisements to visitors on third-party platforms based on prior visits to our website, and to tailor the content and messaging displayed to visitors based on their browsing behavior and interactions with our Services.
Web analytics. We and our service providers use analytics tools to collect aggregated and individual-level data about how visitors interact with our website, including pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, and general geographic region. This data is used for internal analytics, website improvement, and marketing optimization purposes. Where technically available, we configure these services to anonymize IP addresses prior to storage.
Data collected through the Services may be processed and stored on servers located in the United States or other countries outside your jurisdiction. We rely on our third-party service providers' own data transfer mechanisms, which may include the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, standard contractual clauses, or other appropriate safeguards recognized under applicable law. We encourage EEA and UK visitors to review the privacy policies of our third-party service providers for further information about how they handle international data transfers.
For EU and UK visitors, where required by applicable law, we will only deploy analytics, marketing, or advertising cookies with your prior explicit consent, which may be withdrawn at any time.
MFFU may also set its own first-party cookies to support on-site personalization, targeted messaging, and marketing activities, including displaying tailored banners, content, and communications based on your browsing behavior and interactions with our Services. Where required by applicable law, such cookies will only be placed with your prior consent.

Browser Controls
Most web browsers allow you to control Cookies through their settings, typically found under the "Help," "Settings," or "Privacy" menu, including options to block or delete Cookies and to receive notification when a Cookie is set. For information about controlling Cookies through your specific browser, please visit the browser developer's website. If you disable or delete certain Cookies, some features of the Services may not function properly.
Analytics Opt-Out
The Services use analytics platforms that offer the opt-out mechanisms described below.
Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics sets cookies including _ga, _gid, and _gat that collect information about how you interact with the Services. You may opt out by installing the browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout . For more information: policies.google.com/privacy .

HubSpot sets persistent cookies including hubspotutk (tracks visitor identity across sessions and is passed to HubSpot on form submission), __hstc (tracks visitors across sessions), __hssc (tracks sessions), and __hs_opt_out (records your cookie opt-out preference). You may opt out via the "Cookie Preferences" tool on the Services or at legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy .
California and Other U.S. State Residents

You may exercise these rights through the cookie consent banner on our website or by broadcasting a recognized opt-out preference signal (such as the Global Privacy Control) through your browser. For more information about your privacy rights, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
