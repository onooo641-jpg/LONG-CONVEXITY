Is City Traders Imperium legit?
Yes. CTI has been funding traders since 2018, with 8 years
of uninterrupted payouts across over 178 countries. We are
independently reviewed on Trustpilot and Google, and every payout request is processed within
24 hours of approval.
Why choose CTI?
CTI has been paying funded traders since 2018, one of the
longest track records in the industry. All our programs
are made by traders, for traders, and they are the most
trader-friendly in the industry. Every program includes
balance-based drawdown, no time limits, payouts any day
including weekends, scaling up to $4M, and CTI Academy.
Will I actually get paid if I pass?
Yes. CTI has been paying funded traders continuously since
2018. Payouts are approved and processed within 24 hours
of your request. We pay via bank transfer or Direct
Crypto. CTI has made 8 years of uninterrupted payouts to
traders in over 178 countries.
I've failed at other prop firms. Will I pass at CTI?
Most traders fail because the rules force them to trade
against their natural style, not because their strategy
is bad. CTI is different. We use balance-based drawdown,
not equity, so one bad trade won't wipe you out. There
are no time limits and no hidden consistency rules. You
can trade the news and hold trades over the weekend.
What if I don't have a tested strategy yet?
Yes. Every paid program includes the full CTI Academy
library. It has hundreds of videos covering how prop
trading works and proven trading strategies, so you can
build a strategy from scratch. If you already have a
strategy, our 1-on-1 coaching helps you improve it and
increases your chances of passing and getting funded.
Can I try CTI before I pay?
Yes. You can try CTI with a free trial account, no card
required. You get full platform access on MT5 or Match
Trader, plus direct access to our support team. This lets
you test CTI before you commit.
Which countries are restricted from using CTI?
CTI does not offer services to residents of North Korea,
Iran, Syria, Cuba, or Russia, or to anyone under
international sanctions. Outside of these jurisdictions,
traders from over 178 countries can access CTI funding
programs. Full details are in our
Terms and Conditions .
Does CTI offer discount codes?
CTI occasionally runs limited time promotions on funding
programs. The safest way to get a valid code is directly
from our official website or verified social channels.
