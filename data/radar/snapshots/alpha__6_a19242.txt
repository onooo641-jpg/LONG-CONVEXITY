Standard Account is back with some upgrades!.
​
In order to pass a Standard Evaluation: there are two conditions to be met, the trader must reach (+ maintain) the profit target, and satisfy the 50% consistency rule .
There is only one way to fail on Evaluation Accounts, do not allow the Account Balance to hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit .
​
​ Benefits of Standard Account:
1.) Double the withdrawal caps of the Zero Account
2.) No Daily Loss Guard on Evaluations.
3.) No Activation Fee
4.) 90% profit split from the start, no tiered system.
5.) Pay yourself up to 4 times per month. Withdraw when you want after 5 winning days of $200 or more, no assigned payout dates.
6.) No "MLL slides to $0 at first withdrawal" rule. Other firms punish you for taking small payouts, not here. If MLL was at $49,200 before payout, it will be after as well.
