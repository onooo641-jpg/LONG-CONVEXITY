Who is allowed to join BrightFunded?
April 20, 2026
BrightFunded caters to retail traders worldwide who are looking to showcase their trading skills, improve their performance, and potentially earn profits through the Funded program. BrightFunded provides an opportunity for individuals with a passion for trading to participate, regardless of their previous trading experience.
​
BrightFunded is open to traders from various backgrounds and experience levels. Both aspiring traders and experienced professionals are welcome to join. All clients must be at least 18 years old .
Additionally, BrightFunded does not provide services to individuals either in or from Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Pakistan and Vietnam.
Lastly, BrightFunded does not provide services to persons who appear on sanction lists, those with criminal histories involving financial crimes or terrorism, and those who have previously been banned due to contract violations.
​
For risk management purposes, BrightFunded does not provide services to clients in or from Vietnam.
