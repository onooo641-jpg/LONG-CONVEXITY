How do payouts work on 2-Step Bright?
First payout 14 days after your first funded trade, then every 14 days. No buffer, no minimum.
Updated over a week ago
Table of contents
On 2-Step Bright your first payout is available 14 calendar days after your first funded trade. After that you can request a payout every 14 days. There is no profit buffer and no minimum amount.
When can I request a payout?
Payout
When
First payout
14 calendar days after your first funded trade
Next payouts
Every 14 days
With the Weekly Payouts add-on
Every 7 days
Each payout cycle starts counting from your first trade of that cycle.
Example. You place your first funded trade on day 0. Your first payout opens on day 14. If you request it that day, your next cycle starts with your next trade and the following payout opens 14 days after that trade.
How much can I withdraw?
You can withdraw your profit multiplied by your profit split. There is no buffer to leave in the account and no minimum payout.
Profit split
Who gets it
80%
Default on 2-Step Bright
90%
With the 90% Payout Split add-on
How do I request it?
Close all your open trades and pending orders.
Go to the Payout section of your dashboard.
Choose your payment method and send the request.
You can be paid in USDC (ERC-20) or by bank transfer in euros. Our finance team processes the payment in a maximum of 1 day. It can take a little longer to show in your wallet or bank.
Want faster payouts?
Add Weekly Payouts when you buy the account to request every 7 days.
