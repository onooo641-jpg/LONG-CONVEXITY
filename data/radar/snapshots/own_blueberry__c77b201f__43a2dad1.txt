Overview of Evaluation Programs
Blueberry Futures offers two distinct evaluation programs to match your trading style: the Ascent (EOD) challenge and the Accelerated (Trailing) challenge. Both programs require a specific Profit Target to be met while adhering to a Drawdown limit, but they differ primarily in how the Drawdown is calculated and the minimum time required to pass.
If you’re not familiar with these drawdown models, see Drawdown Types: EOD vs. Trailing Explained for a full breakdown of EOD vs. Trailing.
Ascent - EOD:
This plan uses an End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown , which is calculated only at the end of the trading day. This offers more flexibility during intraday trading. It requires a minimum of 2 trading days to pass.
Accelerated - Trailing:
This plan uses a Real-Time Trailing Drawdown that follows your peak profit level. It offers a faster path, requiring only 1 trading day to pass.
