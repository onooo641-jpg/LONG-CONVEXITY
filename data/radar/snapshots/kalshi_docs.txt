This changelog covers Kalshi’s REST, WebSocket, and FIX APIs across both the
Predictions and Margin exchanges. Use the entry tags to filter by API
surface ( REST , WebSocket , FIX ) or exchange ( Predictions , Margin ).
FIX API changes, previously tracked on a separate page, now live here under
the FIX tag.
TBD REST Margin
When a stock is listed after you submitted your stocks application, you
confirm that your disclosures still stand before trading it. Until you do,
POST /margin/orders and POST /margin/orders/{order_id}/amend on that
stock’s markets are rejected with 409 and code
stock_perps_confirmation_required . Confirm in the Kalshi app; the next
order goes through. Orders sent with reduce_only are not affected, so an
existing position can always be closed, and stocks you could already trade
stay tradable. 403 stock_perps_insider_blocked is unchanged and takes
precedence. This is not live yet. See
Order rejections .
November 26, 2026 REST Margin
GET /margin/corporate_action_payment_history returns the cash your account received or paid
for cash dividends, special dividends and cash spin-offs over an inclusive UTC date range,
optionally filtered by ticker and subaccount . Each entry has the corporate_action_id
(the id of the notice in GET /margin/corporate_actions ), type , market_ticker ,
payment_time , payment_amount (positive when received) and quantity , the signed position
the payment was computed on. Stock split adjustments are not listed. Subaccount-restricted API
keys read their locked subaccount’s payments, on the same terms as GET /margin/funding_history .
See Corporate Actions .
November 26, 2026 REST Predictions
On-request concert markets (series starting with KXRFQCONCERT ) now resolve on what the show’s
JamBase listing first shows within the market’s period, from its creation to the show’s
scheduled start, instead of on the listing’s status at one reading after the show’s date. A
cancellation or postponement first shown in that time counts even if the show is later
reinstated, and a show that is postponed and then canceled resolves all three covers to Yes;
one first shown after the scheduled start doesn’t count. The postponed cover now pays on a
postponement or a move to a later date, not on any other date. close_time is now one minute
before the show’s scheduled start (it was the scheduled start); a change the listing first
shows in that last minute still counts. rules_primary and rules_secondary of new markets
say so. latest_expiration_time is now 10:00 AM ET one week after the final day of the market’s
period at the venue, the day of the last moment before the show’s scheduled start (it was seven
days after expected_expiration_time ). Endpoints, fields and
expected_expiration_time are unchanged. See
On-Request Concert Markets . These markets are not live yet.
November 26, 2026 REST Predictions
The on-request market endpoints now limit how many new markets one account may
create in a rolling 24 hours, beside the existing limit across all users:
POST /weather_point_markets , POST /concert_cancellation_markets and
POST /flight_disruption_markets . Past your account’s limit, a request that would
create a market returns 429 with the endpoint’s existing code
( weather_point_market_limit_exceeded , concert_cancellation_market_limit_exceeded
or flight_disruption_market_limit_exceeded ), and details says it is your
account’s limit. A request that returns an existing market ( created: false ) never
counts toward it and is still answered at the limit, so you can keep requesting RFQs
on markets that exist. No codes are added or changed. See
On-Request Weather Markets .
November 26, 2026 REST Predictions
POST /concert_cancellation_markets now holds a market that already exists to the same
7 days as a new one. Once the market’s close_time or the show’s start as now listed,
whichever is earlier, is 7 days away or less, the request returns 409 with code
concert_cancellation_market_unavailable and the reason in details , where it used to
return the market until it closed. The market itself is unchanged: it stays open until its
close_time , positions in it are unaffected, GET /markets/{ticker} still returns it, and
it settles as before. A new market is held to the same 7 days: its show must start more
than 7 days from now, where 24 hours used to be enough. See On-Request Concert Markets . These
markets are not live yet.

November 26, 2026 REST Predictions
A new weather point market is now described in the time zone of its
location, which Kalshi determines from the coordinates. The timezone in
POST /weather_point_markets still decides which span of time your date
and hours mean, and the request body, the response and tickers are
unchanged. What changes, for markets created from now on:
title and rules_primary , as returned by GET /markets and
GET /events , give the window in the location’s zone, whatever zone
created the market. A request for 11pm to 3am in America/New_York at a
point in Portland, Oregon used to produce a market reading in Eastern
time, “from 11pm EST Thu, Nov 19 to 3am EST Fri, Nov 20, 2026”; it now
reads “on Thu, Nov 19, 2026, 8pm–12am PST”, and its rules “from 8pm to
12am PST (America/Los_Angeles) starting Thu, Nov 19, 2026”. A market
requested in its location’s own zone reads as before.
Where GET /weather_point_markets/availability returns
max_window_days: 1 , a new market is refused with 409
weather_point_market_unavailable when its window crosses midnight at
the location, and accepted when it doesn’t, even if your request sets
end_date . It no longer depends on the dates of the first request for
the event, which replaces what the entry below says.
A window that crosses midnight at the location and is longer than 24
hours there returns 400 invalid_parameters , even when your request
names it within one date of another zone.
A location whose time zone can’t be determined returns 409
weather_point_market_unavailable .
Events that already exist keep their description, and a new threshold on
one of them reads like the rest of that event. See
On-Request Weather Markets .

November 26, 2026 REST Predictions
A weather point market’s window can now run from one local day into the
next, for example 11pm to 3am:
POST /weather_point_markets takes an optional end_date . Left out, or
equal to date , the request means what it did before. Set to the day
after date , the window runs from start_hour on date to end_hour
on end_date . Such a window is at most 24 hours long, so end_hour must
not be after start_hour ; a longer window, or any other end_date ,
returns 400 invalid_parameters .
end_hour no longer has to be after start_hour when end_date is the
day after date . For a window on one day it still must be.
GET /weather_point_markets/availability returns max_window_days (2
where a window may cross midnight, otherwise 1) and max_window_hours
(the longest such window, 24). Where max_window_days is 1, a new
market whose window is stored across two local dates returns 409
weather_point_market_unavailable . A market stores the dates of the
first request for its variable, point and window, normally your own. When
that event already exists, its dates decide: a one-date request from
another time zone for an event stored across midnight returns the 409,
and a request with end_date for an event stored on one date succeeds.
Markets that already exist are returned either way.
Tickers keep their shape: the window is still its UTC start and its length
in hours, so 11pm November 23 to 3am November 24 in New York is
…-26NOV2404Z-4H-… . Existing markets and requests without end_date are
unchanged. See On-Request Weather Markets .
November 19, 2026 REST Predictions
POST /weather_point_markets no longer snaps the location to the center of
the NOAA MRMS grid cell containing it. The market’s location is now the
requested latitude and longitude rounded to three decimal places (about
100 m), and the response’s latitude and longitude return that point.
Requests share a market only when their coordinates agree to three
decimal places. Two points in the same grid cell used to resolve to one
market and now resolve to two.
A rounded location that lies exactly on the boundary between two grid
cells is moved 0.001° north or east, into one of them.
Tickers keep their format. The location segment ( 40785N73965W ) is the
market’s point, which is no longer always a cell center.
Markets created before this change keep their location and stay
reachable by requesting those coordinates.
November 19, 2026 REST Margin
subaccount_type on margin balance, position and risk rows gains the value
swaps : a cross-margined wallet for trading event contracts on the margin
exchange. Its subaccount is the wallet’s swaps code, 1 through 63, which
is separate from the primary account and numbered subaccounts. Swaps
wallets are created by Kalshi. Clients that switch exhaustively on
subaccount_type should accept the new value.

An orderbook_snapshot for a market with no recorded change, such as a new
market that hasn’t had an order yet, can have an empty market_id , and its
clock is 0. Markets created before the WebSocket server you’re connected
to started already worked this way. Markets created after it started used
to get their UUID and creation clock instead. Identify these markets by market_ticker . The first orderbook_delta for
the market carries its market_id .
November 19, 2026 REST Predictions Margin
A request whose path or query string contains a NUL byte ( %00 ) or bytes
that are not valid UTF-8, for example GET /markets?tickers=KX%00TEST or
GET /historical/trades?ticker=%ff%fe , now returns 400
invalid_parameters . It previously returned 500 internal_server_error
on endpoints that looked the value up. A cursor carrying such text is now handled the way each endpoint already
handles a cursor it cannot decode, instead of returning 500. Most endpoints
return 400. A few, including GET /markets , ignore a cursor they cannot
decode and return the first page. No valid request is affected, and cursors returned by the API are
unchanged.

November 19, 2026 WebSocket FIX Margin
During the overnight session (8 PM to 4 AM ET), every equities perps order must be priced within 20% of the market’s 4 PM ET settlement price from the day the session opened. Orders outside the band are rejected with invalid price . When the band takes effect or moves, resting orders outside it are canceled: margin user_orders and orderbook delta messages report last_update_reason: "SettlementBoundsCancel" , and FIX ExecutionReports (35=8) carry a Text (58) of SETTLEMENT_BOUNDS_CANCEL . See Price Banding .

November 5, 2026 REST FIX Margin
The Perps price band changes sides. It no longer limits how far an order
may rest from its own side of the book. Instead it limits how far an order
may cross the other side:
A bid is rejected above the higher of twice the best ask or the best ask
plus 1,000 ticks.
An ask is rejected below the lower of half the best bid or the best bid
minus 1,000 ticks.
When the other side of the book is empty, there is no limit. Rejected orders
and amends return invalid price , as before. Orders that sweep the book with
a far limit price, for example a bid at 1000.00 into a best ask of 10.00 ,
now fail and should carry a limit within the band. See
Price Banding .

November 5, 2026 WebSocket Predictions Margin
orderbook_delta subscriptions accept an optional update_interval_ms of 100 or 1000 . Each window produces one net held delta per changed price level; zero-net levels are skipped, and processing and transport can delay delivery. Updates to your own orders within any subaccount restriction and snapshots bypass the interval. Changes to other participants’ orders, including liquidity your trades consume, remain held. Apply each snapshot as a replacement for that market’s local book. Omit update_interval_ms or set it to 0 for real-time updates, the default. Other signed 64-bit integer literals return error code 29; values that fail integer decoding return code 1 for the whole command. Each connection has one orderbook_delta subscription. A repeated subscribe adds markets to the same sid and must use the same interval (omission means 0 ); a mismatch returns error code 11. It does not resynchronize existing markets. Use update_subscription with add_markets to extend the current subscription, or unsubscribe and then subscribe again to change its interval. Apply deltas in the subscription’s seq order. Predictions clock values identify each level’s latest included change and can decrease; they cannot be used to combine independently conflated streams. Public levels can lag your own orders and temporarily appear locked or crossed. Recover sequence gaps with get_snapshot for all subscribed markets, or unsubscribe , wait for unsubscribed , then subscribe again. When switching connections, rebuild from the new connection’s snapshot. See Orderbook Updates for Predictions and Margin for the full recovery contracts.

October 1, 2026 REST FIX Predictions Margin
Predictions and Margin REST amend requests accept expiration_time .
Omit it to preserve expiry, use 0 to clear it,
or supply a future Unix timestamp in seconds. Send the current price and total
quantity for an expiry-only amendment; queue priority is preserved. FIX replace ( 35=G ) requires GTD ( 59=6 ) with a future ExpireTime ( 126 )
to set a custom expiry; Day ( 59=0 ) without 126 sets the end-of-day ET
deadline, and GTC ( 59=1 ) without 126 clears it. 126 alone and expired
deadlines, including the Unix epoch, are rejected. As a Kalshi-specific
convention, omitting both tags preserves expiry. Kalshi guarantees FIFO
preservation for expiry-only amendments. Price changes and size increases
retain their existing queue-priority rules. Replaced reports confirm the accepted
deadline with GTD and 126 (including normalized Day deadlines), or GTC without
126 when expiry is cleared.

August 20, 2026 REST Margin
Stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing-stop triggers on margin positions are now available
over REST:
PUT , GET , and DELETE on /trade-api/v2/margin/isolated/positions/{ticker}/exit_trigger
PUT , GET , and DELETE on /trade-api/v2/margin/cross/positions/{ticker}/exit_trigger
PUT and DELETE on /trade-api/v2/margin/cross/positions/{ticker}/exit_trigger/{trigger_id}
A position holds at most one live trailing stop. Non-isolated positions may hold up to 20
live bracket (stop-loss / take-profit) triggers at once, so long as their counts fit the
position. Isolated positions hold one. kind selects the family: on GET and DELETE ,
omitting it covers both, while a PUT without one writes a bracket . Orders fired by a
trigger are reduce-only. Order-anchored triggers return to pending_on_entry after closing their current quantity,
because the anchor order can still add fills — so status is not monotonic, and only a
filled trigger with no anchor_order_id is done. A count or anchor_order_id create
appends a trigger, so it requires client_trigger_id and replaying one returns the trigger
the first request made; the key belongs to the position it created on, and reusing it for
another position returns 409 rather than that position’s trigger. Non-isolated positions take a subaccount , and additionally support partial triggers via
count , triggers tied to an order via anchor_order_id , and the {trigger_id} routes for
managing one of several triggers on the same position. Isolated positions hold a single
trigger per kind and always close in full. A supplied count must be a whole number of
contracts. If an anchor order cannot currently be validated, its trigger request returns 409 with
error code anchor_order_unavailable before writing a trigger. Retrying that response is safe.

July 2, 2026 FIX Predictions
Previously, some OrderCancelReplaceRequest (35=G) and
OrderCancelRequest (35=F) failures came back as an OrderCancelReject (35=9)
with Text<58>=INTERNAL_ERROR , even though the exchange had cleanly rejected
the request for a specific reason. The most common case was replacing an order
with a price the market does not accept, for example a sub-tick price on a
market that does not support fractional prices. These rejects now report the underlying reason in Text<58> , with a
corresponding CxlRejReason<102> :
Invalid price (off-tick, out-of-band, $0 , or $1 ): Text<58>=INVALID_PRICE , CxlRejReason<102>=99 (Other)
Unknown market: Text<58>=MARKET_NOT_FOUND , CxlRejReason<102>=99 (Other)
Duplicate client order ID: Text<58>=ORDER_ALREADY_EXISTS , CxlRejReason<102>=6 (Duplicate ClOrdID)
A rejected cancel or replace does not change the original order; it continues
to rest unchanged. Clients that branched on Text<58>=INTERNAL_ERROR for these
cases should switch to reading the specific reason text.

May 6, 2026 REST WebSocket Predictions
Order, Fill, and Trade responses now include two new normalized direction
fields. Each carries the full directional bit on its own — combining
action with side is no longer required to know what the user is
positioned for. The two fields encode the same bit in two vocabularies:
bid is equivalent to yes , ask is equivalent to no .
outcome_side ( yes | no ) — the outcome the user profits from.
Buy-yes and sell-no produce yes ; buy-no and sell-yes produce no .
book_side ( bid | ask ) — same bit in book vocabulary.
Affected REST responses ( svc-api2 ):
Order (GetOrders, GetOrder, GetHistoricalOrders, and order-write responses)
Fill (GetFills, GetFillsHistorical)
Trade (public) — fields are named taker_outcome_side and taker_book_side to match the existing taker_side
Affected WebSocket channels ( apiexternal-ws ):
user_orders
fill
trade — taker_outcome_side and taker_book_side
outcome_side describes directional exposure only; it does not change
the order’s price. An order at price p with outcome_side=no is
matched by an order at the same price p with outcome_side=yes —
both parties trade at the same price, just on opposite directions. Existing action , side , is_yes , purchased_side , and taker_side
fields are now marked deprecated. outcome_side and book_side are
the canonical way to determine order/trade direction going forward.
The legacy fields will not be removed before May 28, 2026 — please
migrate to the new fields when integrating against these endpoints. See the Order direction reference
page for the full migration table and equivalence rules.

With Logon flag 21026=Y , the gateway always emits a standalone New<0> execution report before any Trade<F> report, even when an order takes liquidity in the same matching cycle as its placement
Without 21026 , the New ack continues to be folded into the first Trade report when both events arrive in the same batch (existing behavior)
Useful for clients whose state machines require an explicit 39=0 ack before they can issue replaces against the order

Feb 19, 2026 REST Predictions
Kalshi now partitions exchange data into live and historical tiers. Historical data must be accessed via the new historical API endpoints. The GET /historical/cutoff endpoint returns the cutoff timestamps that define this boundary. Cutoff timestamps and what they mean:
market_settled_ts — partitioned by market settlement time . Markets and their candlesticks that settled before this timestamp are only available via GET /historical/markets and GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks .
trades_created_ts — partitioned by trade fill time . Fills that occurred before this timestamp are only available via GET /historical/fills .
orders_updated_ts — partitioned by order cancellation or execution time . Orders canceled or fully executed before this timestamp are only available via GET /historical/orders . Resting (active) orders are unaffected and always appear in GET /portfolio/orders .
New endpoints:
GET /historical/cutoff — returns the market, trade, and order cutoff timestamps
GET /historical/markets — settled markets older than the cutoff
GET /historical/markets/{ticker} — single historical market by ticker
GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks — candlestick data for historical markets
GET /historical/fills — trade fills older than the cutoff
GET /historical/orders — canceled/executed orders older than the cutoff
Impacted live endpoints:
GET /markets , GET /markets/{ticker} — settled markets older than market_settled_ts will not appear
GET /events with with_nested_markets=true — nested markets older than market_settled_ts will not be included
GET /series/{series_ticker}/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks , GET /markets/candlesticks — candlestick data is tied to the market; historical markets’ candlesticks must be fetched from GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks
GET /markets/trades , GET /portfolio/fills — fills older than trades_created_ts will not appear
GET /portfolio/orders — completed/canceled orders older than orders_updated_ts will not appear (resting orders are unaffected)

Jan 12, 2026 WebSocket Predictions
Added sharding support to the communications WebSocket channel for high-throughput RFQ/quote consumers. New subscription parameters:
shard_factor (int): Number of shards to divide messages across (e.g., 4)
shard_key (int): Which shard this connection receives (0 to shard_factor-1)
Messages are sharded by market_ticker using consistent hashing. Clients can run multiple connections with different shard_key values to distribute load while ensuring complete coverage. Validation:
shard_factor must be > 0 when provided
shard_key must be >= 0 and < shard_factor
shard_key requires shard_factor to be set
Example subscription:
{
"id" : 1 ,
"cmd" : "subscribe" ,
"params" : {
"channels" : [ "communications" ],
"shard_factor" : 4 ,
"shard_key" : 0
}
}
No breaking changes: When these parameters are omitted, all messages are received as before. Existing integrations are unaffected.
