🎉 No more waiting until End of Day.
Once you’ve hit your profit target and trading requirements , you can now upgrade your Evaluation Account to a Sim Funded Account instantly —and start trading the very same trading day .
As soon as your account meets the criteria, a quick sync from your dashboard is all it takes to unlock your Sim Funded journey.
How to Instantly Upgrade Your Evaluation Account
Step 1: Open Your MyFundedFutures Dashboard
Log in to your dashboard and navigate to the Stats Page
Step 2: Select Your Passed Evaluation Account
Click on the Evaluation Account that has met your profit target and trading requirements .
Step 3: Click the Sync Button
At the top of the page, click the Upgrade button.
This will:
Verify that all requirements have been met
Instantly trigger your upgrade to a Sim Funded Account
What Happens Next?
✅ Your Evaluation Account is marked as passed
✅ Your Sim Funded Account is created and ready to be activated.
✅ You can begin trading without waiting for your End of Day update.
If the Sync button does not appear or your account does not upgrade, double-check that all evaluation rules and profit target have been satisfied.
Need Help?
If you believe you’ve met all requirements but are unable to upgrade, reach out to our support team at [email protected] and we’ll be happy to take a look.
🚀 Happy trading and welcome to your Sim Funded!
