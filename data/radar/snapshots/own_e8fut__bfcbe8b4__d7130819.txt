Can I trade news?
August 27, 2026
You can trade news on E8 Zero or E8 Signature accounts without any restrictions (This includes SimFi™ challenge account (phase-1) and SimFi™ performance Account )
While we permit news trading in some scenarios, users should be aware that slippage may occur (as it would if this were a real market) and traders should factor slippage into their strategy and position sizing.
​
E8 Markets cannot protect users from positions closing beyond their expected level.
​
E8 Markets is a simulated platform designed to replicate real-market conditions; just as slippage is a risk in a real market, it also is a risk on our simulated platform that tracks real market data. Therefore, we recommend that users avoid trading during high-impact news releases.
T1 news events are defined as economic or geopolitical announcements that have the potential to significantly influence financial markets. This includes, but is not limited to, central bank decisions, economic indicators, and major political events.
News releases can be followed in our economic calendar .
