Customization
LucidDaily evaluation accounts may have an End-of-Day drawdown or an Intraday drawdown depending on which option you select at check out.
As standard, LucidDaily Funded accounts use an Intraday Drawdown, unless customized otherwise at the time of evaluation purchase.
Drawdown Type
The drawdown type dictates how your MLL is calculated
Intraday drawdown brings your MLL up with your real-time P&L
EOD drawdown brings your MLL up based on your P&L at the end of a session
The MLL increases up to a defined point
Once your account exceeds the Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks and no longer moves
