Lux Prediction Market Rules & FAQ
Lux Prediction Markets 101
Welcome to Lux Prediction Markets , the premier platform designed to help you scale your trading career without risking your own capital!
We know that elite traders often lack the substantial liquidity required to fully capitalize on macroeconomic shifts, political outcomes, and global events. That is why Lux is expanding its horizons—offering a unique opportunity to monetize your situational awareness, analytical skills, and geopolitical insights without needing a massive personal bankroll.
Lux Prediction Markets gives you the ultimate arena to put your forecasting and hedging strategies to the test and secure a Funded Account.
Step 1: Choose Your Evaluation Size
To begin your journey, select the evaluation account size that best aligns with your risk management style and financial goals. We offer accounts in GBP, USD, and EUR. (For example, a standard 10,000 account comes with an affordable entry fee of 85 in your chosen currency).
Step 2: The Evaluation Challenge
Once your evaluation account is active, your sole objective is to demonstrate your performance and risk management skills under realistic market conditions.
There is absolutely no time limit to pass this stage—you can take as long as you need to achieve the 10% profit target (e.g., a 1,000 profit on a 10,000 account). Please note that the profit target must be reached through fully closed positions (account balance); floating or unrealized profits from open trades will not count toward this benchmark. Meet this benchmark requirement while respecting our drawdown rules, and your account will be immediately upgraded to the funded tier.
Strict Risk Management & Institutional Boundaries
To protect firm liquidity and promote professional trading habits, the platform enforces strict automated risk controls (all values scale equally based on your account currency):
4% Trailing Maximum Loss Drawdown: Your account operates on a 4% Trailing Drawdown model based on your highest closed account balance at any time (All-Time High). On a 10,000 account, your initial maximum allowed loss threshold is set at 9,600. As you realize profits and reach new high-water marks on your closed balance, this 4% drawdown limit trails upward in real-time. If your settled account balance drops below this trailing threshold at any point, the account will be automatically breached.
Position Limit (Max 1 Trade Per Question): Traders are strictly restricted to a maximum of 1 trade per prediction market question. Once you open a trade on a specific market question, you cannot open secondary or duplicate positions on that same question, regardless of whether your initial position is active or closed.
Max Exposure Per Big Topic (1%): To prevent reckless over-leveraging and concentration risk, you can risk a maximum of 1% of your total account size (e.g., a 100 maximum allocation on a 10k account) across all active trades combined within a single Big Topic category at any given time. This 1% allocation actually represents  25% of your total initial risk capital (e.g., 100 out of 400 on a 10k account). Furthermore, your downside is strictly capped—even if a prediction turns out to be a total loss and settles at 0%, no Black Swan event can wipe out your full account, as you can only ever lose the specific amount you choose to risk, up to that maximum 1% of your initial account balance.
Max Earn Per Trade (2x Risk / Up to 2%): To mitigate volatility shocks on illiquid prediction markets, the maximum eligible profit you can secure from a single trade/event contract is capped at double (2x) the risk allocated to that specific trade, up to a maximum of 2% of the account size (e.g., a 200 maximum win for a 100 risk trade on a 10,000 account). Any excess profit above this cap will not count toward your target.
Welcome to the Funded Stage
Once your single evaluation phase is successfully completed, you will be promoted straight to a Funded Account . At this elite stage, you are no longer simulating; you will start earning real, substantial payouts based on your successful, consistent market predictions, with withdrawals guarded by our consistency rules.

The Profit Target (10%)
Your objective during the evaluation phase is to grow your account balance by 10% through fully closed positions. Floating or unrealized profits from open trades will not count toward this milestone.
For a standard 10,000 account, your target is to reach a realized profit of 1,000.
There is no time pressure to rush; you can trade at your own pace and wait for the perfect macroeconomic, political, or global events to build your track record.
Maximum Loss Drawdown (4% Trailing)
To ensure strict capital preservation,  your account operates on a 4% Trailing Drawdown model based on your highest closed account balance reached at any time (All-Time High).
For a standard 10,000 account, your initial maximum allowed loss threshold is set at 9,600. As you realize profits and reach new high-water marks on your closed balance, this 4% drawdown limit trails upward in real-time. This rule applies identically to both the Evaluation Phase and the Funded Stage. If your settled account balance drops below your 4% trailing drawdown threshold at any point, it will result in an automated breach of the account.

Trading Policies, Hedging & Fair Play
To maintain a fair, transparent, and compliant environment for all participants, Lux Prediction Markets enforces strict operational guidelines. Our platform is built for independent thinkers and macro forecasters; manipulation, cheating, or external management will result in immediate disqualification.
1. Account Management & Individual Access Policy
Individual Access Only: Account management, account passing, or sharing credentials with third parties is strictly prohibited. Every Lux account must be accessed and traded exclusively by the individual owner registered on the KYC documentation.
Prohibition of Copy Trading & Account Mirroring: Copy trading in any form—whether manually, via third-party software, or by mimicking another trader’s positions across single or multiple accounts—is strictly prohibited. Every trade must reflect the trader’s own independent analysis and execution. Any account detected copying or mirroring predictions will be immediately terminated.
Multi-Account Copy Trading Prohibited: For traders operating multiple accounts, duplicating or spreading the exact same active predictions across different accounts is strictly forbidden. Each account must be traded independently, and copying open positions across your multiple accounts is a direct violation of our fair play policy.
2. Strict Anti-Hedging Policy
Hedging is fundamentally banned on the Lux Prediction Markets platform. This infrastructure is designed to reward directional market evaluation, not statistical arbitrage or system exploitation.
Internal Hedging: You are not allowed to purchase opposing YES and NO contracts on the exact same event market within a single account to lock in riskless movements.
Cross-Account Hedging: Utilizing multiple Lux accounts (or coordinating with other users) to buy opposite outcomes on the same event market is strictly forbidden. Any detected hedging patterns will result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of fees.
One Position Per Prediction Question: Traders are permitted to have only one position per specific prediction question in total. Opening duplicate positions, hedging, or placing additional trades on the same prediction question is strictly prohibited at any time, including after a previous trade has been closed or settled.
3. Data Integrity, Automation & Fair Play
Automated Trading, Scripts, Bots & HFT Ban: The use of automated trading algorithms, external scripts, execution bots, browser extensions, or High-Frequency Trading (HFT) strategies is strictly forbidden. All predictions must be placed manually through the standard user interface. Accounts detected using automated tools or HFT execution will face immediate disqualification and account cancellation.
Exploitation of Latency: Traders are strictly prohibited from exploiting wrong, delayed, or outdated contract prices caused by data latency, API delays, or software glitches (this applies to both pre-event and live real-time trading).
Reporting Obligation: If you notice an obvious mispricing, a frozen contract market, or an error in live data feeds, you must report it to our team immediately. Exploiting these anomalies to gain an unfair advantage is classified as cheating.
Post-Trade Corrections: Lux reserves the right to correct, void, or cancel any trades affected by incorrect or outdated pricing, even days after the event has settled. Account balances, equity logs and targets will be adjusted retroactively if latency exploitation is detected.
Consequences: Repeated attempts to trade on delayed data, utilize unauthorized tools, or exploit system errors will lead to permanent disqualification without a refund.
4. Permitted Entry Price Range ($0.10 – $0.90)
To maintain market integrity, prevent system exploitation, and promote realistic directional forecasting, Lux Prediction Markets enforces an entry price boundary.
Allowed Odds Limits: Position entries are strictly permitted only when the contract price is between $0.10 (10¢) and $0.90 (90¢) at the moment of execution.
Prohibited Range: Opening positions on outcomes priced below $0.10 or above $0.90 is strictly forbidden.
System Enforcement: Orders placed outside this price range will be automatically rejected. Any attempt to bypass this rule will result in the trade being voided and potential account review.

The Payout Windows
Initial Window:  You are eligible to request your first payout exactly 10 days after your Funded Account is created.

Your cycle earnings are subject to a maximum eligibility cap based on your initial account size to manage tail-risk. Any profits generated above these percentages will be disqualified from the payout calculations:

Third & Future Withdrawals Example:  The eligible profit cap permanently locks at 50% (5,000). Furthermore, your profit split permanently upgrades to 80%, netting you up to 4,000 per cycle.

Lux Prediction Markets FAQ
Q: Are Prediction Markets the same as sports betting?
A: No. While you are forecasting real-world outcomes, you are not placing traditional fixed-odds bets. Instead, you are buying and selling YES/NO contracts whose prices fluctuate in real time between 1 and 99 cents based on market supply and demand, exactly like shares on a stock exchange. You can buy or sell your positions at any point before the market closes.
Q: Why does the platform cap Big Topic allocations and trade wins?
A: These boundaries (1% max exposure per Big Topic and max earn capped at 2x trade risk / up to 2%) are enforced to eliminate reckless gambling and “all-in” behavior. Our goal is to find traders who utilize structured, thoughtful hedging and risk allocation, which is vital for long-term institutional capital growth. Highly volatile or extremely high-risk “lottery” positions are not permitted.
Q: How do targets process if I have multiple open contracts?
A:  Evaluation milestones are processed sequentially based on the order of market resolution (which event finishes first) and results in fully closed positions. Your profit target is calculated strictly from your realized account balance; floating or unrealized profits from other open contracts will not count toward this milestone. If your realized balance successfully reaches the +10% target, your account instantly progresses to verification—there is no need to wait for your longer-term open contracts to close.  Conversely, if at any point your settled account balance drops below your 4% trailing drawdown threshold, the account is terminated immediately.
Q: How often can I request a payout on a Funded Account?
A: You can request your first payout exactly 10 days after your Funded Account is created. After that, you are eligible to request new payouts every 10 days from the date of your last approved withdrawal. Please note that all active trading positions must be fully closed at the time of the payout request, as withdrawals are processed exclusively from your settled account balance. There is no minimum number of trades required to request a payout.
Q: What happens to the profits that exceed the monthly/cycle cap?
A: The cap specifies the maximum eligible profit that can be calculated for a cash withdrawal during that specific 10-day cycle (35% for the 1st payout, 45% for the 2nd, and 50% for subsequent payouts). Any earnings generated above these specific thresholds within a payout window are disqualified and will be permanently removed from the balance during the payout processing.  Your account remains active and in good standing as long as your settled balance does not drop below your 4% trailing drawdown threshold.
