Becoming a FuturesElite affiliate
What you get as a partner, how to apply and what happens after you apply.
October 22, 2026
Table of contents
Our affiliate program is for creators and trading communities. Approved partners get their own community card on our Affiliates site, where communities are ranked by how much their traders have been paid.
What you get
Your own community profile : a shareable home for your community card, your traders' payouts and your social links.
Exclusive trader promos : give your audience a reason to use your code.
Commission on every sale made through your code.
Dedicated support : a contact to help you get started.
How to apply
Open our Affiliates site and click Get your community card .
Fill in the form: your full name, email address and community or brand name, at least one link where your community can be found, and a description of your community and how you'd work with FuturesElite (up to 1,000 characters). The size of your audience is optional.
Click Apply for your community card .
What happens next
Our team reviews your community and contacts you, using the details you gave us, to discuss the partnership.
If you're approved, we help you set up your profile and collect your logo and photo.
Your community card joins the lineup on our Affiliates site.
Applying doesn't create a card or confirm acceptance. We use the information you submit to review and respond to your application.
To see what a community card shows and how traders use your code, see Community cards and community codes .
