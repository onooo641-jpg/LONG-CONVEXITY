How to connect your Tradovate account to TradingView
August 20, 2026
If you have purchased your account on the Tradovate trading platform, it is possible to trade your account directly from TradingView without any additional cost!
​
1. First, log in to your Tradingview account ( Register if you do not have one).
​
2- Go to the Chart section, select Trading Panel , then Locate Tradovate.
​
3. Select Demo , insert your credentials from the E8X dashboard , and click on connect.
​
Do not forget that if you have multiple accounts, you can easily switch between them.
​
Now you are ready to trade , and nothing stops you from using the full potential of your account with the TradingView tools !
​
​
Unable to connect your E8 Signature Futures account to TradingView?
If you want to use Tradingview, you need to first log in to the Tradovate platform and accept all documents; otherwise, you won't be able to connect the account to the Tradingview platform.
