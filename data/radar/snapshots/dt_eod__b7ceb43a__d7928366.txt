All Collections General Paying for Your Order Paying for Your Order
Accepted payment methods, the DT Wallet, coupon codes and promotions, and how to fix a declined payment.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
You can pay with a card in your own name or with your DT Wallet balance, and add one coupon code per order.
Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit or debit cards in your own name.
DT Wallet, for one-time purchases of evaluations, S2F and S2L, including bulk orders. The wallet must cover the full order, and it can't pay for Monthly plans, add-ons or Pro Account activation.
Apple Pay and Google Pay are coming soon.
Coupon codes
One code per order.
Codes work on evaluations, S2F and S2L, but not on Pro Account activation.
On Monthly plans, the discount applies to every month thereafter.
Not sure which code to use? Enter DT at checkout: it applies the best promotion running at the time.
You need to enter a code at checkout to receive bulk purchase discounts.
If you arrive through an affiliate link with a code in the URL (e.g. ...?c=CBUTXBPY), the code is applied at checkout for you.
Discounts can't be applied after purchase, so a missed sale can't be added retroactively.
We don't pre-announce sales. To hear about them first, sign up for email updates on daytraders.com or follow us on Discord and Instagram .
Payment declined?
Check the card number, zip code and CVV (the 3-digit code on the back, or the 4-digit code on the front of an American Express card).
Turn off any VPN: a VPN at checkout may cause the payment to be blocked. Safari's built-in privacy features can look like a VPN, so try another browser such as Chrome.
Make sure the card is in your own name.
Still declined? Ask your bank about restrictions on your card, try another card, or contact support.
