Quests
62 quests in nine families pay extra XP and credits: daily, weekly, monthly, evaluation, funded, Instant, social, milestones and certificates. Quests never pay skill points. The current season ends on 30 November 2026.
Prestige
From level 10 you can prestige: back to level 1 with a fresh skill tree, in return for an emblem, credits and a free pack. You keep your credits, quests, certificates, ranks and running cooldowns.
