All Collections General Dashboard Stats and Account Chart Dashboard Stats and Account Chart
What each Dashboard column and account state means, and when stats update (5:00–6:00 PM ET on trading days).
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
The account chart on your Dashboard shows each account's latest stats. It isn't live: it updates once a day on trading days.
When it updates
Daily reports load between 5:00 and 6:00 PM ET on trading days.
Charts and the pass email can take until midnight ET to show them.
Nothing loads on holidays (the CME sets the schedule; see Trading Hours, Holidays and Overnight Positions ). The Dashboard catches up after the market closes on the next trading day.
Need real-time numbers? Check your platform (DayTraders Platform or R|Trader Pro) instead. See the R|Trader Pro column guide .
What each column means
Column
What it shows
TRADE DATE
The last recorded trading day. A new account that hasn't traded shows 1/1/2000.
TYPE
Account type and size, e.g. 50K Trail DayTraders.
ACCOUNT NAME
Your account number. Include it in any support ticket.
PNL
The most recent recorded profit or loss.
TDAYS
Trading Days: every day the account traded, whether or not it qualified.
QDAYS
Qualifying Days: days that met the minimum daily profit and followed every rule, including consistency. They count toward passing or toward a payout.
STOP
Your drawdown level. On Static accounts it doesn't move.
BALANCE
The current account balance.
STATE
The account status (below).
Account states
Active: in good standing, but hasn't met every requirement to pass yet (evals).
Passed: reached the profit target and the required Qualifying Days. You can now activate a Pro Account.
Blown: the balance touched the drawdown level or has been upgraded to Pro. Buy a new evaluation to start again.
Stats still missing?
If you think something is wrong, contact support with your account number.
