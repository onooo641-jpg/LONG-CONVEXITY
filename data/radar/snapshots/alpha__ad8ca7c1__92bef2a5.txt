Alpha Futures Performance Fee Rules Explained (2026)
By the Alpha Futures Team · Updated October 23, 2026
Update — July 12, 2026: The Premium plan has been discontinued. All active Premium accounts are being refunded and closed automatically — no ticket needed. Zero, Advanced, and Direct plans are unaffected. References to Premium below are kept for historical accuracy.
Part of our futures prop series. Start with What is a futures prop firm? , then explore: Platforms · Best futures prop firms (2026) · Alpha Futures review (2026) · Alpha Group · Futures vs forex prop firms (2026) · Best futures prop firms for beginners (2026) · Fastest payout futures prop firms (2026) . · what a winning day is
On Alpha Futures, Qualified Traders may request performance fees up to 4 times per month after 5 winning trading days of $200+ (days need not be consecutive). You may request up to 50% of account profit per cycle (subject to plan minimums and maximums). You receive 90% of the requested amount; processing is five business days or less after standard approval, according to the latest operational update . Current rules differ by plan: Advanced allows up to $15,000 per request; Zero has lower caps plus a 40% consistency rule on Qualified Accounts. Premium is discontinued; its historical caps are not available to new traders.
For the full payout rules by plan, see Alpha Futures payout rules .

Frequently Asked Questions
What are Alpha Futures performance fee rules?
Qualified Traders request performance fees after 5 winning days of $200+, up to 4 times per month, with 90% of the requested amount paid per policy. Minimums, maximums, and consistency differ by Zero, Standard, and Advanced.
How often can you request performance fees at Alpha Futures?
Up to 4 times per month per account, after every 5 winning trading days of $200 or more.
What is the Alpha Futures performance split?
90% to the trader on published plans — applied to the withdrawal request amount.
What is the maximum performance fee on Alpha Futures?
Advanced: up to $15,000 per request. Standard: $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 by size. Zero: $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,500 by size. Always confirm live.
Does Alpha Futures have a consistency rule on performance fees?
Zero Qualified Accounts: 40% between requests. Advanced Qualified: none. Standard evaluations: 50%; Advanced evaluations: 40%; Zero evaluations: none. Premium is discontinued.
How long do Alpha Futures performance fees take?
five business days or less after standard approval, according to the latest operational update
Why do performance fee requests get delayed?
Performance fee requests usually get delayed when eligibility rules are not met yet (fewer than 5 winning days of $200+, request caps, or plan consistency rules), when processing is delayed beyond the current five-business-day standard timing because of weekends, holidays, or verification checks, or when the account needs a compliance or identity check. On Alpha Futures, eligible requests are processed in five business days or less after standard approval, according to the latest operational update
