REFERRAL AND INVITE A FRIEND FEATURE
How We Process Invitation Data
E8 Funding offers a voluntary Referral Program that allows registered users to invite others to join the Platform. When a registered user submits another person’s email address through the referral feature, we process that email address solely for the purpose of delivering a one-time invitation email on behalf of the referring user.
We process invitee email addresses under our legitimate interest in facilitating personal user-to-user introductions to the Platform. The invitation is personal in nature (sent explicitly by a user who knows the recipient), is a one-time communication, and is not promotional spam.
What We Do With Invitee Data
Purpose limitation: The invitee’s email address is used exclusively to send the one-time invitation email. It is not added to any marketing list, newsletter, retargeting audience, or advertising platform.
Retention: If the invitee does not register for an account within thirty (30) days of the invitation being sent, their email address is automatically deleted from our systems.
No follow-up communications: We will not send additional emails to the invitee’s address unless they independently register for the Platform and separately consent to receive communications.
Invitee rights: Recipients of invitation emails may ignore the invitation with no further contact from E8. The invitation email will clearly identify who sent the invitation, why the recipient is receiving it, E8’s identity and contact details, and how to disregard the invitation.
Referral Rewards and Inviter Data
Where the Referral Program includes platform credits or rewards, those rewards are granted solely to the referring user (the Inviter) and are not communicated to the invitee as part of the invitation. Referral reward activity is recorded in the Inviter’s account and may include: the fact that an invitation was sent, whether the invitee registered, and any credit awarded as a result.
We send invitation emails only when a registered user requests us to do so. We process the recipient’s email address solely for the purpose of delivering the invitation and do not store or use it for marketing unless the recipient independently registers and consents.
User Responsibility
By submitting another person’s email address through the Referral Program, you confirm that you have that person’s permission to share their contact details with E8 for the purpose of sending an invitation, and that doing so complies with all applicable laws in your jurisdiction, including GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL, and UK PECR where applicable.
