Signature, explained.
What is the Signature Plan? Our permanent plan with payouts on demand, in three sizes: Signature 5K, 10K and 20K.
How many days do I need to trade? One day in the evaluation. Hit the profit to pass while your balance stays above your max loss and you can move to Sim Funded. Payouts have no minimum day count either.
What are the payout requirements? Once you pass, request all your profits anytime at an 80% profit split: $100 minimum on Signature 5K, $250 on 10K and 20K. Close your positions, then submit the request in your dashboard. Payouts are subject to plan terms, eligibility and review.
What happens when I pass? You move to a Sim Funded account of the same size. Your max loss stays where it was and never moves, and you can request payouts at any time: $100 minimum on Signature 5K, $250 on 10K and 20K, with an 80% profit split.
How big can I trade? Up to 5 micro contracts on Signature 5K, 10 on 10K, 20 on 20K. The size limit is the same in the evaluation and in Sim Funded.
When can I move to a Live Account? After 5 payouts or $5,000 requested, you can be considered for a Live Account. Signature accounts are simulated until then.
