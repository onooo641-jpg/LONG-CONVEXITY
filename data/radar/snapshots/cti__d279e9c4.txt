Editorial Standards
Editorial Policy
This page explains how we research, write, and review the educational articles and guides published on the City Traders Imperium blog, so you know exactly who is behind the content and how it is checked before it goes live.
Scope: This policy covers our blog and educational articles only. It does not apply to our program, pricing, or product pages, which describe our funding programs directly and are governed by our official Program Rules and Terms & Conditions.
How We Work
Our Editorial Process
Who Writes Our Content
Our blog content is produced by our in-house content team, often working directly with members of our trading team who bring first-hand experience of funded trading, drawdown rules, and payouts. Some articles are produced with freelance writers, briefed against our own program rules and FAQs to keep facts accurate.
Review Before Publishing
Every article is reviewed by a senior member of our content team before it is published, separate from the person who wrote it. This review checks the piece for factual accuracy against our current program rules and for clarity before it goes live.
How We Source Information
Where an article references our own programs, pricing, or rules, it is checked against our official Program Rules and FAQ pages. Where an article references information outside CTI, such as market data or regulatory guidance, we aim to cite the original source directly.
Corrections & Updates
If you spot an error in one of our articles, you can report it to our support team and we will review and correct it once confirmed. Articles display the date they were last updated so you can see when the information was most recently checked.
Found A Mistake
How To Report An Error
1
Include The Article Title And Link
Copy the headline and the page URL so we know exactly which article you are referring to.
2
Describe The Error Clearly
Explain what is wrong and, where possible, what the correct information should be.
3
Add Any Supporting Evidence
Screenshots, links to primary sources, or documentation that support the correction help us verify it faster.
4
Send It To Our Support Team
Email support@citytradersimperium.com with the subject line Content Correction. We will review the report and update the article once confirmed.
Spotted Something Wrong?
Let us know and we will review it. Corrections are made once confirmed, and the article’s last-updated date will reflect the change.
