What Failed, Paused and Inactive mean, why you see 'Only Admins Can Place Trades', penalties for violations and what to do after failing.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Your account status shows whether you can keep trading. Here's what each one means.
Account statuses
Status
What happened
What it means
Failed
Your balance touched the drawdown level.
The account is closed and can't be reset.
Paused
You hit the Daily Loss Limit.
Open positions are closed and trading resumes at 6:00 PM ET, when the next trading day starts. The account isn't failed.
Inactive
You missed the Minimum Activity Policy.
The account is closed and can't be reactivated.
"Only Admins Can Place Trades"
This message in your platform means trading is disabled: the account has hit its drawdown level (failed) or its Daily Loss Limit (paused until the next trading day).
Rule violations
If a prohibited strategy is confirmed, all accounts involved are closed and unpaid profits on them are forfeited. Serious or repeat violations lead to a full ban. Payouts approved before the finding are honored.
After failing
Buy a new evaluation from your Dashboard. Resets aren't available, and you can start again as many times as you like.
A failed Monthly account stops rebilling, including a failed Pro Account.
To skip the evaluation, you can buy an S2F account instead.
