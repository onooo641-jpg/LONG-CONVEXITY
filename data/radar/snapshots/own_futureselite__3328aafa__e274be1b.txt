Quick guide
Elite if you want no consistency rule or buffer once funded.
Prime if you want to pass fast: no minimum days and no consistency rule in the challenge.
Nitro if you want frequent payouts and no daily loss limit.
Instant if you want to skip the evaluation entirely.
