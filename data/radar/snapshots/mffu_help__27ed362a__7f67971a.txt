Deepchart/Deepdom Download and Connection Guide
August 24, 2026
Table of contents
Getting started into Deepchart/Deepdom is a straightforward process. Simply click the Platforms button from your MFFU dashboard , select Deepchart/Deepdom, and you will be directed to log in to the dxFeed web platform.
When you click the link, you will be prompted to log in with your platform credentials, which MyFunded Futures will send you via e-mail.
Login Screen:
When you reach the login screen, you will be prompted to enter your credentials. Please enter them carefully, as the password is case-sensitive, and you will gain access to the dxFeed dashboard.
Looking to Get Funded to trade on DEEPCHART/DEEPDOM? Start here.
Once you log in to dxFeed:
Please select your preferred platform.
2. Sign the platform agreements to proceed.
After signing all the agreements, go to the Download Platform section on your dxFeed dashboard and download the setup for your chosen platform.
Once the setup is complete, launch the Deepchart/Deepdom desktop platform;
In the Startup Wizard, select the Exchange(s) from which you would like to receive market data. Please keep in mind that we only support: CME, COMEX, CBOT, NYMEX
Select dxFeed as the Feed source and Propfirm as the server. Enter your login credentials, which are available on your MFFU dashboard.
Feed Selection
After completing the setup, the Volsys/Volbook dashboard will launch. To connect your MFFU trading account, ensure you select the data feed to confirm connection to dxFeed. Without selecting a data feed, trading on the platform will not be possible.
Click Select a connection on the Volsys/Volbook dashboard.
Select MFFU.
Once you choose the data feed MFFU , a successful connection will be confirmed with a green dot. You are now online and ready to trade via Volsys/Volbook!
