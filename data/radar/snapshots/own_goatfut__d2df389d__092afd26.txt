Two things to understand before you buy
GOAT Daily is not a faster version of Flex. Two rules are stricter, and both catch traders out:
Your funded account uses intraday trailing drawdown, not end of day. The limit moves in real time with your highest equity, including open positions. The evaluation runs end of day trailing.
The daily loss limit is optional. You choose at checkout whether to trade with it or without it, and the choice carries into your funded account. If you choose it, hitting it closes the account on the first hit.
Both are explained in full below.

How to pass
Reach the profit target of 6% of your starting balance.
Satisfy the consistency rule you chose at checkout (40% or 50%). With 40%, your profit needs to be spread across at least three trading days.
Stay within the trading rules and loss limits below.
There is no time limit. Once you pass, your evaluation period is reviewed and your funded account is activated within 2 business days.
Maximum trailing drawdown
Your maximum drawdown is the level your balance or equity cannot fall below. How it trails depends on the phase:
Evaluation. End of day trailing. The limit moves up with your profits at the end of each trading day.
Funded. Intraday trailing. The limit moves in real time with your highest equity, including open positions, rather than waiting for the end of the day.
In both phases the limit stops trailing permanently once it reaches your starting balance plus $100. Breaching it is a hard breach and closes the account immediately.

Consistency rule, evaluation only
The consistency rule limits how much of your total profit can come from a single trading day.
Evaluation: no single day can account for more than 40% or 50% of your total profit, depending on the option you chose at checkout. Landing exactly on the threshold is allowed.
Funded: no consistency rule. Trade without a per-day profit cap.
Example on a GOAT Daily 25K. Your best day produced $800. With $1,500 in total profit, that day is 53% of the total, so the evaluation is not complete yet. Your total profit needs to reach $2,000 before that $800 day is no longer above 40%.
Exceeding the threshold does not terminate your account. You simply cannot complete the evaluation until your best day is no longer above 40% of your total profit, so keep trading.
The daily progression rule
At least 50% of every payout request must be profit generated since your last approved payout. Put another way, you can request up to twice the profit you have made since your last payout, within the minimum and maximum above. For your first payout, all profit above the buffer counts as new profit.
Example on a funded GOAT Daily 50K: Your balance is $53,400, which is $1,300 above the $52,100 buffer. Your last payout was approved yesterday and you have made $700 since. The progression rule lets you request up to $1,400, twice your new profit. The 50K maximum is $1,000, so you request $1,000 and receive $800 after the 80/20 split, less the 3% processing fee, so $776 arrives. With the 90/10 add-on you receive $900, so $873 arrives. Your balance becomes $52,400 and your drawdown limit stays locked at $50,100.
If you do not meet the progression rule, the request is declined. That is not a breach. Keep trading and request again once at least half of the amount you want is profit made since your last payout.
Two checks run on every request. The progression rule above is one. The 1 minute holding rule below is the other. Both have to pass, and a request can fail either one on its own.
Payouts
Profit split: up to 90%. You keep 80% by default, or 90% with the optional 90/10 add-on for 20% of the challenge price. Accounts bought before the plan moved to 80/20 keep 90/10 from the first payout.
Eligibility: submit your first request 24 hours after you place your first trade on the funded account, then one request every 24 hours. The clock starts when the trade is placed, not when it closes. No fixed windows, no minimum trading days, no winning day requirement.
Profit buffer: your balance must be at or above the buffer level below before you can request. The buffer stays in the account and is not withdrawable.
Minimum payout: $500 on every account size.
Processing: within 2 business days of your request.
Processing fee: 3% is deducted from every payout to cover payment and transaction costs, so the amount that arrives is slightly below the figure on your payout certificate.

Holding time: the 1 minute rule
GOAT Daily uses the same 1 minute holding rule as every GFF plan.
When you pass the evaluation, and at each payout request for the period since your last payout, both of the following must be true: at least 70% of your closed trades were held longer than 1 minute, and at least 70% of your net profit came from trades held longer than 1 minute. If either is not met, we deduct the profit from winning trades held under 1 minute in that period from your payout and pay the rest. If that profit is more than the amount you requested, we decline the payout and deduct the requested amount from the account instead. This is not a breach and your account stays open.
Tick scalping, meaning opening and closing positions within seconds to capture small tick profits, is not permitted on any GFF account including GOAT Daily. Profit generated that way may be reviewed and deducted.
Trading style restrictions
GOAT Daily is a manual trading product. Bots, Expert Advisors and automated strategies are not permitted. Grid, martingale and high frequency strategies are not permitted.
News trading
A 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events in both the evaluation and the funded phase. You cannot open, close, or trigger pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release. Positions opened before the window began may be held through the event.
This is stricter than our other evaluations, which have no news restriction during the evaluation phase.
Overnight and weekend holding
Neither is permitted. Any position still open is closed automatically at 15:55 CT, five minutes before the session ends.
Inactivity
Place at least one trade every 14 calendar days, in both phases. If no trades are placed for 14 consecutive days the account is breached and closed, and the fee is not refunded. This 14-day rule applies from 7 November 2026. There is no time limit on passing the evaluation, but the account does need to stay active. Tell support in advance if you are going to be away.
Account limits and copy trading
You can hold up to 5 funded GOAT Daily accounts. This is a separate allowance from the other plans, so 5 GOAT Daily accounts can sit alongside your EOD, 1-Day-Pass or Flex funded accounts.
Copy trading is permitted between your own GOAT Daily accounts only. Copying to or from another trader's account is not permitted, and copying between a GOAT Daily account and any other plan is not permitted.
