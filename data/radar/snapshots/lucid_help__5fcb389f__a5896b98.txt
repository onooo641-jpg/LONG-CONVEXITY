LucidDaily Funded Account Benefits
Objectives on our dashboard update in real-time within 5-30 minutes of your last closed trade
Traders may request payouts daily when eligible, including multiple per session if a Daily Loss Limit is enabled
There is no Consistency Percentage on LucidDaily funded accounts
Optional End-of-Day Drawdown add-on at the time of evaluation purchase
News Trading Not Permitted
Trading U.S high impact red folder news in a LucidDaily funded account is not permitted and is a hard breach. This restriction applies only when BOTH of the following are true:
Impact = High
Currency = USD
You must be flat from 1 minute before through 1 minute after the news event. You may not hold or open new positions through that window. Your funded account will be breached.
