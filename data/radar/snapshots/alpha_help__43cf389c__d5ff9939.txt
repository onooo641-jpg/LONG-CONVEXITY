Can I copy across my Alpha Futures Accounts?
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
July 17, 2026
Many platforms have built in trade copiers. Copy trading in general is permitted as long as it is a single user executing the trades, and it is the single individual listed on the Alpha Futures Accounts.
​
​ Automated, Group, and Reverse Trading are strictly prohibited.
​
To avoid complications with copy trading, use your smallest account/one with the least amount of profit or leverage as your leader account. If the accounts have different leverage or buying power trades may not copy.
​
Please be aware copy traders are not perfect and impose risk, even if they are available directly on your trading platform. Alpha Futures is not liable or able to fix any issues you may face while using a trade copying software. It's a risk traders accept while scaling.
