💡Tip: How to read this
If you have a $50k Account , you need at least $2,600 in total profit to click the withdraw button.
Why? Because you must leave $2,100 (Buffer) in the account, and the minimum you are allowed to take out is $500 .
$2,100 + $500 = $2,600.
If your total profit is $2,500, you are not yet eligible for a payout because withdrawing $500 would drop you below the $2,100 buffer.
