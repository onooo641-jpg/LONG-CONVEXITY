Buffer, daily payouts and caps.

Table of contents
Your first payout
Build the full buffer: $1,100, $2,100, $3,100 or $4,600 above your starting balance.
Then make at least $500 more, so your balance reaches $26,600, $52,600, $103,600 or $155,100.
The first payout is available 24 hours after your first trade on the Sim Funded account.
After that
Request every 24 hours, as long as you have at least $500 above the buffer.
Each payout is capped at $1,000 (25K), $2,000 (50K), $2,500 (100K) or $2,800 (150K).
The buffer is never withdrawable. Example on 50K: at a $52,700 balance you can withdraw $600.
No consistency rule applies to payouts. The split is 90% at Sim Funded and Live.
