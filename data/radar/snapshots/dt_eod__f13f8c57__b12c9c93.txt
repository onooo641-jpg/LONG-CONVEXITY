How to activate
Within 10 days of passing, open the Funded Accounts tab on your Dashboard.
Click the passed evaluation's account number and sign the Pro Account Agreement.
Pay the $5 activation fee. Your Pro Account is active within 30 minutes; restart your platform to see it.
If the account fails
Touching the drawdown level closes the Pro Account. It can't be reset; start a new evaluation to continue.
