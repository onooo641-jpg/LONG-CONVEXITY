Futures Funding FAQs
What is Goat Funded Futures?
GOAT Funded Futures (GFF) is a futures proprietary trading firm that provides traders with simulated funded accounts to trade CME, COMEX, NYMEX, and CBOT listed futures products.
We offer a variety of plans: GOAT Daily, the EOD Challenge, the Flex Challenge and the 1-Day-Pass-Plan (evaluation-based), plus Instant (no evaluation required).
How do futures prop firms work?
Futures prop firms give traders access to accounts under a defined set of trading rules. Many require an evaluation, where traders must reach a profit target without exceeding drawdown limits. Others offer instant funding plans. At Goat Funded Futures, the route depends on the plan you choose. Each plan sets out the account type, trading rules, reward requirements and profit split.
What funding plans does Goat Funded Futures offer?
Goat Funded Futures offers GOAT Daily, Flex, EOD, 1-Day Pass and Instant plans. The evaluation plans require traders to meet the relevant targets before progressing to a funded account. The Instant plan skips the evaluation stage.
How much does an evaluation cost?
The evaluation fee depends on your plan and account size. For example, a $50K GOAT Daily evaluation is listed at $198 (pre-discount), with no activation fee after passing and reset fees from $90. See the pricing table for current prices and discounts.
What does the evaluation involve?
During an evaluation, you trade a simulated account and aim to reach the profit target while staying within the plan's trading rules. For GOAT Daily, during the evaluation, no single day can account for more than 40% or 50% of your total profit, depending on the option you choose at checkout. Once funded, there is no consistency rule. Once you meet the requirements, you can progress to a funded account.
Rules vary between plans, so check the requirements for your chosen account before purchasing.
What are the drawdown types and main trading rules?
There are two drawdown types depending on the plan: End of Day trailing drawdown (EOD, Flex, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Instant and GOAT Daily during the evaluation), which updates only at the close of each trading day so your intraday swings have room, and intraday trailing drawdown (GOAT Daily funded accounts), which updates in real time. On the 1-Day-Pass-Plan the End of Day drawdown locks at the starting balance. Core rules apply across all plans: no holding positions overnight or over the weekend, and a 2 minute buffer around high impact news events on funded accounts (on GOAT Daily, in both phases). Daily Loss Limits and consistency requirements vary by plan: for example EOD has a funded daily loss limit, while on GOAT Daily, Flex, the 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Instant the daily loss limit is optional at checkout. If selected, hitting it closes the account.
How does the profit split work?
It depends on the plan. GOAT Daily uses a 90/10 split from your first reward. EOD, the 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Flex and Instant use an 80/20 split by default, with 90/10 available as a paid add-on. The split is simply applied to the profit you withdraw, with no hidden conditions. A 3% processing fee is deducted from every reward.
How fast do I receive my rewards?
We back our rewards with a guarantee: once you request a reward, it is processed within 2 business days (within 48 hours on GOAT Daily). If your reward ever takes longer than that, you receive an extra $500 as a reward. Rewards can be requested via stablecoin (USDC / USDT) and bank transfer where available, with a $500 minimum. A 3% processing fee is deducted from every reward.
Which trading platforms can I use?
Goat Funded Futures lists NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView (via Tradovate), TickBlaze, DeepCharts, ATAS, Tradesea and WealthCharts. Check the platform information for supported connections, market data arrangements and any setup requirements before choosing.
Can I hold trades during news events?
Yes, during the evaluation phase news trading is unrestricted while you work to pass your challenge, except on GOAT Daily. On funded accounts, and on GOAT Daily in both phases, a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events, meaning you cannot open, close, or trigger pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release. This lets you trade your strategy while keeping conditions fair once you are on funded capital.

Goat Funded Futures offers GOAT Daily, Flex, EOD, 1-Day Pass and Instant plans. The evaluation plans require traders to meet the relevant targets before progressing to a funded account. The Instant plan skips the evaluation stage.
How much does an evaluation cost?
The evaluation fee depends on your plan and account size. For example, a $50K GOAT Daily evaluation is listed at $198 (pre-discount), with no activation fee after passing and reset fees from $90. See the pricing table for current prices and discounts.
What does the evaluation involve?
During an evaluation, you trade a simulated account and aim to reach the profit target while staying within the plan's trading rules. For GOAT Daily, during the evaluation, no single day can account for more than 40% or 50% of your total profit, depending on the option you choose at checkout. Once funded, there is no consistency rule. Once you meet the requirements, you can progress to a funded account.
Rules vary between plans, so check the requirements for your chosen account before purchasing.
What are the drawdown types and main trading rules?
There are two drawdown types depending on the plan: End of Day trailing drawdown (EOD, Flex, 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Instant and GOAT Daily during the evaluation), which updates only at the close of each trading day so your intraday swings have room, and intraday trailing drawdown (GOAT Daily funded accounts), which updates in real time. On the 1-Day-Pass-Plan the End of Day drawdown locks at the starting balance. Core rules apply across all plans: no holding positions overnight or over the weekend, and a 2 minute buffer around high impact news events on funded accounts (on GOAT Daily, in both phases). Daily Loss Limits and consistency requirements vary by plan: for example EOD has a funded daily loss limit, while on GOAT Daily, Flex, the 1-Day-Pass-Plan and Instant the daily loss limit is optional at checkout. If selected, hitting it closes the account.
How does the profit split work?
It depends on the plan. GOAT Daily uses a 90/10 split from your first reward. EOD, the 1-Day-Pass-Plan, Flex and Instant use an 80/20 split by default, with 90/10 available as a paid add-on. The split is simply applied to the profit you withdraw, with no hidden conditions. A 3% processing fee is deducted from every reward.
How fast do I receive my rewards?
We back our rewards with a guarantee: once you request a reward, it is processed within 2 business days (within 48 hours on GOAT Daily). If your reward ever takes longer than that, you receive an extra $500 as a reward. Rewards can be requested via stablecoin (USDC / USDT) and bank transfer where available, with a $500 minimum. A 3% processing fee is deducted from every reward.
Which trading platforms can I use?
Goat Funded Futures lists NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView (via Tradovate), TickBlaze, DeepCharts, ATAS, Tradesea and WealthCharts. Check the platform information for supported connections, market data arrangements and any setup requirements before choosing.
Can I hold trades during news events?
Yes, during the evaluation phase news trading is unrestricted while you work to pass your challenge, except on GOAT Daily. On funded accounts, and on GOAT Daily in both phases, a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events, meaning you cannot open, close, or trigger pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release. This lets you trade your strategy while keeping conditions fair once you are on funded capital.
