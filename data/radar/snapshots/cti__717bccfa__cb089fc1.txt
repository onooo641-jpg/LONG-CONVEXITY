What is CTI Instant Funding?
CTI Instant Funding lets you bypass the challenge
entirely and start funded the moment you sign up.
Choose an account from $2,500 to $80,000, receive
50% of that balance as your starting capital, and
earn a 50% profit share from your very first trade.
There is no phase to pass and no time limit.
How is Instant Funding different?
Unlike the 1-Step and 2-Step Challenges, Instant
Funding has no evaluation phase. You’re funded
from day one instead of hitting a profit target
first. It uses a 6% static drawdown with no daily
drawdown limit, and scales up to $2,000,000 per
account ($4,000,000 across two), well beyond the
$200K/$400K cap on the Challenge programs.
How does the 6% drawdown work?
Your 6% maximum drawdown is calculated from your
initial account balance, not your peak equity, so it
never trails your profits. There is no separate daily
drawdown limit and no daily pause on your trading, so
a strong trading day never shrinks tomorrow’s
buffer.
How does scaling work?
You start with 50% of your chosen account size as
your starting balance. Hit a 10% profit target and
your account doubles to full size. Every additional
10% milestone after that adds another 100% to your
balance, up to a maximum of $2,000,000 per account
($4,000,000 across two accounts), while your profit
share climbs from 50% up to 70%, then 80%, and
eventually 90% or 100% through the CTI VIP program. To scale, you also need a 20% consistency score.
