The problem
In the world of charts, fundamentals, and risk, the gap between learning and succeeding is a canyon. Most traders bridge that gap with their personal capital - and lose.
​
Simulated Finance and E8 Markets are the bridge. We provide the simulated capital; you provide the skill. We are a training ground, the trading cockpit where you can test, fail, and see your progress with crystal clear data feedback. At E8, we do not just provide a testing platform — we reward proven performance.
​
The Mission
Our mission is to give you top-quality simulated trading with tools and platforms that will not only guide your learning in the world of trading but also help you discover who you are as a trader and understand your weaknesses.
The best part is that you can test your skills without risking your personal capital. You will know precisely what services you are getting and their exact cost.
We provide a space where you can learn and get access to the simulated trading platform, free tools, and statistics, as well as data from your trading history: all in one place.
Once you prove your skills in the Challenge stage, you'll advance to the SimFi™ Performance account . This is where you can qualify for Payouts based on your performance. It won't be easy; you need to trade with discipline, follow the guardrails (Rules), and create clean performance data. Once you do, you can finally develop as a trader.
​
Why us?
Established in 2021, $71 million has been processed in Payouts, 300,000 + registrations.
You know exactly what you are paying for.
The challenge fee is the only cost.
You get access to the simulated environment when you can test your skills, improve, and get rewarded when your performance data speaks for itself.
You have access to numerous trading tools and 5 trading platforms
You can follow your track record with our modern E8X Dashboard tool
You get guidance throughout the whole process
You can test your skill and eventually understand whether you are ready to trade with personal capital in live markets.
What we are not:
We do not act as a broker or fund.
We do not take customer deposits
We do not manage users' money or trade on anyone's behalf
We do not offer investment advice
NOTE: E8 Markets provides a structured simulation environment for individuals seeking to develop trading skills and demonstrate performance under defined conditions.
