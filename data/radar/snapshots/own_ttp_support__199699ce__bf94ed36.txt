Futures Instant — How It Works
Futures Instant gives you an account that starts directly in the Earning Phase. There's no evaluation to pass and no waiting period. You buy the account, connect your platform, and every profitable trade counts toward your first reward.
October 19, 2026

All trading takes place on demo accounts using simulated funds.
Why traders choose Futures Instant
No Challenge, no evaluation
You don't need to prove yourself in a Challenge first. Your account is in the Earning Phase from your first trade.
90% profit split
You keep 90% of the profits you generate.
Rewards every 5 days
After you complete 5 trading days and meet the payout requirements, you can request a reward every 5 days. Rewards are paid by bank wire or crypto within 1 business day.
No time limit
Futures Instant accounts have no fixed duration, so you can trade at your own pace. The only requirement is to place at least one trade every 14 days.
A drawdown that locks in your progress
The Maximum Drawdown is calculated on your end-of-day balance, so intraday swings don't count against you. As you grow the account, the drawdown trails upward until it reaches your starting balance, and then it stops trailing.
A Daily Pause
If you hit your daily loss limit, your positions are closed and trading pauses until the next day. Your account stays active. The limit also grows as your profits grow.
Trade the news
News trading is allowed with no restricted windows.
Run two accounts and copy between them
You can hold up to 2 Futures Instant accounts and copy trades between your own accounts.
Your choice of platform, with free data
You can trade on NinjaTrader, Tradovate+TradingView, ATAS, Quantower, DeepCharts or R|Trader. CME Group Level 1 data is included at no extra cost.

How it works in 4 steps
1. Choose your account. In your Client Area, select Futures Instant and pick $50,000 or $100,000.
2. Connect and trade. Log in to your platform with the credentials we send you and start trading intraday futures.
3. Meet the payout requirements. Trade for at least 5 days, reach the profit target for your account size, and keep your best day at or below 20% of your total profit for the cycle. Your dashboard tracks all of this in real time.
4. Request your reward. Close all positions and request your payout. After each approved payout, a new cycle starts, and you can request again every 5 days.
