What is the CTI 1-Step Challenge?
The 1-Step Challenge is CTI’s most direct route
to funded trading. Pay a one-time fee, hit an 8%
profit target while staying within a 5% trailing
drawdown, and you’re funded, trading CTI
capital with an 80% profit share that scales up to
100% through our VIP program.
No second phase to slow you down. No rolling deadline
to manage. News, Expert Advisors, Martingale, and
weekend holds are all allowed.
How does the 5% trailing drawdown work?
Your drawdown floor is your highest balance minus a fixed 5% of your starting balance. As your balance makes new highs, the floor rises with it. When you take a payout, the floor drops by the amount you withdraw, so you keep exactly the same room you had before and never need to build a buffer before withdrawing your profits.
Can I trade news, use EAs, or hold over weekends?
Yes to all three. No restrictions on news, Expert
Advisors, Martingale, overnight holds, or weekend
holds.
Can I try before I pay?
Yes — free trial. Same platforms, same
conditions. No card needed.
