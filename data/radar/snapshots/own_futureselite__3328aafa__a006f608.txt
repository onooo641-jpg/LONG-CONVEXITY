After you pass, KYC is usually reviewed within about 24 working hours. If your KYC is already approved, your funded account is created instantly once all pass rules are met. Then sign your contract. Elite has no activation fee.
KYC rules
KYC is done in your dashboard and verified by Veriff.
It must be done by the person who bought the account and trades it, not a friend or family member.
KYC is reviewed every time an account is upgraded. If two or more people are involved, the account is breached.
If Veriff can't accept your document, the upgrade waits until you provide one it supports.
