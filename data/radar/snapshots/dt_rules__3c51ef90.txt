One login per household, no account transfers or sharing, VPN/VPS use, and the conduct we expect from traders.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Each DayTraders login and its accounts are for one person's own use.
One login per household
One login per household. If multiple accounts in a household are detected, all accounts may be subject to closure and all simulated or live profits forfeited. If you have any questions, please contact support.
No sharing or transfers
Accounts (evaluation, Pro, S2F or live) and login details can't be transferred to anyone else.
Don't share your credentials or let someone else trade your account, and don't trade accounts for other people.
If you no longer want an account, cancel it from your Dashboard.
VPN and VPS
Using a VPN or VPS for trading is allowed. Using a VPN during checkout may cause your payment to be blocked, so turn it off before you buy.
Code of conduct
We expect professionalism toward our team, other traders and the company, respect for our rules and procedures, and a backup plan for technical problems.
