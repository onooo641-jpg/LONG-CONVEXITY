Trading platforms
Which platforms CFD and Futures accounts use: MT5 and Match Trader for new CFD accounts, NinjaTrader (Tradovate) for all new Futures accounts. Existing TradeLocker and Deepcharts accounts are unaffected.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 20 November 2026
Short answer
CFD: new accounts are available on MetaTrader 5 or Match Trader (US clients: Match Trader only).
Futures: all new Futures accounts, including Zero, Elite, Reserve and Direct, trade on NinjaTrader (Tradovate) .
TradeLocker (CFD) and Deepcharts (Futures) are no longer available to purchase. Existing TradeLocker and Deepcharts accounts stay exactly as they are. If you have an existing challenge, you'll receive your funded account on the same platform you used for the challenge.
CFD platforms
CFD accounts are available on:
MetaTrader 5 (MT5)
Match Trader
US clients can only use Match Trader.
TradeLocker
TradeLocker is no longer offered from 20 November 2026 . If you have questions about an existing TradeLocker account, contact support through live chat.
Can I change my CFD platform?
Only if no trades have been placed on the account. Once you've traded, the account can't be moved because of platform limitations. You can also ask to change platform when you move to a new phase, as long as you haven't traded on the new account yet.
Giveaway accounts can't change platform.
Only the registered account holder can request a change. Requests from anyone else won't be processed.
Every request needs internal approval.
Futures platform: NinjaTrader (Tradovate)
Every new Futures account, evaluation and funded, on every plan, trades on NinjaTrader. NinjaTrader and Tradovate are the same platform, so you may see either name.
Account and execution: cloud-based and fast, usable in a browser, on desktop or on mobile.
Charting and tools: advanced charting, custom indicators, backtesting and professional execution tools.
NinjaTrader supports strategy automation, but our automated trading rules still apply: fully automated trading isn't allowed. See Prohibited trading strategies .
Deepcharts (DXFeed)
Deepcharts and the DXFeed data feed are no longer offered from 16 November 2026 . If you have questions about an existing Deepcharts account, contact support through live chat.
