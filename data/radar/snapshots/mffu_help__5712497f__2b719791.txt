MyFundedFutures accepts payment by credit/debit card and by cryptocurrency. This article outlines each accepted payment method, the supported networks for crypto payments, and what to do after completing a purchase.

After Making a Purchase
Complete your purchase using one of the accepted payment methods above.
Log in to your My Funded Futures Dashboard .
Navigate to the Stats tab.
Locate your account credentials.
Important Notes
Card payments must be made with a card in your name.
Google Pay and Apple Pay are available at certain states only.
Crypto payments must be sent to the correct address on a supported network. Confirm both the currency and the network before sending.
Check your Stats tab for the credentials after making a purchase.
