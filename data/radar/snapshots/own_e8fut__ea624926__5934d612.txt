Can I try E8 Markets for Free?
Try our free E8 trial account and experience what it's like to trade with us.
September 10, 2026
Table of contents
We believe that it is important to provide users with a glimpse at what we can offer them. Simply register with us and request our “ try before you buy ” trial account to see what it is like to trade in a simulated environment with real market data before you purchase one of our challenges.
​
Before you start
You are eligible for 2 free E8 trials every 28 days .
Users using a trial account are not eligible to receive any payouts from E8 Markets. The free trial is only for testing purposes.
E8 trial mimics the same conditions as E8 One accounts on the Matchtrader platform or as E8 One Perpetual accounts on the E8 Terminal platform (Based on which option you choose, it will mimic the same conditions and instruments as you would have on challenges).
If you want to use Matchtrader Trial, you need to be verified; if you want to go for E8 Terminal, you need to be at least registered through a Google or Discord account.
Right now, there is no option to test Futures challenges with a free trial.
How to receive a Free Trial account
When you log in to your profile through the E8X dashboard, please click on Start Trading, select Classic Markets, and choose an E8 Trial
Please note that all trial accounts are automatically closed after 30 days from the date the account was created.
One-click Trial
Skip the steps; you can now start a trial in a single click, directly from the E8 Markets homepage.
Click Free trial on the homepage.
If you're within your limit (2 free trials every 28 days), your trial account is created automatically - no checkout, no manual setup.
​
You are redirected straight into your trial dashboard; instantly, and with one click, you can be logged into E8 Terminal.
All the same conditions and limits above still apply - trial accounts are not eligible for payouts and close automatically after 30 days. This trial is only on the E8 Terminal platform. If you want to try Matchtrader, you need to go through the checkout process.
