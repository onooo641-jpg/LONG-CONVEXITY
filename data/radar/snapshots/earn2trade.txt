Futures Prop Firm?
A futures prop firm gives traders access to its own capital to trade the futures markets, rather than risking their own money. You first pass an evaluation that proves you can trade within defined risk limits. Once funded, you trade the firm's capital and keep a share of the profits you make. Futures prop firms like Earn2Trade let traders prove their skills in an evaluation first, then trade a funded account with the firm's capital.
