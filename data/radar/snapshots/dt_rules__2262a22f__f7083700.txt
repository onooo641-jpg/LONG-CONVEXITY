What is the rTrader Pro Copier?
The rTrader Pro Copier is a trade-copying feature provided by Rithmic that allows traders to replicate trades across multiple accounts. This tool enables users to designate a Leader Account , which then mirrors executed trades onto selected Follower Accounts .
This 3rd Party Plug in allows traders to copy on each platform that is compatible with our DayTraders Rithmic accounts >HERE< .
How do I purchase the Trade Copier and how much is it?
Purchased from the Member Dashboard under "Rithmic Add Ons"
Price: $10 per month.
**Important Note:
Expires at 5:00 PM ET on the last day of the month, regardless of purchase date
It is best to purchase the add-on on the first day of the month to get full value.
How do I set the Trade Copier?
To activate the Trade Copier in rTrader Pro:
Open rTrader Pro.
Locate the Launch Window on the right-hand side.
Select Trade Copier .
Add your Leader Account – trades placed on this account will be copied to linked Follower Accounts.
Add all Follower accounts that should mirror trades from the Leader Account.
Enable or disable individual accounts as needed.
Adjusting Quantity Multiplier and Other Settings
To adjust settings such as Quantity Multiplier or Fractional Quantity Rounding:
Disable the Copy Status on the account you wish to modify.
Make the necessary adjustments to your preferences.
Once all settings have been configured, re-enable the Copy Status to resume copying trades.
How do I cancel orders using the Trade Copier?
If you need to cancel orders manually within rTrader Pro:
Navigate to the Positions tab in the Launch Window.
Always cancel the orders from the Leader Account first to ensure that all associated Follower Accounts are also canceled.
If an order is canceled from a Follower account before canceling from the Leader Account, it may result in an unintended reverse position for that Follower Account.

How do I prevent unintended positions when canceling orders?
Disable the copier function before canceling orders from any Follower Account.
Always pull orders from the Leader account first before modifying or closing any positions on Follower Accounts.

Can I Use the rTrader Pro Copier with Other Trade Copiers?
No, the rTrader Pro Copier provided by Rithmic cannot be used in combination with other trade copiers. Using multiple copiers simultaneously may cause execution errors, duplicate orders, or unintended trading behavior. To ensure accurate order replication and avoid potential conflicts, it is recommended to use only the rTrader Pro Copier when copying trades across multiple accounts.
