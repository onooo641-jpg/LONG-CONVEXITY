What is E8 Zero?
Built for Futures traders who want minimal payout restrictions. The consistency rule is only in the challenge stage, which means that traders can enjoy no consistency rules, no daily profit cap, and no minimum profit requirements in the performance stage. The only limitations are payout caps and a maximum of 5 payouts per account, but it still offers the biggest freedom of any E8 program.
​
E8 Zero comes in 2 versions - E8 Zero Starter and E8 Zero Max. Both versions have identical rules; the only difference is that the starter version is cheaper with smaller payout caps, and Zero Max has a slightly higher price but offers bigger payout caps.

Frequently asked questions for E8 Zero
Can I request daily payouts?
​
Yes, you can request daily payouts as long as you reach a minimum payout amount of $100. With E8 Zero, you can enjoy daily payouts without a consistency rule or minimum profitable days
Can I request profits left over from a previous payout cycle?
​
No. Each cycle's payout comes only from profits generated in that cycle. Whatever stays in the account after a request is considered a buffer — it simply remains in the account.
What happens after I request 4 payouts?
​
After you request 4 payouts, no matter what amount you requested in total, the cycle is closed, the account is deactivated, and you move on with a free Challenge account of the same size.
Is there a consistency rule?
​
There is a 40% Consistency rule only in the challenge stage. With this rule, you must ensure that no single trading day exceeds more than 40% of your total generated profits.​ Since it is only in the challenge stage, you can enjoy freedom and daily payouts in the performance stage.
