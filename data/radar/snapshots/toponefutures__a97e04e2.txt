Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 9, 2026
Top One Futures LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you interact with our website, services, and trading programs, including when you contact us through our live chat. By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy.
‍
If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please refrain from using our services. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact us at support@toponefutures.com.
We take your privacy seriously.
This policy describes what personal information we collect and how we use it.
1. Information We Collect
Personal Information
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:
Register for an account
Subscribe to our services
Contact customer support, including through our live chat or by email
Participate in surveys or promotions
This may include:
Name
Email address
Phone number
Physical address
Billing address
Payment Information
When you make purchases, we may collect payment details such as:
Credit/debit card numbers
Billing information
Other payment-related details
Payment data is securely processed by third-party providers. We do not store or manage your full payment details. Please review your payment provider's privacy policy for additional information.
Automatically Collected Information
When you interact with our platform, we automatically collect information related to your device and usage patterns, including:
IP address
Browser type
Device type
Operating system
Referring URLs
Interaction with our services
We use this data to improve security, monitor performance, and enhance user experience.¸¸
Live Chat Information
When you use the live chat on our websites, we collect:
Your name (required to start a chat)
Your email address (optional, but needed if you want to talk to a member of our support team)
The messages you send and receive
Files you send us in the chat, such as images, PDFs and videos (up to 50 MB per file)
The page of our website from which you started the chat
Your IP address and network, and the country our network provider Cloudflare derives from it
A random identifier stored in your browser, so your chat can continue when you come back to our website
Your rating of the chat (1 to 5), if you choose to give one
Security signals used to tell people apart from automated bots: a bot check run by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Turnstile) and basic typing patterns, such as the time to your first keystroke, the number of keys pressed, whether text was pasted, and how long the chat window was open. We do not record what you type for this purpose; we only measure these patterns.
Please note: while you are typing a message in the chat, our support team may see a live preview of the text before you press send.
Do not share sensitive details in the chat. Never send full card numbers, card security codes (CVV), passwords or identity documents through the chat. We may ask for the last 4 digits of the card you used, to find your payment. If a full card number is sent, our system automatically masks it so only the last 4 digits are kept.
2. How We Use Your Information
We process collected information for the following purposes:
Account Management : Creating and maintaining user accounts.
Customer Support : Responding to inquiries and providing assistance, including through our live chat and by email. This includes verifying your email address with a one-time code before we discuss any account information, and sending you follow-up emails, chat transcripts and rating requests.
AI-Assisted Support : Using artificial intelligence (AI) tools to give first answers based on our public help-center articles, to help our team draft replies, to summarise chats, to translate messages between your language and English, and to check replies against our internal procedures. See "Use of Artificial Intelligence" below.
Quality and Training : Reviewing chats and ratings to check and improve the quality of our support.
Security and Abuse Prevention : Detecting spam, bots and abusive use of our live chat, and blocking email addresses, browsers or networks that misuse it.
Service Improvement : Enhancing website functionality and user experience.
Fraud Prevention : Detecting and preventing unauthorized or fraudulent activities.
Legal Compliance : Meeting legal and regulatory obligations.
Use of Artificial Intelligence
Our live chat uses AI models provided by Anthropic (Claude). When you use the chat, the content of your conversation is sent to Anthropic so the AI can:
Answer your question first, using only our public help-center articles
Help our support team draft replies
Summarise the conversation for our team
Translate messages between your language and English
Check our replies against our internal procedures
Support quality reviews of our chats
AI answers are general information. They may not always be correct and they do not change our Terms and Conditions or our help-center articles. If you need a member of our team, you can ask for one in the chat; you will need to give and verify your email address. The AI does not make decisions about your account, payouts or bans on its own. Anthropic does not use the content of your chats to train its models.

Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website usage, improve platform performance, and provide personalized content and advertisements. These technologies may automatically collect information such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, session duration, and user interactions across our website. This helps us better understand user behavior and optimize our services.
Cookies allow us to:
Estimate audience size and usage patterns
Store your preferences and customize services based on your interests
Improve website speed and performance
Recognize you when you return to our website
Enhance security and fraud prevention
Support analytics, attribution, and advertising performance tracking
Web Beacons
Pages of our website and emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These help us count users who have visited certain pages, opened emails, and measure the effectiveness of our communications and marketing campaigns.
Third-Party Cookies
Some content, services, or advertisements on our website may be served by third parties, including analytics providers, advertising partners, payment processors, and fraud prevention providers. These third parties may use cookies, pixels, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies to collect information about your activity across websites and services.This information may be associated with your personal information and may be used for behavioral advertising, conversion tracking, remarketing, and improving service performance.
Analytics & Advertising Tools
We may use third-party tools including but not limited to:
Google Analytics

These tools help us understand visitor behavior, improve user experience, analyze user journeys, optimize conversion rates through A/B testing, improve marketing campaigns, and measure advertising performance.
Affiliate Tracking Cookies
Affiliate and ambassador referral links may place cookies on your device for a limited period of time (for example, up to 60 days). This allows us to properly track referrals and ensure the correct affiliate receives commission for qualifying purchases or subscriptions made during that period.
In cases where multiple affiliate sources are involved, the most recent valid attribution may apply.
Live Chat Storage and Bot Check
Our live chat stores a random identifier in your browser's local storage (a technology similar to a cookie). It lets your chat continue when you return to our website. It is not used for advertising. You can remove it by clearing your browser's site data; your previous chat will then no longer appear in the chat window.
Before a chat starts, Cloudflare Turnstile runs a check to confirm you are not an automated bot. Cloudflare may process information about your browser and device for this check.
Managing Cookies
You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or via the cookie banner displayed on our website. Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or disable cookies.
Please note that disabling cookies may limit certain features, functionality, or accessibility of some parts of the website.

6. Data Retention
We retain your information only as long as necessary for legal, operational, and security purposes. When no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymized.
Live Chat Data
Chat conversations are kept for 24 months. After that we delete them, or strip out anything that could identify you, such as names, emails and account or payment details, and keep only the anonymous text.
Files sent in the chat are kept for 24 months and then deleted.
Network blocks used to stop spam or abuse last 7 days. Short holds on networks that send many chats at once last 15 minutes. Blocks on an email address or browser stay in place until our team lifts them.
Copies held by our service providers are deleted under their own retention terms.
We may use that anonymous chat text, and share it with partners, to improve our support and to train AI models. It contains nothing that identifies you.
We may keep information longer where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce our Terms and Conditions.
7. Subscription & Payments
We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your information, including:
Data encryption

Access controls
Email verification with a one-time code before we discuss account information in the live chat
Automatic masking of full card numbers sent in the live chat (only the last 4 digits are kept)
Spam, bot and abuse protection on our live chat
However, no system is completely immune to security breaches. Always take precautions when using online services.
