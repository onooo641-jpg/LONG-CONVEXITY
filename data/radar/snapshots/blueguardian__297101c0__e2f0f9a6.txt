How Blue Guardian works
How evaluations, funded accounts and instant accounts work for CFD and Futures, what happens when you pass, and the Trader Agreement.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 15 November 2026
Short answer
Choose an evaluation and reach its profit target while following the rules to earn a funded account, or buy an instant account and start funded straight away. Once funded, you keep a share of the profits you make.
From evaluation to funded trader Replay
The path most traders follow. Instant accounts skip the evaluation and pass steps.
CFD accounts
Evaluations: 1 Step Nano, 1 Step Standard, 2 Step Standard, 2 Step Nano and Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL). Pass your evaluation and a funded account is issued.
Instant accounts: Instant Standard and Instant Starter give you a funded account immediately, with no evaluation.
Account sizes: $5K, $10K, $25K, $50K, $100K, $200K, $300K and $400K. The size is the balance you trade with.
Futures accounts
One-phase evaluation: Zero, Elite and Reserve accounts use a single evaluation phase. Reach the profit target while following the rules, and your account is reviewed and then funded. Zero lets you choose your drawdown type, daily loss limit and consistency rule at checkout, and pays out daily once funded.
Direct (instant funding): start on a funded account immediately, with no evaluation.
Express: no longer available to buy from 16 November 2026. Existing Express accounts keep their rules.
Account sizes: $25K, $50K, $100K and $150K.
One-time fee: every Futures account is a single purchase. There are no monthly subscriptions, renewal fees or activation fees when you get funded.
When does my evaluation start?
CFD: your evaluation starts with your first trade. The inactivity rule, however, counts from the moment the account is created, so don't leave a new account untouched for more than 30 days.
I passed. What happens next?
CFD: within 6–12 business hours you'll receive your funded account login details by email, together with your Trader Agreement and Funded Trader Certificate .
Futures: once you hit the profit target, your account goes through a final review. After it's approved, you move on to your funded account.
The Trader Agreement
Your relationship with Blue Guardian as a funded trader is governed by the Blue Guardian Trader Agreement . It's a legally binding contract, issued after you pass, and it must be signed before your funded account is issued.
Am I trading with real money?
CFD: no. All CFD accounts, including funded accounts, are demo accounts in a simulated environment with virtual funds. They use real market prices from liquidity providers so that trading conditions are as realistic as possible.
Futures: funded accounts start as simulated (sim) accounts . Traders can progress to a Live Account . Zero has its own route: see Zero Path to Live . For Elite, Reserve and Direct, see Elite, Reserve & Direct Path to Live .
