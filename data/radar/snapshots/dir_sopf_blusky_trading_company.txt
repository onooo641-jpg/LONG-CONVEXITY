Evaluation consistency
Conditional
Varies by plan: Launch evaluations 50%, Propel/Static 34% (21% on the 300K), Orbit evaluations none. The Buffer Zone between evaluation and Sim Funded is 34% (21% on the 300K).
Funded consistency
Conditional
Sim Funded consistency varies: Launch/Propel/Static Sim Funded has none; Orbit Sim Funded is 40% (resets each processed payout); Direct to Funded/Instant Sim Funded is 21%.
News trading
Allowed
Yes. BluSky Trading Company lets you trade during news releases at your own risk, including in live accounts unless your account has been specifically restricted. Expect slippage and wider spreads around major reports.
Swing trading
Not allowed
Copy trading
Allowed
Yes. BluSky Trading Company allows trade copiers across your own BluSky accounts. Mirroring other traders’ trades or following signal services is prohibited, and all trading must reflect your own independent decisions.
Algorithmic trading
Conditional
Automated/algorithmic trading is permitted; using automated software (AI, ultra-high-speed, bulk data entry) to manipulate or secure an unfair advantage is prohibited and can trigger account closure.

High frequency trading
Conditional
BluSky allows automated trading under its Ethical Trading Policy, but bans ultra-high-speed automation and strategies that exploit latency or market delays. Ordinary automated strategies may be reviewed; latency-exploiting HFT is not permitted.
VPN use
Allowed

KYC
Applies
Yes. BluSky Trading Company requires identity verification through Veriff after you pass an evaluation, and moving to a live brokerage account requires full KYC/AML checks by the broker before you can trade real capital.
Evaluation time limit
Conditional
Launch/Propel/Static evaluations have no hard deadline (30-day subscription renews without resetting an open account); Orbit is a one-time purchase valid 30 days and expires unpassed.
Inactivity rule
Applies
Sim Funded and Live Brokerage accounts must place at least one trade every 30 days; accounts left untraded past 30 days are subject to closure by the broker.
Clear path to live
Conditional
BluSky reviews Sim Funded traders for Live Brokerage after about $10,000 total profit (Orbit at its Sim Funded profit ceiling). Approved traders either start a live account at $0 with eligible Sim profit in a Bonus Vault, or take a one-time cash buyout equal to 50% of the account’s drawdown.
