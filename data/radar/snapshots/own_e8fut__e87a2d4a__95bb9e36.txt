Can I purchase an account with someone else's card?
March 22, 2026
Table of contents
It is not possible to use someone else's card for the purchase.
Before you purchase any of our offered challenges , you need to make sure that:
Your registered name matches your name on the card
Your provided address is the same as your billing address on your card
You have enough funds on your card, and there are no set limits that can prevent you from making the purchase
Using someone else's card or card chargebacks can lead to the termination of your personal account and the closure of all of your active challenges and performance accounts, due to breaching our Terms and Conditions.
Single User (E8X) Profile policy
Users are only allowed to have a single user profile, so all purchases of our challenges must be made through one registered user profile. All accounts must be traded solely by their respective owners.
Creating multiple user profiles under different emails or trading accounts from different users is a violation of our terms and conditions and may lead to the temporary or permanent suspension of your active accounts and user profile.
​
If you have unintentionally created an additional profile, please reach out to our support team at support@e8markets.com and we will assist in deleting the extra profile promptly.
