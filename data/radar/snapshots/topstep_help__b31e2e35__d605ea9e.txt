How to Activate
Log in to the Topstep Dashboard
Click Accounts on the left side
Click Add-ons on the top left
Click Activate on the Practice Account option
👉 To close your Practice Account, follow the same steps and click Unsubscribe instead.
👉 To Reset your Practice Account: click Unsubscribe, then click Activate to create a new one.
Practice Account FAQs
I can't activate my Practice Account — what should I check?
In order to have a Practice Account, you must have an active Trading Combine subscription. If your subscription is canceled or you only have an Express Funded Account, the Practice Account add-on will not work.
Additionally, an incomplete IDV verification may be the cause. Check your email for the IDV form, complete it, then return to the Add-ons page and try again. IDV is also required for new Trading Combine purchases.
My Practice Account shows as Ineligible and I can't Unsubscribe — what do I do?
My Practice Account shows as Ineligible and I can't Unsubscribe — what do I do?
If your Trading Combine subscription is already canceled and your Practice Account shows as Ineligible, the Unsubscribe button may not be available. In this case, please contact our Support Team and we'll close the account for you.
Is there a limit to how many times I can Reset a Practice Account each day?
Yes, you can Reset a Practice Account up to 10 times per day. All Practice Account Resets are free, but the limit applies.
​
How long do I have access to the Practice Account?
As long as you have an active Trading Combine subscription.
