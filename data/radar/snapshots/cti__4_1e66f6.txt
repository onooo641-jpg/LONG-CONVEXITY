Section 8 of 25
Types of Personal Data We Collect
We may collect, use, store and transfer different types of personal data about you. This mainly includes the following:
Identity data: first and last name and username.
Contact data: address, email address and telephone number.
Identity verification data: the identity document, date of birth, a selfie and other information you submit when we verify your identity, and the result of the check. Our identity verification provider collects this on our behalf.
Transaction data: details of payments you make, the products you buy from us, and the payout details you give us so that we can pay you rewards or profit shares. We do not receive or store your card details; they are handled by our payment processor.
Technical data: IP address, approximate location worked out from it, login data, browser type and version, time zone setting, operating system and device type.
Usage data: how you use our website, client dashboard and emails.
Profile data: username, password (stored only in encrypted form), preferences, feedback and survey responses.
Marketing and communications data: your marketing preferences and your correspondence with us.
The term personal data (or personal information) means any information that can identify an individual. Anonymous data, where identity details have been removed, does not fall under this category.
Apart from the biometric data used for identity verification (see below), we do not collect any special categories of personal data, including:
Race or ethnicity.
Religious or philosophical beliefs.
Sex life or sexual orientation.
Political opinions.
Trade union membership.
Health-related information.
Genetic data.
Criminal convictions or offences.
Biometric data for identity verification. When we verify your identity, our identity verification provider compares your selfie with the photo on your identity document (a face match) and checks that the selfie is of a live person. This involves biometric data. We use it only to verify your identity and prevent fraud, with your explicit consent, which you give during the verification process. We keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes and to meet our legal obligations.
Important notice: our website and services are not intended for individuals under 18 years old. If you are under 18, please log out immediately.

We rely on our legitimate interest in preventing fraud. These tools run for all visitors, because they cannot protect us otherwise. They keep the minimum data needed:
On our website, they keep a session identifier that is deleted when you close your browser.

We use it to detect fraud, account sharing and abuse, and to protect your account. We use this data for fraud prevention and security only.
You can object to this processing (see section 21), and we will consider your objection against our need to prevent fraud.

Cookies: Your Choice, Your Control
We use cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, on our website and client dashboard. We use the following categories:
Strictly necessary (always on): needed for the website to work, for example to remember your cookie choice, keep the site secure and keep you logged in.
Preferences (optional): remember choices you make, such as language or region.

Cookies last from the end of your visit up to two years, depending on their purpose. In our client dashboard, we record activity for all users for fraud prevention and security (see section 13).

How We Protect Your Personal Data
We take your data security seriously and ensure that all information you provide is stored on secure servers.
Your responsibility: if we have given you (or you have chosen) a password to access certain parts of our platforms, it is your responsibility to keep it confidential. Do not share your password with anyone.
Our security measures: we implement strict security procedures to prevent your data from being lost, misused, or accessed without authorization. In the event of a suspected data breach, we have procedures in place to:
Notify you as required by law.
Inform relevant regulatory authorities when necessary.
How long we keep your data
We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for:
Client records (identity, contact and transaction data): six years after the client relationship ends, for tax, regulatory and insurance purposes.
Identity verification records: for as long as we need them to meet our legal obligations and to prevent fraud, and at least as long as the law requires.
Your account: while your account is open. You can ask us to close your account and delete your data at any time. We then keep only the records this section says we must keep.

Support conversations, records of your consents and your data protection requests: while your account is open. After that, we keep them only as long as we need to show that we handled your requests correctly, or to deal with a legal claim.
Our own website analytics: detailed events for 90 days and visit records for 180 days. Summary records with no identifier in them are kept longer.
Fraud-prevention data: full IP addresses for 48 hours, or for 90 days where the visit led to a purchase.
We may anonymise data so that it can no longer be linked to you, and use it for statistics.

Section 21 of 25
Your Rights Over Your Personal Data
As we’ve said, you’re in charge—and under international data protection laws, you have the right to take specific actions regarding your personal data. You can:
Request access – Also known as a “data subject access request,” this allows you to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and confirm that we are processing it lawfully.
Request correction – If any of the personal data we hold about you is incomplete or inaccurate, you can request corrections.
Request erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”) – You can ask us to delete or remove your personal data if there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it. You can also request erasure if you have objected to processing (see below).
Object to processing – If we process your personal data based on legitimate interests (ours or a third party’s), and you believe that your specific situation gives you a reason to object, you have the right to do so.
Object to direct marketing – You can ask us to stop processing your personal data for marketing purposes at any time.
Request restriction of processing – You can request that we suspend processing your personal data in certain situations (e.g., if you need us to verify its accuracy or explain why we are processing it).
Request data portability – You can ask us to transfer your personal data to another party in a structured, commonly used format.
Withdraw consent – Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.
Automated decisions – You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you (see section 14).
Complain – You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to deal with your concerns first, so please contact us.
