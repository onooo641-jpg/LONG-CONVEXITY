At DayTraders.com, we value transparency and fairness in all transactions. As part of our commitment to maintaining a secure and professional trading environment, we strictly discourage chargebacks.
Payment Disputes and Chargebacks
Before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or card issuer, you agree to contact our support team and work with us in good faith to resolve the issue. Filing a dispute without contacting us first may be deemed account abuse and can result in immediate account suspension, investigation, or other enforcement actions under these Terms.
Consequences of a Chargeback
Initiating a chargeback may lead to account suspension and enforcement actions.
How to Resolve Payment Issues
If you have any questions or issues with your payments, please contact us directly through our Help Desk. Our team is dedicated to addressing and resolving concerns to ensure a smooth experience for all clients.
By working together, we can maintain a fair trading environment for everyone. Chargebacks can cause unnecessary delays and complications, so we strongly encourage you to reach out to us for assistance before taking any action with your financial institution.
