🔐 Ethics, Compliance & Whistleblower Policy
At Audacity Capital , we are fully committed to maintaining a culture of transparency, integrity, and accountability — both internally and externally. We have zero tolerance for fraud, corruption, or any unethical conduct, whether it’s carried out against us , on our behalf , or within our own operations .
Our standards are clear:
We follow a consistent, values-driven code of business conduct that guides every part of how we operate. This includes holding our team members and business partners to high ethical standards, with no exceptions.
To help uphold this culture, we have a dedicated Whistleblower and Anti-Fraud policy in place. This ensures that:
Any suspicious, dishonest, or unethical activity can be reported safely.
Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
The identity of anyone making a report will be protected.
If you believe you’ve witnessed any form of fraud, misconduct, or violation of our standards, please report it to us directly: info@audacitycapital.co.uk
We take every report seriously and handle all submissions with discretion and care.
