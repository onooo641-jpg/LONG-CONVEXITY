How payouts are processed
From request to your bank or wallet.

Table of contents
Request your payout from the dashboard once you qualify.
We review it. Payout requests are reviewed within 24–72 working hours (Monday–Friday). On average it takes about 24 hours to approve and 24 hours to process.
Approved payouts go to your Credits balance (Dashboard → Payouts).
Once approved, the payout is processed within 1–3 business days to Rise or crypto. Crypto payouts are limited to $500 per request; Rise has no limit. Or spend the credits on new challenges.
Rise
Create a free Rise account and complete a one-time KYC before your first payout.
Each cycle choose a local-currency bank transfer, stablecoins or crypto, or a split.
100+ crypto assets, 90+ currencies, 190+ countries.
Fees
Every payout has a 3% processing fee, whatever the route: Rise, crypto, or a refund to your original card or payment method. The fee is deducted from the amount sent.
Anti-money laundering
FuturesElite is based in Italy and follows Italian and EU anti-money-laundering law. Up to the total amount you have paid us, all or part of a payout may be returned to your original payment method as a refund; the rest is sent to Rise. The note on your payout shows the split, and the route cannot be chosen. Card refunds can take longer because of bank processing times.
Taxes
Traders are independent contractors and are responsible for their own taxes under their local laws.
