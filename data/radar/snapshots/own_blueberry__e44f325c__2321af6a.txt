How do I get paid as an affiliate?
Affiliate Payout
September 25, 2026
Payouts are processed every Friday via Riseworks . You can submit a withdrawal request from your affiliate Blueberry Fut ures dashboard  once your affiliate balance reaches the $200 minimum.
​
Affiliate payouts are processed every Friday via Riseworks . Once your affiliate balance reaches the $200 minimum , you can request a withdrawal from your affiliate Blueberry Futures dashboard. Here's the full process:
​
1. Build up your balance
Every eligible commission from your referrals is added to your affiliate balance. Only commissions from completed, retained sales count toward your withdrawable total. If a referred user cancels or is refunded, that commission is voided or deducted.
​
2. Reach the $200 minimum
You can submit a withdrawal request once your balance reaches $200 . If you're below the threshold, your balance simply carries over and keeps building until you get there; nothing is lost.
​
3. Submit your request in your dashboard
Go to the Affiliate Dashboard in your Blueberry Futures dashboard and submit a withdrawal request for your available balance.
​
4. Get paid every Friday via Riseworks
Payouts run on a weekly cycle every Friday through Riseworks , our payments partner. To receive funds, make sure your Riseworks account is set up, and your details are verified (a one-time step for first-time affiliates).
