PRIVACY POLICY – LUCID TRADING
Effective date: November 13, 2026
Lucid Trading Group LLC (“Lucid Trading”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) explains in this Privacy Policy what personal information we collect and how we use and share it. It applies to lucidtrading.com , our trader dashboard, our mobile application, our support and community channels, and the trading programs we offer (together, the “Platform”). This Privacy Policy is incorporated into our Terms of Use. By using the Platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Platform.
Information We Collect
We collect information in several ways:
Information you provide to us;
Information gathered automatically as you navigate through the Platform; or
Information third parties share with us, such as payment processors and their fraud and dispute networks, identity verification providers, trading platform and brokerage providers, advertising and analytics partners, and affiliate or referral partners.
Information we collect may include:
Identifiers and contact information, such as your name, contact details, and account credentials;
Identity verification information, which we collect before a payout or when you open a live trading account, such as government-issued identification, biometric data, proof of address, and tax information;
Device and usage information, such as IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address or device, device and network characteristics, login activity, and information collected through cookies and similar technologies in accordance with your cookie preferences;
Trading information, such as your activity, performance, account status, and payouts on your evaluation, funded, and live accounts;
Financial information, such as payment, billing, transaction, and payout account details;
Communications and content, such as messages you send us, support conversations, and content you post in our community channels; and
Information from third-party accounts you choose to link.
The information we collect automatically is statistical data and may include personal information, but we may maintain it or associate it with personal information we collect in other ways or receive from third parties. This process helps us to operate, secure, and improve our Platform and to deliver a better and more personalized service, including by enabling us to estimate our audience size and usage patterns; enhance our evaluation programs and funded trading offerings; store information about your preferences, allowing us to customize our Platform according to your individual interests; recognize you when you return to our Platform; and detect and prevent fraud, multiple-account abuse, and violations of our program rules.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
Provide, secure, and improve the Platform and our trading programs
Verify identity and prevent fraud and abuse, including through automated means
Communicate with you and market our services
Create aggregated or de-identified information
Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements and program rules
How We Share Your Information
We may share or disclose your information with third parties where necessary to provide services through the Platform, to comply with our legal obligations, or other purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, including:
Other users and the public, through leaderboards and community features
Service providers
Trading platform providers, brokerages, and futures commission merchants
Advertising and analytics partners
Affiliate and referral partners
Corporate affiliates and successors to our business
Government authorities and others, where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and prevent fraud
We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify any individual.
Your Privacy Choices
Requests. You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information through our request form at https://my.datasubject.com/56qIEhpsc9/75324 or by emailing [email protected] . We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by applicable law. We may decline or limit a request where the law permits, and deleting your personal information requires closing your account. If we decline a request, you may appeal by emailing [email protected] .
Marketing. You may opt out of marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or by contacting us. You will continue to receive account and transactional notices.
Cookies and advertising. You may manage cookies and opt out of targeted advertising through the “Cookie Preferences” and “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” links in the footer of our website.
Cookies and Tracking
We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to operate and secure the Platform, and others that are used for functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can review or change your preferences at any time through the “Cookie Preferences” link in the footer of our website or through your browser settings. We treat the Global Privacy Control browser signal as an opt-out request where applicable law requires. We do not otherwise respond to “Do Not Track” signals.
Data Security and Retention
We use reasonable measures designed to protect your personal information, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee its security.
We retain personal information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and as required by law.
Automated Decision-Making
We use automated systems, including services provided by third parties, to verify identity, prevent fraud, and enforce our program rules. These systems may restrict or delay registration, access, payouts, or account activity. If you believe an automated action was taken in error, you may request review by a person by contacting us through the support chat on our website.
International Transfers
We are based in the United States, and your information is processed in the United States and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. Those countries may not provide the same level of data protection as your home jurisdiction. Where required by law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.
Biometric Information
When you complete identity verification, our identity verification provider may collect and process biometric information. This information is used only to verify your identity and prevent fraud, and is not sold, leased, or traded. Biometric information is retained only for as long as necessary to verify your identity and prevent fraud, and no longer than permitted by applicable law.
Your U.S. State Privacy Rights
Categories collected. In the preceding 12 months we may have collected the following categories of personal information, from the sources and for the purposes described above: identifiers; customer records; commercial information; internet activity; geolocation data; inferences; and sensitive personal information.
Sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. We share identifiers, commercial information, and internet activity with advertising, analytics, and referral partners, which some state laws may consider a “sale” or “sharing.” You may opt out through the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link in the footer of our website or by enabling the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser. We do not sell or share sensitive personal information, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; to opt out of targeted advertising, sale or sharing, and certain profiling; and to appeal our decision on a request. You may exercise these rights, directly or through an authorized agent, as described under “Your Privacy Choices.” We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
California residents may also request information about our disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes by emailing [email protected] , and may contact the Complaint Assistance Unit of the Division of Consumer Services of the California Department of Consumer Affairs in writing at 1625 North Market Blvd., Suite N 112, Sacramento, California 95834, or by telephone at +1 800.952.5210.
Rights of Individuals in the EEA and UK
Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information; to receive it in a portable format; to withdraw consent; to object to direct marketing; not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects; and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact [email protected] .
We process personal information to perform our contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, for our legitimate interests (including securing the Platform, preventing fraud, enforcing our rules, and promoting our services), and, where required, with your consent.
Children’s Privacy
The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have done so, we will delete that information. If you believe we may have information from or about a person under 18, please contact [email protected] .
Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date, and we will provide additional notice where required by law. Your continued use of the Platform after changes are posted is deemed to be acceptance of those changes.
