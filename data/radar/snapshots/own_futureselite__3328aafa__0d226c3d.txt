Table of contents
You can buy unlimited challenges. The limits apply only to funded accounts you hold at the same time.
10 active funded accounts in total.
Up to 5 of them from Elite, Instant and Nitro combined.
Nitro: at most 3 funded accounts, inside those 5.
Prime: up to 10 funded accounts.
Multiple accounts, one person
Accounts can't be merged, but you can trade several at once with our in-house copy trader.
Copying trades between your own accounts is allowed.
Create one registration only. Matching strategies, IPs or wallets across separate registrations can lead to suspension.
