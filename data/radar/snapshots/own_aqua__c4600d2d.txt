What is a Forex Funded Account?
A forex funded account is a fully operational account that has been allocated a certain amount of capital that you can use to trade on the forex market. This type of account is provided by a forex prop trading firm like AquaFunded, which allows you to trade forex using our firm’s capital instead of your own money.

How Forex Funded Accounts Work:
The Complete Process
Forex funded accounts work in a very similar way to regular forex trading accounts, with one key difference - you will be using our firm’s capital rather than your own. This allows you to leverage capital that you don’t have and make trades that would otherwise not be possible.
In return, the profit you generate from forex trading is split 90/10 (90% for you and 10% for us). You can also keep 100% of the profit you generate using our capital - just make sure to check the “100% Profit Split” add-on during checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions about Forex Funded Accounts
What is a forex funded account and how do I qualify?
A forex funded account is a trading account provided by a proprietary (prop) firm that allows you to trade forex using the firm’s capital instead of your own money. The qualification requirements for a forex funded account vary across the different prop firms.To qualify for an AquaFunded forex funded account, you either need to complete the steps while staying within the trading parameters of our evaluation challenge or skip this entire process and get immediate access to a funded account. To do that, simply choose the “Instant Funding” entry model.
How much does it cost to get a forex funded account with AquaFunded?
The cost of a forex funded account with AquaFunded varies depending on a number of factors, including the account size you select, the entry model (one-step, two-step, three-step, or instant funding), the model type, and if you want any optional add-ons such as 100% profit split.To give you an idea, you can get an instant funded forex account from just $60, which gives you a trading capital of $2,500. To see the cost of all available account sizes and funding models, head further up the page to the “Get Funded Today” section.
