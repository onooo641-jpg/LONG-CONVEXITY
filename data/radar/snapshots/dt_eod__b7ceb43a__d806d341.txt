All Collections General DT Wallet FAQ DT Wallet FAQ
The DT Wallet allows you to receive an approved simulated payout as DayTraders account credit instead of sending the funds to your bank, if you are eligible.
Updated over a week ago
Copy for LLM
Wallet funds are available immediately after payout approval and can be used toward eligible purchases on DayTraders.
What is the DT Wallet?
The DT Wallet is a DayTraders account credit balance.
If you are eligible, you can choose to have an approved simulated payout credited to your DT Wallet instead of receiving it as a bank payout.
Wallet funds can be used to purchase eligible accounts on DayTraders.
How does the DT Wallet work?
When requesting a payout, you may be able to choose where the payout should go:
Bank
DT Wallet
Bank is selected by default.
If you select DT Wallet and your payout is approved, the funds are credited to your wallet immediately.
What is the benefit of using the DT Wallet?
The DT Wallet allows you to use approved payout funds faster.
Benefits include:
Instant wallet credit after payout approval
No bank transfer waiting period
No transfer fee
Ability to purchase a new eligible account right away
The trade-off is that wallet funds remain as DayTraders credit and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
Can I withdraw DT Wallet funds as cash?
No.
DT Wallet funds cannot be withdrawn as cash.
Wallet funds can only be used for eligible purchases on DayTraders.
Is the DT Wallet available for all payouts?
No.
The DT Wallet is only available for simulated account payouts if you are eligible.
Live account payouts cannot be sent to the DT Wallet and must be sent to the bank.
What is the minimum payout amount for the DT Wallet?
The minimum amount that can be sent to the DT Wallet is:
$500
If the payout amount is under $500, the wallet option will not be available.
Why do I not see the DT Wallet option on my payout request?
The DT Wallet option may not appear if:
The payout is under $500
The payout is from a live account
The payout is not eligible for wallet credit
If the wallet option is not shown, the payout will need to be requested through the available payout method.
When are DT Wallet funds available?
DT Wallet funds are available immediately after the payout is approved.
Once approved:
The payout leaves your trading account
Your wallet balance is credited
The funds can be used for eligible purchases
A confirmation email is sent with the credited amount and updated wallet balance
Can I change my payout from Wallet back to Bank?
Once the payout has been approved, the wallet selection cannot be reversed.
Wallet funds cannot be converted back into a bank payout.
Please make sure you want the payout credited to your DT Wallet before confirming your request.
Where can I view my DT Wallet balance?
You can view your wallet balance from the Wallet section in your DayTraders member area.
The Wallet page may show:
Current wallet balance
Total amount added
Total amount spent
Wallet activity history
Running balance after each wallet movement
Related account or purchase information
How can I use my DT Wallet funds?
DT Wallet funds can be used at checkout for eligible DayTraders purchases. Specifically, the DT Wallet can be used for one-time purchases of evaluations and S2L accounts, including bulk orders.
If you have an available wallet balance, the wallet payment option may appear alongside other payment options at checkout.
Can I pay part with Wallet and part with card?
No, split payments are not supported.
The DT Wallet must cover the full order amount. If your wallet balance does not cover the full order, you will need to use another payment method.
What happens if my wallet balance is not enough for the full order?
If your wallet balance does not cover the full order, the wallet option will not be selectable at checkout.
The checkout page may show:
Your available wallet balance
The remaining amount needed
A note that the wallet must cover the full order
Example:
“$500.00 available, $90.00 short. Wallet must cover the full order.”
Can I use DT Wallet funds on monthly plans, add-ons or Pro-Accounts?
No, the DT Wallet can only be used for eligible one-time purchases (Evaluations and S2L).
Wallet funds cannot be used on monthly plans.
Are wallet purchases refundable?
No, purchases made with DT Wallet funds are final and non-refundable.
Can I buy multiple accounts with my DT Wallet?
Yes, you can use your DT Wallet for eligible bulk purchases as long as your wallet balance covers the full order amount.
If the wallet balance does not cover the full order, the wallet cannot be used.
What if I use my wallet and the account does not appear?
If a purchase does not complete properly after wallet funds are used, the system may return the funds automatically and provide a reference number.
If you do not see the account or the returned wallet funds, contact support with:
Your account email
The purchase details
A screenshot of the issue
Any reference number shown
Important DT Wallet Rules
Please keep the following in mind:
Minimum wallet transfer: $500
Available for simulated payouts only, if you are eligible
Live payouts are not eligible
Cash withdrawals are not available
Split payments are not supported
Wallet must cover the full order (Eligible for one-time purchases of Evaluations and S2L. Including bulk orders)
Monthly plans are not eligible
Wallet purchases are final and non-refundable
Approved wallet payouts cannot be reversed back to bank payout
