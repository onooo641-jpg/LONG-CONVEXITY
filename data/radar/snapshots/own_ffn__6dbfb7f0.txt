How many accounts can I copy trade?
You can copy trade a maximum of 5 accounts. You cannot have more than 5 accounts trading at one time on the same product simultaneously. If that happens, none of the accounts that took that trade will be eligible for funding.

What is considered a T1 News Event?
T1 News Events cannot be traded. Tier 1 News includes:
For All Traders in Exhibition and Funded Accounts: FOMC Meetings (Wednesdays 2pm ET), FOMC Minutes, Employment Report (NFP), CPI
For Energy Traders: EIA
For Agricultural Traders: Agricultural Reports
You must be flat , meaning no positions or orders (including limit orders) can be open 1 minute before and 1 minute after the release. Having a resting order on your chart is not considered flat. All positions must be flattened at least one minute before the scheduled T1 News event, you must remain flat until 1 minute after the event's start time.
For example, if news is at 8:30, positions must be closed by 8:29:00 or earlier, and may only be reopened after 8:31:00.
Although news Trading Protocols are in place, you are permitted to trade around other news releases if you so choose. However, it's important to recognize that broker data issues or disruptions can happen during these events. Therefore, it is highly recommended to avoid trading during news releases to minimize potential risks. FFN does not assume responsibility for any problems that may occur if you decide to trade during these times.

How do Payouts work?
Be aware of the restricted times you are not allowed to trade during news events. Please refer to the rules page "trading the news" text and "Allowed Hours" video on our website.
Sim Funded Pro Account: Payouts can be requested the same day you're eligible, provided you have met your account minimum threshold and are within the consistency rule (40% for OG and Standard MAX funded accounts, 25% for Express MAX funded accounts). This account has an 80/20 profit split . When a withdrawal is initiated & processed, FFN will take 20% and distribute the remaining 80% to the trader. Maximum withdrawal of $10,000 per user per payout . This amount is cumulative across all of your accounts and you are not permitted to request several withdrawals across several accounts if they are copy traded.
FFN Institution (Live) Account: No consistency rule, and same-business-day payouts. Traders promoted to the FFN Institution start with $3,000 of real FFN capital , which can never be withdrawn. Institution accounts have a 90/10 profit split : you keep 90% of eligible live profit, and FFN's 10% generally stays in your account as additional FFN capital. Payouts must leave your Permanent Risk Buffer in place to keep the account open (see Article 4.3).
Consistency Reset: When you make a withdrawal, your consistency rule resets on that account. The consistency reset is per account after each payout. For example, if you copy trade 5 accounts and withdraw from 2 of them, only those 2 accounts have their consistency reset: the other 3 are unaffected. Note: your best day P&L never resets.

Can I Hedge?
No. When you are in the Evaluation, Exhibition and Funded stage, hedging is considered a broken rule making you no longer eligible for funding. Hedging means holding a long and a short position at the same time in the same product, or across the ES and NQ index futures.
The same product includes its mini and micro contracts. For example, you cannot go long on NQ and short on MNQ, or long on ES and short on MES.
ES, MES, NQ and MNQ count as one family in any size. A long in one and a short in another is hedging in any combination, for example ES against NQ, MES against MNQ, ES against MNQ, or MES against NQ. No other pair of different products is covered by the hedging rule.
Can I keep all the funds I make if I decide to close out all of my accounts?
If you choose to close your accounts, you will forfeit your profits on any accounts closed. This includes funded accounts.
