Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) is allowed on every plan: evaluations, Pro Accounts, S2F, S2L and live accounts.
What DCA is
DCA means building a position in parts at different prices instead of entering all at once, so your average entry price reflects each fill. It's allowed as long as your total open position stays within your max contracts.
Example (10-contract account): you buy 2 ES, then add 2 more at a lower price. Your 4 contracts have one average entry price, and you're still within your 10-contract limit.
Your drawdown level and any Daily Loss Limit still apply to the whole position, including open losses.
DCA vs Martingale
DCA (allowed)
Martingale (prohibited)
What you do
Add to an open position
Double the size of new trades after closed losses
Size limit
Within your max contracts
Keeps growing until a win or the account fails
Repeatedly doubling size on new trades after losses is Martingale, even if each trade stays within your max contracts.
