How many accounts can I have?
How many evaluations and funded accounts you can hold on CFD and Futures, what happens over the limit, and the CFD scaling plan.
Applies to: CFD and Futures Updated 15 November 2026
Short answer
You can buy as many evaluations as you like. The limit is on funded accounts: CFD caps your total active funded balance at $400,000, and Futures allows up to 5 funded accounts at once, across all Futures plans including Zero. Extra funded accounts wait in a queue until there's room.

Funded accounts

What happens if I go over the limit?
If passing an evaluation or buying an instant account (CFD Instant or Futures Direct) would take you over your limit, the new account stays inactive until there's room. That happens when one of your active funded accounts is closed, breached or otherwise stops counting towards your limit. The next eligible account is then activated.
CFD example: you already have a $200,000 Instant account. You can add up to $200,000 more in funded or instant accounts across any plan. Anything beyond that waits in the queue.
Futures example: you have 3 funded Zero accounts and 2 funded Reserve accounts. That's 5 funded Futures accounts, so any new funded account waits in the queue until one of them closes.
Futures: Zero accounts
Zero accounts count towards your 5 funded Futures accounts. If a funded Zero account makes $8,000 profit in one day, all your funded Zero accounts close or move to Live, depending on the option you choose. See Zero account rules .
CFD: queued accounts and inactivity
The normal 30-day inactivity rule still applies to CFD accounts. However, if an account breaches only because it was inactive while waiting in the allocation queue, our team will reactivate it.
To avoid this, place a minimum trade (0.01 lots) on each queued account at least once every 30 days.
CFD: scaling plan
You can grow your CFD allocation up to $4,000,000 over time. To qualify, make 12% profit within 3 months while following all the rules. Each time you qualify, your account size increases by 30% of your initial balance .
