Where does BrightFunded's Data Feed come from?
September 25, 2025
BrightFunded operates independently we are not a broker, nor are we connected to one . Instead, we source our data feed from top-tier market data providers that serve institutional-grade clients and ensure a robust, stable, and competitive trading environment . Our data feed is designed to reflect real market conditions while providing reliable pricing for traders.
It’s important to note that BrightFunded does not deal directly with a liquidity provider, nor are we directly connected to one. Instead, we are integrated with leading data feed providers , who aggregate market data from multiple top-tier data sources , ensuring consistency and depth in the price feed we offer.
