What is Blackarrow?
Blackarrow is the trading platform you will use for your Blueberry Futures evaluation and funded accounts.
September 22, 2026
BlackArrow is a professional multi-platform trading terminal developed by Nelogica and used by Blueberry Futures to provide access to the futures markets through:
Desktop App Download
Web Terminal (browser-based)
Mobile app ( iOS / Android )
Scan to download
Instead of trading directly from an exchange website or a basic web chart, BlackArrow connects your evaluation or funded account to the futures markets with fast execution, depth of market, and advanced risk-management tools.
​
Black Arrow is the only platform available at Blueberry Futures; we do not offer Tradovate, Rithmic, or NinjaTrader.
