What Is CFD Intraday?
CFD Intraday is our CFD Challenge for traders who open and close their positions within the trading day. It's available as a 1-Phase or 2-Phase Challenge.
You keep the same Profit Targets, 80% profit share and 14-day payout cycle as our Swing account. Because you close your positions every day, you get a lower Challenge price and leverage of up to 1:100.
All trading takes place on demo accounts using simulated funds.

How Do I Choose Between Intraday And Swing?
Intraday is selected by default when you buy a CFD Challenge. If you select overnight and weekend holding at checkout, your account switches to Swing and the price changes to Swing pricing.
Once you've purchased your Challenge, you can't switch between Intraday and Swing, so make sure you choose the right option before you complete checkout.
Which Account Sizes Are Available?
• 1-Phase: $2,500, $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 and $200,000.
• 2-Phase: $2,500, $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000 and $100,000.
Prices for each size are shown on the CFD product page and at checkout.
What's The Difference Between The Challenge And The Earning Phase?
Both phases take place on a demo account under real market conditions. In the Challenge, your profit is not withdrawable — it only proves your trading ability. Once you pass, you receive an Earning Account, where you can request rewards based on your trading results and scale up over time.
