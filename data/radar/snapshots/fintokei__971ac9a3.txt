How and how often can I withdraw my performance rewards, and what is the minimum amount?
Learn how to request payouts in MyFintokei. Enjoy bi-weekly payouts, instant Walletory transfers, crypto or wire options, and minimum withdrawals from €100.
Updated over 3 weeks ago
You can request your withdrawal inside the MyFintokei dashboard. Funds are sent as a wire transfer to any bank account, as a wallet-to-wallet transfer or crypto wallet you own. You will provide the payment details in the dashboard. You need to successfully pass the KYC Process before you can proceed with the payout request.
💡 New: Instant Payouts now available!
A new payout option designed to make the process faster and more flexible is introduced. Walletory method enables wallet-to-wallet payouts processed immediately. That means you can have your funds available in your wallet within just a few seconds!
To learn more about when and how you will receive your payout, check out this article.
👉 When will I receive my payout?
You can withdraw performance rewards you make on the virtually funded ProTrader, ProTrader Swing or StartTrader account as long as at least 14 days have passed from your last successfully processed payout or since the first trade placed. Before requesting a performance reward withdrawal, make sure you close all your open trades.
You can withdraw performance rewards you make on a virtually funded SwiftTrader account if you achieved Minimum profit for payout calculated as 3% or more from your initial balance and at the same time at least 14 days have passed from your last withdrawal or since the first trade placed. Before requesting a withdrawal, make sure you close all your open trades.
You can withdraw any amount with a minimum of 100 EUR, 100 USD, 2 000 CZK, 20 000 JPY . That’s just to make sure it’s economical to send the money. Fintokei will cover the withdrawal fees required to send you the funds. The only exceptions would be for 400 000 EUR ProTrader plans where the minimum value for EUR and USD is changed to 120 EUR/USD and SwiftTrader plans with its rule of Minimum profit per Payout .
You could also withdraw the performance reward in cryptocurrencies.
For the ETH and USDT is fees:
2.5% of the amount withdrawn + 10 EUR will be deducted as a commission.
For the BTC and USDC is fees:
2.5% of the amount withdrawn + 20 EUR will be deducted as a commission.
​
For the other cryptocurrencies (LTC, etc.)
2.5% of the amount withdrawn will be deducted as a commission.
⚠️ Due to technical limitations, we are unable to offer cryptocurrency payout options in a number of countries worldwide. For this reason, performance rewards can be requested via the crypto payout method only in the following countries:
Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Gibraltar, Greece, Hong Kong, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino, Singapore, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan and the United Kingdom.
If your country of residence is not listed, cryptocurrency payouts are currently unavailable and you can request your performance reward via other available methods instead.
