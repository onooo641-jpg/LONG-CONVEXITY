What happens if I blow my funded account? If your funded account is closed, you don’t have to start over. You can restore it with a Funded Reset, available up to three times per account.
Can I move to live market execution? Advancement is reviewed individually, case by case, and is never guaranteed. Traders who meet certain criteria on their simulated funded accounts may be moved to a Professional Stage (Pro) account. From a Pro account, you may become eligible for a live market execution environment after meeting either of these milestones: submitting a payout request under the Professional Stage payout rules, or reaching $5,000 in recognized profit in your Pro account. If you qualify, our live team will contact you about next steps. Meeting these milestones does not mean the move happens right away.
What is a simulated funded account? A simulated funded account is a sim account: your orders never reach a live market. Trade within the plan rules and you can request payouts, paid by Funded Family One.

Who is behind Funded Family One? Funded Family One is led by CEO Manuel Meraz and backed by a global team of 50+ professionals. Our 24/7 human support team is here to help traders whenever they need assistance.

What can I trade? You can trade a wide range of CME-listed futures, including the S&P 500, Nasdaq, crude oil, natural gas, gold, and silver. We also support many commodity futures that most prop firms don’t offer. You can find the full list of supported contracts in our Help Center.
