Ascent Account Specifications
The Ascent is our premium evaluation, designed to give you maximum breathing room with an End-of-Day drawdown.
​
The Rules:
Drawdown Type: End of Day (EOD)
Time to Complete: 30 Days
Minimum Trading Days: 2 Day (Challenge), 5 Days (Funded) (To count as a valid minimum trading day, you must generate a net profit of at least $200 on that day).
Daily Pause: None, unless you want it and add it yourself.
Ascent Challenge (EOD Drawdown)
Best for swing traders and those wanting end-of-day drawdown protection.
