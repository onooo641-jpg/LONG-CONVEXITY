How Much of My Trading Profits Do I Keep?
Blueberry Futures offers a 90% profit split on both Ascent and Accelerated Funded Accounts.
​
This means you receive 90% of every approved payout , while Blueberry Futures retains the remaining 10%.
​
Example
If your approved payout is $1,000 :
You receive: $900
Blueberry Futures retains: $100
The profit split applies to approved payouts and remains subject to the applicable payout caps, required buffers, profitable-day requirements, consistency rules, and Maximum Drawdown threshold.
