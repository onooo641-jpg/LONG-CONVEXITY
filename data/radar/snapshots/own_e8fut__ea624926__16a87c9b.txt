The SimFi ™ Performance account is a simulated trading account that allows you to qualify for payouts of up to 100% based on your performance. Think of it as a professional flight simulator: we provide the simulated capital and real-market conditions so you can master your strategy without the risks of losing personal savings with brokers, and qualify for payouts in the process.
​
To reach this stage, you first complete the SimFi ™ Challenge , where you demonstrate your skills by completing profit targets and following our safety guardrails. We aren't here to test you - we're here to train you. Once you successfully navigate through the Challenge account, you graduate to the SimFi ™ Performance stage , where your discipline in a simulated environment leads to payouts.
​
The process
First, you pay a fee for one of our available products . You receive access to the SimFi™ Challenge account. Once you complete the profit target, while following all guardrails (Rules), you will get access to SimFi™ Performance account. This is the stage where you can obtain payouts based on your trading performance.
