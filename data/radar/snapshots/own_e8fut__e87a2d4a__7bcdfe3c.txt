How to purchase an account
September 15, 2026
Before you can qualify for payouts as a SimFi Performance Account holder, you need to pass the SimFi ™ Challenge - and for that, you first need to select and purchase it.
​
Simply create your account, choose the product you would like to purchase, and follow the steps to complete your order. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and cryptocurrencies.
​
Currently, customers can pay using Apple Pay, Google Pay, cryptocurrency through our partner Confirmo, or by card through Nuvei.
Log in/ or register to E8X .
Click Start Trading to begin.
Select your preferred market, account type, platform, account size, and any add-ons. (This article might help you with selection)
Confirm that your billing information is correct.
Choose a payment method - from credit/debit card to cryptocurrency (via any wallet or exchange).
Order details can be found on this page , and we will also send you an invoice to your email (credentials can be found in your dashboard after you pay).
Processing times may vary depending on the chosen payment method. Cards are instant, but if paying via cryptocurrency, it takes time before it is confirmed on the blockchain. Also, ensure you account for network transaction fees.
​
If you did not receive your account within 30 minutes after the payment was successfully processed, please contact our support at support@E8markets.com .
Once the payment is confirmed and the order is paid, your login credentials will immediately appear under the Accounts Overview tab in your dashboard or directly on the page of the paid order. You are ready to start trading!
