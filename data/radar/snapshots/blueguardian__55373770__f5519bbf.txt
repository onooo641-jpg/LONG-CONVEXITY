1 Step Standard rules
CFD 1 Step Standard: 9% target, 4% daily loss, 6% trailing drawdown, 3 trading days, Guardian Shield, payouts every 14 days.
Applies to: CFD Updated 23 November 2026
Short answer
1 Step Standard is a one-phase CFD evaluation. Reach 9% profit over at least 3 trading days, staying within a 4% daily loss and a 6% trailing drawdown. Once funded, you need 3 profitable trading days in every payout cycle, and you can request a payout every 14 days (or every 7 days with the add-on), with an 85% profit split (90% with the add-on).

Closes all trades at 2% floating loss

Leverage (evaluation)
FX 1:50, indices and commodities 1:20, crypto 1:2
Leverage (funded)
FX 1:50, indices and commodities 1:10, crypto 1:2
The evaluation
Profit target and trading days
Reach 9% profit while following all the rules, with at least 3 trading days where you make 0.5% profit or more. The days don't need to be consecutive. Your account then has a final review, and once approved you become a funded trader.
Maximum daily loss: 4%
Your daily loss limit is 4% of your starting balance , measured from whichever is higher at the 5:00 PM New York reset: your balance or your equity.
Floating profit at reset: on $100K with $2,000 floating profit, equity is $102,000, so it can't drop below $98,000 the next day.
Floating loss at reset: with $2,000 floating loss, your $100,000 balance is higher, so equity can't drop below $96,000.
No open trades: the limit is your closed balance minus 4% of your starting balance.
Maximum drawdown: 6% trailing
Your maximum drawdown trails 6% below your highest closed balance . Once you reach +6% , it locks at your starting balance and stops trailing. Drawdown includes closed profits and losses, swaps and commissions.
Example on $100K: you start with a daily limit of $96,000 and a max drawdown of $94,000. After making $5,000 (balance $105,000), the next day's daily limit is $101,000 and your max drawdown trails up to $99,000. Once you've made more than $6,000, it locks at $100,000.
Funded account
Guardian Shield
If your open trades reach a combined 2% floating loss , Guardian Shield will usually close all open trades. You can keep trading straight away, but there are consequences:
1st time: your profit split drops to 50% permanently.
2nd time: the account is permanently breached.
Payouts
Minimum trading days: you need 3 trading days with 0.5% profit or more in every payout cycle before you can request a payout. The days don't need to be consecutive.
When: your first payout is available once 14 days have passed since your first trade, then every 14 days after your previous request. With the 7-day payout add-on , it's every 7 days.
Conditions: account above starting balance, minimum trading days met, no rule violations and all positions closed.
1% withdrawal buffer: once you reach +6% and your drawdown locks, you must leave 1% of your starting balance in profit. Example: with exactly $6,000 profit on $100K, you can withdraw up to $5,000.
Profit split: 85%, or 90% with the add-on.
Trading rules
News: allowed during the evaluation. On funded accounts, don't open or close trades from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after high-impact news on affected instruments. See CFD news trading rules .
Tick scalping: on funded accounts, hold each trade for at least 2 minutes . This doesn't apply during the evaluation.
Overnight and weekends: allowed.
Expert Advisors: allowed, if they're your own setup. VPNs and VPS are allowed, but not to get around copy trading, group trading or signal rules.
Copy trading: only between accounts you legally own, including between your own evaluation and funded accounts.
Stop losses: not mandatory, but you're responsible for staying within the rules.
