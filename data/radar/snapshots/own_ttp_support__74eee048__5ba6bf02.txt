Why Do I Have To Pay?
It depends on which route you choose.
With our evaluation Challenges — although they are referred to as “Demo Challenges”, they are evaluation “tests”.
If you pass, you become a Trading Pit Partner and move on to the Earning Phase, where you trade a funded demo account under real market conditions and can request payouts on the profits you generate. Depending on the product, your evaluation fee may be refunded and your account scaled up over time (see the CFDs and Futures pages for the exact terms).
With CFD Instant, you skip the Challenge phase and go straight to the Earning Phase — there is no evaluation to pass. Please note that the CFD Instant fee is not refundable.
Do I Have To Use My Own Funds To Trade With The Trading Pit?
At no point during your trading journey with us will you be required to invest your own funds in order to trade. The only expense on your part is the one-time initial purchase of one of our Challenges.
For Futures Prime, new accounts from 26/03/2026 do not have an activation fee. Existing accounts and reset breached accounts are subject to an activation fee before progressing to the Earning Phase.
Do You Have Any Vouchers Or Promotions Available?
We occasionally run special promotions and exclusive vouchers. These offers are announced either on our website or through our social media channels. To make sure you never miss out, we recommend following us on social media for the latest updates and announcements.
Follow us on social media: Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Discord and Telegram.
Do You Offer Trading Advice?
No — we don't provide trading advice or tell you what or when to trade. What we do offer is educational content: videos, webinars and blogs on our website to help you develop your own trading.
