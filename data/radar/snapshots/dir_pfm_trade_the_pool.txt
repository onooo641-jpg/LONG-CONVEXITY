Consistency Rules
FLEX Day & FLEX Swing:
A 50% best-position consistency rule applies during Evaluation and the Funded stage.
Edge:
A 40% consistency rule applies during the Funded stage.
Firm Rules
Prohibited Practices
Trade Duration:
FLEX: A minimum 30-second duration is required for a position's profit to count. The system uses precise timestamps to determine eligibility.
Edge: Every trade must remain open for at least 30 seconds.
Trade Range:
FLEX: Profitable positions must have at least a $0.10 difference between their weighted average entry and exit prices for the profit to count.
Edge: There must be at least a $0.10 difference between the entry and exit prices for the trade to qualify.
Trading Halts:
Active trading is not permitted during an applicable trading halt, including symbol-specific trading pauses, market-wide circuit breakers, trading outages or when an instrument is marked untradeable.
Executing orders on halted symbols or placing orders to open or add to halted positions is prohibited. Trades executed during a halt may be reversed or deleted.
Risk Limit
Minimum Volume Rule (The 5% Rule) — FLEX & Edge:
The volume of any opening trades (opening or adding to a position) must not exceed 5% of the trading volume in the previous one-minute candle for that instrument.
Multiple entries and orders on the same instrument within the same minute are aggregated for enforcement.
Violating trades may be invalidated and could result in additional restrictions or account review.
FLEX: If the previous minute has no trading volume, the most recent one-minute candle with volume is used.
Trading Conditions
Copy Trading:
FLEX:
You may copy trades between a maximum of two accounts. All trades must be executed directly by the trader. The use of external or automated third-party copying tools/software is strictly prohibited.
FLEX Day: Accounts must be paired according to the following size requirements: $5K, $25K and $50K. A $50K account can be paired only with one $25K or one $5K account.
FLEX Swing: Eligible account sizes are $2K and $10K.
Manual copy trading is allowed for Evaluation and Funded accounts.
TraderEvolution's copy trading feature follows the same guidelines. However, this platform feature is permitted only on Evaluation accounts and is not allowed on Funded accounts.
Take Profit or Stop Loss orders will not be automatically created for the follower accounts.
Edge:
You may copy trades between a maximum of three Edge accounts, including between $50K and $100K accounts. Copying is allowed between Edge Evaluation and Edge Funded accounts.
Copying between Edge accounts and any other account type, including FLEX Day and FLEX Swing, is not allowed.
Automated Trading (FLEX):
Support for automated trading, including SignalStack integration, is currently in beta. Availability is not guaranteed, and additional conditions may apply to particular accounts, instruments or strategies.
Traders are responsible for all trades conducted on their accounts, whether manually or automatically created. A rate of no more than 2 requests per minute should be targeted. Authorization may be revoked.
News Trading (FLEX):
News trading is permitted, subject to restrictions on holding positions during scheduled earnings reports.
Inactivity Rules (FLEX):
A 14-day inactivity rule applies during Evaluation and the Funded stage. Traders expecting to exceed the inactivity period must notify customer support in advance.
Overnight and Weekend Holding:
FLEX Day: 10 minutes before the end of the trading day (normally 15:50 ET), all open positions and pending orders will be liquidated. New positions may be opened after market close using reduced overnight buying power.
FLEX Swing: Overnight and weekend holding is permitted. Buying power remains the same during regular and extended trading hours.
Permitted Order Types During Extended Hours (FLEX):
Overnight Execution Off (Nasdaq): During standard pre-market and post-market hours, only limit type orders can be executed. Buy Limit, Sell Limit, Stop Limit and Take-Profit (TP) orders are permitted. Market, Stop and Stop-Loss (SL) orders are not permitted.
Overnight Execution On (Blue Ocean): Trade The Pool offers extended 24/5 trading via Blue Ocean on supported instruments only. Market, Stop, Stop-Loss (SL), Buy Limit, Sell Limit, Stop Limit and Take-Profit (TP) orders are permitted.
Minimum Trading Volume (FLEX Swing):
To open a position intended to be held overnight, the stock must have an average daily trading volume of at least 500,000 shares over the previous 14 calendar days.
Earnings, Stock Splits & Dividends (FLEX):
During earnings season, overnight positions cannot be held in a reporting company or related single-stock leveraged or inverse ETFs tracking that company.
Positions will be automatically liquidated at 3:50 PM ET when a scheduled earnings report is due that night.
All positions must be closed before stock splits, provided the split was announced at least one day in advance.
All positions must be closed before the stock trades ex-dividend and may be reopened once it begins trading ex-dividend.

Payout Policy
Shared across all programs
Payout Frequency: Every 14 calendar days, subject to payout eligibility.
FLEX:
Profit Split: 70%.
Minimum Withdrawal: $300, except for $5K accounts, where the minimum is $150.
Minimum Profitable Days: 3 profitable trading days per payout cycle, each generating at least 0.5% of buying power in profit. The days do not need to be consecutive.
Minimum Trades: 10 trades for FLEX Day and 5 trades for FLEX Swing are required to qualify for scaling or payout requests.
Consistency Rule: The best position cannot account for more than 50% of total valid profit.
Payout Waiting Period: The 14-day waiting period restarts when a new scaled Funded account is created.
Edge:
Profit Split: 80%.
Minimum Withdrawal: $500.
Maximum Withdrawal: $1,500 per payout for $50K accounts and $2,000 for $100K accounts.
First Withdrawal: Requires reaching 4% profit on the Funded account.
Subsequent Withdrawals: Require reaching 1% profit for $50K accounts and 1.5% for $100K accounts.
Consistency Rule: A 40% consistency rule applies during the Funded stage.
Drawdown and Payout Buffer — FLEX:
Once account equity reaches three times the Daily Loss Limit in profit, the maximum drawdown level moves to the initial account balance.
This adjustment includes unrealized profits and occurs immediately when the threshold is reached.
Withdrawals must leave sufficient equity above the locked drawdown level to avoid account termination.
