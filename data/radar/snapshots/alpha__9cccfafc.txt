Payout Policy
How to get paid out and profit split
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
July 27, 2026
Alpha Futures payout policies are extremely straight forward; follow the rules and you will be paid out. No additional hoops to jump through or hidden denials. Make sure to review our News Trading Policy and Prohibited Practices .
​
There are core similarities in the payout policy across both Evaluation to Qualified account types (Standard, Advanced, and Zero) at Alpha Futures:
Traders have the ability to pay themselves up to 4 times a month. Request every 5 winning trading days of $200 profit, or more
The winning trading days do not need to be consecutive, but 5 need to be accumulated between each withdrawal request.
You may request up to 50% of the profit in your account each withdrawal request (up to withdrawal limit), the rest of the balance stays on the account for drawdown or future withdrawals.
All withdrawal requests are the amount that will be removed from your trading account, trader received 90% of this request.
Withdrawal minimum, maximum, and consistency rule will depend on the account type chosen (outlined below).
Direct Accounts have the same profit split, but no winning day requirement, like our Evaluation to Qualified Accounts. Full details on each:
​
​ Advanced Qualified Accounts
The minimum withdrawal request on Advanced Qualified Accounts is $1,000, maximum is industry leading $15,000 on all Advanced Account sizes.
There is no Consistency Rule on Advanced Qualified Accounts.
​
​ Zero Qualified Accounts
Traders that choose the Zero Account must also adhere to a 40% Consistency Rule to be eligible for a withdrawal request.
​
The minimum withdrawal request on Zero Accounts is $200:
25k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $1,000
50k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $1,500
100k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $2,500
​
​ Standard Qualified Accounts
Traders that choose the Standard Account also adhere to a 40% Consistency Rule to be eligible for a withdrawal request, but with larger caps than the Zero Account.
​
The minimum withdrawal request on Standard Accounts is $500:
50k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $3,000
100k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $4,000
150k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $5,000
​
​ Direct Qualified Accounts
Direct Account users can enjoy skipping the Evaluation step, but they do need to reach their Withdrawal Profit Target as well as a 20% Consistency Rule in order to make a withdrawal request.
​
Withdrawal Profit Targets are the profit needed to earn in between withdrawal requests. After each withdrawal, your profit target resets and you must earn fresh profit from new trades to reach the next target. Leftover profit from the previous cycle does not carry over.
​
The minimum withdrawal request on Direct Qualified Accounts is $500. Details on each account size Maximum Withdrawal and Withdrawal Profit Target on Direct Qualified Accounts below:
​

​

​
