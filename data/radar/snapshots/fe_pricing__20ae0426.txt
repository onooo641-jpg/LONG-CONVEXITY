1. Personal Identification Information
Full name

Phone number
Date of birth
Address
Proof of identity (e.g., government-issued ID, utility bills)
2. Financial & Trading Information
Payment details (e.g., billing address, transaction history)

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Upon request, we will securely delete or anonymize your data when no longer required.

Access the personal data we hold about you
Request correction of inaccurate data
Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@futureselite.com .

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
