Available Instruments
DayTraders.com strictly limits the instruments available for trading to ensure consistency with our
educational goals and supported platforms. Customers are permitted to trade only simulated futures
contracts listed on the following regulated exchanges: the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME), the
Commodity Exchange (COMEX), the New York Mercantile Exchange (NYMEX), and the Chicago Board of Trade
(CBOT). These exchanges offer a broad range of futures products, providing ample opportunities for
traders to engage in diverse market sectors.
Please note that trading in other financial instruments, including but not limited to spot currencies
(Forex), contracts for difference (CFDs), individual stocks, options, and cryptocurrencies, is
strictly prohibited within the DayTraders.com ecosystem. Our programs, vendors, and platforms do not
support or facilitate trading in these instruments, as they fall outside the scope of our
futures-focused educational services and evaluation programs.
This restriction is in place to maintain a clear focus on futures trading, as well as to ensure
compliance with the guidelines established by our service providers and the regulatory frameworks
governing the markets we support. Engaging in simulated trading activities outside these approved
instruments is a violation of our policies and may result in account suspension or termination.

Evaluation Disclaimer
Pass rates for the DayTraders.com evaluation program vary by program type, account size, market
conditions, and individual trader experience. Not all participants who purchase an evaluation will
pass. The evaluation is designed to be challenging and to assess a trader's ability to generate
consistent simulated profits while adhering to defined risk management rules. Participants should
not interpret any historical pass rate as a guarantee or prediction of their individual likelihood
of success.
Our Evaluation and Pro Level Account programs are structured to simulate live market conditions as
accurately as possible. This includes incorporating commissions, fees, and other relevant factors
that traders would encounter in an actual trading environment. The primary purpose of the evaluation
is to assess a trader's skill, discipline, and strategy in a realistic and controlled setting. It is
important to emphasize that these programs are designed as comprehensive tests of trading
competency, not as training platforms. Participants are expected to have a solid foundation in
trading principles and risk management strategies before attempting the evaluation.
Passing the evaluation requires a high level of proficiency and is intentionally challenging, even
for traders with significant experience. The evaluation is structured to measure a trader's ability
to adhere to risk management rules, execute trades effectively, and maintain consistency over time.
Due to its demanding nature, this program is not recommended for individuals with little or no
trading experience. Novice traders may find the evaluation process particularly difficult and are
encouraged to seek further education and practice before considering participation.
We recommend that individuals carefully evaluate their level of trading knowledge, experience, and
preparedness prior to enrolling in the evaluation program. While the evaluation can serve as a
valuable benchmark for experienced traders, it is not intended as a learning tool for beginners. By
participating, traders acknowledge the inherent challenges and accept full responsibility for their
trading decisions and performance outcomes.
