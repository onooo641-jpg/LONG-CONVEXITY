The Basics
One place. Everything in real time. The Dashboard is where you track progress, manage accounts, and handle Payouts and Resets — the moment you need to. To get started, sign up here .
What You Can Do on the Dashboard
Instant Express Funded Account® (XFA) Activation — Hit your Profit Target and activate your XFA in real time.
Payout Tracker & Instant Deductions — Monitor Payout requests in real time. Amounts deduct instantly so you can get back to trading.
Resets Extend Rebill Dates — Each Reset adds 30 days to your rebill date.
Performance Stats — Real-time trading progress and metrics.
Account & Profile Management — Manage Resets, rebill dates, and Payouts in one place.
IDV & Funded Trader Certificates — Submit IDV documents and view funded certificates and Payout statuses directly in the platform.
Training Camp — Learn the basics of Futures trading in Training Camp through a series of interactive lessons.
Set up your Trader Profile and refer a friend!
How does the Reset Bank work?
Every Trading Combine® Rebill adds 1 Reset Credit to your Reset Bank. Credits are tied to a specific account size and path (Standard, Daily Loss Limit, or No Activation Fee) and expire 1 year after issue.
👉 Your Dashboard shows: how many Reset Credits you have, which sizes and paths they apply to, and expiration dates. The system always applies the oldest matching Reset Credit first.
How to Apply a Reset
Log into your Topstep Dashboard.
Select the active Trading Combine® you want to Reset.
Click Reset .
If you have eligible Reset Credits, you'll see how many apply.
Confirm the Reset.
No eligible Reset Credit? You can purchase one directly for that account size and path.
Changing Your Email Address
To update your email, contact Trader Support with:
Your Topstep username
Last 4 digits of the card on file
This verifies your identity so we can process the request quickly. Review our Single Profile Policy below before reaching out.
Please note: this process covers email address updates only; it does not apply to converting a Google/social login to an email login, which is not currently supported.
Changing Your Name
Minor typos or errors in your legal name? Trader Support can correct those.
We cannot update a profile to reflect a completely different name, and we cannot transfer profiles between individuals. Contact Trader Support to get started.
Single Profile Policy
Each Trader is permitted one Topstep profile . Multiple profiles violate our Terms of Use and may result in:
Profiles or Trading Combines® closed without warning
Temporary or permanent account suspension
Do not create a new profile when purchasing additional Trading Combines®. All accounts must live under a single profile. Profiles cannot be transferred between individuals.
Set up your Trader Profile
Your Trader Profile is a public facing page where you can show who you are and share your trading journey. It's optional, and it has no effect on your Trading Combine®, funded account, or Payouts.
Where to find it: Dashboard → Profile → Public Trader Profile.
What you can show:
Avatar, background, and a custom handle
Location, bio, and time with Topstep
Discord handle (X, Reddit, and Instagram coming soon)
Trading preferences, badges, Proof of Payouts, stats, and Profit Calendar
Nothing displays by default. Every section has its own toggle, so you choose what's visible and when.
⚠️ Your handle is permanent. Pick carefully: it can't be changed after you set it. Handles are 6 to 16 characters, no numbers, special characters, or spaces.
Trader Profile: Referral Codes
Every Trader with an active Trader Profile can generate a referral code. Anyone new to Topstep who enters it at checkout gets 15% off their first account purchase. Once that purchase completes, the referrer gets a 15% reward discount code by email for a purchase of their own. Learn more about Referral Codes here .
Trader Profiles FAQs
Who can create a Trader Profile?
All active Topstep Traders. Practice Accounts won't show up in the list of accounts you can select for your profile.
Who can see my profile?
Only people you share your link with. Profiles live at social.topstep.com with a unique URL and aren't discoverable on the public web.
Will my dollar amounts be public?
Only if you turn on All-Time Payouts, Proof of Payouts, Trading Stats, or Profit Calendar. Those show real dollar amounts and are optional.
Can I edit, hide, or delete my profile?
Yes, any time. Changes to your sharing preferences take effect within seconds. Your handle is the 1 thing you can't change.
Can I have more than 1 profile?
No. 1 profile per Trader account and email.
Can I edit my stats?
No. Stats pull from Topstep data for accuracy. You only choose whether to display them.
What happens to my profile if my account closes?
It stays as is. You can hide or delete it any time, regardless of account status.
Go through Training Camp
Training Camp is a guided practice mode inside your Topstep Dashboard that teaches Trading fundamentals and builds confidence. You have access to this before you even buy a Trading Combine!
