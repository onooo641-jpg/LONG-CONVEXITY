Written by Benjamin Chaffee
October 28, 2026
In an effort to implement trader feedback and diversify our offerings, Alpha Futures has launched the ZERO Plan. An account type with $0 Activation Fee, permanently.
​
In order to take a Trading Evaluation, a monthly subscription is required.
There is only one way to fail on Evaluation Accounts, do not allow the Account Balance to hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit .
​
​ Benefits of Zero Account:
1.) ONE DAY PASS! No Consistency Rule on Zero Evaluations means you may pass in one day.
2.) NO ACTIVATION FEE, ever. These accounts are named after their $0 Activation Fee .
3.) Offered in 25k, 50k, and 100k account size, ideal for traders getting started with low barrier to market.
4.) 90% profit split from the start, no tiered system.
5.) Pay yourself up to 4 times per month. Withdraw when you want after 5 winning days of $200 or more, no assigned payout dates.
6.) No "MLL slides to $0 at first withdrawal" rule. Other firms punish you for taking small payouts, not here. If MLL was at $49,200 before payout, it will be after as well
