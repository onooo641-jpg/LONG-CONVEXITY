Fake Reviews Policy
Our commitment to transparency, integrity, and authentic trader feedback.
1. Our Commitment
At Audacity Capital, maintaining the highest level of transparency and integrity is paramount. We are dedicated to providing our traders with institutional-grade reliability, and that begins with honest, unmanipulated feedback. Authentic reviews are crucial for our community to make informed decisions, and we enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against fake, manipulated, or incentivized reviews across all platforms.
2. Purpose of This Policy
To establish clear definitions of what constitutes a fake or manipulative review.
To outline our proactive measures for monitoring and maintaining review authenticity.
To provide transparent reporting mechanisms for our community to flag suspicious activity.
To protect the Audacity Capital brand and our traders from malicious impersonation or misinformation.
What We Consider Fake
Reviews that do not reflect a genuine trading experience with our firm, including:
Reviews posted by individuals who have never held an account with us.
Reviews generated by automated bots or third-party agencies.
Coordinated attacks aimed at artificially deflating our rating.
Reviews containing false claims regarding our trading rules or payouts.
Our Review Standards
To ensure absolute integrity, Audacity Capital explicitly DOES NOT:
× Purchase reviews from any third party.
× Offer compensation, discounts, or account upgrades in exchange for positive reviews.
× Mandate reviews as a condition for payouts or support.
× Alter or selectively publish reviews on our managed platforms.
Trustpilot Statement
Audacity Capital utilizes Trustpilot as an independent, third-party platform for collecting trader feedback.
We strictly adhere to Trustpilot’s robust guidelines regarding fake reviews. We actively monitor our profile and utilize Trustpilot’s reporting tools to flag reviews that violate their terms of service, such as suspected bot activity or reviews from unverifiable users. Our TrustScore reflects the genuine, aggregated experiences of our trading community.
Brand Impersonation & Protection
We actively monitor the web and social media for bad actors attempting to impersonate Audacity Capital or disseminate false information via fabricated reviews. Our legal and compliance teams take swift action against fraudulent profiles to protect our traders from scams.
Our enforcement strategy includes:
⌁ Issuing formal DMCA takedown requests for unauthorized use of our intellectual property.
⌁ Filing trademark complaints with social media platforms and domain registrars.
⌁ Collaborating with hosting providers and search engines to de-index and remove fraudulent content.
Monitoring & Compliance: This policy is subject to periodic reviews to ensure continued alignment with industry best practices and evolving regulatory standards.
Reporting Fake Reviews
If you encounter suspicious reviews or believe you have found a fake profile impersonating Audacity Capital, please report it immediately.
Contact Compliance compliance@audacity.capital Required Reporting Details:
Platform name (e.g. Trustpilot, X)
Direct link to the review or profile
Supporting evidence or screenshots

Does Audacity Capital publish fake reviews?
No. Audacity Capital does not create, purchase, or publish fake reviews. We are committed to ensuring that customer feedback reflects genuine trading experiences. We do not offer incentives, rewards, or compensation in exchange for positive reviews.
Does Audacity Capital pay customers for positive reviews?
No. We do not provide financial incentives, discounts, free accounts, trading benefits, or any other rewards in exchange for customer reviews. We encourage honest feedback regardless of whether it is positive or negative.
Why does Audacity Capital have a Fake Reviews Policy?
We created this policy to demonstrate our commitment to transparency and authenticity. It explains how we approach customer reviews, outlines our position against fake or manipulated reviews, and helps traders understand how to identify genuine feedback.
What happened to Audacity Capital's Trustpilot profile?
Our Trustpilot profile became unavailable following issues involving third-party impersonation and platform-related actions. Audacity Capital has never knowingly published fake reviews or encouraged review manipulation. We remain committed to collecting authentic feedback from genuine traders and customers.
