The Basics
Topstep Challenges give you a chance to put your futures trading skills to the test and work toward a fixed cash payout. Pay a one-time fee, hit the Profit Target in two rounds, and keep 100 percent of your Challenge payout when you pass.
Unlike the Trading Combine®, Challenges don't lead to a funded account. They're a separate way to trade for a payout.
Originally limited to Topstep Labs , Challenges are now a permanent part of Topstep's lineup.

How it Works
Pick Your Challenge. Pay a one-time fee to get started in the Challenge Round.
Pass Round 1. Trade until you hit your Profit Target. Trading locks, and your account is marked as passed.
Pass Round 2. Activate the Payout Round in your Dashboard (allow up to 15 minutes), then trade to hit your Profit Target again.
Request Your Payout. Once you've passed both rounds, request your fixed, one-time Payout in your Dashboard. Keep 100 percent of your Payout. Your account closes afterward.

FAQs
Where do I find Challenges?
Head to the Challenges page in your Dashboard to purchase!
​
Launchpad Challenge is only available to Traders who have never passed a Trading Combine or Challenge, and do not currently hold a Starter Challenge. Qualifying Traders are emailed the link to purchase automatically, which is the only way to purchase.
Who can buy a Challenge?
All Traders, new and existing, can purchase most of our Challenges. However, only Traders who have never purchased with Topstep can purchase the Starter Challenge.
Can I buy more than 1 Challenge?
For all challenges except the Starter Challenge, yes. While the Starter Challenge can only be purchased once, you can buy up to 5 of each other type of Challenge per month.
How many Challenges can be in the Payout Round at once?
Up to 5. That's separate from the 5 Express Funded Account® (XFA) limit. There's no limit on Challenges in the Challenge Round.
What's the difference between a Topstep Challenge and a Trading Combine?
Challenges are designed around a fixed, one-time payout. Complete both rounds, request your Payout, and the account closes.
Trading Combines are the path to getting funded with Topstep. Pass your Trading Combine to qualify for an Express Funded Account, where you can work toward multiple Payouts and potentially a Live Funded Account.
Can I trade a Challenge alongside my other accounts?
Yes. Challenges are separate products. They don't replace or affect your Trading Combines®, XFAs, or Live Funded Accounts® (LFAs) . You can trade them at the same time.
Do Challenges lead to a Live Funded Account?
No. How you trade a Challenge has no effect on your eligibility to be called up to Live. If you're called up based on your XFA performance, your Challenges and Payout Rounds stay open and eligible.
What is a static Maximum Loss Limit?
A static Maximum Loss Limit is a fixed loss floor. It does not trail up with the account balance like standard Trading Combines. You can learn more about different types of Maximum Loss Limits here .
What happens if I fail a Challenge?
If you hit your Maximum Loss Limit, your Challenge ends. Challenges don't offer Resets, so you'll need to purchase a new one to try again.
​
The Starter Challenge and Launchpad Challenge are the exception. They both include 2 free Resets.
What happens if I want a refund?
Our standard Topstep Refund Policies apply.
What are minis and micros?
Term
Meaning
Example
Mini
A Mini is a smaller futures contract that trades electronically. It’s a fraction of the size of a standard futures contract, so it takes less capital and margin to trade.
Examples of a mini you'll trade in TopstepX include E-mini S&P 500 (ES) and E-mini NASDAQ 100 (NQ).
​
Learn more about what products you can trade here !
Micro
A Micro is a mini-sized Mini. It tracks the same market at about 1/10 the size, so you get a lower cost to enter and more control over your position size. In TopstepX, 1 Mini = 10 Micros.
Examples of a micro you'll trade in TopstepX include Micro E-mini S&P (MES) and Micro E-mini NASDAQ 100 (MNQ).
