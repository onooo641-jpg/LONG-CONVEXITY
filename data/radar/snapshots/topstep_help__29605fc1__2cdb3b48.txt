Am I Eligible to Trade with Topstep?
We fund Traders worldwide. Where you're from — and where you live — both matter.

Overview
Eligibility is based on both citizenship and permanent residency . OFAC sanctions and partner restrictions apply. Some countries can access Express Funded Accounts® (XFA) but not Live Funded Accounts® (LFA).
Requirements
No US citizenship required
Minimum age: 18
Citizens or residents of OFAC-sanctioned or partner-restricted countries are not eligible to trade, earn funding, or receive payouts

Eligibility FAQs
I'm Canadian — can I earn a Funded Account?
Yes. You trade on a sub-account of Topstep's master account, considered a US-based account. Many US brokerages don't open Canadian accounts due to regulations — this structure is how it works.
Citizen of an eligible country but living in an ineligible one — can I trade?
No. Residing in an ineligible country makes you ineligible — regardless of citizenship.
Citizen of an ineligible country but living in an eligible one — can I trade?
Possibly. Submit a government-issued photo ID proving permanent residency in the eligible country, plus a bank statement from that country. Topstep's Trust team reviews it. Contact Trader Support →
What if I'm traveling through an ineligible country?
Don't trade while there. Trading from an ineligible country — even briefly — may trigger a review by the Trust team.
Why are some countries XFA-only and not eligible for Live?
Brokerage restrictions prevent Live Funded Account access in certain countries. Those Traders can still earn payouts and build skills in the XFA and Pro Account.
When are more countries being added to the eligible list?
No official timeline. Topstep is actively working on it as partnerships expand.
What about countries with human rights violations?
While we do not control every country's rules and regulations, we will continue to support as many countries as OFAC and our brokers will allow us to.
My country was previously eligible, but now it's ineligible. Am I still eligible to trade with Topstep?
A country may be deemed ineligible for several reasons, including, but not limited to, OFAC sanctions and restrictions with our partners. In these cases, traders are unable to trade with, get Funded by, or receive payouts from Topstep.
November 17, 2026 Update: Newly Ineligible Countries
What is changing, and who does it affect?
Effective Nov. 17, Topstep no longer offers new accounts or purchases to Traders whose account address is in Andorra, Cambodia, Ethiopia, Guinea, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Macao, Mozambique, Namibia, Senegal, Serbia, Uzbekistan or Zimbabwe. Traders in these countries will have new purchases and profile edits blocked. Trading, Payouts, call-ups and Express Funded Account activation fees still work through December 31, 2026.
Why is this happening, and is it about me?
Recently, we've seen a sharp rise in coordinated abuse from several countries. When we closed those accounts, the same activity returned through new profiles, so we made the difficult decision to restrict access at the country level. We know this affects many Traders who had no part in it, and we don't take that lightly.
​
This is a business decision. For a long time, Topstep worked to keep Traders in these countries eligible, going further than many other firms in our industry. We’ve decided we can no longer offer new accounts there. It’s based on where you live, not on you or how you trade, and we’re grateful to everyone in these countries who has been part of the Topstep community.
What happens to my funded accounts?
You keep them. Express Funded Accounts, Live Funded Accounts, and any Challenge or Labs drop that has reached its funded stage keep trading, requesting Payouts and getting called up to Live under the usual rules, before and after December 31. However, Back2Funded is not available. If a funded account closes after Dec 31st, a new one can’t be started, including through Back2Funded or a new Trading Combine.
What happens to my Trading Combine?
Your subscription is cancelled and won’t rebill, so you won’t be charged again. You can keep trading until your paid-through date (find this on your Billing page) or 5 p.m. CT on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026, whichever comes first. There’s no extension, and subscriptions can’t be restarted.
​
​ Can I still use Resets or buy new ones?
You can use banked Resets on any Trading Combine still in its paid period. This is an exception to the usual rule that a cancelled Trading Combine can’t be Reset. You can’t buy new Resets, or any other product (Trading Combines, Back2Funded, Challenges, Labs drops), including offers you already received. Although you can use a banked Reset, a Reset doesn’t extend how long the Trading Combine stays open. It will only Reset the balance and violation history.
I passed my Trading Combine but haven’t activated my XFA. Is there a deadline?
Yes. Normally there’s no deadline, but for Traders in these countries activation closes at 5 p.m. CT on Thursday, Dec. 31, 2026. This includes Trading Combines passed before Nov. 17.
What about my Challenge or Labs drop?
It keeps trading until it expires under its usual terms or the cutoff, whichever comes first. If it reaches its Funded stage before then, it continues as a funded account and will not close on December 31st outside of standard account closure parameters (Maximum Loss Limit breach).
What about Level 2 Data?
Level 2 Data will also be canceled on December 31st. You will have access through the end of the subscription period.
