Account reset
August 12, 2026
Table of contents
If you have failed one of your accounts at any stage, you are eligible for a 10% discount to restart your journey from the Challenge phase.
﻿​﻿
﻿This option will appear in your dashboard once you fail one of your accounts. When you locate the account you have failed, simply click on the retry button. This will generate a new order with the applied discount code, for which you can immediately pay and start your journey from the challenge phase again.
﻿​﻿
﻿This 10% discount will be set for the same settings of the account you failed.
How to redeem your discount reset
1. login to your dashboard
2. Go to your account overview
3. Slide down to the "Closed accounts" section, where you can retry your account.
Important things to note:
Availability: This offer is only valid for 7 days following the account failure.
Consistency: The discount applies to the same account size and settings as the failed account.
Existing Orders: If you already clicked the button, your discounted invoice is waiting in your Order List .
