How to log into your AlphaTrader account
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
May 27, 2026
Alpha Futures is excited to offer AlphaTrader .
Please find the tutorial video for the platform here and instructions to login below!
To use this integration, you must have an Evaluation, or Qualified Account utilizing AlphaTrader.
First, log in to AlphaTrader using the username and password provided in your dashboard in Account Information.
You can launch AlphaTrader directly from your user dashboard with the "Launch AlphaTrader" button. You may also choose to sign in directly using this link .
https://futures.alphatrader.com/signin
Once you have launched AlphaTrader, you will be prompted to sign in with the credentials provided in your “Account Info”.
​
Once logged in, you MUST sign your Non-Professional Agreement before your account will receive real-time data or be able to trade on AlphaTrader . Be sure to select the 'Simulation' when prompted to select a Trading Mode.
You are now successfully connected and can enjoy trading your Alpha Futures Account(s) on AlphaTrader!
