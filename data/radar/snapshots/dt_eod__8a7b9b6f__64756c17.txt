Here is a quick guide on how to access all information about your affiliate commission, clicks, and more.
LOGGING IN
Make sure you are logged into your Member Dashboard > HERE <
REVIEW YOUR CODE
Once you have successfully logged into your Member Dashboard, click "Affiliates" on the left-hand side.
Select "Banners and Links" within your affiliate dashboard to review your custom code and custom links to share with your friends and followers.
REVIEW YOUR STATISTICS
If you like to review your clicks generated and commission earned, choose "Statistics" from within your affiliate dashboard.
This will show you an overall view on all clicks and commissions earned, separated by month.
You can review each month in greater detail, with a day-by-day breakdown, by selecting the specific month you wish to examine.
Please ensure that your referrals use your coupon code during the sign-up process to generate your commission.
COMMISSION PAYOUTS
Review past affiliate commission payouts under "Payouts" within your Affiliate dashboard.
Click > HERE < to see all requirements and additional information on affiliate commission payouts.
PAYOUT METHOD
To ensure your payout is processed smoothly, please make sure your payout method is set up and completed accurately.
Fill out all the information required and save the information entered.
