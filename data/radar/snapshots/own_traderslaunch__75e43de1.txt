01 Evaluation Rules
One-step evaluation with no Phase 2
Profit in one session cannot go above 40% of the profit target (50% on static plans)
No minimum trading days on futures plans; a plan card that shows one has it
60-day maximum time limit to pass
At least 1 trade in any 7 days on every account; an evaluation with no trade for 7 days in a row is breached
EOD drawdown locks at starting balance with no intraday trailing
Hedging is not permitted, including same-instrument, correlated-instrument, or coordinated cross-trader hedging
Reasonable scaling and averaging are allowed; excessive martingale-style trading may trigger an account review
Scalping trades held for 5–30 seconds are allowed; HFT-style trading is not permitted, and accounts may be paused for review when more than 10% of trades are opened and fully closed within two seconds
Stay outside the price limit zone: while an index future is within 2% of its CME daily limit (7% today, so a 5% move from the previous settlement, up or down), close your position and do not trade it until it is back out
02 SimFunded Account Rules
Eligible for payouts once funded account profit is above +1%
Stay outside the price limit zone: while an index future is within 2% of its CME daily limit (7% today, so a 5% move from the previous settlement, up or down), close your position and do not trade it until it is back out
One payout request every 24 hours per trader, across all accounts
No per-payout cap; at $100,000 in total payouts the next request pauses for a review of up to 5 business days
No consistency rules once funded
No activation fees
No additional fees once funded
Payouts and funded account restrictions are subject to program rules and Terms
03 Markets and Profit Split
Trade common contracts including NQ, ES, GC, YM and CL. Standard accounts offer a 60% or 80% profit split with full-session trading.
04 Multiple Accounts
You can hold up to five funded futures accounts at a time. Payouts and SimFunded account restrictions are subject to program rules and Terms.
