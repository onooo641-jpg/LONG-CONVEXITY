Written by Benjamin Chaffee
November 13, 2024
We would like to emphasize that we do not accept payments made via a third-party card or account. All transactions related to assessment purchases and account trading must be conducted in the name of the cardholder.
As part of our onboarding process for qualified trader accounts, we conduct cross-verification of the name on the payment card with the KYC (Know Your Customer) profile to ensure consistency. This precautionary measure is implemented to mitigate the risk of credit card fraud and safeguard the integrity of our platform.
