What is the Minimum Trading Days rule?
📅 How many days must you trade? Learn about the requirements for Evaluation and Funded accounts.
November 24, 2025
Table of contents
Minimum Trading Days
Passing isn't just about hitting the profit target; it's about showing consistency. You must trade a minimum number of active days to be eligible for passing or withdrawing profits.
The Requirements:
Account Status
Minimum Days
🚀 Accelerated Challenge
1 Day
🏔️ Ascent Challenge
2 Days
💰 Funded Accounts
5 Days (per payout cycle)
What counts as a "Trading Day"?
To count towards the minimum requirement, a trading day must be "active" and profitable.
​
Definition : You must generate a Net Realized Profit of at least $200 on that specific day.
✅ Counted: You profit +$250 on Tuesday. (Tuesday counts as 1 day).
❌ Not Counted: You profit +$50 on Wednesday. (Wednesday does not count towards the minimum).
❌ Not Counted: You have a losing day of -$500 . (Losing days do not count).
This rule applies across ALL account sizes ($50k, $100k, and $150k) and stages (Evaluation & Funded).
