What is BrightFunded Futures?
How the program works from buying an Evaluation to getting paid, with all the key facts in one place.
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Table of contents
BrightFunded Futures is our futures prop trading program. You buy an Evaluation , pass it by reaching a profit target without breaking the rules, and then trade a SIM Funded Account where you can request payouts , paid in USDC (ERC-20) or by bank transfer, from the balance you generate. One phase, no time limit, no daily loss limit and no monthly fees. Traders with a strong payout record can later be invited to a Live account with the Prime Program .
How does it work?
Step 1: Buy an Evaluation
Choose a plan ( BrightPlus or BrightCore ) and an account size ( $25K, $50K, $100K or $150K ). You pay a one-time fee . There is no activation fee and no monthly subscription.
Step 2: Pass the Evaluation
You only need to pass one phase , with no time limit. You pass when all of these are true:
You reached the profit target (6% of the account).
The Consistency Rule is met.
The Minimum Trade Duration rule is met.
Your equity never touched the Max Loss Limit floor .
Step 3: Get your SIM Funded Account and request payouts
After you pass, your KYC must be approved and you sign the Futures contract . Your SIM Funded Account then activates on the same account number , with the balance back at the starting balance. From there you can request payouts from the balance you generate, paid in USDC (ERC-20) or by bank transfer .
Step 4 (optional): Go Live with the Prime Program
A Live account trades real CME capital and starts at $0 . You earn it: when you reach one of these thresholds, you become eligible for the Prime Program:
5 payouts on one account.
10 payouts across all your accounts.
20% profit on one account (for example, $10K on a 50K account).
Our Risk team decides, based on your consistency and discipline, and can invite you earlier. Then you choose to Go Live or Cash out . Read What is the Prime Program? for all the details.

Frequently asked questions
Are the accounts real?
The Evaluation and SIM Funded Accounts are simulated , but the payouts are paid to you in USDC or by bank transfer , from the balance you generate on your SIM Funded Account. A Live account is different: it trades real CME capital and you earn it through the Prime Program.
Do I have to pay every month?
No. You pay a one-time fee when you buy the Evaluation.
Is there a time limit to pass?
No. You can take as long as you need, as long as you place at least 1 new trade every 30 calendar days and follow the rules.
​
Can I have more than one account?
Yes. You can run unlimited Evaluations at the same time and have up to 5 active SIM Funded Accounts per household.
Do I keep the same account number after I pass?
Yes. You keep one account number per purchase through the Evaluation, SIM Funded and any reset. Your dashboard shows a new card for each stage.
Can I request my whole profit?
No. The payout cap is the main limit on both plans. On BrightCore you can also request at most 50% of the current profit of your account (your balance minus your starting balance). BrightPlus has no 50% limit, but you can request only what is above the buffer. The profit you do not withdraw stays in the account and can be requested in later payouts.
How do I get a Live account?
You earn it. Reach one threshold (5 payouts on one account, 10 payouts across all your accounts, or 20% profit on one account) and you become eligible for the Prime Program. Our Risk team decides, and then you choose to Go Live or Cash out. A Live account starts at $0.
