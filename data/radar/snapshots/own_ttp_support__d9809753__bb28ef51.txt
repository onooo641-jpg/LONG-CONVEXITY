Which Platform Do I Use For Stocks?
Stocks are traded on the Volumetrica platform — it’s web-based and needs no installation, with desktop and mobile also available. A video manual is available in your Dashboard under Web Platform.
What About Market Data?
Level 1 market data is included. Level 2 (depth of book) is available as a paid add-on, which you can subscribe to in your dashboard.
Is There Leverage, And What Are The Costs?
There is no leverage on stocks — you trade the actual share size. The costs are the broker commission and exchange fees; there are no overnight financing fees, since positions aren’t held overnight.
What Can I Trade?
Standardised, exchange-traded shares on global exchanges, during regular exchange hours.
You can only open a position in a stock whose average daily trading volume over the past 14 days is greater than 200,000 shares, so trading stays in liquid names.
