Scalping rules
When a trade counts as a scalp and what happens.
Updated today
Table of contents
A scalp is a trade held 10 seconds or less, or making a net profit of $20 or less per contract ($2 on micro contracts). Scalping is never a breach.
Accounts
Held
Or moves
Elite, Prime, Nitro, New Instant
10 seconds or less
Net profit of $20 or less per contract ($2 on micro contracts)
Old Starter, Pro and Instant (bought before 24 Nov 2025)
Under 10 seconds
5 points or less on NQ, or the equivalent
What happens
Evaluation: if your scalp profit is more than 50% of the profit target, the upgrade is held for a manual review.
Funded: scalping is never a breach. Scalp profit is added to your payout target and removed from the withdrawable amount.
New Instant: scalped profit doesn't count toward the payout profit goal.
