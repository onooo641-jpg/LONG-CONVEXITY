What broker do I trade with?
Simulated Trading feed
September 1, 2026
E8 Markets is not a broker and does not accept users' deposits. E8 Markets provides a structured simulation environment for individuals seeking to develop trading skills and demonstrate performance under defined conditions.
​
E8's simulated environment is powered by real market data from third-party institutional data providers. Market data for Futures products is sourced directly from CME (Chicago Mercantile Exchange). If you are trading Perpetual Futures, the feed and market data are taken directly from HyperLiquid.
