Signature plans are MyFundedFutures' line of distinct plan structures.
Each Signature plan has its own evaluation and payout rules.
The plans formerly released under Studio are now Signature 5k, Signature 10k and Signature 20k. All three share the same rules and payouts, with one loss rule to remember: a static Max Loss Limit (MLL).
Signature plans are sold only on the dedicated Signature page ( http://myfundedfutures.com/plans/signature ).

What makes Signature different
Static Max Loss Limit — Your MLL is set when the account starts and never moves. There is no trailing drawdown and no end-of-day reset of the floor during Evaluation or Sim Funded.
No Daily Loss Limit — There is no soft pause or hard daily loss limit on Signature.
No consistency rule — Not in Evaluation and not in Sim Funded.
Payouts anytime — On Sim Funded, you can request all (or part) of your net profits anytime once positions are closed. Minimum $100/$250 before the split. No waiting period, buffer, or qualifying days.
Evaluation stage
Hit the profit target without breaching the Max Loss Limit to pass. There is no minimum trading-day requirement and no consistency rule.

Daily profit cap
On a Sim Funded account, net realized profit above the daily cap in a single trading day is forfeited and cannot be withdrawn:
Signature 5k: $2,500
Signature 10k: $5,000
Signature 20k: $10,000
Anything at or under the cap for that day remains available under normal payout rules.

FAQ
What is the difference between Signature 5k, 10k and 20k?
Same plan shape at two sizes:
Signature 5k is $5k (MLL $250, 5 micros, $2,500 daily profit cap).
Signature 10k is $10K (MLL $500, 10 micros, $5K daily profit cap).
Signature 20k is $20K at (MLL $1,000, 20 micros, $10K daily profit cap).
Rules and payouts are otherwise the same.
How does the Max Loss Limit work?
It is static. It is set when the account starts and never moves during Evaluation or Sim Funded.
When can I request a payout?
Anytime on Sim Funded once your positions are closed.
Minimum $100 on Signature 5k
Minimum $250 on Signature 10k and 20k
Is there a limit on daily profits?
Yes, on Sim Funded only. Net realized profit above:
$2,500 (Signature 5k)
$5,000 (Signature 10k)
$10,000 (Signature 20k)
Can I have more than one Signature account?
No. One active Signature account at a time — either Evaluation or Sim Funded.
Which platform do Signature accounts use?
Hyperprop only. More platforms coming soon.
When do I become eligible for Live?
After 5 approved payouts or $5,000 total requested. Live starts at 40% of total requested, up to $2,000. Our team may also move you earlier.
​ Are there activation fees?
No. There are no activation fees for any of our Signature Plans.
Is news trading allowed?
Yes.
Signature plan terms are defined in your Sim Funded agreements.
