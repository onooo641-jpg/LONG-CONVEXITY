Server time
Updated over 2 weeks ago
Due to Daylight Saving Time, at the beginning of spring and at the end of autumn, the Server time is being changed. The Server time is being changed to UTC + 2 at the beginning of November and to UTC + 3 at the end of March
Currently, the server time is set to UTC + 2
