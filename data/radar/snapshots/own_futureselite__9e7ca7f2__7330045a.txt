Live accounts: loss floor, cushion and reserve
Live accounts: loss floor, cushion and reserve
How a live account protects capital and pays you.
October 18, 2026
Table of contents
Loss floor
Every live account starts at $0 with a loss floor below it ($2,000 on a 50K).
The floor moves only at the daily close, to sit one floor-width below your best close. Intraday dips below it are safe if the day closes above.
Once it reaches $0 it stops there permanently.
If a trading day closes below the floor, the account is lost, including any cushion it holds. There is no daily loss limit.
Cushion
Half the starting floor ($1,000 on a 50K). Your first profits fill it.
Profit above the cushion is withdrawable from day one.
The cushion is released after 15 profitable days, not necessarily in a row. A day counts when realised profit reaches the daily minimum ($150 on a 50K).
Withdrawals never move the floor.
Reserve
Unlocks begin after 15 profitable live days and $5,000 of total live profit.
Then every extra $5,000 of profit unlocks $2,500.
Locked Reserve can't be withdrawn. Released Reserve can be withdrawn or used for margin.
Gambling, oversizing or reckless trading forfeits Reserve unlocks.
Getting paid
Payouts are daily with a $200 minimum: live profit above the cushion plus any released Reserve. The split is 90%.
