LucidBlack Funded Account Scaling Plan
The scaling plan controls how much of your max size is available to trade based on the simulated profits in your account. As your simulated profits increase and decrease, your max tradable contract size moves up and down based. See the table below for details.

Circumventing the Scaling Plan
Some trader may be inclined to try and get around the scaling plan. Our back-end systems track for this. If we see repeated behavior attempting to get around the limits, we may review your account.
