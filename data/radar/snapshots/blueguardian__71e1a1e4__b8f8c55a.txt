Zero Path to Live
How Zero traders move to Live: the $8,000 day choice, how your Live Allocation and starting balance are set, how you earn your Live Bonus and Live rules.
Applies to: Futures Updated 23 November 2026
Short answer
Zero has its own path to Live, separate from our other Futures plans. If a funded Zero account makes $8,000 profit in a single day, you choose: take a $3,600 payout for each account that reached $8,000, or move to Live with that amount as your starting balance. Our Risk Team may also offer Live to Zero traders with consistent payout performance.
When you move to Live, we deposit real funds into your Live Account. If you have a pending payout, that amount becomes your starting balance. If you don't, you start with the Live Allocation for your account sizes.
You can then earn a Live Bonus equal to your starting balance, released in two halves as you trade consistently and withdraw profit.
Zero Live Accounts pay daily at a 90% split, with an end-of-day trailing drawdown and no daily loss limit or consistency rule.
Once you reach an $8,000 day or you're offered Live, you can't keep trading your funded account. It's closed whichever option you choose.
How funded Zero accounts move to Live Replay
Moving to Live merges all your funded Zero accounts into one Live Account. You receive a Live Bonus for each funded account as soon as your Live Account is set up, and each account adds to your Live drawdown, up to the cap shown above. How the Live drawdown moves Replay
On Live, your loss limit moves up with your highest end-of-day closing balance. It locks once your Live profit equals your starting Live drawdown, or as soon as you request a payout before that point.
How you're considered for Live
The $8,000 day: if a funded Zero account makes $8,000 net profit in a single trading day, we email you to choose between taking the payout and moving to Live.
Risk Team offer: our Risk Team may also identify Zero traders with consistent payout performance for Live. If you're identified, our team will reach out to you directly. These offers are made at the Risk Team's discretion.
Can I keep trading my funded account instead?
No. After an $8,000 day or a Live offer from our Risk Team, your funded account is closed. You can take the payout instead of moving to Live, but you can't continue trading on the funded account.

Your starting balance
How much we deposit into your Live Account depends on whether you have a pending payout when you move to Live.
If you have a pending payout
This includes the payout from an $8,000 day, or any payout pending when our team reaches out to you about Live. You choose one of two options:
Take the payout. It's paid to you as normal and your funded account is closed. A 30-day cooldown then applies before you can buy a new evaluation.
Move to Live. Your pending payout isn't paid out. Instead, the full amount is deposited into your Live Account as your starting balance, in place of the standard Live Allocation.
Your account is closed once you've made your choice.
Example: one funded $50K account reaches $8,000 in a day and you choose Live. Your Live Account starts with a $3,600 balance.
If you don't have a pending payout
We deposit your combined Live Allocation as your starting balance.
Example: our team reaches out to you about Live with one funded $50K account and no pending payout. Your Live Account starts with a $2,000 balance.

How the end-of-day drawdown works
Your maximum loss limit moves up with your highest end-of-day closing balance. It's calculated at the close of each trading day, not during the day.
It stops trailing and locks at $100 above your starting balance once your Live profit equals your Live Allocation, or as soon as you request a payout before reaching that point.
If you breach your Live Account
Live Accounts can't be reset or reactivated.
Any part of your Live Bonus that hasn't been released yet is forfeited.
A 30-day cooldown applies before you can buy a new evaluation.
Repeated breaches through reckless risk-taking may lead to longer cooldowns.
