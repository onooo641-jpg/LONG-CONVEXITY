What kind of company is BrightFunded and how can you benefit?
October 21, 2025
BrightFunded is a revolutionary online demo trading evaluation firm that enables retail traders worldwide to monetize their trading skills with no risk beyond the one-time evaluation fee. After successfully passing the evaluation, Funded traders receive a profit split of at least 80% on the account they qualify for. To participate, traders pay a fee that depends on their chosen target capital size.
At BrightFunded, traders earn BrightFunded Tokens based on their trading volume on both Challenge and Funded accounts. These tokens can be redeemed for various trading perks that provide an additional edge in the market.
Traders can initially manage up to USD 400,000 at BrightFunded, with the potential to scale their capital indefinitely under the firm’s scaling plan .
BrightFunded covers all losses, if they occur.
