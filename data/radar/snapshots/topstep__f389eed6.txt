This page explains how Topstep's Affiliate Program (the " Program ") works. These terms work alongside Topstep's Terms of Use , in particular Section 14 (Promo code usage) , which are incorporated by reference. If anything on this page conflicts with the Terms of Use, the Terms of Use control.
By using or redeeming an Affiliate Code, you agree to the Terms of Use.
How the Program works
Topstep may approve content creators and marketers (each, an " Affiliate ") to share a unique code issued by Topstep (an " Affiliate Code ") with their audiences. When a new trader (a " Code User ") uses an Affiliate Code to complete their first simulated proprietary trading evaluation program (e.g., Trading Combines®, Topstep Labs and/or Evaluation Challenges), which are operated by TopstepTrader LLC and marketed publicly under the Topstep brand (the “ Prop Program(s) ”) purchase, the Code User receives 20% off that purchase, and the Affiliate may be compensated by Topstep.
Affiliates are independent third parties. They are not employees, agents, or representatives of Topstep and have no authority to make statements or commitments on Topstep's behalf.
For Code Users
An Affiliate Code applies only to a first-time Prop Program purchase by someone who has never previously purchased one from Topstep or any Topstep-affiliated entity.
You may redeem only one Affiliate Code, one time, in your lifetime.
The Affiliate Code does not apply to account Resets, Back2Funded, market data add-ons, or any other purchase.
Affiliate Codes cannot be combined with other promo codes, discounts, or Referral Codes.
Affiliate Codes apply only to Topstep's Prop Programs. They do not apply to any brokerage account or service offered by Topstep Brokerage LLC.
Becoming an Affiliate
Participation in the Program is by approval only. Topstep selects Affiliates at its discretion and may decline, suspend, or end any Affiliate's participation at any time. Approved Affiliates are issued a unique Affiliate Code and must follow Topstep's content and conduct guidelines, including required disclosures, and may not make guarantees or promises of profit, payouts, or funded status. If you're interested in being considered, please contact partners@topstep.com. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee approval.
Affiliates may share their own Affiliate Code as permitted by Topstep. This is a specific, limited exception to the general restriction on sharing and commercially exploiting promo codes in Section 14 of our Terms of Use.
Affiliate disclosures
Affiliates may be compensated by Topstep when you use their code. Look for disclosures such as "#TopstepPartner" or "Topstep Partner." An Affiliate's views are their own and do not represent Topstep's. Nothing an Affiliate says is investment, trading, tax, or legal advice, and Affiliate results are not typical or a guarantee of your results. The Trading Combine® is a simulated trading program that is difficult to pass. Please review Topstep's Risk Disclosures before purchasing.

Trading Combine and Topstep Labs Experiments. The Trading Combine, Express Funded Accounts and Topstep Labs experiments are realistic simulations of trading under actual market conditions and are difficult to pass even for experienced traders. Simulated performance results have certain limitations. Unlike an actual performance record, simulated results do not represent actual trading. Also, because the trades have not actually been executed, the results may have under- or over-compensated for the impact, if any, of certain market factors, such as lack of liquidity. Simulated trading programs, in general, are also subject to the fact that they are designed with the benefit of hindsight. Simulated trading does not involve financial risk, and no simulated trading record can completely account for the impact of financial risk in actual trading. For example, the ability to withstand losses or adhere to a particular trading program in spite of trading losses are material points which can also adversely affect actual trading results. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profit or losses similar to those shown.
2025 Trader Performance Statistics.
