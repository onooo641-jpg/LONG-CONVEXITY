How max contracts work: your total open position across all instruments, with 10 micros counting as 1 mini.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Your max contracts is the largest total open position you can hold at once, across all instruments combined.
How it's counted
10 micro contracts count as 1 mini contract.
Positions in different instruments add up. On a 10-contract account, 5 NQ + 50 MNQ uses your full limit.
Working orders don't count until they fill.
An order that would take you over the limit is rejected.
