To ensure instant account activation and secure processing, we currently accept Credit and Debit Cards exclusively.
​
Accepted Cards:
✅ Visa
✅ Mastercard
✅ American Express

Do you accept Crypto or Bank Wire? Not at this time. To maintain the speed of our automated setup process, we do not currently support Cryptocurrency, PayPal, or Wire Transfers for purchasing challenges or subscriptions.
