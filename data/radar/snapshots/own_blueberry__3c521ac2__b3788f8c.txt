Is Scalping Allowed? (Minimum Position Time)
⚡ Yes, manual scalping is allowed. Tick-scalping and HFT are not.
November 24, 2025
Table of contents
Scalping & Minimum Position Time
We welcome scalpers who profit from quick market moves. However, we strictly distinguish between Legitimate Scalping (Skill) and Tick Scalping (Manipulation).
Is there a minimum time I must hold a trade? We do not enforce a hard "1-minute" rule for every single trade. However, our Risk Team monitors for Tick Scalping behavior.
​
❌ The Red Flag
Trades that are consistently opened and closed in sub-second intervals (e.g., 0-5 seconds) are characteristic of High-Frequency Trading (HFT) and arbitrage bots. This trading behavior is prohibited.
​
✅ The Safe Zone
If you are manually entering and exiting trades based on market structure, you have nothing to worry about. Legitimate scalping typically lasts significantly longer than the latency of a data feed.
