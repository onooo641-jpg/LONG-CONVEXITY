Find out how and when DayTraders.com processes and pays out affiliate commissions each month.
Updated over a month ago
Copy for LLM
Affiliate commission reports are generated on the first day of each month. Compensation for the previous period is processed and sent by the 15th.
For example, commissions earned between August 1 and August 31 will be reported on September 1, with payment processed by September 15.
Your payment should arrive in your bank account within 3-7 business days after the 15th, depending on your bank’s processing speed.
Please wait a full 7 business days before submitting a support ticket regarding your payment. Sometimes banks can be slow, so patience is appreciated.
The minimum compensation threshold is $100 with any given month. If you don’t reach $100 in a month, your earnings will roll over to the next month until you do. For example, if you earn $30 in June, no compensation will be processed in July. But if you earn $100 in July, bringing your total to $130, you’ll receive payment on August 15.
Affiliate Requirements for Commission: To qualify for a commission, affiliates must meet the following criteria within the calendar month:
3 new paying customers
50 clicks
$100 in commissions
If these requirements are not met, the commission will not be processed for that month. Keep going, and you’ll get there!
