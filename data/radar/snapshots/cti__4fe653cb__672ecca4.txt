From Struggles to Stability: The Evolution of Xing’s EA
Xing’s system did not arrive finished. It is the product of more than a decade of building, testing and rebuilding, and today it handles his trading end to end: a fully automated EA that runs around the clock across 28 currency pairs plus gold and silver, holding swing positions for up to 20 days or more. Here is how it got there.
The Early Struggles in Forex Trading
Xing began developing his EA in 2011, but it wasn’t a smooth start. Like many traders, he faced numerous challenges and setbacks. Over the years, he refined his approach, learning valuable lessons about risk management and patience. Through countless modifications and a shift in mindset, Xing’s EA evolved into a robust system capable of consistent performance.
Risk Management: The Key to Xing’s Success
One of the standout features of Xing’s trading approach is his meticulous risk management. He controls his total risk across all open positions, keeping it within a small percentage of his total equity. This disciplined approach has allowed him to maintain an average loss of less than 0.1%, with his largest loss being only 0.4%.
The Power of Automation: A Hands-Off Trading Strategy
What makes Xing’s journey unique is the completely hands-off nature of his trading. His EA runs 24/7, making decisions without any manual intervention. Xing has learned to trust his system entirely, understanding that human emotions often lead to mistakes. By allowing his EA to operate independently, he has removed the pitfalls of emotional trading.
The Strategy Behind the EA: Simple Yet Effective
Xing’s EA is based on straightforward strategies that can be found online, but the edge lies in his intensive backtesting and optimization. Using software like MetaTrader 5 (MT5), he conducts rigorous tests to refine his strategies and ensure they are effective in various market conditions. This combination of simplicity and thorough testing has been crucial to his success.
Advice for Aspiring Funded Traders
When asked for advice, Xing emphasizes the importance of mindset and patience. He encourages traders to understand the markets deeply through years of practice and to manage their expectations realistically. According to Xing, success in trading is a marathon, not a sprint.
Why City Traders Imperium Is a Perfect Fit
Xing has found the conditions and support at City Traders Imperium to be an excellent match for his swing trading style. He appreciates the flexibility and structure provided by the funding program, which allows him to focus on his long-term trading goals.
What Traders Can Learn From Xing’s Story
Xing Tao’s story is a testament to the power of automation and disciplined trading. By dedicating years to developing and refining his EA, he has created a system that works tirelessly while he enjoys the freedom it brings. His journey is an inspiration for traders looking to achieve consistent success with a hands-off approach.
