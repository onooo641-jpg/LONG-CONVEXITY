Introduction
Lighter is a decentralized trading platform designed for security, scale, and performance in a zero-fee trading environment for Standard accounts. It is the first exchange to offer verifiable order matching and liquidations while delivering performance comparable to traditional exchanges.
The Lighter platform is available at:
https://app.lighter.xyz/
