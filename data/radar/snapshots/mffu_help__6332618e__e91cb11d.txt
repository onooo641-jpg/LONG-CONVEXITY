Your 1099 Tax Documents
March 4, 2026
Traders making preparation for the upcoming tax season should expect the following as it pertains to receiving 1099’s.
​
​
Traders who successfully achieved payouts through the Rise payment platform will be sent their 1099 tax documents directly from Rise's tax processing department. These documents will be mailed to the address on file with your Rise account. For detailed information regarding the specific timeline for document delivery or processing status updates, please reach out directly to Rise customer support through their dedicated help channels.
Traders who successfully achieved payouts via Plaid/ACH direct bank transfer methods will have their complete 1099 tax documents sent via their registered MyFunded Futures mailing address by February 17th, 2026.
Please ensure you monitor your mailbox to avoid missing these important tax documents.
