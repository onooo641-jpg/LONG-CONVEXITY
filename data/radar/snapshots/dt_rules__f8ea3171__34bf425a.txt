All Collections General Glossary Glossary
Key DayTraders terms in plain English: account types, drawdown, DLL, QDays, consistency, statuses and more.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Key DayTraders terms, using the names you'll see across the Help Center.
Term
Meaning
Evaluation
A simulated account where you prove your strategy by reaching the profit target within the rules.
Pro Account
The simulated funded account you activate after passing an evaluation. It earns payouts.
S2F
Straight to Funded: a simulated funded account with no evaluation (Instant at checkout).
S2L
Straight to Live: a simulated evaluation with a set path to a live account (Live at checkout).
Live account
An account that trades in live markets, from S2L or by invitation from the firm.
Profit target
The profit you need to pass an evaluation, net of commissions.
Max drawdown / drawdown level
The most your account can lose. The drawdown level is the balance you must stay above; touching it fails the account.
Intraday trailing / EOD trailing / Static
How the drawdown level moves: with your peak balance in real time, only at the end of each trading day, or never.
Daily Loss Limit (DLL)
The most you can lose in one trading day, measured from that day's starting balance. Hitting it pauses the account.
Trading day
6:00 PM to 4:59 PM ET the next day.
Minimum daily profit
The smallest daily profit that counts toward Qualifying Days and the activity requirement.
Qualifying Day (QDay)
A trading day that meets the minimum daily profit, follows the rules and ends within consistency.
Consistency
Your best day's profit as a percentage of your total profit. Each plan sets a maximum.
Failed / Paused / Inactive
Failed: the drawdown was touched and the account is closed. Paused: the DLL was hit; trading resumes at 6:00 PM ET. Inactive: the activity requirement was missed and the account is closed.
Dashboard
Your member area on daytraders.com for accounts, billing, activation and payouts.
Payout Method
Where payouts go: your bank through Plane, or your DT Wallet.
DT Wallet
DayTraders credit from a payout, usable for one-time purchases. It can't be withdrawn as cash.
Rithmic / rTrader Pro
Rithmic provides your accounts' market data and order routing; rTrader Pro is its trading platform.
Mini / micro
Contract sizes. 10 micros count as 1 mini toward your max contracts.
Slippage
The difference between the price you expected and the price your order filled at.
