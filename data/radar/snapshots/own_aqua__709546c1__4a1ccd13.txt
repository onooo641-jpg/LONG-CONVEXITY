FAQ
What is AquaFunded?
AquaFunded is a proprietary trading firm offering CFD and Futures trading accounts. Traders can access simulated trading capital, demonstrate their trading skills and earn real cash rewards from eligible profits.
How does a prop firm account work?
Choose your account size and trading model. You can either complete an evaluation by reaching the profit target without breaking any rules, or start directly with an Instant Funding account. Once funded, you can earn real cash rewards from your trading profits, provided you meet the account's payout requirements.
Which markets can I trade?
AquaFunded offers CFD trading across forex, indices, metals, energies and cryptocurrencies, as well as futures trading across major CME Group markets. Available instruments depend on your chosen trading account and platform.
Which trading platforms do you offer?
AquaFunded supports MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader and TradeLocker for CFD trading. For futures, we offer Tradovate, NinjaTrader and DeepCharts. Platform availability depends on your account type.
How fast can I get funded?
With Instant Funding, you can access a funded account without completing an evaluation. For evaluation models, funding is available once you successfully meet the trading objectives and pass the required account review.
How fast are rewards paid?
AquaFunded processes rewards in approximately 4 hours on average. We also offer a 24 business hour payout guarantee, with an additional $1,000 if an eligible payout isn't processed within the guaranteed timeframe. Rewards are available through Rise and cryptocurrency.
How much does it cost?
Account pricing depends on your chosen trading model and account size. AquaFunded operates on a one-time purchase fee, with no monthly subscription. Optional upgrades may be available depending on the model.
Is AquaFunded legit?
Yes. AquaFunded has been operating since 2023, providing traders with simulated funded accounts and real cash rewards. Traders can review our published payouts, transparent trading rules and customer feedback before purchasing an account.
