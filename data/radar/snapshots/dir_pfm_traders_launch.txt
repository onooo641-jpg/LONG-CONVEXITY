Trading Rules
No stop-loss requirements.
News trading is allowed.
Consistency Rule (40%)
Premium EOD and Legacy NYC
the evaluation consistency rule is based on 40% of total profits, not the fixed profit target. Static: 50% evaluation rule (daily profit cap at 50% of the target). No consistency requirement once SimFunded.
Minimum Trading Days
No minimum on futures plans.
Inactivity Rules
During evaluation: missing a week deactivates your account. In your Simfunded account: missing a week pauses it — you remain eligible for profits and payouts but need to reactivate.
DCA/Martingale Rule
You can average into a position up to 5 executions maximum on the same symbol. Martingale-style sizing — where you increase position size specifically to recover losses — is prohibited.
Reasonable scaling and averaging are allowed. Repeatedly adding size to losing positions, especially as a routine part of most trades, counts as excessive martingale-style trading and may lead to an account review.
Hedging
Hedging is not permitted. This includes hedging the same instrument across multiple accounts, such as being long NQ in one account and short NQ in another; hedging through correlated instruments, such as being long NQ and short ES; and coordinating with another trader or group of traders to create offsetting positions across separate accounts. This rule applies whether the accounts belong to the same trader or to different traders acting together.

CME Price Limit
Holding or opening a position in an index future (ES, NQ, YM and their micros) within 2% of its CME price limit is prohibited.
