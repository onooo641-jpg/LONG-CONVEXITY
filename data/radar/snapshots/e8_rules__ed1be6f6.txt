Understand key differences between our flagship programs
Updated over 2 weeks ago
All of our currently offered products are Single-Phase challenge models, but they’re built for different trader types.
​
​ E8 Zero is the most flexible option, built for Futures traders who want minimal payout restrictions. The consistency rule is only in the challenge stage, which means that traders can enjoy no consistency rules, no daily profit cap, and no minimum profitable days requirements in the performance stage. The only limitations are payout caps and a maximum of 5 payouts per account, but it still offers the biggest freedom of any E8 program.
​
​ E8 One is designed for experienced traders comfortable with tighter Drawdowns and higher upside potential. There are no payout caps, but a Daily Drawdown (hard breach) and a Dynamic Drawdown apply. Available on Classic markets and perpetual futures.
​
​ E8 Signature is the more structured and cheaper version than the E8 one. It includes payout caps and a Daily Pause (soft breach) on SimFi Performance Accounts, giving traders better protection before a full breach occurs. Designed for Futures, it suits traders who focus on consistency and want more forgiving drawdowns.
​
​ E8 Pro is built for traders who prioritize daily payouts. With no payout caps and a static drawdown, it offers a predictable, structured environment where you always know exactly where your limits stand. Available on Classic Markets and Perpetual Futures.

At E8, we have focused on building 1-Phase challenges, with each product designed differently to suit different types of traders.
​
Maybe you like consistency and are happy paying a standard price for standard industry metrics — then E8 One is for you.
​
Maybe you would rather skip Best Day and consistency rules — then E8 Pro is the better fit.
​
Confused by buffer mechanics, or would like to finish consitency in challenge to have more freedom in performance? E8 Zero is built for you.
​
Prefer a static Drawdown over a Dynamic one? Choose E8 Pro .
Looking for a bigger drawdown and full customization? E8 One is the way to go.
We have designed it this way so everyone can find the product that fits them. Yes, our prices may be premium — but that is the trade-off for premium service, customization, and product variety.
