Privacy Policy
Welcome to Goat Funded Trader, a trade name of Wishes Tower International Limited (76428795) (the “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). We have created this privacy notice to explain our data collection and processing practices. By using our Services, you acknowledge and agree to our policies and procedures regarding personal information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at support@goatfundedtrader.com . If you do not agree with our policies, please refrain from using our Services.
What Information Do We Collect?
Personal information is gathered from you through voluntary means when you register on our Services, show interest in learning about us or our products/services, take part in activities on the Services, or contact us.
The personal information we collect is based on your interactions with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products/features you use. This can include:
Names
Phone numbers
Email addresses
Physical addresses
Billing addresses
Sensitive Information
We do not collect sensitive information such as social security numbers or other information not necessary to the operation of the Company’s business.
Payment Information
In case of any purchases, we may gather data necessary to process your payment, such as the payment instrument number and security code linked to your payment method. The storage of all payment information is handled by our payment processors. Their respective privacy notices can be accessed on their websites.
It is crucial that all personal information you provide us is truthful, comprehensive, and up to date. In the event of any changes to your personal information, it is your responsibility to notify us.
Information Automatically Collected
During your visit, usage, or navigation of the Services, certain information is automatically collected. This information, while not revealing your specific identity (e.g., name or contact details), may include:
Device and usage data such as IP address, browser and device specifications, operating system, language preferences, source URLs, device name, country, location, usage information of our Services, and other technical details.
This information is mainly utilized for ensuring the security and functioning of our Services, as well as for internal analysis and reporting purposes.
Like other businesses, we also gather information through cookies and similar technologies.
We Collect Information:
Log and Usage Data
This refers to information related to service, diagnostics, usage, and performance, which our servers automatically gather when you access or utilize our Services. This data is stored in log files and may include:
IP address
Device information
Browser type and settings
Information about your activities within the Services (such as timestamps, pages/files viewed, searches performed, and feature usage)
Device event information (such as system activity, error reports, and hardware configurations)
How Do We Process Your Information?
Your personal information is processed for several purposes, based on your interaction with our Services. These may include:
Facilitating account creation and authentication
Identifying fraudulent activity
Seeking feedback and communicating with you regarding your usage of our Services
Processing information to protect vital interests, such as preventing harm
What Legal Bases Do We Rely On To Process Your Information?
We process your personal data only when necessary and when we have a valid legal justification, such as:
With your consent
To comply with laws
To provide services
To fulfill contracts
To protect your rights
To serve our legitimate business interests, including protecting the Company against fraud
For Users in the EU or UK
Under GDPR and UK GDPR, we process your information based on:
Consent : You can revoke consent at any time by contacting support@goatfundedtrader.com .
Legitimate Interest : When necessary for our business interests and when they do not override your rights.
Legal Obligation : To comply with law enforcement, legal rights, or evidence in legal proceedings.
Vital Interests : To safeguard your vital interests or those of others.
For Users in Canada
We process your information with your express or implied consent. In exceptional cases, we may process it without consent if:
It is in an individual’s best interests and consent cannot be obtained promptly.
It is necessary for fraud investigation, legal obligations, or other legally permitted reasons.
When And With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?
Your personal information may be shared in the following cases:
Business Transfers : During a merger, sale of assets, financing, or acquisition.
Working with Vendors : To ensure adherence to our Terms & Conditions and prevent fraud.
Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?
Yes, we use cookies and tracking technologies (e.g., web beacons, pixels) to access or store information. Refer to our Cookie Notice for more details.
How Long Do We Keep Your Information?
We retain personal information as long as necessary or legally required. If deletion is not feasible, we securely store it and restrict access until it can be deleted.
How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?
We take technical and organizational measures to secure personal information. However, no online system is completely secure. Users must also take precautions to protect their data.
Do We Collect Information from Minors?
No, we do not intentionally collect information from individuals under 18. If we discover such information, we will delete it. Contact us at support@goatfundedtrader.com for concerns.
What Are Your Privacy Rights?
Depending on your location, you may have rights such as:
Accessing and copying your personal information.
Requesting corrections or deletion.
Limiting processing or requesting data portability.
Objecting to data processing.
How Can You Review, Update, Or Delete Your Data?
To review, update, or delete your data, email us at support@goatfundedtrader.com .
Do California Residents Have Specific Privacy Rights?
Yes, California residents have additional rights, including:
The right to request deletion of data.
The right to know what personal information is collected and shared.
The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.
To exercise these rights, contact us at support@goatfundedtrader.com .
Do We Make Updates to This Notice?
Yes, we may update this privacy notice. Updates will be indicated by a revised date and will take effect immediately upon posting.
How Can You Contact Us?
For questions, email us at support@goatfundedtrader.com .
