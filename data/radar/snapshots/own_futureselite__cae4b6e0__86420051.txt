Skills & Quests: XP and levels
How you earn XP and what each level pays.
October 13, 2026
What you do
XP
Spend on challenges
1 XP per $10
Close a green trading day
30 XP, once a day
Pass an evaluation
22 XP, then 8 for each extra pass that day
Reach a payout
300 XP, then 100 for each extra payout that day
Complete a quest
8 to 200 XP
Red day or breach
No XP
Going from level L to L+1 takes 120 + 25 × L XP: 145 XP for level 2, up to 36,505 XP in total for level 50.
Each level pays 1 skill point and credits worth 500 × the level. Level credits and level packs are paid once.
Levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 add a free pack or perk.
An active trader earns about 1,120 XP a month, reaching level 10 in roughly two months.
Trader Core ($99, one-time) adds 5% to every XP gain.
