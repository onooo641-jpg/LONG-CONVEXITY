CFD trading conditions: markets, commissions and leverage
CFD markets, commissions, spreads, leverage by account type and the stop-out level.
Applies to: CFD Updated 13 November 2026
Short answer
You can trade forex, indices, gold and commodities, and crypto. Commission is $5 per lot on FX and commodities, and $0 on indices and crypto. Leverage depends on your account type, and the stop-out level is 100%.

Stop-out level
The margin stop-out level is 100% . If your account falls below it, your least profitable open trade is closed automatically.
