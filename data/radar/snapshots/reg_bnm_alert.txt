What you need to know
The Financial Consumer Alert (FCA) List helps the public identify entities or schemes that may falsely claim to be licensed, authorised, or regulated by Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM).​
The list is updated based on reports received from the public and BNM’s assessments of those reports. Consumers can use the FCA List as one of the ways to check whether an entity or scheme has been identified as not being authorised by BNM to offer regulated financial products or services.​
However, if an entity or scheme is not on the FCA List, or has been removed from it, this does not mean that it is licensed or regulated by BNM. Consumers should always conduct their own checks and exercise caution before investing or participating in any financial scheme.​
Note:
Potential clone entity refers to entities that may impersonate legitimate organisations or entities, presenting themselves as authentic while attempting to replicate the identity, branding or operations of the legitimate organisation/entity including business name, logo and business registration details. These entities are flagged as 'potential clone entity' due to their resemblance to the legitimate entity and likelihood of misleading others.
