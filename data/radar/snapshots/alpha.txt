EOD drawdown

Frequently Asked Questions
How do futures trading challenges work at Alpha Futures?
Alpha Futures offers paid simulated trading evaluations, often called futures trading challenges. Choose Zero, Standard or Advanced, meet the profit target, and follow the plan's loss and consistency rules. Passing can qualify you for a simulated Qualified Account. Direct skips evaluation and starts on a Qualified Account. Performance fees require you to meet the applicable payout conditions.
Are Alpha Futures evaluations and Qualified Accounts simulated?
Yes. Alpha Futures evaluations and Qualified Accounts use simulated funds. Eligible Qualified Traders may request performance fees based on their simulated trading performance, subject to their plan's rules. Alpha Futures also offers a separate live trading program for selected traders following a performance review. Learn about the path to live trading .
Does Alpha Futures use EOD or intraday trailing drawdown?
Alpha Futures evaluations and Qualified Accounts use an end-of-day trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL). It follows the highest end-of-day account balance rather than intraday profit spikes and stops trailing at the starting balance. Equity can still breach the active limit during a session. See our EOD versus intraday drawdown guide .
Which Alpha Futures plans have no consistency rule?
Zero evaluations have no consistency rule. Standard evaluations use 50%, and Advanced evaluations use 40%. On Qualified Accounts, Zero and Standard use 40%, Direct uses 20%, and Advanced has no consistency rule. These requirements affect evaluation completion or payout eligibility, depending on the stage. Check the current consistency rules or use our consistency rule calculator .
How do Alpha Futures payouts and performance fees work?
Zero, Standard and Advanced Qualified Traders can request performance fees after accumulating five winning trading days of at least $200 each, up to four times per month. Direct uses withdrawal profit targets and a 20% consistency rule instead of winning days. These plans offer a 90% performance split, with plan-specific payout limits and eligibility requirements. Standard approved payouts are currently paid within five business days or less. Review the payout policy and latest payout update .
Which trading platforms does Alpha Futures support?
Alpha Futures supports AlphaTrader, TradingView through Plus500 connectivity, WealthCharts, Quantower and DeepChart. Each platform offers different charting, execution and analysis tools. Review the available trading platforms and their setup requirements before choosing your account.
Can I trade news at Alpha Futures?
There are no news trading restrictions on evaluation accounts or Advanced Qualified Accounts. On Zero, Standard and Direct Qualified Accounts, no orders may be executed within two minutes before or after relevant high-impact news events. Existing positions may be held through news under the published policy. Prohibited trading practices still apply across all accounts. Read the news trading policy .
How do I choose the best futures prop firm for my strategy?
Compare total evaluation costs, drawdown rules, consistency requirements, supported platforms and payout conditions. The best fit depends on your trading style, budget and location. Check whether rules change between evaluation and Qualified Accounts, and verify current terms before purchasing. Use our guide to compare futures prop firms .
