All Collections Trading Rules and Guidelines Automation, AI and Trade Copiers Automation, AI and Trade Copiers
You can use AI, scripts, bots, trade copiers and a VPS, as long as you don't trade in an HFT style or trade for others.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
You can use AI tools, automations, scripts, custom algorithms and trading bots, as long as they follow all account rules and don't operate as High-Frequency Trading (HFT).
What's allowed
AI for research, trade planning, journaling, alerts and decision support.
Scripts, bots and strategy automation that trade at normal execution speeds.
Trade copiers that copy trades across your own accounts, including accounts at other firms.
Running your platform or tools on a VPS.
What isn't allowed
Anything that places, modifies or cancels orders in milliseconds, spams orders or relies on latency.
Trading for other people, including copying trades into accounts that aren't yours.
Using a copier or automation to hedge or to exceed your max contracts.
Your responsibility
Every trade a tool places counts as your account activity, including any rule breaks, losses or drawdown breaches it causes. DayTraders doesn't set up, troubleshoot or review custom tools. Test a tool before using it on an active account, monitor it while it runs and stop it if it behaves unexpectedly.
