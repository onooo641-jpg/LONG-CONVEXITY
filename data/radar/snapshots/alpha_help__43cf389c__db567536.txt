How many accounts can I have?
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
July 27, 2026
Maximum Allocation for each account type is:
​
Zero Account: 5 Qualified Accounts
​
Direct Account: 5 Qualified Accounts
​
Standard Account: 5 Qualified Accounts (any standard accounts prior to July 27th, 2026 are limited to 3)
​
Advanced Account: 3 Qualified Accounts
​
Any user that has an active Advanced Qualified account will be limited to 3.
​
Only one username per individual is permitted, and one individual per household may use our services.
