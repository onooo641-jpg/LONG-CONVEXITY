What Is The CFD Intraday Reward Policy?
You can receive a reward of up to 80% of the notional profit in your Earning Account. The minimum reward is $100 per request.
When Can I Request A Reward?
Your first reward can be requested 14 days after you receive your Earning Account. After that, you can request a reward every 14 days, for any amount above $100. Requests are processed daily, Monday to Friday, within 24 hours on business days.
Trading Requirements Before A Reward
• All 2-Phase accounts and 1-Phase $2,500 to $50,000: at least 3 profitable trading days, each gaining at least 0.5% of the initial balance.
• 1-Phase $100,000 and $200,000: at least 3 trading days, and the 50% Consistency Rule must be met. For accounts created on or after 26 November 2026, it's calculated on your Total Profit, including losing days. See Consistency Rule in CFD Intraday Rules.
• No news trading in the Earning Phase.
Transfer Timeframes
• Crypto: within the day.
• International wire / SWIFT: 5–7 business days.
• SEPA transfer: 1–3 business days.
Can I Withdraw Anything I Make During The Challenge?
No. The Challenge is traded on a demo account with simulated funds, so there's nothing to withdraw. You can request rewards only after you pass the Challenge and move to the Earning Phase.
Can I Get A Refund After I Move To The Earning Phase?
Yes. Once you reach the Earning Phase, your Challenge fee is refunded after your 3rd approved payout. If you don't reach that payout milestone, no refund is issued.
Are There Any Reward Fees?
A 1% fee is deducted from your payout to cover transaction and gas fees. It's calculated on your final reward share amount, not on your total account profit.
In What Currency Can I Receive My Reward?
Accounts are supported in EUR, USD (for bank transfers) and cryptocurrency. You choose the method and currency when you submit your request. Currencies other than EUR or USD are converted at the exchange rate on the day of the transaction.
What Withdrawal Methods Are Available?
Rewards are paid by bank wire or cryptocurrency. For bank wires, you'll need documentation showing the bank name, branch, account number and account holder's name.
