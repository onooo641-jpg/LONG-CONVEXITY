Account Ownership and Registration Policies
The one account per user policy, prohibited registration actions, KYC identity verification, and the consequences of violations.
October 15, 2026
Table of contents
To maintain a fair, secure, and compliant trading environment for all of our traders, we enforce strict rules regarding account registration and ownership . Please review these policies carefully before creating your profile.
The single account policy
We operate under a strict one account per user policy. Every trader is permitted to have only one registered profile within our system. All of your activities, including purchasing evaluations, taking Evaluations, and managing funded accounts, must be conducted through this single, original profile.
Strictly prohibited actions
To ensure the integrity of our platform, several actions are strictly forbidden:
Prohibited action
Detail
Multiple profiles
You may not register for more than one user profile under any circumstances. Creating new accounts using different email addresses to bypass rules, restart an Evaluation, or mask your identity is prohibited
Registering under another identity
You must register using your own legal identity. You cannot create an account using the name of a friend, family member, or fictitious person
Third-party trading
You must be the sole individual executing trades on your account. Sharing your login credentials or allowing a third party to trade on your behalf is not allowed
Identity verification (KYC)
To enforce these policies, all traders must pass a Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification process before receiving any payouts. Your registered profile name must exactly match your government issued ID. If we discover that your profile details do not match your legal identity, your account will fail the verification process.
​
Submitting a counterfeit, forged, edited or altered document during verification is also a violation of these policies and leads to the same consequences listed below. You must also complete the verification on your own, without anyone else assisting you or being present. If we notice that you were assisted during the verification, the same consequences apply.
Consequences of policy violations
We actively monitor our platform for duplicate accounts and proxy trading. If our system or risk management team detects a violation of our registration rules, strict actions will be taken:
Consequence
Detail
Accounts breached
All profiles and trading accounts associated with the user will be instantly breached and deactivated
Profits forfeited
Any accumulated profits or pending payouts across all connected accounts will be voided and forfeited
Permanent ban
The user will be permanently banned from using our services in the future
Lost access to your email or need to update your details? Please do not create a new account. Instead, contact our support team directly for assistance in updating your existing profile.
