Discord Verified Roles
Discord Verified Roles
Link your Topstep account to Discord. Get the right role. Access the right channels.
Verified Traders get role-based channels, deeper conversations, and a safer community. Unverified members can view most channels but can't chat, engage, or access support and coaching.
How to Verify Your Discord Role
Sign into your Topstep Dashboard .
Go to Account Settings and select Link Discord .
Authorize access through the Discord role bot.
Sign into Discord and follow the prompts to complete verification.
Once verified, your role will be automatically assigned based on where you are in the program.
Watch the walkthrough video
FAQ
What happens if I don't verify?
Your roles will be removed and participation limited to Onboarding channels. You can still view most channels and announcements, but you won't be able to chat, engage, or receive support.
​ Why do I need to verify?
Verifying links your Topstep account to your Discord profile so your role updates automatically as you progress through the program. It ensures you have access to the right channels for your current stage.
​ Will other members see my role?
Yes. Roles are visible to the community by design — so members know who they're trading alongside.
​ My role is incorrect. What do I do?
Start by unlinking and re-syncing from your Trader Dashboard. That usually resolves it. If not, contact Trader Support.

​ Community Rules
Be respectful — no personal attacks, harassment, or toxic behavior.
No referral links or self-promotion — no affiliate links, codes, or personal channel promotion without mod approval.
No account selling or sharing — buying, selling, or sharing Topstep accounts violates our TOS.
Keep it on topic — posts must relate to Topstep, futures trading, or the trading journey.
No financial advice — share your experience, but don't tell others what to trade or guarantee outcomes.
No misinformation about Topstep — don't spread unverified claims about rules, Payouts, or policies. Link official sources when possible.
No spam or low-effort posts — contribute meaningfully.
Respect privacy — don't share others' personal info, account details, or private communications without consent.
FAQs
Who moderates the Topstep Reddit?
There are both Topstep team members and ambassadors moderating Reddit, just like on Discord.
Can I become a Topstep Reddit moderator?
When Topstep needs additional ambassadors for moderation, we reach out directly to traders that we have seen are active regularly and go above and beyond for others.
I got kicked out. How can I get back in?
Please contact Support with the username that you use in the community in which you were kicked out, and our team will look into why you got kicked out.
