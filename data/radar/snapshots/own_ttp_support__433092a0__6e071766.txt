How Do I Sign Up For The Affiliate Program?
Register on our affiliate sign-up page: affiliate registration . A member of our Affiliate Management Team will then contact you to go through the details. Once setup is complete, you’ll get access to your affiliate dashboard and can start earning.
How Do I Get My Affiliate Link?
Log in to your Affiliate Portal .
On your dashboard, find your unique link in the “Quick Link” section.
Copy it and share it with the people you refer to start earning.
If you have any questions, your Affiliate Manager or our support team can help.
How Do I Start Referring?
Share your affiliate link anywhere you promote — every click is tracked to you for 60 days, so any Challenge those visitors buy in that window counts toward your commission.
Do You Offer Marketing Resources?
Yes. Once you join, you get access to an extensive library of marketing materials, all with tracking links that attribute referrals to you:
Logos, animated and static banners
Landing pages and custom tracking links
Videos, webinars, articles and e-books
Reviews, plus reporting and analytics
What Are The Affiliate Rules?
Promoting The Trading Pit comes with a few clear expectations.
Do
Follow all guidelines and policies in the affiliate agreement.
Use only the marketing materials we provide or approve.
Represent our services accurately and never misleadingly.
Disclose your affiliate relationship in your promotions.
Keep our proprietary information confidential.
Don’t
Bid on our brand or ad words on Google or any paid search engine — this is a direct breach of our terms. Use misleading or deceptive marketing that could harm our reputation.
Spam or send unsolicited communications to promote us.
Use our trademarks, logos or other intellectual property without authorisation.
Engage in unfair competition or anything that breaks applicable laws
