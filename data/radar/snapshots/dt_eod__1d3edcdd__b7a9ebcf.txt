How live account scaling works: Risk Team review for extra contracts and Daily Loss Limit changes.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Once your live account's drawdown has locked at your starting balance, you can ask support for a Risk Team review.
Scaling can include increased contracts , a percentage-based Daily Loss Limit, or removing the Daily Loss Limit entirely.
We can also increase the maximum loss limits or issue additional live accounts to copy trade across.
Scaling isn't guaranteed; it depends on consistent and risk-managed trading.
