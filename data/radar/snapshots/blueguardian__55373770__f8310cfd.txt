CFD news trading rules
When CFD traders can and can't trade high-impact news, the 5-minute window, FOMC events and the full list of affected instruments.
Applies to: CFD Updated 18 November 2026
Short answer
News trading is allowed during evaluations. On funded accounts and Instant Standard, don't open or close trades on affected instruments from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after high-impact (red folder) news, or any FOMC event of any impact level (including low/yellow and medium/orange). Profit made in that window, or heavily driven by the news, will be removed, but your account won't be breached. Instant Starter allows news trading.

What the restriction means
Don't open or close trades on an affected instrument from 5 minutes before a high-impact news release until 5 minutes after it.
The same applies to FOMC speeches, statements, releases and news events of ANY impact level: high (red), medium (orange) and low (yellow) FOMC events are all restricted. The red-folder-only rule applies to other news, not to FOMC. Any trades involving FOMC events are subject to profit removal.
Profit made in the window, or significantly influenced by the news, will be removed. This isn't a rule violation, so your account won't be breached.
Trades that try to get around the rule are treated the same way.

Futures
Futures accounts have their own rules: news trading is allowed on evaluation and funded accounts, but not on the Live Account.
