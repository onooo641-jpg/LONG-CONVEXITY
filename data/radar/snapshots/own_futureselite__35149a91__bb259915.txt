Community cards and community codes
How the community leaderboard and profiles work, and how traders use a community code.
October 22, 2026
Table of contents
Every approved partner gets a community card and a public profile on our Affiliates site.
The community leaderboard
Communities are ranked by the total paid out to the traders they referred.
View all communities lists every partner community, with search, sorting, a platform filter and a tab for new communities.
Community payout figures show what the traders referred by each community have been paid. They aren't the affiliate's commission or the affiliate's own trading results.
What a community profile shows
Community payouts : the total paid to traders referred by that community, all-time, in USD.
Traders paid, payout transactions and the largest trader payout .
About the community and links to its channels.
The community code , with a button to copy it.
Giveaways : rewards for traders who buy with the community's code, when the community has one running.
For traders: using a community code
Open the community's profile on our Affiliates site and copy its code.
Use the code when you buy your account.
Check the profile's Giveaways tab to see if there's a reward for buying with the code.
For affiliates: your card
After approval, we set up your profile with you and collect your logo and photo.
Use Share profile or Copy profile link to send your profile to your audience.
To apply, see Becoming a FuturesElite affiliate .
