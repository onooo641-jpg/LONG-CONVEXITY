When can I get my first Payout?
You can request your first payout once you meet all eligibility requirements: 5 Profitable Days , the Consistency Rule , and you have enough profit to cover the Required Buffer + Minimum Withdrawal .
At Blueberry Futures , we believe traders should have the flexibility to access their profits without arbitrary waiting periods. Once you satisfy the trading conditions, you can request a withdrawal immediately.
Reward Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a payout, you must meet the following three conditions within your current trading cycle:
Complete the Minimum Trading Days
You must have at least 5 Profitable Days in the current cycle.
Remember: A "Profitable Day" is defined as a day with $200+ in net profit.
👉 [ What is the Minimum Trading Days rule? ]
Meet the Consistency Rule
Your trading must show stability. No single day can account for more than a specific percentage of your total profit.
Accelerated Accounts: Max 20% from a single day.
Ascent Accounts: Max 35% from a single day.
👉 [ What is the Consistency Rule? ]
Clear the Buffer Threshold
You must have enough total profit to leave the Required Buffer in the account after taking out your minimum withdrawal.
Example ($50k Account): You need at least $2,600 total profit ($2,100 Buffer + $500 Min Payout) to make a request.
👉 [ Do you have payout caps? ]
Frequency
As soon as a payout is processed, your cycle resets. You can qualify for your next payout as soon as you complete another 5 Profitable Days and meet the conditions again.
