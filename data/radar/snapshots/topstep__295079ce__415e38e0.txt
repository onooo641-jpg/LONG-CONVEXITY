FAQs
Who can use a referral code?
Anyone new to Topstep who hasn't purchased yet, on their first account purchase only. Codes are public, so you can share yours anywhere.
Is this the affiliate program?
No. Referral Codes are open to all Traders with a Trader Profile.
Can I see who used my code?
Not yet. The reward email is the only signal right now.
Where does my reward show up?
Email only. It's a discount code, not cash, and nothing is added to an account balance. Check spam and promotions if you don't see it.
More than 5 people used my code. Where are the rest of my rewards?
The cap is 5 reward codes in a month. Rewards past that aren't banked. Once a new month starts, you can earn 5 more reward codes if 5 more referrals make purchases using your code.
I signed up but didn't see the discount. Did I miss it?
No. The referral code applies to your first account purchase , not at sign-up. Enter it at checkout!
Affiliate Codes
What is Topstep’s affiliate program?
Topstep partners with a select group of creators, some of whom have an affiliate code to share with their community. When an eligible trader purchases with Topstep using an affiliate code, they receive the affiliate code discount
What is the affiliate code offer?
The standard offer is 20% off an eligible Topstep purchase (Trading Combine, Topstep Labs, or an Evaluation Challenge) when you check out with an affiliate’s code. During select promotional periods, the affiliate code discount may be higher than 20%. The discount percentage and dates for any promotion will be shown at checkout or in the promotion details, and the discount in effect at the time of your purchase applies. When a promotional period ends, the discount returns to the standard 20%.
Who is eligible to use an affiliate’s code?
You are eligible to use an affiliate code on an eligible purchase of a Topstep Trading Combine, Topstep Labs, or a Topstep Evaluation Challenge if you have never previously purchased with Topstep, or if your last purchase with Topstep was 6 or more months ago as of the day of purchase. You can only use one affiliate code, one time, ever.
I want to purchase more than one account. Does the code apply to all purchases?
No, affiliate codes are eligible to be used on one eligible purchase with Topstep. Affiliate codes do not apply to Resets, Back2Funded, market data add-ons, or anything you buy after that eligible purchase.
Can I stack an affiliate code with another promo or code?
No, you cannot stack with another promo code, discount, or a referral code. There is one exception: if you opt to add a DLL to a No Activation Fee Trading Combine during your eligible purchase with Topstep, you can still use an affiliate code to receive the affiliate code discount in addition to the Responsible Trading Advantage savings.

Go to your Billing Page on the Dashboard
​

Scroll to Subscriptions
Click the Edit icon next to the subscription you want to update
Select your preferred card and save
☝ Important: At least 1 card must remain on file while a Trading Combine is active. Removing all cards will close your account. Once you set a default card, new purchases will automatically be tied to that card. To update an existing subscription’s payment method, you must change your default card and update the individual subscription.
Payment Troubleshooting
Use a card in your name — must match your Topstep profile exactly. Using someone else’s card violates the Terms of Use.
Check your billing address — must match your bank’s records exactly, including formatting.
Delete and re-enter payment details manually — avoid autofill.
Wait at least 1 hour between retry attempts — rapid retries can trigger bank blocks.
Contact your bank — ask the Fraud department to check for holds, confirm online/international transactions are enabled, and allowlist Topstep.
Browser checks — clear cache and cookies, try a private/incognito window, disable VPNs and ad blockers, or try a different browser/device.

Chargebacks at Topstep
Have a question about a charge? Call us first. Always.
Most fees at Topstep are non-refundable, but we’re always happy to review your account and clear up confusion. Filing a chargeback before reaching out could limit your options and put your account at risk.
What’s the Difference Between a Chargeback and an Unjustified Chargeback?
A chargeback is when you dispute a charge with your bank and request a reversal.
An unjustified chargeback is filing a dispute without a valid reason — for example:
Forgetting to cancel a subscription
Misunderstanding a charge
Overlooking Topstep’s refund policies
Attempting to bypass the standard refund process
What Happens If You File a Chargeback?
Filing triggers an account review. Excessive or unjustified chargebacks may result in account suspension or permanent removal from the Topstep program.
Initiating chargebacks is listed as prohibited conduct .
Already filed one? You can still contact Trader Support to discuss your options.
What If You Want to Dispute a Charge?
Contact Trader Support first. We’ll review your situation and let you know what’s available under our refund policies .
