Lucid Trading is a funded firm that helps traders practice strategies and improve risk management. By offering access to capital to trade in live markets, Lucid Trading allows traders to grow while taking on less personal financial risk.
Our Mission
Lucid Trading was created to bridge the gap between modern funded firms and traditional proprietary trading desks. We believe that funded firms are an excellent entry point for new traders, but when a trader is ready for live markets, they deserve professional-level support.
Our mission is to offer a clear, step-by-step path for traders to move from evaluation to live trading, supported by experienced professionals and a structure modeled after prop trading desks.
What We Promise
We are committed to setting a higher standard in the funded trading industry. Here’s what every trader can expect from Lucid Trading:
All trading rules are clearly defined with no ambiguity.
Our customer support is responsive and dedicated to helping you succeed.
We are setting a new standard for what it means to support traders who are ready to go live with real capital.
The space is long overdue for some change. Firms need to get back to actually funding traders who are ready for the next phase of their journey. Traders can count on Lucid Trading.
