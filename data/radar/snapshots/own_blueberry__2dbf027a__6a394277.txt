How do I qualify for a live trading account?
To qualify for a live trading account at Blueberry Futures, you must:
Pass the Evaluation Challenges , and
Respect all trading rules at every stage, including profit targets and EOD/Trailing Drawdown limits, and
On a single funded account , achieve one of the following milestones:
Complete 7 successful payout cycles , or
Reach $28,000 in total withdrawals from that account.
Meeting one of these milestones makes you eligible for review , but promotion to a live account is not automatic and remains at the sole discretion of Blueberry Futures . Our team will assess your consistency, risk management, and overall trading behavior before upgrading you.
What does the live account look like?
When you are approved, your live funded amount equals the Max Drawdown of your original plan , with End-of-Day drawdown only :
$25,000 plan → $1,000 live account
$50,000 plan → $2,000 live account
$100,000 plan → $3,000 live account
$150,000 plan → $4,500 live account
Appropriate contract limits will be assigned to your live account, and all standard trading rules continue to apply. Ongoing compliance is required to retain live status; serious or repeated breaches may result in the live account being reduced, suspended, or closed.
