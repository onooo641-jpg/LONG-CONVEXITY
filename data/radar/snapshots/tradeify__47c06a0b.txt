Tradeify Evaluation Services Agreement

PREAMBLE
This Evaluation Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into by and between you, the “Trader,” and Tradeify Holdings, Corp. and its affiliates, successors, and/or assigns (collectively, the “Company,” “Tradeify,” “we,” or “us”). This Agreement governs your use of Tradeify’s evaluation programs, simulated funded accounts, and live funded accounts, together with allrelated services.
‍
1. TRADER APPOINTMENT
1.1 Independent Contractor Status : The Company appoints the Trader as an independent contractor to manage and execute electronic trade orders on the Company’s proprietary trading platforms. The Trader acknowledges and agrees that the Trader is not an employee or agent of the Company and shall not represent themselves as such.
1.2 Trading Equipment : The Company may provide the Trader with specific equipment, software, and related electronic tools to facilitate trading activities. The Trader agrees to use this equipment exclusively for trading on the Company’s platforms.
1.3 Approved Platforms : The Trader shall utilize only the trading platforms and tools explicitly sanctioned by the Company. Any deviation from this requires the Company’s prior written consent.
1.4 Surveillance and Compliance : The Company utilizes advanced surveillance systems to monitor all trading activities. Suspicious or non-compliant trading behaviors may result in immediate account restrictions or termination.
1.5 Account Funds : All funds in the trading account remain the sole property of the Company. The Trader acknowledges they have no ownership or claim over these funds, which are exclusively demo funds unless otherwise stated.
1.6 Documentation, Identity Verification, and Compliance : The Trader agrees to promptly provide any documentation or information requested by the Company, including, without limitation, tax documents, identity verification documents, proof of address, payment records, and trading records. The Trader must comply with all Know Your Customer (“KYC”), Know Your Business (“KYB”), and other verification requirements established by the Company.
To protect customer information and prevent unauthorized, fraudulent, or mistaken account closures, the Trader must successfully complete all KYC and identity verification requirements requested by the Company before any request to close or delete a Tradeify account may be processed. The Company will not process or complete an account closure or deletion request until the identity of the requesting account holder has been successfully verified.
1.7 Marketing Materials : Any promotional materials representing the Company or its services require the Company’s prior written approval.
2. COVENANTS AND WARRANTIES OF TRADER
2.1 Compliance : The Trader commits to familiarizing themselves with and strictly adhering to the Company’s trading guidelines, procedures, and other directives.
2.2 Accuracy of Information : The Trader guarantees that all personal and professional information provided to the Company is accurate and up-to-date. Any changes must be reported within seven (7) days.
2.3 No Legal Hindrances : The Trader confirms they have no legal or contractual restrictions that might hinder their performance under this Agreement.
2.4 Exclusive Use of Account : The Trader acknowledges that the managed trading account provided by the Company is solely for use on the Company’s platform.
2.5 Single Tradeify Account Per Individual : For purposes of this Agreement, a "Tradeify account" means the single customer profile and login through which a Trader accesses the Tradeify dashboard, purchases products, and requests payouts. It is distinct from, and does not refer to, (i) the broker platform accounts (such as Tradovate, Rithmic, or WealthCharts) connected to it, or (ii) the Evaluation and Simulated Funded Accounts held under it, of which a Trader may hold multiples subject to Section 4. Each individual may register for and hold only one (1) Tradeify account. A Trader may not maintain personal and business Tradeify accounts in parallel, nor register for, open, or control more than one Tradeify account, whether directly or indirectly, unless expressly authorized in writing by the Company. A Trader may register their single Tradeify account using business information, or trade under their personal account and complete Know Your Business ("KYB") verification when requesting payouts. This restriction applies on a per-individual and per-household basis. Violation may result in suspension or termination of all associated accounts and forfeiture of any funds or profits.
2.6 Proprietary Data : All trading data and related information generated on the Company’s platform are proprietary. Unauthorized reproduction, distribution, or use of this data is prohibited.
3. NON-SOLICITATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY
3.1 Non-Interference : The Trader agrees not to disrupt the Company’s business operations, including its contractual relationships and business strategies.
3.2 Non-Solicitation : During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years post-termination, the Trader shall not solicit or entice any of the Company’s employees, contractors, or clients.
3.3 Confidentiality : The Trader shall maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information about the Company, its operations, and clients.
4. EVALUATION  PROGRAMS, FUNDED  ACCOUNTS, AND PAYOUTS
4.1 Evaluation Paths and Account Rules : The Company offers a variety of evaluation paths and simulated funded account programs (including, without limitation, Advanced Challenge, Growth Challenge, Lightning Funded, Select Flex, and Select Daily accounts). The specific rules, parameters, fees, drawdown methodology, profit targets, position sizes, consistency rules, payout thresholds, payout caps, and minimum balance requirements applicable to each evaluation path and funded account program are maintained on, and may be updated from time to time at, the Company’s Help Center.
For complete and current details on all evaluation paths and funded account rules, the Trader shall refer to: https://help.tradeify.co/en . The Trader agrees that the rules published at the Help Center are incorporated into this Agreement by reference and are binding upon the Trader. The Company reserves the right to modify these rules at any time in accordance with Section 16 (Amendments).
4.2 Maximum Number of Simulated Funded Accounts: The Company limits the maximum number of Simulated Funded Accounts that may be held by any single individual to five (5). This limit applies on a per-individual and per-household basis and across ALL funded account types, including Advanced Challenge funded accounts, Growth Challenge funded accounts, Lightning Funded accounts, Select Flex funded accounts, and Select Daily funded accounts.
Example: A trader may NOT hold three (3) Lightning Funded accounts, two (2) Select Flex accounts, and one (1) Growth funded account. The maximum total is five (5) funded accounts in any combination.
Tradeify Elite Live Restrictions.
Traders who are transitioned to Tradeify Elite Live (Section 5) may NOT hold ANY other funded accounts, challenge accounts, or straight-to-sim accounts while their Elite Live account is active.
No other member of a Trader’s household may hold any Tradeify accounts (Challenge, Funded, or Elite Live) while the Trader’s Elite Live account is active.
4.3 Compensation Structure (General): Profit splits, payout schedules, payout caps, and related conditions for each account type are set forth in the rules published at the Help Center referenced in Section 4.1. Unless otherwise specified for a particular account type, profits withdrawn are split90% to the Trader and 10% to the Company. Any amounts not withdrawn remain at risk of loss from the Trader’s activity. The Trader will be paid as a 10-99 independent contractor, not as an employee.
4.4 Payout Processing: Once a payout request is approved, funds are withdrawn from the Trader’s account and payment is typically issued within twenty-four to forty-eight (24-48) hours. Payout requests submitted outside of normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EST/EDT, excluding U.S. Federal holidays) may take up to seventy-two (72) hours to process. The Company reserves the right to take up to the end of the next payout cycle to approve, review, or deny a payout request.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company may suspend the processing of any payout request, and may withhold payment of any payout that has been approved but not yet paid, pending completion of a review or investigation into suspected non-compliance with Section 6 (Prohibited Trading Activities) or with the rules published at the Help Center. The time limits stated in this Section 4.4 do not run during any such suspension. The Company will notify the Trader that a payout has been suspended and will complete its review within a commercially reasonable period. Payouts withheld under this paragraph are subject to forfeiture, and to adjustment following any re-statement of the account, in accordance with Section 6.7(h), Section 6.11(e), or Section 7.6, as applicable.
4.5 Payout Policy Selection (Select Accounts): Where an evaluation path requires the Trader to choose between payout policies (for example, between Select Flex and Select Daily upon passing a Select Challenge), that choice is permanent and irrevocable for the specific funded account in question. No refunds or exchanges will be provided if the Trader later prefers a differentpayout policy. Traders who pass multiple Select Challenges may select different payout policies for each funded account, and each account’s policy selection is independent.
5. MOVE TO LIVE (TRADEIFY ELITE LIVE)
The Company does not hold a broker-dealer license and does not provide brokerage services; all Tradeify Elite Live accounts are issued through and facilitated by NinjaTrader and GFF Brokers, third-party regulated brokerages.
5.1 Discretion to Move Traders to Live: Tradeify reserves the right, in its sole and absolute discretion, to move a Trader from a Simulated Funded Account to a Tradeify Elite Live account at any point in the Trader’s funded account journey, including at any time before, at, or after any particular payout milestone. The decision to transition a Trader to Live is solely Tradeify’s, andTradeify may also elect to keep the Trader on a Simulated Funded Account at its discretion.
5.2 Internal Policy Guidelines: As an internal policy guideline (and not as a binding entitlement to the Trader), Tradeify generally evaluates Traders for transition to Tradeify Elite Live who have achieved either:
ten (10) approved payouts since the Trader’s last live account or, where applicable, since services with Tradeify commenced; or
three (3) approved payouts on a single Simulated Funded Account.
These thresholds are internal benchmarks used by Tradeify’s risk management team and do not create any right or obligation to be transitioned to Live. Tradeify may, in its sole discretion, transition a Trader to Live earlier or later than these thresholds, or not at all.
5.3 Live Account Rules: The specific parameters of Tradeify Elite Live accounts (including, without limitation, starting balance allocation, availability requirements, profit splits, dynamic daily loss limits, scaling rules, and payout mechanics) are maintained at the Company’s Help Center referenced in Section 4.1 and are incorporated by reference. Tradeify Elite Live accounts aresubject to dynamic risk parameters that adjust based on the Trader’s performance.
5.4 Restrictions on Other Accounts: A Trader who has been transitioned to a Tradeify Elite Live account is prohibited from opening or holding new Challenge, Straight-to-Sim, or any other Tradeify funded accounts while the Live Funded Account remains active. No other member of the Trader’s household may hold any Tradeify accounts during this period.
5.5 Live Account Failure: In the event of failure of a Tradeify Elite Live account, Tradeify’s risk management team will contact the Trader to determine next steps.
5.6 Restricted Jurisdictions: If a Trader resides in a jurisdiction that renders the Trader ineligible for a Tradeify Elite Live account, Tradeify’s risk management team will contact the Trader to discuss alternative options. Such Traders may remain on Simulated Funded status for the duration of their relationship with Tradeify, subject to Section 16 (Geographic Restrictions).
5.7 Transition Process: Upon notification of a Live Funded Account transition, the Trader will receive an official email from Tradeify. Trading activity must cease until further instructions are provided. The account balance at the time of transition will be used in determining the starting balance of the Live Funded Account, in accordance with the rules published at the Help Center.
6. PROHIBITED TRADING ACTIVITIES
6.1 Exploitation of Errors: The core of Tradeify’s guidelines is a mutual understanding between Tradeify and the Trader that the Trader acts in good faith. Traders are prohibited from using strategies that seek to exploit or create errors within our platform. This includes taking advantage of discrepancies in price displays, delays in updates, or any other technical errors in our services. Such actions are considered unethical and can lead to serious consequences, including termination of the Trader’s trading account. Trading in Expired Contracts, and execution at a Non-Market Price, are separately and independently addressed in Section 6.11, without regard to intent.
6.2 Disruptive Practices: The Trader is forbidden from using strategies that incorporate disruptive practices.
6.3 External or Slow Data Feed: Trading based on an external or delayed data feed is not allowed.
6.4 Manipulative Trades: The Trader must not engage in trades with the intent to manipulate, abuse, or gain an unfair advantage on the Company’s platform. This includes short-term or high-frequency trades. Opposing Positions are separately and independently prohibited under Section 6.7, without regard to intent.
6.5 Consistency in Trading: The Trader must adhere to a consistent trading plan in terms of size, stops, and targets, avoiding maximum or larger contract sizes on single trades, refraining from flipping contracts just to register a “trading day,” maintaining consistent trading sizes, avoiding dollar-cost averaging and high-frequency trading, and demonstrating consistency in the trading plan.
6.6 Automated Bots: The use of automated trading bots or algorithms (“bots/algos”) is permitted only under the following conditions:
Ownership: The Trader must be able to prove that the Trader is the sole owner of the bot or strategy and that no one else has access to or is using it. This ensures that the bot/algorithm is not being shared with other traders or firms.
Exclusive Use: While the Trader may use the bot on the Trader’s personal accounts, using it across multiple firms is against Tradeify’s policy. The bot must be solely for the Trader’s own use within Tradeify.
No High-Frequency Trading (HFT) Bots: Personal bots are allowed as long as they are not high-frequency trading (HFT) bots. Tradeify has specific risk measures in place to detect and monitor such activities.
Compliance & Verification: Tradeify reserves the right to request information or documentation if our risk measures flag the Trader’s account for potential violations of these guidelines.
6.7 Collaborative Trading and Hedging
(a) Definitions. For purposes of this Section 6.7:
"Trader-Controlled Accounts" means all Tradeify accounts of any type (including, without limitation, evaluation accounts, Simulated Funded Accounts, and Tradeify Elite Live accounts) that are registered to the Trader, that were purchased using the Trader's payment method, or that the Trader directly or indirectly owns, controls, trades, funds, or directs, whether alone or together with any other person.
"External Accounts" means any evaluation, simulated, funded, or live account held with any proprietary trading firm, broker, or funding program other than Tradeify that the Trader directly or indirectly owns, controls, trades, funds, or directs.
"Product Group" means a group of futures products designated as such by the Company and published at the Help Center referenced in Section 4.1, which the Trader agrees is incorporated into this Agreement by reference for the purposes of this Section 6.7. Product Group membership is assigned by the Company for the purposes of this Agreement only, and does not constitute, and shall not be construed as, any representation or assertion by the Company as to the statistical, economic, or market correlation of any product with any other product. The Company publishes Product Groups including, by way of example and without limitation, Equity Index, Energy, Metals, Currencies, Interest Rates, Grains, Livestock, and Volatility. The Product Group list in effect at the time the relevant positions were held shall govern.
"Opposing Positions" means one or more long positions and one or more short positions held at the same time, whether in a single account, across two or more Trader-Controlled Accounts, or across one or more Trader-Controlled Accounts and one or more External Accounts, and irrespective of contract size, contract month, quantity, notional value, order type, holding period, or whether the positions were entered simultaneously or at different times.
(b) Independent Trading Requirement. The Trader shall execute the Trader's trading strategies independently. Team trading, collaborative trading, group trading, the management of a Trader's account by or on behalf of any third party, and the copying or mirroring of trades between the Trader and any other person are strictly prohibited. The Trader is responsible for the Trader's own trades, and all trading decisions must reflect the Trader's individual strategy and discretion. For the avoidance of doubt, this Section 6.7(b) restricts trading conduct between the Trader and other persons; the copying or mirroring of the Trader's own trades across the Trader's own Trader-Controlled Accounts is permitted to the extent expressly allowed by the rules published at the Help Center referenced in Section 4.1, provided always that such activity does not result in Opposing Positions prohibited by Section 6.7(c).
(c) Hedging Prohibited. The Trader is strictly prohibited from holding Opposing Positions in (i) the same futures product, or (ii) two or more futures products assigned to the same Product Group. Conduct described in this Section 6.7(c) constitutes hedging and is a breach of this Agreement regardless of the Trader's intent, purpose, strategy, or state of mind, and regardless of whether the positions in fact offset risk, resulted in a profit or a loss, were held in one account or in different Trader-Controlled Accounts, were held in different contract sizes (including any combination of full-size, mini, and micro contracts), or were held in different contract months. No showing of intent to hedge, to manipulate, to abuse, or to gain an unfair advantage is required to establish a breach of this Section 6.7(c). This Section 6.7(c) applies equally where one or more of the Opposing Positions is held in an External Account. Holding a position in a Trader-Controlled Account opposite a position in an External Account constitutes hedging and is a breach of this Agreement.
(d) Contract Size and Contract Type. The Trader may hold positions in full-size, mini, and micro contracts, and may hold positions in more than one contract size or contract type at the same time, provided that all such positions are in the same direction and do not constitute Opposing Positions prohibited by Section 6.7(c). For the avoidance of doubt, a long position and a short position that together constitute Opposing Positions are prohibited without regard to the contract sizes involved, and a smaller opposing leg (for example, a micro contract held against a mini or full-size position in the same product or in a product assigned to the same Product Group) is a breach of Section 6.7(c) in the same manner and to the same extent as an equally sized opposing leg.
(e) Application. This Section 6.7 applies at all times and to every account type, including without limitation evaluation accounts, Simulated Funded Accounts, and Tradeify Elite Live accounts; applies both during and after any evaluation; and applies both before and after any payout has been requested, approved, or paid. References elsewhere in this Agreement to an evaluation, an evaluation period, or eligibility for funding do not limit the application of this Section 6.7 to any account type or stage.
(f) Purpose. These restrictions are in place to ensure consistency, accurate performance evaluation, and adherence to risk management protocols. This Section 6.7(f) is descriptive only and does not limit the operation of Sections 6.7(b) through 6.7(d).
(g) Monitoring. The Company operates automated and manual monitoring across all Trader-Controlled Accounts on a continuous basis and for all account types. The Company's own trading and account records are the evidentiary basis for any determination under this Section 6.7. The Company reserves the right to request additional information or documentation if an account is flagged. The Company may request statements or trading records for any External Account. Where the Company notifies the Trader that positions may constitute a breach, the Trader shall flatten the relevant positions promptly.
(h) Non-Compliance. Any breach of this Section 6.7 will result in every account involved in the breach being placed in violation status, and in forfeiture of any profits generated during the period of the violation, whether or not those profits have been requested for payout. The Company may additionally terminate any or all of the Trader's accounts and permanently ban the Trader from all Tradeify services. These consequences apply equally to evaluation accounts, Simulated Funded Accounts, and Tradeify Elite Live accounts.
(i) Cross-Platform Hedging. For purposes of this Section 6.7(i), a "Cross-Platform Account" means any evaluation, funded, simulated, or live account the Trader holds, directly or indirectly, with any proprietary trading firm, prop-trading platform, or funded-trader program other than the Company. The Trader is strictly prohibited from holding Opposing Positions between a Trader-Controlled Account and a Cross-Platform Account, on the same terms, and subject to the same absence of an intent requirement, as apply to Opposing Positions among Trader-Controlled Accounts under Section 6.7(c). The Company may rely on information provided to it by NinjaTrader, acting in this context as an authorized third-party trade-monitoring service (and not in its capacity as a Tradeify Elite Live brokerage facilitator under Section 5), as evidence of Opposing Positions held on a Cross-Platform Account, in addition to the Company's own trading and account records referenced in Section 6.7(g). This Section 6.7(i) is in addition to, and does not limit, Section 6.7(c).
6.8 Microscalping: Microscalping refers to executing trades with the intent of capturing small profits from minimal price movements, such as a few ticks or points typically within a very short period of time. Trades executed in less than ten (10) seconds are difficult to reliably replicate or copy, so we have implemented the following guideline: 50% or more of the Trader’s account profit must come from trades held longer than ten (10) seconds.
6.9 Maximum Account Idle Time: To maintain an active account status, the Trader is required to execute at least one trade per calendar week. Failure to comply with this requirement may result in the Trader’s account being classified as inactive.
6.10 Audit Clause: The Company reserves the right to audit and review the trading activities and styles of all Traders to ensure compliance with the Company’s rules and guidelines. This includes, but is not limited to, monitoring for adherence to prohibited trading practices as outlined in the Company’s policies. The audit may be conducted at any time, without prior notice, and Traders are required to fully cooperate with any requests for information or documentation. Failure to comply with the Company’s rules or audit process may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of trading privileges.
6.11 Contract Selection and Simulated Market Data
(a) Definitions. For purposes of this Section 6.11:
"Active Contract" means, for any futures product, the contract month that is the most actively traded at the relevant time as measured by volume reported by the listing exchange. For products listed by CME Group, the Active Contract is the contract identified in the "GLOBEX CODE" column of the applicable CME Group contract calendar. The Company may additionally designate the Active Contract for any product and publish that designation at the Help Center referenced in Section 4.1, which the Trader agrees is incorporated into this Agreement by reference for the purposes of this Section 6.11. The Active Contract in effect at the time the relevant order was placed or filled shall govern.
"Expired Contract" means any contract month at or after its last trading day, expiration date, or final settlement date as established by the listing exchange, irrespective of whether the Company's platforms, any third-party platform, or any market data provider continues to display, quote, chart, or accept orders in that contract month.
"Non-Active Contract" means any listed contract month for a product other than the Active Contract for that product, including without limitation deferred, back-month, and future-dated contract months.
"Non-Market Price" means a price that does not reflect the prevailing price of the Active Contract for the same product at the same time, including without limitation any price arising from market data that is stale, frozen, delayed, interpolated, simulated, erroneous, or absent, from a platform or data outage, or from the absence of genuine liquidity in the contract traded.
(b) Requirement to Trade the Active Contract. The Trader shall use best efforts to trade only the Active Contract for each product at all times, and shall roll positions into the succeeding Active Contract in line with the listing exchange's contract calendar and prevailing market volume. Where the Trader has a genuine trading reason to hold a position in a Non-Active Contract, the Trader remains subject in full to Sections 6.11(c) and 6.11(d).
(c) Expired Contracts Prohibited. The Trader is strictly prohibited from placing an order in, entering, holding, adding to, or exiting any position in an Expired Contract. Conduct described in this Section 6.11(c) is a breach of this Agreement regardless of the Trader's intent, purpose, strategy, or state of mind, and regardless of whether the conduct resulted in a profit or a loss. No showing of intent to exploit, abuse, manipulate, or gain an unfair advantage is required to establish a breach of this Section 6.11(c). For the avoidance of doubt, the fact that an Expired Contract remained visible, quoted, charted, selectable, or tradeable on any platform made available by the Company, and the fact that an order in an Expired Contract was accepted or filled, do not constitute permission and are not a defence to a breach of this Section 6.11(c).
(d) Exploitation of Non-Active Contracts and Simulated Market Data. The Trader shall not trade, or seek to trade, any Non-Active Contract for the purpose of obtaining, or in a manner that obtains, execution at a Non-Market Price. Without limiting Sections 6.1 or 6.3, this includes trading illiquid, deferred, or future-dated contract months in order to take advantage of pricing that is stale, frozen, delayed, or erroneous in the simulated environment, and includes trading during or immediately following any platform, data feed, or exchange disruption for the purpose of obtaining a Non-Market Price. Any profit obtained at a Non-Market Price, whether under this Section 6.11(d) or otherwise, is subject to Section 6.11(e) whether or not the Trader's intent is established.
(e) Consequences of Non-Compliance. Where the Trader has traded in breach of Section 6.11(c) or 6.11(d), or has obtained execution at a Non-Market Price, the Company may, at its sole discretion, take any or all of the following steps:
(i) void, cancel, or adjust the affected orders, fills, and positions, and re-state the affected account's balance, profit and loss, drawdown, high-water mark, trading days, and any related account metrics as if the affected trades had been executed at the prevailing price of the Active Contract at the relevant time, or as if the affected trades had not occurred. Where re-statement under this paragraph causes an account to have exceeded a loss limit, drawdown threshold, or other account parameter, that breach is treated as having occurred at the time of the affected trade;
(ii) place any account involved in the breach in violation status, and forfeit any profits generated during the period of the violation, whether or not those profits have been requested for payout; and
(iii) terminate any or all of the Trader's accounts and permanently ban the Trader from all Tradeify services.
The Company's ordinary response to an isolated occurrence that does not form part of a pattern of conduct is to act under paragraph (i), together with paragraph (ii) where profits were obtained at a Non-Market Price, and to notify the Trader of the correct contract to trade. Termination and a permanent ban under paragraph (iii) are reserved for conduct that is repeated, or that otherwise indicates a deliberate pattern of exploiting Expired Contracts, Non-Active Contracts, or Non-Market Prices. Nothing in this paragraph limits the Company's discretion under this Section 6.11(e), and the Company's election to take a lesser step on any occasion does not waive its right to take that or any other step on any subsequent occasion. These consequences apply equally to evaluation accounts, Simulated Funded Accounts, and Tradeify Elite Live accounts.
(f) Application. This Section 6.11 applies at all times and to every account type, including without limitation evaluation accounts, Simulated Funded Accounts, and Tradeify Elite Live accounts; applies both during and after any evaluation; and applies both before and after any payout has been requested, approved, or paid.
(g) Monitoring. The Company operates automated and manual monitoring of contract selection and execution prices across all accounts. The Company's own trading, order, and market data records are the evidentiary basis for any determination under this Section 6.11. The Company reserves the right to request additional information or documentation if an account is flagged. Where the Company notifies the Trader that a position may constitute a breach of this Section 6.11, the Trader shall flatten the relevant position promptly.
(h) Timing of Enforcement. Determinations under this Section 6.11 are made on review of the Company's records and are not made in real time. The Company may exercise any right under Section 6.11(e) at any time after the relevant conduct, including in respect of conduct occurring in one or more earlier calendar months, in an earlier evaluation, or in an account that has since passed an evaluation, been funded, or received a payout. Any delay by the Company in identifying conduct or in exercising a right under this Section 6.11 does not waive that right, and continued trading, the passing or approval of an evaluation, or the making of a payout in the intervening period does not cure, ratify, or excuse the underlying breach.
