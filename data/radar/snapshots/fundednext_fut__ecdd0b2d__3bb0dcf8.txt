How much is the Reward Share in FundedNext?
September 9, 2026
In Express FundedNext Accounts, traders receive a 60% Reward Share on their first withdrawal. Reward Share increases with subsequent withdrawals to 75% and then 90%.
​
For Evaluation, Stellar Lite, Stellar 1-Step, and Stellar 2-Step FundedNext Accounts, traders initially receive an 80% Reward Share. With Scale-Up , this increases to 90%.
Please refer to the image below for a detailed breakdown of Reward Shares for different models.
For Stellar 2-Step and Stellar Lite accounts, the reward share depends on the First Withdrawal option selected at checkout:
Standard (21 Days): 80%
3 Days Performance Reward: 60%
On-Demand Performance Reward: 90%
Traders who purchase the Lifetime Reward 95% Add-On will receive 95% under any of the three options.
Please refer to the image below for a detailed breakdown of Reward Shares for different models.
💡Note:
Traders can increase their Reward Share up to 95% with add-ons. To learn more, click here .
​
For Challenge Accounts Purchased Before January 12, 2026
Traders with Challenge Accounts purchased before January 12, 2026 will continue to follow the previous structure, where traders will initially receive a 90% performance reward for the Stellar 1-Step FundedNext Account.
​
For Challenge Accounts Purchased On or After January 12, 2026
If you purchase a new Challenge Account or reset your existing Challenge Account on or after January 12, 2026, the new structure will apply which is traders initially receive an 80% performance reward for the Stellar 1-Step FundedNext Account.
Please note that, effective March 18th, 2025, we no longer offer the Express and Evaluation models to our new clients. Existing clients currently using this account plan will continue to have access as usual. For more details, please click here .
