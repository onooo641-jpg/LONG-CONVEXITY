Do you charge activation fees?
Activation Fees
September 25, 2026
No! Blueberry Futures does not charge any activation fees.
​
When you pass your evaluation, there's no separate charge to "activate," "turn on," or upgrade your account. Nothing stands between passing and getting access to your funded account.
​
What you pay to get started
The challenge/evaluation subscription shown on our pricing page — that's what gets you started.
No activation fee to move from a passed evaluation to your funded account.
No fee to upgrade or "turn on" your account once you qualify.
​
What is an activation fee — and why does this matter?
​
Some prop firms split their pricing in two: what you pay to attempt the evaluation, and a second activation fee charged once you pass, before you can trade the funded account. That structure means the headline price can understate the real cost of getting funded. Blueberry Futures has no such charge, the price on our pricing page is what you pay to get started.
