The Road to Live
At Blueberry Futures , our ultimate goal is to find disciplined traders and put them directly into the live market. While you start in a Performance Account (Simulated Funded), your journey is designed to lead you to the freedom of a Live Account .
The Milestone: 7 Payout Cycles
We use a specific milestone to determine when you are ready for live capital.
The Rule: You become eligible for review after successfully completing 7 Payout Cycles on your Performance Account.
The Review: Once you reach this milestone, our Risk Management Team automatically reviews your trading history to determine if you are ready for the Live Market.
Review Outcomes
Once reviewed, one of two things will happen:
1. Promotion Approved (Graduation)
You will be moved to a Live Account .
Transitioning is mandatory . Once approved, you cannot refuse the upgrade.
2. Promotion Delayed (Continue Trading)
If our team decides you aren't quite ready for live capital yet, you will continue trading your current Simulated Funded Account.
You remain eligible for the next review cycle and can continue earning performance rewards in the meantime.
Rules for Live Accounts
Trading a Live Account is a privilege that requires exclusive focus. Please understand the following rules regarding your other accounts:
One Live Account Policy: You can maintain only ONE Live Account at a time.
Closure of Sim Accounts: Upon promotion to Live, ALL your other active Simulated Funded accounts will be closed .
New Evaluations (Standby Rule): You are allowed to purchase and trade new Evaluation/Challenge accounts while trading Live.
However, if you pass a new challenge, that new Funded Account will be placed on Standby .
It cannot be activated until your Live Account is breached or closed.
Can I request a transfer early?
No. You cannot request to transfer to the Live Program manually. The review is triggered automatically by your performance milestones. Exceptionally consistent performance may lead to an early promotion at our discretion, but this is rare.
