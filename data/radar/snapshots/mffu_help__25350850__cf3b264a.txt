At MyFundedFutures, we believe that a trader's career hinges on their ability to progress without compromise. Our goal is to support this progression from simulated funding stages to live funding stages, ensuring every trader has the opportunity to succeed.
Introducing the new Pro Plan at MyFundedFutures!
Effective November 23rd, 2026:
A trader can hold one active Sim Funded Pro account at each size: 50K, 100K and 150K. All three can be active at the same time.
Example: a trader with an active Sim Funded Pro 50K can still have a Pro 100K or Pro 150K moved to Sim Funded.
When a Sim Funded account is closed or paid out, the trader's next Pro account of that size can move to Sim Funded.
Traders who already hold more than one Sim Funded Pro account at a size keep those accounts, and none are closed or changed. They cannot get another account at that size until they have no active Sim Funded Pro account at that size.

Live Account Transition
Our Pro Plan is designed to reward traders for consistent performance. The parameters for transitioning to a live account are dynamic and aim to provide greater payouts.
A trader is reviewed for a transition to a live account as a result of any of the below:
Three (3) consecutive payouts on the same Pro account.
Reaching the $100,000 payout cap. Profits above $100,000 are forfeited.
An invitation from the risk managements team. This invitation can happen at any time.
Reaching $20,000 in profit. This triggers a review only. It does not guarantee a transition to a live account.

The Reserve
The Reserve is Sim Funded profit that MyFundedFutures holds while the trader is on a live account. It ends in one of two ways.
Up to $5,000 is placed in the Reserve Program and any remaining amount in excess of the Sim Funded Cap is forfeited.

If the trader moved more than one Pro account to Live, the Reserves pay out as one bonus. The payout threshold goes up with the number of accounts moved.
2. Live Account Hits the Max Loss Limit
The Reserve pays off the negative Live balance.
Any Reserve left over goes back to the Dormant Sim Funded account. The trader can only withdraw from it. The trader cannot trade it.
Profit above the buffer is paid at 80/20.
The buffer is paid based on how long the Sim Funded account was traded:
Fewer than 30 trading days: 50% of the buffer.
30 trading days or more: 80% of the buffer.
After that withdrawal, the Sim Funded account closes.
A 21-day cooldown starts.
Example (Pro 50K): The Reserve holds $5,000. The Live account closes at -$1,500.
$1,500 comes out of the Reserve to cover the Live loss.
$3,500 goes back to the Dormant Sim Funded account.
The first $2,100 of that is the buffer. The account was traded for fewer than 30 days, so the trader receives 50%: $1,050.
The remaining $1,400 is above the buffer. The trader receives 80%: $1,120.
Live Post-Breach Cooldown
For 21 calendar days after the Live account closes:
The trader cannot trade any Sim Funded account.
The trader cannot buy new evaluations, resets or accounts.
After 21 days, the restrictions end. Dormant accounts are turned back on.
Existing Pro Accounts
Accounts on the old Pro products keep every old rule.
Old Live accounts keep a Static drawdown and the old unlock (20 winning days at 4% of start + 3 payouts).
Old Pro Sim Funded accounts do not go into Reserve when the trader moves to Live.
