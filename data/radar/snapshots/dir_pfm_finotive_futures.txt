Consistency Rules
One Step:
The Consistency rule is 40%, and it applies to only Funded stage which means your single day profit can’t be greater than 40% of your overall profit.
Instant:
The Consistency Rule on Instant Accounts is 20%, which means your single day profit can’t be greater than 20% of your overall profit.
Firm Rules
News Rule
One Step Accounts
Evaluation: Allowed
Funded: News trading is restricted by a 4-minute window (no trades 2 minutes before or after high-impact news)
Instant Accounts
News trading is restricted by a 4-minute window (no trades 2 minutes before or after high-impact news)
Positions opened before the window began may be held through the event. Any profit made from activity inside the restricted window is removed, while any loss stays on the account.
Expert Advisors
Yes. Trading bots and automated trading systems, including EAs and personal automated strategies, are allowed in Instant and One step accounts, as long as they match your normal trading style and do not exploit system vulnerabilities, latency, or data feed anomalies.
Copy Trading
Copy trading is permitted only between accounts that you personally own and control. This includes your Finotive Futures accounts and accounts held with other prop firms, provided you are the sole owner of every account involved.
Micro Scalping
Microscalping is allowed, but it cannot be your primary source of profit. No more than 50% of your total profit can come from trades held for under 30 seconds. In other words, at least half of your profit must come from trades held for 30 seconds or longer.
Inactivity Rule
Accounts with no trading activity for 15 consecutive days lead to deactivation)
Trading Hours
The main trading session runs from Sunday 17:00:00 CT (Central Time) through Friday 16:00:00 CT. There is a daily maintenance break from 16:00:00 CT to 17:00:00 CT, when trading pauses. After the break, a new trading day begins.
On Finotive Futures accounts, all open positions are automatically closed 5 minutes before the actual daily close, at 15:55:00 CT, so you never carry a position into the maintenance break, overnight, or into the weekend.
Prohibited strategies
For more details: https://help.finotivefutures.com/en/articles/56-prohibited-trading-strategies
Hedging
Hedging within a single account as part of a normal trading strategy is permitted.
Placing opposing positions across multiple accounts, whether personally owned, held by another trader, or held at another prop firm, with the intent to offset or negate market risk.
Latency Arbitrage and Feed Error Exploitation
Exploiting delays in price updates between systems or data feeds to trade ahead of a price that has already moved in the real market, or knowingly profiting from mispriced quotes, feed errors, or data discrepancies, regardless of whether the fill is later corrected.
Market Manipulation and Deception
Spoofing, layering, price manipulation, fraudulent order entry, or any conduct designed to distort market prices, create false impressions of supply and demand, or deceive other market participants.
High-Frequency and Algorithmic Exploitation
Using HFT systems, tick scalpers, bulk order automation, or any algorithmic tool designed to exploit execution speed advantages, platform inefficiencies, or simulated fill conditions, rather than to express a genuine directional market view.
Coordinated and Group Trading
Coordinating trade entries, exits, or directions with any other person across separate accounts, whether through shared signals, private communication, shared environments, or any form of linked execution.
