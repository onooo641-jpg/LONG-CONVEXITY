Right now is the best time to join the Friends Program, as you can receive $5 right away for setting up your public name.
The Friends Program is designed to reward our community for growing the E8 network. Through this program, you can connect with fellow traders and share your trading performance while becoming eligible for a $50 credit bonus for every friend you invite.
You can also invite friends who are already registered and have purchased an account. You won't receive a credit for them, but you can see their payouts, number of active accounts, and much more.
How to receive a $50 bonus?
For every friend you refer to E8 Markets, you can receive a $50 credit that is usable for your challenge purchases. Before you receive this bonus, you and your friend must meet certain conditions, such as:
You must be verified to send invitations and participate in the program.
Your friend must be a new user with no registration and no previous purchase history on our platform.
Your friend must purchase and begin trading on an account with a paid value of at least $50 .
If your friend uses an affiliate or company discount code (such as E8 ), the final amount paid for the order must be greater than $50.
How to redeem your collected credit?
Any credits you have collected are automatically deducted from the total price of the Challenge at checkout.
​
Friends Program has priority over the Affiliate commission system - As an affiliate if one of your referred users uses your Discount at checkout, but he was previously invited through Friends Program, you are not entitled to receive a commission from this user.
