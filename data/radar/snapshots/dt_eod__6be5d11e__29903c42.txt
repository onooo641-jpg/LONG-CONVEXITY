ArcTrader is DayTraders’ in-house trading platform built to give traders access to charting, order placement, account management, and market monitoring tools in one workspace.

With ArcTrader, traders can view charts, monitor symbols, manage orders, track positions, use Depth of Market, access watchlists, and manage account activity directly from the platform.
ArcTrader includes many advanced charting features, including multiple chart layouts, chart trading tools, order tickets, bid/ask lines, Japanese chart types, drawing tools, and more.
What is ArcTrader?
ArcTrader allows traders to:
View live charts
Search and select symbols
Place trades
Manage open positions
Review working orders
Monitor account status
View account balance and P&L
Access order entry tools
Use Depth of Market
Organize symbols and layouts
Use drawing tools and indicators
Main Platform Areas
ArcTrader may include:
Trading
Main trading workspace
Used for charts, orders, positions, and account activity
Copier
Used for copy trading functionality when available
Stats
Used to review account or performance-related information when available
Chart Area
Displays the selected symbol and price movement
Order Ticket
Used to place Market, Limit, and Stop orders
DOM
Used to view Depth of Market and place orders from the ladder when supported
Account Manager
Displays positions, orders, order history, account balance, P&L, and drawdown information
What Can You Do in ArcTrader?
Depending on your account access, ArcTrader may allow you to:
Select a trading account
Select a market symbol
Change chart timeframe
Add indicators
Use drawing tools
Place Buy or Sell orders
Use Market, Limit, and Stop orders
Add Take Profit exits
Add Stop Loss exits
View open positions
View working orders
Review order history
View account status
View account balance
View open P&L
View daily closed P&L
View drawdown information
Flatten open positions
