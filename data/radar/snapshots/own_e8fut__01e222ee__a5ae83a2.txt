How can I request my affiliate commissions?
August 10, 2026
You are eligible to apply for a first payout after 28 days through Rise or WorkMarket, starting from registration in our affiliate program.
​
Additional payouts are available after 28 days from the date the payout is processed. This process might take up to 7 business days.
​
The minimum amount to request is 200 USD.
​﻿
​ You need to receive at least 3 commissions from 3 different clients.
