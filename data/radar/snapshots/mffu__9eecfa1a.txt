A few things to know
Studio, explained.
The details matter. Here’s where to start.
Visit MyFundedFutures ↗
What is MyFundedStudio? MyFundedStudio is where MyFundedFutures releases new ways to get funded: limited-time plans, each with its own evaluation requirements and payout rules. Same firm, new plans.
Are the Studio plans limited-time? Yes. Studio #001, #002 and #003 are offered for a limited time, so they may not always be available to start. The rules and price shown on this page apply while they are on offer.
What’s included in the Studio plans? Three evaluations: Studio #001 ($10K account, $140), Studio #002 ($20K account, $280) and Studio #003 ($5K account, $75), each a one-time payment. Each has a static Max Loss Limit, no consistency rules, no DLL and no further restrictions.
Can I have more than one account? No. You can have one active account at a time, either an evaluation or a Sim Funded account. To start another, finish or close your current one first.
How does the Sim Funded stage work? Sim Funded rules are the same as your evaluation: the same static Max Loss Limit (MLL) and the same maximum size.
What are the payout requirements? You can request all your profits anytime, paid at an 80% profit split. To request, close all your positions and submit the request in your dashboard. The minimum is $250 on Studio #001 and #002, $100 on Studio #003. The requested amount is deducted from your account right away, so don’t trade until it has been deducted. The payout is then reviewed, approved and paid. Payouts are subject to plan terms, eligibility, and review.
Is there a daily profit limit? Yes. On a Sim Funded account, net realized profit above $5,000 in a single trading day (Studio #001), $10,000 (Studio #002) or $2,500 (Studio #003) is forfeited and can’t be withdrawn. Only the first $5,000, $10,000 or $2,500 counts.
What if I stop trading? A Studio account with no trading activity for 7 calendar days may be closed, and any profits you haven’t requested may be forfeited. Request your payout before then.
What happens after payouts? After 5 payouts or $5,000 requested, you can be considered for a Live Account. Its starting balance is 40% of the total amount you’ve requested, up to $2,000. The Risk team may also contact you to move you to Live at any time.
