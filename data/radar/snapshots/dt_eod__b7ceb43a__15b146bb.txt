Which plan is for you
Trail : Larger drawdown and no DLL, follows highest peak equity (includes open positions).
Static : You want a fixed drawdown level that never moves, for predictable risk.
EOD : You want the drawdown to move only at the end of the day, not with every intraday high.
S2F : You want to skip the evaluation and trade for payouts from day one.
S2L : You want a set path to a live account in live markets.
At checkout, S2F appears as Instant and S2L as Live . Pro Accounts keep the drawdown type and contract limits of the evaluation you passed, and an account can't be changed after purchase.
