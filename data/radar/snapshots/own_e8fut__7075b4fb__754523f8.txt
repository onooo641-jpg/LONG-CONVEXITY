Maximum Drawdown (Tournament)
Maximum Drawdown (only for Tournament accounts) represents the maximum total loss your account can incur based on your Initial Balance . Currently, this Drawdown is available only for the Ongoing Tournament.

Quick Explanation (Read this first)
It is a Fixed Floor: It does not move up, and It stays anchored to your starting point.
The "Hard Stop": A permanent violation occurs if your account’s Equity or Balance falls below this level at any point.
