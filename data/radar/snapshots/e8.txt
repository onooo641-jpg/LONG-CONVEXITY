Frequently Asked Questions
What is E8 Markets?
E8 is a SimFi (Simulated Finance) platform where traders develop and demonstrate their skills using simulated capital. You pay a one-time challenge fee, trade under real-market conditions, and qualify for payouts based on your validated performance data. No personal capital at risk beyond the challenge fee.
What am I risking?
Only the challenge fee. There are no deposits, no margin requirements, and no personal capital deployed on your behalf. The environment is simulated - the rules and market data are not.
What do I need to start?
A device, an internet connection, and a trading strategy. Select your account size, complete KYC, and you are ready to begin the SimFi Challenge.
How can I start earning?
Pass the SimFi™ Challenge by hitting the profit target while staying within the guardrails. Once you reach the SimFi™ Performance Account stage and meet the payout conditions, you can request payouts based on your validated performance data.
Where is your proof?
$81M+ in payouts processed since 2021, verified independently on PropFirmMatch, and PayoutJunction. Our Discord community of 42,461+ traders is open - ask anyone who has been through the process.
