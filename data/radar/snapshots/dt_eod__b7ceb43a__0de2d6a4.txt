All Collections General Maximum Number of Accounts Maximum Number of Accounts
How many evaluations, Pro, S2F, S2L and live accounts you can hold, and how simulated and live accounts interact.
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Copy for LLM
Evaluations are unlimited. Simulated funded accounts and live accounts have limits.
Account type
Max allowed
Evaluations (Trail, Static, EOD)
Unlimited
Simulated funded accounts (Pro + S2F)
15 in total
EOD Pro Accounts
5, within the 15
S2F accounts
5, within the 15
S2L evaluations
Unlimited (only 5 can be upgraded to live)
Live accounts
5, with only one tradeable at a time
You can mix the 15 any way you like within those limits, for example 12 Pro Accounts + 3 S2F, or 10 Pro Accounts (up to 5 EOD) + 5 S2F.
Live accounts
You can't trade simulated and live accounts at the same time. Once you're live, all your simulated accounts, including other S2L evaluations, are closed, and you can't buy new S2L evaluations while trading live.
If you have multiple S2L accounts and only some of them are passed, reach out to support and we can hold off on the transition to live until you finish trading all S2L accounts. We will generally hold passed accounts for 1 month.
