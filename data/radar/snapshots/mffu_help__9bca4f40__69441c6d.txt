How to Log in to Tradovate
If this is your first Tradovate account, please follow these steps:
1. Visit https://tradovate.com
2. In the upper right corner of the homepage, click Login .
3. Enter your MyFundedFutures credentials, which can be found in your MFFU dashboard .
4. Review and sign the required agreement to complete your account setup.
5. Click Continue below Simulation .
6. You’re now logged in and ready to trade on the Tradovate platform! Make sure you select the correct account to trade on, if you purchased multiple evaluations.

How to enable TradingView trading with Tradovate
If it’s your first time logging in, you will need to enable TradingView as an add-on to avoid errors when logging in.
To enable TradingView:
1. Login to https://www.tradovate.com following the steps in the "How to Login to Tradovate" guide.
2. In the top-right corner of your dashboard, click the Application Settings button.
3. You’ll now be in the Application settings . Select “ Add-Ons ”
4. Click Activate on the TradingView option.
5. You should see TradingView now greyed out with ‘ Activated ’ next to it, along with a pop-up message confirming that the activation was successfully processed.
6. You can now log in to TradingView using Tradovate. Open your TradingView and navigate to the Trading Panel at the bottom of your window.
7. Select ‘Tradovate’
8. Enter your login details, which can be found in your MyFundedFutures dashboard . Ensure you are on the ‘ Demo ’ tab and enable “ Don’t remember me ” if you are on a public computer.
9. Click Connect.
a. If you receive an error indicating that TradingView is not enabled, please return to Step 1 and start over. If that doesn’t work, open a support ticket on our website for assistance.
10. If you left “ Don’t remember me ” unchecked and are using the Desktop version of TradingView, you may be prompted to save the credentials.
