Ongoing KYC/Documentation Monitoring
August 24, 2026
To ensure all submitted documents are valid, the Sumsub system performs daily checks. These checks verify that the applicant's identity document has a suitable validity date. The criteria for validity are as follows:
The document's expiration date must be on or after the current date.
Alternatively, the document will be accepted if the validity period is adjusted by a defined number of days.
This automated process ensures that only active and valid identity documents are used for verification.
