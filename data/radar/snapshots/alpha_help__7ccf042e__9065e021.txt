What can I use to receive my Performance Fees?
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
May 27, 2025
Alpha Futures is dedicated to convenience and flexibility for their traders, offering multiple payment options. All payments are made in USD.
The available payout methods are:
- ACH (US bank accounts)
- Wire Transfer
-SWIFT
- Wise
- Rise
If Rise is selected, user will be emailed an agreement they must sign before receiving first payout.
