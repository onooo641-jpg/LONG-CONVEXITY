Simfi™ Challenge account
SimFi ™ Challenge is your first step, where you demonstrate your skills by completing profit targets and following our safety guardrails. Once you successfully navigate through the Challenge account, you move to the SimFi ™ Performance account , where your discipline in a simulated environment leads to payouts.
Simfi™ Performance account
The SimFi ™ Performance account is a simulated trading account that allows you to qualify for payouts of up to 100% based on your performance. Think of it as a professional flight simulator: we provide the simulated capital and simulated market conditions so you can master your strategy without the risks of losing personal capital with brokers, and get paid in the process.
What happens when you pass the Simfi™ Challenge account?
After you meet all objectives (profit target) while following all guardrails, your challenge account will be closed, and you will receive a SimFi™ Performance account.
​
You will receive a new account with new credentials, usually within 30-90 minutes after finishing your Challenge. Sometimes you also need to reconfirm your personal information, provide a new document, and wait for approval from our team, which can take up to 24 hours.
​
Remember that you will always receive all instructions in the email.
