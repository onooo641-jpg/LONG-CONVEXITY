Do You Have An Affiliate Program?
Yes. You earn a percentage commission whenever someone you refer purchases a Challenge — and with our MLM (multi-level) structure, you earn not only from your direct referrals but also from the sub-affiliates they bring in, across several levels:
Level
Commission
Level 1 — your direct referrals
10%
Level 2 — their referrals
5%
Level 3
3%
Level 4
2%
There are also exclusive, discretionary incentives for outstanding affiliate performance.
Can I Get a Better Deal Ror Referring Many Traders?
The standard rate is 10% on your direct referrals, with an enhanced rate available for affiliates who show consistent, successful results. If you’re performing strongly, speak to your Affiliate Manager about a custom deal.
How Are My Referrals Tracked?
When someone clicks your affiliate link, a cookie is stored on their browser for 60 days. The 60 days resets each time they return to the website, so a referral is only lost after 60 consecutive days without a visit.
When And How Can I Request My Commission?
You can request a payout once your balance reaches the minimum of $100.
Commissions are paid monthly — what you earn in a month becomes withdrawable early the next month (your eligible amount is calculated through the previous month’s end).
Before you can request, you need: a completed payment method, verified KYC, and a balance of at least $100 (the Request Payment button appears once you’re eligible).
How To Request
In your Affiliate Portal dashboard, click “Request Payment”.
Review the pop-up showing your eligible commission (calculated through the previous month’s end).
Choose to auto-generate an invoice or upload your own, then submit.
Requests are processed within 24 business hours.
If a request is already pending, you’ll be asked to wait for a response; if one is declined, check the Payments section and read the “Admin Message” for the reason.
What Payment Methods Can I Use?
We currently pay affiliate commissions by Bank Wire and Cryptocurrency. You set or update your payment method in your Affiliate Portal before requesting a payout.
