1
Profit Target
A required profit target to complete the Challenge. Calculated on closed-position P&L.
Rapid Pro : ⁦25K⁩: ⁦$1,500⁩ · ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$3,000⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$5,000⁩
Rapid Daily : ⁦25K⁩: ⁦$1,500⁩ · ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$3,000⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$5,000⁩
Flex : ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$2,500⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$5,000⁩ · ⁦$150K⁩: ⁦$8,000⁩
Direct : No Profit Target
Once the FundedNext Account stage is reached, there is no profit target.
2
Daily Loss Limit
A daily loss cap that, when breached, pauses trading for the rest of the session.
Rapid Pro : None
Rapid Daily : ⁦25K⁩: ⁦$500⁩ · ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$1,000⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$1,250⁩
Flex : No daily loss limit
Direct : ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$1,000⁩
Learn More →
On Rapid and Direct, breaching the DLL is a soft breach — trading pauses for the rest of the session and resumes the next day. Resets daily at ⁦⁦5:00⁩ PM CT..
3
Maximum Loss Limit
A cap on how much can be lost across the lifetime of the account. Calculated as a fixed dollar amount, set per account model and size. Breaching it closes the account.
Rapid Pro : ⁦25K⁩: ⁦$1,000⁩ · ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$2,000⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$2,500⁩
Rapid Daily : ⁦25K⁩: ⁦$1,000⁩ · ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$2,000⁩ · ⁦$100K⁩: ⁦$2,500⁩
Flex : ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$1,500⁩ · ⁦100K⁩: ⁦$2,500⁩ · ⁦150K⁩: ⁦$4,000⁩
Direct : ⁦50K⁩: ⁦$2,000⁩
Learn More →
The MLL is calculated on the highest end-of-day balance. It trails up as the account hits new closing highs, locks once it reaches the initial balance, and does not decrease during losses.
4
Consistency Rule
Your largest single trading day must stay at or under ⁦40%⁩ of total profit.
Rapid Pro : FundedNext only
Rapid Daily : No consistency
Flex : Challenge phase
Direct : ⁦20%⁩ perpetual Consistency across withdrawal
For challenge phase, when the cap is breached, the profit target recalculates upward using the formula: Highest Daily Profit ÷ ⁦40%⁩ = New Required Profit. This raises the bar instead of closing the account.
5
Reward Share
The trader's share of profits generated on the FundedNext Account.
Flex : ⁦95%⁩ reward share.
Rapid : ⁦90%⁩ reward share.
Direct : ⁦90%⁩ reward share.
6
Inactivity
Accounts cannot stay inactive for longer than a set number of consecutive days.
Challenge Account : ⁦30⁩ consecutive calendar days of no trades. Account is breached and deactivated.
FundedNext Account : ⁦30⁩ consecutive calendar days of no trades. Account is marked inactive and deactivated.
Direct Funded Account : Minimum one trade per week is required to keep your account active
Learn More →
A trade must be placed within the window to keep the account active. The window counts calendar days, including weekends, and resets when at least one trade is executed. Breached Challenge Accounts can be reset; deactivated FundedNext Accounts cannot.
