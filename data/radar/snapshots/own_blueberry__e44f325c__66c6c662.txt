Are there any restrictions on how I promote Blueberry Futures?
Affiliate Violations
September 25, 2026
Yes. As a Blueberry Futures affiliate you're representing our brand, so all promotion must be honest, compliant, and in line with our brand guidelines and applicable advertising laws. These rules keep the program fair and protect both you and our brand.
​
What's not allowed
​
❌ Misleading or false claims
No guarantees or exaggerated promises, especially about income, profits, or success rates. Never suggest that passing an evaluation or earning payouts is guaranteed, and don't misrepresent our rules, pricing, profit split, or funding model.
​
❌ Spam tactics
No unsolicited bulk email, mass DMs, comment spam, or repetitive posting across forums and social platforms. Promotion should be genuine and welcomed by your audience, not forced on people.
​
❌ Impersonation
Don't pose as Blueberry Futures, our staff, or our official accounts, and don't create pages, profiles, or channels that could be mistaken for official Blueberry Futures properties.
​
❌ Brand or trademark misuse
Use our name, logo, and assets only as set out in our brand guidelines. Don't alter our branding or imply an official partnership, sponsorship, or employment relationship beyond your role as an affiliate.
​
❌ Anything unlawful or non-compliant
Follow the advertising and consumer-protection laws that apply to you and your audience, including clearly disclosing that you're an affiliate who may earn a commission. Don't promote to prohibited audiences or in restricted jurisdictions.
​
Better ways to promote
Be transparent — disclose your affiliate relationship in your content.
Be accurate — describe our product, rules, and pricing as they actually are; when in doubt, link to our official pages.
Add real value — honest reviews, tutorials, and genuine recommendations convert better and keep you compliant.
What happens if the rules are broken
Violations may result in withheld commissions, suspension, or removal from the program , depending on severity. If you're ever unsure whether something is allowed, check with us first at [email protected] .
