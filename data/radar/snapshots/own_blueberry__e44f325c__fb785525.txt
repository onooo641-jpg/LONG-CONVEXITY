How does the Affiliate Program work?
Affiliate Program
September 24, 2026
The Blueberry Futures Affiliate Program lets you earn commission every time someone you refer makes a purchase, and keep earning from that client over time.
​
Here's the full flow:
​
1. Get your referral link
Log in at portal.blueberryfutures.com/portal/refer . As soon as you're in, your unique referral link is available in your affiliate dashboard.
​
2. Share it with your audience
Promote your link however you like, social media, YouTube, Discord, a website, or direct sharing. You can also request a custom discount code (35% OFF) for your audience that tracks your sales, and use marketing materials like banners and landing pages to boost conversions.
​
3. Your referrals are tracked automatically
When someone clicks your link, they're tracked for 180 days . If they purchase any time within that window, the sale is credited to you, including repeat purchases.
​ 4. Get paid
Commissions build up in your affiliate balance. Once you reach the $200 minimum , you can withdraw via the Payout tab , payouts are processed every Friday through Riseworks .
​
