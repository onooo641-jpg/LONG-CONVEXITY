What is the Maximum Capital Allocation?
📊 Understanding our limits: You can have up to $450k in total funding across a maximum of 3 active accounts.
November 26, 2025
Table of contents
Account Limits & Allocation Rules
We allow traders to diversify their strategies by holding multiple accounts. However, different limits apply depending on whether you are in the Evaluation phase or the Funded phase.
1. Evaluation Phase (The Challenge)
During the evaluation, limits are applied per account type .
Limit: You can hold a maximum of 3 Active Accounts of the same type at one time.
The Math:
Since we have two distinct account types (Ascent and Accelerated), you can theoretically hold:
✅ 3 Active Ascent Accounts
✅ 3 Active Accelerated Accounts
Total: 6 Active Evaluations simultaneously.
2. Funded Phase (Live)
Once you pass, the rules tighten to ensure fair risk management across the firm. The limit becomes a Total Cap , regardless of account type.
Max Funded Accounts: 3 Accounts Total .
Max Capital Allocation: $450,000 .

You can mix and match account sizes ($50k, $100k, $150k) as long as you don't exceed the limits.

What if I pass a 4th account?
If you pass more than 3 evaluations, the extra accounts are placed on Standby. You can activate them only when a slot opens up in your portfolio (e.g., if you close or breach one of your active 3 accounts).
