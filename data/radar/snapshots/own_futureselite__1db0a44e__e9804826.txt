About us
FuturesElite (Quantum SRL) is based in Latina, Italy, and operates under Italian law. We are a technology company that provides trading-challenge credentials on simulated accounts from third-party partner brokers. We are not a regulated broker, we don't take deposits and we don't offer financial services.
Support
Email [email protected] or use live chat on our website. If you hit a technical problem during a challenge, such as an outage or data feed issue, contact us straight away.
Your personal and financial information is protected with encryption, secure payment processing and regular security audits.
