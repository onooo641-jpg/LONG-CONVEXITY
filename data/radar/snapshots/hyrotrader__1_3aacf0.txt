How Our Crypto Prop Firm Challenge Works
A prop firm challenge (also called an evaluation) is a paid trading test on a simulated account. Reach the profit target without breaking its loss limits, and you trade a funded account for a share of the profit. Our three challenges run on live crypto market data, with static loss limits and no time limit.

How Does the Crypto Trading Challenge Work?
All three challenges follow the same order, and only Two-Step adds a second test phase.
Buy and Activate Your Challenge
Pick Hyro Core (from $9), One-Step or Two-Step and an account size, then activate it when you are ready. Until then you can cancel and get your payment back in full. To skip the test, Hyro Instant gives you a funded account once your identity is verified.
Reach the Profit Target Within the Loss Limits
Trade until your profit from closed trades reaches the target, over the minimum number of trading days. Breaking the daily or the maximum loss limit ends the challenge. On Two-Step, a pass opens a second phase with a smaller target.
Pass the Review and Get Funded
The review starts by itself and usually takes a few hours, up to two business days. After an identity check and a signed agreement, your funded account is issued. Each payout covers profit up to 5% of the starting balance and usually arrives within 24 hours in USDT or USDC.

How to Pass a Crypto Prop Firm Challenge
Many traders do not pass the first time. With no deadline the target can wait, but a broken loss limit ends the challenge at once.
1
Size Every Trade Against the Daily Limit
Work out what each trade would lose at your stop-loss and keep it well inside the daily limit. Stop-losses are optional, but we recommend one, as leverage can hit the limit in one move.
2
Pause Trading During the Review
Once you reach the target, stop trading until the review is done. Any trade placed during the review stays in your history and still counts.
3
Plan for News and Weekend Moves
News trading and weekend holding are allowed, though a news-only strategy is not. Check what is scheduled, and trade smaller before news you plan to hold through.
