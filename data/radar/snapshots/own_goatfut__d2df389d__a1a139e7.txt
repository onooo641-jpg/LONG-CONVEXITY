What plans do you offer?
1. EOD Challenge. One-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown. Best for traders who want the most forgiving drawdown and room to manage intraday swings. 50K / 100K / 150K.
2. 1-Day-Pass. One-phase evaluation with end of day trailing drawdown. An optional daily loss limit, chosen at checkout, applies in both phases and closes the account if hit, the split is 80/20 with an optional 90/10 add-on, and payouts open after 5 winning days. 25K / 50K / 100K.
3. Instant. No evaluation. Start trading a funded account immediately. Choose 15% or 20% consistency, with or without a daily loss limit. 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K.
4. Flex Challenge. One-phase evaluation built for flexibility. 50K / 100K / 150K.
No time limit to pass
No daily loss limit by default. You can add one at checkout for a lower price, and hitting it closes the account
The 1 minute holding rule applies at payout, as on every plan, see the holding time article
End of day trailing drawdown
80/20 split by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on for 20% of the challenge price
5. GOAT Daily. One-phase evaluation built around payout frequency. 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K.
Request a payout every 24 hours once funded, with no fixed windows and no winning day requirement
Up to 90% profit split: 80/20 by default, with an optional 90/10 add-on for 20% of the challenge price
Full contract limits from day one, no scaling plan
An optional daily loss limit, chosen at checkout, applies in both phases and closes the account if hit
End of day trailing drawdown during the evaluation, intraday trailing on the funded account
40% or 50% consistency rule during the evaluation (your choice at checkout), none once funded
See the GOAT Daily specifications for the full rules.
All five are a one-time fee. There is no monthly subscription on any GFF product.
Instant Classic and Instant Lite are no longer on sale. Accounts bought before 18 November 2026 keep their original rules.

What all plans have in common
$0 activation fee.
News trading is unrestricted during the evaluation phase on EOD, 1-Day-Pass and Flex. GOAT Daily applies the 2 minute buffer in both phases. On all funded accounts a 2 minute buffer applies around high impact news events, with no opening, closing or triggering of pending orders within 2 minutes before or after a scheduled release.
One holding time rule on every plan: the 1 minute rule, checked when you pass an evaluation and at each payout request: at least 70% of your closed trades and at least 70% of your net profit must come from trades held longer than 1 minute.
No time limit to pass the evaluation on EOD, 1-Day-Pass, Flex and GOAT Daily.
Instant Classic and Instant Lite are no longer on sale. Accounts bought before 18 November 2026 keep their original rules: see the Instant Classic and Instant Lite articles.

GOAT Daily or Flex Challenge: which should I choose?
Both are one-phase evaluations with a 6% profit target, no time limit and the same drawdown percentages. They are built for different things.
Flex is the freedom plan. No daily loss limit unless you add one at checkout, no news restriction during the evaluation, and larger payouts when they come.
GOAT Daily is the cash flow plan. Money out every 24 hours, in exchange for tighter rails while you trade.

Can I upgrade or switch from one plan to another?
Plans cannot be converted or upgraded after purchase. Each account type is a separate product with its own rules and drawdown mechanics. If you'd like to try a different plan, you would need to purchase a new account.
The same goes for the daily loss limit option on Flex. You choose it at checkout, and it can't be added or removed afterwards.
This includes moving between Flex and GOAT Daily. You can hold both at the same time, since account limits are counted separately for each product type. If you are weighing the two up, see GOAT Daily or Flex Challenge: which should I choose?
