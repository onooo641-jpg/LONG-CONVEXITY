How to Scale Up my Funded Account Infinitely ♾️?
March 17, 2026
Your funded account at BrightFunded is being reviewed for the scaling plan from the moment the first trade is opened. If you meet the conditions of the scaling plan, your funded account will be increased by 30% of the original account size. To qualify, you need to meet the following requirements:
Review Period:
All Funded accounts are subject to a four-month consecutive evaluation period calculated from the date of the first executed trade. Should the objective not be achieved within this timeframe, all parameters will be reset and a new evaluation period will commence.
Profitability:
You must achieve profitability in at least two out of the four months, with a minimum total profit of 10% during this four-month period.
Payouts:
You must successfully process a minimum of two payout transactions on your funded account.
Positive Balance:
As the 'scale-up' occurs, the balance on the funded account must be at a breakeven point or show profit.
The sky is the Limit 🚀
At BrightFunded, the sky is literally the limit. That's why BrightFunded's scaling plan has no upside balance limitations. This means if you continuously generate profit on your funded account, we will scale it up infinitely! To make things even better, from the 3rd scale-up onwards, you will receive a 100% reward split .
Important : Please note that each trader is responsible for contacting the support team to request the scaling plan, as this process is not automatic.
