Eligibility depends on your country of legal residence, not your citizenship.
Falsifying your country of residence at registration, purchase or KYC leads to an immediate ban, closed accounts, cancelled transactions and possible forfeiture of balances. We may also suspend payments and report fraud to the authorities.
