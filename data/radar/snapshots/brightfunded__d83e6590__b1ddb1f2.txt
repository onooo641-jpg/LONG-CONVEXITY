BrightPlus or BrightCore: which plan should I choose?
BrightPlus: intraday real-time trailing drawdown, daily payouts, a 50% Consistency Rule and a lower price. BrightCore: end-of-day trailing drawdown, payouts every 5 winning days and a 40% Consistency Rule. Both plans have the same profit target, Max Loss Limit, contract limits and payout caps. Fastest possible pass: 2 trading days on BrightPlus, 3 on BrightCore.

How does the Futures Evaluation work?
One phase with a 6% profit target ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 on 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K), no time limit, no minimum trading days and no daily loss limit. You pass when you reach the target, meet the Consistency Rule (best day at most 50% on BrightPlus or 40% on BrightCore) and the Minimum Trade Duration, and your equity never touched the Max Loss Limit floor.

How does the Futures Max Loss Limit work?
The Max Loss Limit (MLL) is $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 on 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K, and it is the only loss rule. Your floor is your highest point minus the MLL, starting at $24,000 / $48,000 / $97,000 / $145,500. If live equity, open trades included, touches the floor, the account is breached. BrightPlus trails in real time, BrightCore at end of day.

How do Futures payouts work?
BrightPlus pays daily once your balance is above the buffer; BrightCore pays after every 5 winning days (3 with the add-on). The minimum request is $250 and the cap per request is $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,000 on 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K, doubled with the 2x Payout Cap add-on. You keep 80%, or 100% with the 100% Profit Split add-on, paid in USDC (ERC-20) or by bank transfer.

What is the Minimum Trade Duration?
More than 50% of your winning profit must come from trades held longer than 15 seconds, counted net of commissions on winning trades only. Exactly 50% isn’t enough. It is never a breach: in the Evaluation it delays the pass, and on SIM Funded it blocks the payout request, counting only trades closed since your last approved payout. Example: $500 of $1,200 winning profit is 41.7%, not met yet.

Can I trade news or hold trades overnight on Futures?
News trading is fully allowed in the Evaluation and on SIM Funded, including CPI, NFP and FOMC. Overnight and weekend holding isn’t possible: all positions close automatically at 4:45 PM ET, Monday to Friday, and that is never a breach. You trade CME futures on NinjaTrader, Tradovate and TradingView, all included free with every account.
