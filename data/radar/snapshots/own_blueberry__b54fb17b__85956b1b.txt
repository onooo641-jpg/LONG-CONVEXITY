The Payout Cycle Explained
The payout cycle at Blueberry Futures is designed to reward consistent performance.
To request a withdrawal, you must meet specific trading conditions, consistency rules, and profit thresholds.
1. Eligibility Requirements
Before a payout button becomes available, you must meet ALL of the following criteria within the current cycle:
Active Trading: You must have traded at least 5 profitable days .
Profitable Day Definition: A day is only counted if you generate a net profit of $200 or more . (Days with less than $200 profit do not count).
Consistency Rule: No single day can account for more than a specific % of your total profit.
Accelerated: Max 20% from a single day.
Ascent: Max 35% from a single day.
2. Limits & Buffer Rules
You can only withdraw funds that are in excess of your Required Buffer, up to the Maximum Cap.

3. How the Buffer Works (Real-Examples)
Here is exactly how to calculate what you can withdraw based on your total profit.
$25k Account Example
Total Profit Made: $2,000
The Rules: Max Cap ($1,500) | Buffer ($1,100)
The Math: $2,000 (Profit) - $1,100 (Buffer) = $900 Available .
Result: You can withdraw $900 right now.
To get the full $1,500 max payout, you would need to build your profit to $2,600.

4. Account Pause & Reset
Processing: Once you request a payout, your account is temporarily paused (read-only) while we process the funds. You can resume trading immediately after approval.
Cycle Reset: After a payout, your "5 Day Count" resets. You must complete 5 new profitable days, maintain consistency within the new cycle to qualify for the next withdrawal.
