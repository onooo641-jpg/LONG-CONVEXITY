News Trading
News trading is allowed, with no restrictions on when or how you trade.
⁦40%⁩ News Profit Split (FundedNext Accounts and Stellar Instant) : Profits made during the news window count at ⁦40%⁩. Losses count at ⁦100%⁩.
Challenge Phases excluded : Stellar ⁦1⁩-Step, ⁦2⁩-Step, and Lite Challenge Phases are not subject to the split.
News window : ⁦5⁩ minutes before and ⁦5⁩ minutes after a listed high-impact news event tied to the traded symbol.
2
Hedging
Hedging is allowed inside a single account. Across accounts or with external brokers, it's not.
Same account : Opposite positions on the same instrument are permitted as part of your strategy.
Across accounts : Mirrored or opposite positions across two FundedNext accounts are prohibited, even when both belong to you.
External brokers : Hedging between a FundedNext account and an account at another firm is prohibited.

Trading patterns that risk the full or near-full daily loss limit on a single trade may be reviewed as suspected hedging, even when no second leg is visible on FundedNext's side.

4
Copy Trading
Copy trading is allowed only between accounts owned by the same individual.
What's allowed :
– Between your own Stellar ⁦1⁩-Step, ⁦2⁩-Step, and Lite Challenge accounts.
– Between your own Stellar Instant accounts.
– Both VPS-based and local trade copiers are permitted.
What's not allowed :
– Copy trading is not allowed if a FundedNext Funded Account is involved on either side, whether copying to another Funded Account or to a Challenge Account.
– With friends, family, colleagues, or any third party, prohibited in any direction.
– Through third-party copier services like Social Trader Tools and Duplikum.
– Through third-party account management for hire.

FundedNext monitors for identical trades, synchronized entries, and shared strategies across accounts. Violations may result in warnings or account termination depending on severity.
