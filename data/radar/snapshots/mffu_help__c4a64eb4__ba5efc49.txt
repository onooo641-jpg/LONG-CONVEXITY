MyFundedClub: How to Use Refer a Friend
August 28, 2026
Table of contents
The Refer a Friend feature allows MyFundedClub members to earn loyalty points by inviting new traders to MyFundedFutures.
When a new user signs up through your referral link and completes a qualifying purchase, you receive 2,000 loyalty points and the referred user receives 500 loyalty points .
How to Refer a Friend
1. Open MyFundedClub
From your MyFundedFutures dashboard, locate and click MyFundedClub in the left-hand navigation menu.
2. Open the Tasks Section
Once inside MyFundedClub, click Tasks from the navigation menu.
This section contains available activities and milestones that can earn loyalty points.
3. Locate "Refer a NEW Friend"
Scroll to the Refer a NEW Friend task.
This task awards 2,000 points when a new trader joins through your referral link and completes their first qualifying purchase.
Click Collect Now .
4. Copy Your Referral Link
After selecting the referral task, you will be taken to the Refer a Friend page.
Under Yours to give , you will see your unique referral link.
Click Copy Link to copy your personal referral URL.
5. Share Your Referral Link
Send your referral link to friends, family members, or other traders who are not already MyFundedFutures customers.
The referred user must:
Sign up using your referral link.
Complete a qualifying purchase.
Once those requirements are met, referral points will be awarded automatically.
Referral Milestone Bonuses
Members can earn additional loyalty points by reaching referral milestones:
Milestone
Bonus Points
Refer 5 new friends

Refer 10 new friends

Refer 15 new friends

Refer 25 new friends

These bonuses are in addition to the standard referral rewards.
Important Notes
Referral points are only awarded after the referred user completes a qualifying purchase.
Self-referrals are not permitted.
Referral purchases that are refunded, cancelled, or charged back may result in points being removed.
KYC is not required to earn referral points.
This program is separate from the MFFU Affiliate Program. The Refer a Friend feature awards loyalty points rather than affiliate commissions.
Need Help?
If a referral does not appear to track correctly, contact MyFundedFutures Support.
