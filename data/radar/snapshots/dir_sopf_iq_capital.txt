IQ Capital country availability
IQ Capital restricts 20 countries and accepts traders everywhere else , including St. Lucia . Restriction lists change without notice, so confirm with the firm before you buy.
AF Afghanistan BY Belarus CF Central African Republic CU Cuba CD DR Congo ER Eritrea IR Iran IQ Iraq LY Libya ML Mali MM Myanmar NI Nicaragua KP North Korea RU Russia SO Somalia SS South Sudan SD Sudan SY Syria VE Venezuela YE Yemen
Allowlist model: EU/EEA plus roughly 70 named countries are confirmed accepted; unlisted countries require contacting info@iqcapital.io. Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk (occupied regions of Ukraine) are explicitly restricted, but Ukraine itself is on the accepted list. Attempting access from a restricted jurisdiction, including via VPN or other circumvention tools, is prohibited and may result in termination and forfeiture of funds.
