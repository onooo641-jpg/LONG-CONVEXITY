Rapid EOD with DLL – A Comprehensive Look
MyFundedFutures has adopted a streamlined approach to evaluations. Traders can now follow a standardized evaluation process on their path to simulated funding and live account trading. The evaluation rules remain consistent, regardless of the account type you choose.
Rapid EOD is now available in two versions: the Default (No DLL) version and the Add-on (DLL) version. It is the same plan with the same rules, except that the DLL version adds a Daily Loss Limit.

1. Sim Funded Account Parameters
How the Max Loss works
Your Max Loss trails upward each time your account closes at a new end-of-day high.
It does not move intraday. It only moves at the close of each trading session.
While it is trailing, the distance between your account and the Max Loss Limit is always $1,000 (25K) or $2,000 (50K) .
It never moves down.
Max Loss Lock at $100
Once your trailing Max Loss reaches $100 , it locks there .
From that point on, it does not trail anymore .
You must always keep at least $100 in the account.
If your balance goes below $100, the account is breached .
Daily Loss Limit (DLL Version only)
The Daily Loss Limit is $500 (25K) and $1,000 (50K) . It applies in both the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages.
Hitting the Daily Loss Limit is not a breach . Instead, it is a soft pause for the rest of the trading day.
The Daily Loss Limit resets daily at 6:00 PM ET .
The Daily Loss Limit is not the Max Loss Limit:
The DLL resets daily.
The MLL trails EOD and locks at +$100.
News trading
T1 news trading is not allowed on this Sim Funded account.
Contract limits
You may trade up to 2 minis and/or 20 micros (25K) or 3 minis and/or 30 micros (50K) in total.
Exceeding your plan’s contract limit can result in a breach of the trading account.
2. Payouts on Sim Funded (Rapid EOD)
Both versions follow the same payout rules. The DLL version changes only the Daily Loss Limit.
Payout requirements
Required Buffer: $1,100 (25K) / $2,100 (50K)
You must build at least this amount in realized profits in the account before you request your first payout.
Payout Unlock Rule: After the buffer clears, each payout unlocks once your net profit since your last payout reaches $250 (25K) or $500 (50K) .
Consistency Requirement: None . You do not need to meet a consistency rule to request a payout.
Timing & frequency
Payout Frequency: Daily
There is no cap on how much you can request per payout cycle.
Amounts & split
Minimum Payout Request: $500
Profit Split:
90% to the trader
10% to MyFundedFutures

Frequently Asked Questions:
Q. Is Rapid EOD with DLL a new plan?
A. No. It is the same Rapid EOD Plan, now offered with a DLL version.
Q. What is the default version?
A. The default is No DLL, at the updated price.
Q. I already purchased Rapid EOD. Does anything change?
A. No. Existing accounts keep their rules and price, with no DLL.
Q. Can I switch between the DLL and No DLL versions later?
A. No. You can only choose the version at checkout.
Q. What happens when I hit the Daily Loss Limit?
A. Your account hits a soft pause until the 6:00 PM ET reset. This is not a hard breach.
Q. Are there any consistency rules in the Rapid EOD evaluation?
A. Yes. The evaluation phase of the Rapid EOD Plan has a 30% consistency rule, and you can pass it in as little as 4 days.
Q. How is the drawdown different from the standard Rapid Plan?
A. The standard Rapid Plan trails intraday at the Sim Funded stage and requires 2 minimum trading days to pass. Rapid EOD trails end-of-day instead and requires 4 trading days to pass. Your Max Loss moves based only on where your account closes each session, not on intraday swings.
Q. Are there any buffer zones in the Rapid EOD DLL payout policy?
A. Yes. You cannot request your first payout until you clear the $1,100 (25K) or $2,100 (50K) buffer. After that, each additional payout unlocks once you have made $250 (25K) or $500 (50K) in net profit since your last payout.
Q. What is the minimum payout per request on the Rapid EOD Plan?
A. The minimum payout amount on the Rapid EOD Plan is $500.
Q. Are there any activation fees on the Rapid EOD Plan?
A. No. The Rapid EOD account does not have an activation fee.
Q. How do I transition to a Live account on Rapid EOD?
A. See Understanding Rapid Live for transition triggers, Live account parameters, and the Reserve Program. Both versions follow the existing Rapid Live values for their account size.
