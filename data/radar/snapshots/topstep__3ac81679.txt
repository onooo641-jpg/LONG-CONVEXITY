Rules And Payouts
How do I become a funded trader at Topstep? Topstep is a prop firm where traders start in the Trading Combine®, following clear rules to demonstrate consistency and risk management. If you pass, you become a Topstep Funded Trader® , earn access to an Express Funded Account®, and work toward earning payouts. Learn more .
Do I trade with real money? In the Trading Combine® and Express Funded Account®, trading takes place in a simulated environment using real market data. As you progress through our futures prop firm program , you can become a funded trader and earn real payouts in the Express Funded Account based on performance. Live Funded Accounts trade with real capital. Learn more .
How quickly can I get funded? There’s no minimum time requirement to pass the Trading Combine® and become a funded trader. Some traders take their time, while others pass in as little as a few days by meeting the objectives. Learn more .
How do payouts work? Funded traders can request payouts in as little as 3 days. Request up to $6,000 and keep 90% of your payouts. Learn more .
What happens if I hit the Maximum Loss Limit? If you hit the Maximum Loss Limit, you can Reset Trading Combines. If you’re a funded trader, you can reactivate your account with Back2Funded to continue trading. Learn more .
What makes Topstep the leading futures prop firm? Topstep is a leading futures prop firm built to help traders become funded traders and get paid. With more than $1 billion paid out, a 99.26% payout approval rate, and a community of over 200,000 traders, it’s designed to help traders earn real payouts. Learn more .
Why do traders choose Topstep for futures prop trading? For 15 years, Topstep has funded traders, paid out billions, and built a trading platform designed to help traders succeed. Today, Topstep is a leading futures prop firm with a community of 200K+ traders, 211K+ YouTube subscribers, and 174K+ Discord members worldwide. Funded traders can keep up to 90% of profits and request payouts of up to $6,000 through a clear, consistent process, powered by TopstepX, Topstep’s proprietary platform built by traders to help manage risk, stay disciplined, and earn payouts. Learn more .
What to look for in a prop firm? When looking for a prop firm, traders should evaluate the payout structure, trading rules, transparency, platform and tools, support, and overall track record. The best prop firms clearly explain how payouts work, what traders need to qualify, and what expectations come with funded accounts before you sign up.

Trading Combine and Topstep Labs Experiments. The Trading Combine, Express Funded Accounts and Topstep Labs experiments are realistic simulations of trading under actual market conditions and are difficult to pass even for experienced traders. Simulated performance results have certain limitations. Unlike an actual performance record, simulated results do not represent actual trading. Also, because the trades have not actually been executed, the results may have under- or over-compensated for the impact, if any, of certain market factors, such as lack of liquidity. Simulated trading programs, in general, are also subject to the fact that they are designed with the benefit of hindsight. Simulated trading does not involve financial risk, and no simulated trading record can completely account for the impact of financial risk in actual trading. For example, the ability to withstand losses or adhere to a particular trading program in spite of trading losses are material points which can also adversely affect actual trading results. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profit or losses similar to those shown.
