How Identity Verification Works
One-time. Required. Fast.
Updated over a week ago
Table of contents
Topstep works with third party verification software to verify all Topstep Traders. It's required before your second purchase and takes only minutes to complete. Your information is stored on an encrypted server with Topstep's verification partner.
Additionally, identity verification is also required for requesting Payouts, ensuring compliance with Topstep’s policies.
When Is Identity Verification Required?
After your first purchase, you'll receive an email when it's time to verify. You'll also see a banner on your Topstep Dashboard. You can still log in and trade during this time, but no additional purchases — new accounts, Resets, etc. — will be processed until verification is complete.
What You Need
A valid, unexpired original physical government-issued ID: Passport, Driver's License, or National ID card
A device with a camera (phone or computer)
Not accepted: photocopies, images displayed on another screen, or IDs with sticky notes or coverings.
How to Complete Identity Verification
Prepare your unexpired, physical ID
Open the secure link or QR code from your email
Take clear photos of your ID (front and back where applicable)
Take a selfie
Submit
Once complete, your account status updates to Verification Complete .
Troubleshooting
If you're having issues:
Try a different browser (Chrome, Safari) or a different device
Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then restart your device
Make sure camera and microphone access are enabled
Confirm your ID is unexpired and a physical document (not a photocopy)
Resubmit using the secure link or QR code from your email
Avoid using VPNs or VPS — location discrepancies can cause verification issues
Ensure your device's time zone and location settings are accurate
FAQ
Why am I being asked to verify again?
Topstep may request re-verification if a prior submission needs clarification. Respond promptly and provide the required documents.
Can I trade while waiting to complete Identity Verification?
Yes. You can still log in and trade. However, no additional purchases will be processed until verification is complete.
How long does verification take?
It should take a few minutes if your ID is ready and in good condition.
It says my account is locked. What do I do?
If, after attempting to complete the identity verification process, you see a banner that says your account is fully locked and to contact Trader Support, please reach out here
