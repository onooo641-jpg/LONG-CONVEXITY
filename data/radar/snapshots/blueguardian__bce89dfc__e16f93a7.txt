Zero account rules
Short answer
Zero is a one-phase Futures evaluation built around daily payouts with no payout cap. Hit a 6% profit target while meeting your chosen consistency rule and staying within the drawdown. You build your account at checkout: end-of-day or intraday drawdown for the evaluation, with or without a daily loss limit, and 30%, 40% or 50% consistency. Once funded, you can withdraw profit above your buffer every day, with a 90% profit split. Zero is available on NinjaTrader.
How the consistency rule lets you pass Replay

During the evaluation, your best day can't be more than the consistency limit you chose (30%, 40% or 50%) of your total profit. Each extra profitable day raises the threshold, and you can pass once your best day fits under it. Funded Zero accounts have no consistency rule.

How the end-of-day drawdown option moves Replay
If you chose end-of-day drawdown for your evaluation, your loss limit moves up only when you set a new highest end-of-day closing balance, and it never moves down. Funded Zero accounts always use an intraday trailing drawdown instead, which stops trailing at your starting balance + $100. What happens when you hit the daily loss limit Replay
This applies only if you chose an account with a daily loss limit. Hitting it closes your open trades and locks trading for the rest of the day. It isn't a failed account: trading unlocks at the 5 PM New York rollover.
The evaluation
Profit target
Reach 6% profit while following all the rules. Your account then has a final review, and once approved you become a funded trader. There's no time limit and no minimum number of trading days.
Consistency rule
Your best day can't make up more than your chosen percentage of total profit: 30%, 40% or 50%. If it does, you're not breached. You just keep trading until your best day falls below the limit.
Example: on $50K with 40% consistency and a $2,000 best day, you need at least $5,000 total profit to pass.
Funded Zero accounts have no consistency rule.
Drawdown in the evaluation
End-of-day (EOD) trailing: your loss limit moves up only when you set a new highest end-of-day closing balance. It doesn't follow unrealised profit during the day.
Intraday trailing: your loss limit follows your highest equity in real time, including open profit.
Either way, the limit never moves down, and hitting it is a hard breach.
Daily loss limit (if chosen)
If you chose an account with a daily loss limit, hitting it is a soft breach : open trades are closed, trading locks for the rest of the day, and it unlocks at the 5:00 PM New York rollover. The account isn't failed. The same limit carries into your funded account.
Funded account
Contract limits
You start funded at a lower contract limit, and it scales up as your end-of-day balance grows, the same as Reserve. See Futures contract limits and funded scaling .
Drawdown
Funded Zero accounts always use an intraday trailing drawdown that follows your highest equity, including open profit. It stops trailing once it reaches your starting balance + $100 , and stays fixed from then on.

Payouts
Buffer: each account size has its own buffer balance (see Key numbers). You can withdraw profit above it, and the buffer must stay intact when you request a payout. Example: on $50K the buffer is $52,100, so with a $53,000 balance you can withdraw $900.
How often: every day, once the buffer is cleared. There's no payout cap.
Minimum: $500, by crypto or Rise.
Profit split: 90%.

What happens on an $8,000 day Replay
Every funded Zero account has a daily profit limit. If an account reaches it, that account closes for the day and you choose: take a payout for each account that hit the limit, followed by a cooldown, or move to Live with a Live Bonus for each funded Zero account. Profit above the limit that day isn’t kept. Amounts are in the article.
Resets and inactivity
Evaluation reset: if you breach your evaluation, you can reset it from your dashboard. The reset costs the same as the evaluation's current sale price.
Funded accounts: can't be reset or reactivated after a breach.
Inactivity: evaluation and funded accounts are closed after 10 days without a trade. See Account inactivity rule .
Other rules
News trading: allowed on the evaluation. Not allowed on funded accounts.
Trading hours: 6:00 PM to 4:10 PM New York time. Open positions close automatically at 4:10 PM, so you can't hold overnight. See Trading hours and the trading day .
Stop losses: not mandatory, but every trade needs a defined risk. See Futures risk management rules .
Copy trading: only between accounts you legally own.
Microscalping: less than 50% of your profit may come from trades held under 10 seconds. If more of your profit comes from these trades, that profit is forfeited.
Fees: one-time purchase. No monthly or activation fees.
Zero FAQs
How is Zero different from Reserve? Both have the same profit target, drawdown sizes and contract limits. Zero pays out daily with no cap once you clear your buffer, uses an intraday drawdown when funded, and lets you choose your evaluation drawdown type and a 30%, 40% or 50% consistency rule. Reserve pays up to 50% of profit after 5 winning days, with a cap per payout.
Which option should I pick? End-of-day drawdown gives you more room during the day, because it ignores open profit until the close. A daily loss limit and a stricter consistency rule lower the price. Pick the mix that suits how you trade.
Can I change my options after buying? No. Your options are set at checkout and stay with the account.
Can I reset a Zero account? You can reset a breached evaluation from your dashboard, at the evaluation's current sale price. Funded Zero accounts can't be reset.
Does the daily loss limit fail my account? No. It's a soft breach. Trading locks for the rest of the day and unlocks at the 5:00 PM New York rollover.
Can I trade the news? Yes during the evaluation. Once you're funded, news trading isn't allowed.
How fast can I get paid? Once your balance is above the buffer, you can request a payout every day. Requests are processed within 24 business hours. See How payouts work .
What happens if I make $8,000 in one day? That account is closed and we email you with two options: take a $3,600 payout for each account that hit $8,000, followed by a 30-day cooldown; or move to Live with a Live Bonus for each funded Zero account. Profit above $8,000 that day isn't kept.
How many Zero accounts can I have? Up to 5 funded Zero accounts at a time, of any size.
Which platform can I use? Zero is available on NinjaTrader.
