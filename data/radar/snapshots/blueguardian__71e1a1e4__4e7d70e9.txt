Elite, Reserve & Direct Path to Live
How Elite, Reserve and Direct traders move to Live: who qualifies, how your Live Allocation and starting balance are set, Live rules, Live Bonus and Live Buyout.
Applies to: Futures Updated 23 November 2026
Short answer
The Live Account is the highest level of Blue Guardian Futures: real capital, traded with real CME execution. It can't be purchased. Our Risk Team offers it to funded traders who show exceptional performance.
When you move to Live, we deposit real funds into your Live Account. If you have a pending payout, you can choose to take it as normal or move to Live with that amount as your starting balance. If you don't have a pending payout, you start with the Live Allocation for your account sizes. Live Accounts have a static drawdown, daily uncapped payouts and a 90% profit split.
Once you're offered Live, you can't keep trading your funded accounts. They're closed whichever option you choose.
How funded accounts merge into a Live Account Replay
When you're promoted, your funded accounts combine into one Live Account with a single drawdown. Live Bonus milestones are paid as you reach each multiple.

How do I qualify?
There's no set number of payouts, profit target or timeframe. Our Risk Team reviews funded accounts on an ongoing basis and may offer Live to any trader at any time. We look at consistency, risk management, discipline, profitability, account behaviour and any previous Live history. All decisions are made at the Risk Team's discretion.
If you're identified for Live, our team will reach out to you directly.
Can I keep trading my funded account instead of going Live?
No. Once our Risk Team offers you Live, your funded accounts are closed. You can choose a cash option instead of trading Live (your pending payout, or the Live Buyout if you have no pending payout), but you can't continue trading on the funded accounts.

Allocations from multiple eligible accounts are combined, up to $15,000.
Example: 2 × $50K Elite, 1 × $50K Reserve and 2 × $50K Express accounts, each with at least one payout, give a combined Live Allocation of $10,000.
If your combined Live Allocation is over $15,000, it can be split across two Live Accounts. For example, five $150K accounts ($22,500) become one Live Account of $15,000 and one of $7,500.
Your starting balance
How much we deposit into your Live Account depends on whether you have a pending payout when our team reaches out to you.
If you have a pending payout
You choose one of two options:
Take the payout. It's paid to you as normal and your funded account is closed. A 30-day cooldown then applies before you can buy a new evaluation.
Move to Live. Your pending payout isn't paid out. Instead, the full amount is deposited into your Live Account as your starting balance, in place of the standard Live Allocation.
Your funded account is closed once you've made your choice, so you can't keep trading it with either option.
Example: our team reaches out to you about Live while your $50K funded account has a $1,200 pending payout. If you choose Live, your Live Account starts with a $1,200 balance.
If you don't have a pending payout
We deposit your combined Live Allocation as your starting balance.
Example: our team reaches out to you about Live with one $50K funded account and no pending payout. Your Live Account starts with a $2,000 balance.

Live Buyout (cash instead of Live)
If you don't have a pending payout, you can take a one-time cash payment instead of trading Live: 50% of your combined Live Allocation . Your funded accounts are still closed.
Example: a combined Live Allocation of $10,000 gives a $5,000 Live Buyout.
Only available at the moment you're offered Live, and only once per trader.
A 30-day cooldown applies before you can buy a new evaluation.
Live Bonus (first promotion only)
On your first move to Live, you can earn a bonus of 25% of your combined Live Allocation at each of three profit milestones.
Before any bonus is released , you need:
20 winning days on your Live Account, each with net profit of at least 0.5% of your starting balance.
At least 2 withdrawals of trading profit.

Example: you move to Live with a combined Live Allocation of $10,000 and no pending payout, so your starting balance is $10,000. Once you've had 20 winning days of at least $50 and made 2 profit withdrawals, you receive $2,500 when your net profit reaches $10,000, another $2,500 at $20,000 and another $2,500 at $30,000: $7,500 in total.
Good to know:
Only withdrawals of trading profit count. Withdrawals of your starting balance or bonus funds don't.
Each bonus is added to your Live balance as soon as you meet its conditions.
Any bonus that hasn't been released yet is forfeited if your Live Account is breached.
The Live Bonus can only be earned once, on your first promotion. Zero has its own Live Bonus: see Zero Path to Live .
Live Account rules
Drawdown: static. Your maximum loss limit is set when your Live Account opens and stays fixed. It doesn't move up as your balance grows.
No daily loss limit and no consistency rule , whichever plan your accounts came from.
News trading: allowed.
Hedging: strictly prohibited. Breaking this rule can lead to permanent closure and a permanent ban.
Household rule: while one member of a household trades a Live Account, other members of that household can't trade simulated Blue Guardian Futures accounts.
If you breach your Live Account
Live Accounts can't be reset, bought back or reactivated. After a breach:
Any Live Bonus that hasn't been released yet is forfeited.
A 30-day cooldown applies before you can buy a new evaluation.
After that, you can only trade one funded account at a time.
Repeated breaches through reckless risk-taking may lead to longer cooldowns.
