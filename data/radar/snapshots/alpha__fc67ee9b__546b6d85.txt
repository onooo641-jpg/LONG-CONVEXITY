What's the maximum payout request?
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Also included in the Alpha Futures Payout Policy , but below are the maximum withdrawal requests a trader can make on an Advanced, Premium, Zero, or Direct Qualified Account.
​
​ Advanced Accounts
The maximum withdrawal request on all Advanced Account sizes is $15,000.
This is per request, per account, more than 3x the industry leaders.
​
​ Zero Accounts
25k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $1,000
50k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $1,500
100k Zero Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $2,500
​
​ Standard Accounts
50k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $3,000
100k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $4,000
150k Standard Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $5,000
​
​ Direct Qualified Accounts
25k Direct Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $1,000
50k Direct Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $2,000
100k Direct Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $2,500
150k Direct Qualified Account maximum withdrawal is $3,000
Traders in our LIVE program do not have any maximum withdrawal requests.
