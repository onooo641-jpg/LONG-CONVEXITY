First Login Experience
Upon your initial login after purchasing a challenge, your statistics dashboard welcomes you. This dashboard showcases:
Best and worst trades
Average wins and losses
Trade count, contracts, and win rate
Profit factors, net profit/loss, and target profit
Current equity and time until the End of Day (EOD) drawdown reset
Payout Requirements Tracker
Access to T1 News Folder (See image below)
Dashboard metrics refresh daily after market close between 5:01 PM EST and 11:59 PM EST, providing you with a fresh daily perspective ion your trading performance. For any dashboard issues, please reach out to our customer support team for assistance.
