Starting 4 August 2025 , Fintokei becomes the first FX/CFD prop firm in the world to launch Instant Payouts — a completely redefined way of receiving your performance rewards.
Forget delays and stress. From now on, your payout is just a click away.
✨ What are Instant Payouts?
We have rebuilt the entire payout process from the ground up. With Instant Payouts, you can get your well-earned rewards faster and with zero stress. Here's how it works:
✅ You request a payout with a single click.
✅ Your request is automatically approved within seconds* ( our fastest approval time so far is 2.8 seconds ).
✅ The requested amount is deducted from your account, but the account remains active — you can keep trading.
✅ Lightning-fast transfers: When you use the Walletory wallet-to-wallet method, payouts up to $10,000 USD (or equivalent) are processed instantly - meaning the funds hit your wallet in just a few seconds! For standard bank wires, crypto withdrawals, or any Walletory payouts exceeding the $10,000 limit, your funds are sent within 1 business day (and often even sooner).
*Most requests are processed in 10–20 seconds. In rare cases, approval might take slightly longer.
🌟 Why did we introduce Instant Payouts?
The payout is the ultimate milestone. From your first day in the challenge, you work hard to earn it — following rules, maintaining consistency, and staying profitable.
But with other firms, when the moment finally comes, you're often met with delays: frozen accounts, manual reviews, waiting days for confirmation.
At Fintokei, we believe in celebrating your success — not holding it back. That one click to request your payout should be a moment of pure reward. Like kicking back after a long hike or enjoying a cold drink after a tough day. No queues. No stress. Just your reward, instantly.
📋 What else should you know?
Your account stays active even after you request the payout and before we will send you the funds.
Payouts are processed automatically in trading platforms.
You can request your payout once you meet the eligibility requirements.
