What Is The Stocks Reward Policy?
On Stocks you can receive a reward of up to 70% of the notional profit you generate in the Earning Phase. The key terms are:
Eligibility: complete 3 profitable trading days with at least $100 profit each (they don’t need to be consecutive) before your first reward.
Minimum: you need at least $200 in profit to request a reward.
Frequency: you can request a reward every 14 days. Requests are processed daily, Monday to Friday, within 24 hours on business days.
Maximum: there’s no fixed maximum, but a withdrawal must keep your balance above the trailing Max Drawdown — withdrawing an amount that would drop you below it can trigger account closure.
How Long Do Payouts Take?
Crypto: within the day.
SEPA transfer: 1–3 business days.
International wire / SWIFT: 5–7 business days.
Rewards methods and currencies follow The Trading Pit’s general reward policy.
