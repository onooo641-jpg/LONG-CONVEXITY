Do I have to pay monthly data fees?
Do I have to pay monthly data fees?
The short answer: No hidden fees. Here is how our data policy works.
November 24, 2025
Table of contents
We believe in transparency. You shouldn't have to calculate hidden costs just to execute a trade.
📉 Evaluation Phase:
Real-time Level 1 market data is included in your one-time evaluation fee. You do not need to pay extra to trade.
📈 Funded Accounts:
Once you are a funded trader:
Standard Data: Covered by us.
Professional Data (Level 2/DOM): If you require Depth of Market (DOM) data, you can activate this add-on in your dashboard for $61/mo.
Note: Fees are subject to change based on exchange regulations, but we will always notify you first.
