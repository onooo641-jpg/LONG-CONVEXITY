Review past announcements, updates, and status links.
Search through our FAQs using relevant keywords.
The Help Desk provides guidance but doesn't offer direct trading advice or solutions.
Ensure a clear understanding of all trading rules.
95% of your questions can be answered in the FAQs.
If you've done all this and still need help, feel free to reach out. Happy trading!
