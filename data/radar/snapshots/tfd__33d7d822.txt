Cookies can be categorized as either "session" cookies or "persistent" cookies. Session cookies are temporary and exist only while you are actively browsing the specific website. They are typically deleted or removed automatically when you close your browser. Persistent cookies, on the other hand, remain on your device for a predetermined period, such as six months or one year. Each time you revisit a website that has implemented a persistent cookie, that cookie will continue to function until its expiration date. You also have the option to manually delete persistent cookies, as explained earlier.
The specific types of information collected by a cookie and the subsequent use of that data depend on the cookie's specific type and function. Each time you revisit a website that has implemented a persistent cookie, that cookie will continue to function until its expiration date. You also have the option to manually delete persistent cookies, as explained earlier.
