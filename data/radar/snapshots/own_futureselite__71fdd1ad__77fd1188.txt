Minimum trading days must be real trading
How we review payouts that rest on one trade.
October 13, 2026
Minimum trading days are there to show a repeatable trading process, not one lucky outcome. A standout day is fine. What we review is a payout that rests almost entirely on one trade, followed by token trades to fill the remaining days.
At payout, we look at the share of total profit that came from the single best trade, how profit is spread across trades, and how your trading days compare in size and frequency.
There is no fixed cutoff. Decisions are case by case.
A flagged payout is paused, not denied, while we review it.
We also check your other funded accounts for the same pattern.
