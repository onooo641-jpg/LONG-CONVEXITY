Can I advertise E8Markets via Google Ads?
Basic affiliate guidelines
August 10, 2026
We do not accept discount websites that promote every kind of business, or people making advertisements, or scrape discount websites as this goes against our guidelines. Running paid advertising campaigns (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads) to promote your affiliate link or discount codes is strictly prohibited. Furthermore, discount codes must not be distributed publicly, via spam, forums, coupon sites, or aggregator platforms.
​
The goal of our affiliate program is simple. Each affiliate needs to have their own community, promote our company actively in a fair manner, without any misleading claims, follow our offers and updates, and, most importantly, bring new users in a natural manner. Affiliates must maintain compliance with FTC endorsement guidelines, use only approved promotional materials without altering them, and ensure all marketing practices are transparent and honest.
​
Affiliates must also list at least one valid website or social media channel upon registration through the affiliate dashboard .
