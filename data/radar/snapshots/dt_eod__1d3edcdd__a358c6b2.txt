Live payouts: 80/20 split, $1,000 buffer, when payouts unlock and how they're processed.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
Live accounts pay an 80/20 profit split , with no minimum daily profit or consistency rule. Requests start at $500 , in $50 increments.
When you can request
Payouts unlock once your drawdown has locked at your starting balance. You can withdraw any profit above a $1,000 buffer without limit. You can trade into the buffer, but not withdraw it. This is to guarantee at least $1000 of trading room after a payout.

How it's processed
You request a payout from your Dashboard.
The firm locks the account.
The payout is debited and approved, and sent through Plane.
The account is ready to trade again.
Requests are processed between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM ET on business days; later requests are processed the next business day. If your balance drops below starting balance + $1,000 before processing, the payout is skipped and you can request again once you're back above it.
Firm-invited live accounts
These most likely use an EOD or static drawdown and follow the same steps. If your EOD drawdown hasn't locked yet, step 3 happens after it updates in Rithmic between 5:00 and 6:00 PM ET.
