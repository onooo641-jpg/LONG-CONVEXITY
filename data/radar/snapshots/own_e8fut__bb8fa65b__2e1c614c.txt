Do you offer certificates for passing your challenge?
How can you access your Performance Certificate?
August 13, 2026
Table of contents
We believe that every milestone or progress, no matter how small, should be celebrated with your friends, family, or community. This is why we offer a variety of certificates that show and prove your achievements, such as:
​
1. Certificate for passing the Challenge account
Upon the completion of the SimFi™ Challenge account, you can download your certificate from the account overview section in your dashboard. The certificate is not immediately accessible after completing the profit target. You can access it only if your SimFi™ Performance account is created, which usually takes 30-120 minutes after finishing the Challenge stage.
2. Payout certificate and Total Payout certificate
To claim your payout certificates either for a payout on a specific account or a total payout requested with E8, navigate to the Payout History section of your dashboard. From there, you can download a certificate for each payout milestone achieved in your accounts.
Validation of each certificate
Each certificate comes with a unique QR code and number. Thanks to this, you can verify if the certificates you see from other users are valid and their achievements are not fabricated.
