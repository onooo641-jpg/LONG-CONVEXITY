01 What is Express to Live and why is it the fastest path to a LIVE funded account?
The fastest path from signup to a LIVE funded account at Phidias. Express to Live is available in 25K, 50K, 100K, and 150K sizes with static drawdown that never trails, zero minimum trading days, and your very first payout converts the Cash account straight to LIVE with Dorman Trading. The 25K Express to Live can pass in a single trading session and reach the first payout on Day 1.
02 What is the difference between static, EOD, and trailing drawdown?
Static never moves. EOD updates at close. Trailing moves in real time — and it's the #1 reason traders get breached at other firms. Static drawdown (Express to Live accounts) is a fixed dollar amount. EOD drawdown (Fundamental and Premium accounts) is calculated at market close — you can profit $2,000 during the session and your floor stays the same until the day ends. Trailing drawdown (used by most competitors) moves up with every dollar of unrealized profit. A $500 win followed by a $600 pullback can breach your account even though you were net positive.
03 How fast does Phidias actually process payouts?
90% of payouts processed under 30 minutes. Always within 24 hours. With the 25K Express to Live (no minimum trading days), the entire path from signup to first payout can happen on Day 1. Payouts are processed to your Phidias Wallet, which you can withdraw to your bank or reinvest into a new account instantly.
04 Have you ever denied a payout?
No. Zero payouts denied in Phidias history. Ever. Our rules are objective, not subjective. There is no judgment call that can block your payout. You either met the criteria or you did not. Everything is on one dedicated rules page, visible before you buy.
05 What does "no buffer" mean?
Your profits are never locked above an artificial line. At most firms, a buffer means you start at $50,000 and can only withdraw once your balance is above $52,600 — those $2,600 are locked. At Phidias, you do have a threshold to request a payout, but you can withdraw into that threshold. No hidden profit traps.
06 How are Phidias's incentives different from other firms?
Top traders graduate to real LIVE accounts with Dorman Trading. When you succeed, we succeed. Most prop firms generate revenue primarily from evaluation fees — when traders fail and repurchase, that is revenue. At Phidias, our business model is aligned with your success. This is why our rules are designed for trader success, not to keep you stuck in evaluations.
07 Can I hold positions overnight and over the weekend?
Yes — with Premium accounts. Our data shows swing traders have an 83% higher success rate. Most prop firms force you to close all positions before market close. At Phidias, Premium accounts (50K, 100K, 150K) let you hold overnight and over the weekend. This is based on our data from 50,000+ traders — swing strategies consistently outperform when given room to breathe.
08 What is your support like?
Average resolution: under 15 minutes. English, French, and Spanish. Unlike firms where support quality drops once you start requesting payouts, we invest more in supporting funded traders because their success is directly tied to ours. You'll never feel like support is trying to slow you down.
09 What platforms can I trade on?
Rithmic, Tradovate, and DeepCharts. You're never locked into a proprietary platform. Tradovate connects natively to TradingView and enables NinjaTrader with its own built-in data feed. Rithmic / R|Trader provides institutional-grade data feeds and direct access execution. DeepCharts gives you advanced orderflow tools available on web, desktop, and mobile.
10 What is the profit split on Phidias accounts?
Up to 100% profit split on Premium accounts. Most prop firms cap traders at 80% or 90%. Express to Live and Fundamental accounts use a fixed 80/20 profit split (you keep 80%). Premium accounts use a progressive split that climbs from 75% to 100% as you stack payouts: 75% on payout 1, 80% on payout 2, 85% on payout 3, 90% on payout 4, and 100% from payout 5 onwards . After 5 payouts on a Premium account, you keep every dollar of profit you generate.
