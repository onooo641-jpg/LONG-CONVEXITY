S2F Daily Loss Limit by size and what happens when you hit it.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
S2F accounts have a Daily Loss Limit (DLL) : the most you can lose in one trading day. It's separate from the max drawdown.
Size
Daily Loss Limit
25K
None
50K
$1,250
150K
$3,750
If you hit it
Open positions are closed and the account is Paused until the next trading day starts (6:00 PM ET). It isn't failed.
The DLL is measured from your balance at the start of the day. It doesn't trail intraday.
