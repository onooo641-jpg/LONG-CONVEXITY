Is there a minimum number of referrals needed to remain active?
Minimum Referrals
September 25, 2026
No , there's no minimum referral requirement to stay in the affiliate program. Whether you refer one person or hundreds, your account stays active and your referral link keeps working.
​
The only threshold that applies is for payouts :
​
The $200 payout minimum
​
You can request a withdrawal once your affiliate balance reaches $200 USD . This isn't a condition for staying in the program, it just means commissions below $200 remain in your balance and keep building until you reach the threshold. Nothing is lost while you get there.
​
Your referral count doesn't affect your status — but it does affect your rate
There's no minimum to remain active, but the total number of referrals you generate determines your commission tier:
Tier 1: 15% (0–20 referrals)
Tier 2: 18% (20–100 referrals)
Tier 3: 20% (100+ referrals)
So more referrals mean a higher commission rate, but even at zero referrals, your account stays open and ready.
