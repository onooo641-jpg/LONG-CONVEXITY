BrightPlus vs BrightCore: which plan is right for me?
A side-by-side comparison of both plans, with prices, examples and a quick way to choose.
October 26, 2026
Table of contents
Both plans give you the same profit target, the same Max Loss Limit amount, the same contracts and the same payout caps. The difference is how the drawdown moves and how payouts are unlocked . BrightPlus is the faster plan, with daily payouts . BrightCore is the best value plan, with an end-of-day drawdown.

Frequently asked questions
Is the profit target different?
No. It is 6% of the account on both plans.
​
Is the Max Loss Limit amount different?
No. The amount is the same for each size. What changes is how the floor moves. On BrightCore it updates once a day, while the market is closed, between 4:45 PM and 5:59 PM ET.
​
Which plan has daily payouts?
Only BrightPlus. You can request up to one approved payout per trading day, and after each payout you need 1% fresh profit of the starting balance to request the next one. BrightCore has no daily payouts: it unlocks payouts by winning days.
​
Is the maximum payout request different on each plan?
Yes. The payout cap is the same on both plans. On BrightPlus you can request the balance above the buffer, up to the cap, with no limit based on your profit. On BrightCore you can also request at most 50% of your current profit (your balance minus your starting balance), and your balance must stay at or above your starting balance + $100. The maximum you can request is the lowest of the limits of your plan.
​
Which plan has the stricter Consistency Rule?
BrightCore. Your best day can be at most 40% of your total profit, against 50% on BrightPlus.
​
Can I pass faster on one plan?
The fastest possible pass is 2 trading days on BrightPlus and 3 trading days on BrightCore.
​
Can I hold trades overnight on BrightCore?
No. On both plans all positions close automatically at 4:45 PM ET.
