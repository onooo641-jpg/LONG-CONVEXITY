What Is The Futures Instant Reward Policy?
To be eligible for a payout, you must not violate any Trading Rules, Terms & Conditions, or The Trading Pit Account Agreement.
On Futures Instant you receive 90% of the profits you generate. To request a reward, you must complete 5 trading days. Once the requirements are met, you can request payouts every 5 days for any amount above $500, capped by the reward limits below.
The key payout terms are:
• Profit split: 90%.
• Minimum: payouts must be above $500.
• Trading days: at least 5 trading days before your first reward. A trading day is any day you place at least one trade.
• Frequency: once eligible, you can request a payout every 5 days.
• Profit target: $3,000 on a $50,000 account or $6,000 on a $100,000 account — or higher if the Consistency Rule requires it.
• Consistency: your biggest profitable day must not exceed 20% of your total profit before you request a payout.
• Open positions: all positions must be closed when you request a reward.

Profit Target (Payout Requirement)
Futures Instant has no challenge phase, so the Profit Target isn't a pass/fail goal. It's the profit you need in each payout cycle before you can request a payout.
Base target per payout cycle:
• $50K account: $3,000
• $100K account: $6,000
How your target is set:
Your target is whichever of these two is higher:
1. The base target for your account size, or
2. Your best trading day ÷ 0.20 (the amount the 20% Consistency Rule requires)
The target resets after every payout:
Once your payout is approved, a new cycle starts. Your profit count goes back to $0, the consistency calculation starts over, and your target returns to the base amount. Each payout is based only on profit made in that cycle.
Example:
• Cycle 1: you make $3,000 → payout approved
• Cycle 2 begins → profit resets to $0 → you need $3,000 again before your next payout
Your dashboard always shows your current target , so you can see exactly what you need for your next payout at any time.
How Are Rewards Paid?
Rewards are paid by bank wire or cryptocurrency. Payouts are disbursed to your payment method within 1 busines day.
A Rewards Certificate is generated for each reward and is available in your Client Area.
