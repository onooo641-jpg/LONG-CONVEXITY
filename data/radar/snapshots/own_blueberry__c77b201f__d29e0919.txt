Maximum Contract Limits
Risk management is key. We place hard caps on the number of contracts you can open simultaneously to protect your account from over-leveraging.
The Limits:
Account Size

Max Micro Contracts

⚠️ Important Rules:
No Mixing to Exceed Caps: You can mix Minis and Micros (e.g., 1 Mini + 10 Micros on a 50k), but you cannot exceed the total equivalent leverage.
Violation: Attempting to open more than the allowed limit will result in the order being rejected by the platform.
