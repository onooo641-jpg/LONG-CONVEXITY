What is a futures prop evaluation?
A futures prop evaluation is the assessment stage a proprietary trading firm uses to identify disciplined traders before granting access to simulated futures funds on a Qualified Account. At Alpha Futures, passing the evaluation is the gateway to becoming a qualified futures trader and earning performance fee on eligible simulated profits.
Alpha Futures uses the term evaluation for the assessment stage. Many traders call the same step a futures prop challenge — Alpha Futures uses evaluation language, but the profit targets, maximum loss limit (MLL) rules, and simulated environment are identical. For the full model, start with our What is a Futures Prop Firm? (2026 guide) .
How a futures prop evaluation works
Profit target — a percentage gain you must reach to pass; it varies by plan and account size.
Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) — the floor that closes the account if breached. Alpha Futures uses end-of-day trailing MLL on all plans, not intraday trailing.
Consistency rule — caps how much of total profit can come from one session: 50% on Standard evaluations, 40% on Advanced evaluations, and none on Zero evaluations.
Minimum trading days and contract limits apply by plan — verify on the help centre.
Simulated funds only — you trade in a simulated futures environment; your personal savings beyond the fee are not at risk.
Futures prop challenge vs evaluation
"Futures prop challenge" is industry slang for the same evaluation step. The difference is terminology, not mechanics. Alpha Futures runs one-step evaluations on Zero, Advanced, and Premium — there is no multi-phase combine unless rules change. On Zero there is no consistency rule on the evaluation, so a strong single session can qualify you, provided the MLL holds.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a futures prop evaluation?
A structured assessment on simulated futures funds. You must hit a profit target without breaching the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) or daily rules. Passing qualifies you for a Qualified Account where you may earn performance fees.
What is a futures prop challenge?
Industry slang for the same evaluation step. Alpha Futures uses evaluation language; the profit targets, MLL rules, and simulated environment are identical.
What happens if you fail a futures prop evaluation?
If you breach the MLL, daily rules, or other conditions, the simulated account closes. You lose the evaluation fee and must purchase a new assessment or reset. Your personal savings beyond the fee are not at risk.
Is a futures prop evaluation on real money?
No. Trading happens on simulated funds in a simulated futures environment. Performance fees paid to Qualified Traders are real money on published terms, but the evaluation balance itself is simulated.
How do I pass a futures prop evaluation at Alpha Futures?
Hit the profit target without breaching the end-of-day trailing MLL. On Standard evaluations the largest day must stay within 50% of net profit; on Advanced it must stay within 40%. Zero evaluations have no consistency rule.
Is the Alpha Futures evaluation one-step or two-step?
Alpha Futures runs one-step evaluations on Zero, Standard, and Advanced — there is no multi-phase combine unless rules change.
