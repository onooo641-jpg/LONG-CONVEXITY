The LucidMaxx cooldown rule explained: when cooldowns apply, how long they last, and how they affect your evaluation progress and next steps.

The LucidMaxx Cycle
The goal is to create a pathway for consistent traders to stay with Lucid long-term.
LucidMaxx is designed as a repeatable cycle. If a trader earns profits above the live drawdown, there is no limit to how many times a trader may return to the LucidMaxx evaluation.
Cool Down
There is a 2-week cooldown period after a trader blows their LucidMaxx live account.
The cooldown begins only after the final live account is blown
If you have multiple live accounts, the cooldown will not start until all live accounts are closed
Important:
If you still have active LucidMaxx evaluations, you may continue trading and even move back to live
The cooldown will only begin once your last active live account is closed
Fullporting Near Live Max Loss Limit
Traders who engage in reckless trading behavior, such as full porting positions near the live account Max Loss Limit, may face additional consequences.
If trading activity causes drawdown to exceed the defined Max Loss Limit, this may result in:
Extended cooldown periods
Permanent restriction from the program
These decisions are made at the discretion of the Lucid risk team.
LucidMaxx is intended to reward profitable and disciplined traders. Consistent risk management is required to maintain access to the program.
