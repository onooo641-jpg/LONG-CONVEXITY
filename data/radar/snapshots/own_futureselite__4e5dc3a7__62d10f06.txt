Maximum Daily Profit
The moment your net profit for the day reaches the cap, your Sim Funded account goes dormant and your live account is created automatically, with no review step. Profit above the cap on that day is forfeited.

Below the cap, daily payouts run as normal. The cap is a promotion trigger, not a payout ceiling.
Days don't roll over: each day's cap is independent.
The Risk Team can also offer a live account at any time based on consistent payout performance.
