Is there any inactivity rule?
Is there any inactivity rule?
September 23, 2026
While there is no maximum or minimum time limit , please be aware of our Inactivity Rule to keep your account active:
After 60 or 7 days of inactivity (Depending on the market), the account will be closed
You need to place and close at least one trade within this 7 or 60-day period.
This will also affect newly purchased accounts without any trading history
​
60 Day inactivity applies to all Classic Markets/Perpetual Futures accounts.
7 Day inactivity applies to all Futures accounts.
There is no minimum lot size requirement to maintain your account. Even a micro-trade of 0.1 lots counts as a qualifying trade to prevent deactivation.
If you are planning to travel and you know your account will be inactive for the period mentioned above, you are allowed to do so, but you must contact us at Support@E8markets.com before the travel happens.
