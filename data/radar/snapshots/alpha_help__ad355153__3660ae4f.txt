How to log into your WealthCharts Account
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
May 7, 2026
Alpha Futures is excited to offer WealthCharts , a powerful web based platform designed by real, live fund trading champions.​
To use this integration, you must have an Evaluation, or Qualified Account utilizing WealthCharts.
​
First, check your emails from [email protected] for your login credentials
You can launch WealthCharts directly from your user dashboard with the "Launch WealthCharts" button. You may also choose to sign in directly using this link .
Once you have launched WealthCharts, you will be prompted to sign in with the credentials sent to your email.
Next, you will be prompted to set your language preference
Following this you will be required to set your permanent password.
Next, you will be asked to set your display name
Once logged in, you MUST sign your Non-Professional Agreement before your account will receive real-time data or be able to trade on WealthCharts.
Once completed, you will be required to confirm your account
After confirming your account, you must sign your WealthCharts trading disclaimer
Once complete, you are free to select Alpha Futures from the list of prop firm options, and select your desired trading account
You are now successfully connected and can enjoy trading your Alpha Futures Account(s) on WealthCharts!
