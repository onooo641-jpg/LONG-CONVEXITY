What is Payout On Demand?

Most platforms make you wait. Payout On Demand does not.
​
Once you reach the SimFi Performance Account stage on E8 One or E8 Signature and meet the eligibility conditions , you can request a payout at any time - no fixed schedule, no unnecessary waiting.
​ Payout On Demand was introduced to reward the right trading behaviours:
Consistent performance over time
Disciplined risk management
Clean, verifiable performance data
How it works:
To take advantage of the Payout On Demand feature, you must satisfy a few rules (which vary depending on the product you have chosen).
On demand for For the E8 One :
40% Best Day rule : With a 40% best-day rule, you must ensure that no single trading day exceeds more than 40% of your total generated profits.
Your net profit (payout) must be greater than 50% of your daily drawdown.
Example: If you have a $100,000 account and your daily drawdown is 4%, the minimum amount required for a payout is $2,000.01
The earliest first payout (E8 One) can be requested is 3 days from the start of your trading period in the performance stage. This is not a separate waiting rule - it is simply how the Best Day Rule works in practice, as 3 days is the fastest the math can work in your favour.
On-demand does not apply to E8 Pro and E8 Zero models, as these products have daily payouts. With E8 Pro, the minimum is 1%, and with E8 Zero, it's $100.
On-demand rules for the E8 Signature:
35% Best Day rule : With a 35% best-day rule, you must ensure that no single trading day exceeds more than 35% of your total generated profits.
Minimum payout is $100.
​
Amounts are taken from your net profit. Meaning, with 80% payout, you need to request at least $125.
Minimum profitable days between each payout: 5 (does not apply for first payout)
A profitable day is considered as a day where the realized closed PnL equals to 0.3% or more.
You also have to achieve profitable days between each payout cycle. After requesting a payout, your previously achieved profitable trading days will reset to 0.
Payout buffer : Before you can start requesting payout, you need to leave a buffer in your account in the same size as your EOD Dynamic Drawdown.The buffer can not be requested. The reason for this is to protect trades from violating EOD by requesting full profit from the account.
​
If your EOD is 4% and your initial balance $100,000, the buffer is $4000.
