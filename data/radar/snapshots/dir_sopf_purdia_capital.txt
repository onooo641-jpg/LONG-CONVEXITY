News trading
Allowed
Yes. Purdia Capital places no news trading restrictions on its sim accounts, so you can trade through major economic releases. Volatility and slippage around news are your own risk.
Swing trading
Not allowed
Copy trading
Allowed
Yes. Purdia Capital permits trade copiers, including third-party software, for copying trades between your personal accounts. You must actively monitor and manage the accounts; set-and-forget automation is not permitted.
Algorithmic trading
Conditional
Semi-automated tools are permitted while the trader keeps active oversight and monitors every trade and position. Fully automated, unattended and set-and-forget systems are strictly prohibited.
Scalping
Allowed
Yes. Purdia Capital allows scalping, including in micro contracts, with no minimum holding time for trades. Your account’s drawdown and position limits still apply.
Dollar cost averaging
Allowed
Yes. Purdia Capital allows dollar-cost averaging into positions as part of your strategy. Your account’s drawdown and position limits still apply to the full position.
Hedging
Not allowed
High frequency trading
Allowed
Scalping and micro scalping are permitted with no minimum hold-time requirement, though latency abuse can trigger review.
VPN use
Conditional
Prohibited only where a VPN or proxy is used to circumvent household, address, IP, device or country limits. Purdia otherwise treats VPN use as ordinary and advises disabling one only to fix latency.
VPS use
Allowed
KYC
Applies
Yes. Purdia requires identity verification and KYC before you get a Live Funded Account through its broker, Tradovate. Expect to provide a government-issued photo ID, proof of residence and tax identification information. The broker makes the final approval decision, and you trade a Temp SFA while onboarding is completed.
Evaluation time limit
None
Inactivity rule
Applies
No genuine trading activity for 30 days marks the account inactive. The trader then has 30 days to request reactivation before it is permanently disabled.
Clear path to live
Published
Every product runs Evaluation or Instant Funding, then Sim Funded, then Temp Sim Funded, then a genuine live brokerage account.
