All Collections Trading Rules and Guidelines Trading Hours, Holidays and Overnight Positions Trading Hours, Holidays and Overnight Positions
When the trading day starts and ends, how holidays are handled, and the rules for overnight positions, auto-liquidation and news.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
The trading day runs from 6:00 PM ET to 4:59 PM ET the next day. Sunday's 6:00 PM ET session counts as part of Monday.
Market close and holidays
Close trades by 4:59 PM ET. Some markets, such as agricultural futures, close earlier.
On a market holiday, the session isn't a trading day and its trades count toward the next trading day.
Shortened sessions that aren't holidays, such as Black Friday and Christmas Eve, count as normal trading days.
Check the CME Group Holiday Calendar for early closes.
Overnight and weekend positions
Holding positions overnight or over the weekend is allowed but not recommended, because of mark-to-market adjustments. DayTraders doesn't close your positions for you at the end of the session.
Auto-liquidation
There's no firm-set liquidation time. Rithmic auto-liquidates only when you hit your drawdown level or Daily Loss Limit, and the account shows "Admin Only" until it resets. You can set your own time in rTrader Pro: right-click the account, choose View risk parameters and set the auto-liquidation time. It doesn't adjust for daylight saving time.
News trading
Trading around news is allowed but not recommended. Fast markets can hit daily price limits or cause platform lag, and losses from these conditions aren't covered.
