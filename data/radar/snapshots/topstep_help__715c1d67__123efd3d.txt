Quantower Connection Instructions
Quantower connects through TopstepX credentials. No API access needed.
June 18, 2026
Table of contents
Connect using the Topstep Dashboard by selecting TopstepX as your platform. This links your account to ProjectX data — a direct CME feed for faster, cleaner market data with 10:1 Micro to Mini contract conversions.
Quantower via TopstepX is available for the Trading Combine® and Express Funded Account® (XFA) only.
Before You Start
Log into TopstepX™ and sign all required platform and data agreements before launching Quantower. Skipping this step will cause a "Login Blocked" error.
No API access required — just your TopstepX™ credentials.
If prompted to install .NET Framework during setup, allow the installation.
Installing Quantower for the First Time
Download Quantower and click Extract when prompted. The platform launches with DX Feed by default — leave it.
Click Connections at the top of the platform.
Disconnect from DX Feed.
Select ProjectX from the available connections.
In the Server dropdown, choose TopstepX .
Enter your TopstepX™ credentials and log in.
Already Have Quantower Installed?
Open Quantower and click Connections .
Disconnect from any active connection.
Select ProjectX from the available connections.
In the Server dropdown, choose TopstepX .
Enter your TopstepX™ credentials and log in.
