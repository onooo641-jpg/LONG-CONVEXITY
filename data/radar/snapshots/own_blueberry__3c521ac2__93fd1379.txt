Do I need to use a Stop Loss?
🛡️ Not mandatory, but you are 100% responsible for your risk.
September 15, 2026
Table of contents
Stop Loss Policy
We do not force you to place a hard StopLoss (SL) on every ticket, but we highly recommend it.
​
Why use one?
Protection: In the event of a platform disconnect, internet outage, or extreme market flash crash, a hard stop loss is your only protection against hitting your Max Drawdown limit instantly.
​
Liability: If a sudden move blows your account because you didn't have an SL, the breach is valid and cannot be reversed.
Best Practice
Always protect your downside. A stop loss is cheaper than a new evaluation.
