Accelerated Account Specifications
The Accelerated account is for disciplined traders who want the lowest cost of entry. It features a live trailing drawdown.
​
The Rules:
Drawdown Type: Trailing (Intraday)
Time to Complete: 30 Days
Minimum Trading Days: 1 Day (Challenge), 5 Days (Funded) (To count as a valid minimum trading day, you must generate a net profit of at least $200 on that day).
Daily Pause: None, unless you want it and add it yourself.
