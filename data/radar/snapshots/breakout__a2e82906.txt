Any registered Breakout user (“Referrer”) can generate and share a unique referral link through the Breakout customer portal. A new customer who signs up through that link (“Referred Friend”) receives 5% off the purchase price of their first evaluation. No form submission, evaluation purchase or identity verification is required for a Referrer to generate or share a referral link. Before Referral Credit is issued, the Referrer must complete identity verification and satisfy Kraken’s geo-eligibility check (see Section 8). A Referrer in an excluded jurisdiction at the time of credit issuance will not receive Referral Credit.
5. Reward
The Referrer earns USD $25 credit (“Referral Credit”) for each Qualified Referral. A “Qualified Referral” is a Referred Friend who completes KYC verification.

Following a Qualified Referral, Referral Credit is created in a pending state and held for review for 14 days, during which it is not spendable. It is then issued to the Referrer’s account and becomes spendable. Referral Credit expires 30 days after it becomes spendable if unused, automatically and without extension, reissuance, or reactivation.
Referral Credit cannot be combined with discount codes. Where a discount code and Referral Credit would both otherwise apply to the same purchase, the discount code applies instead.
6. Caps
Maximum $500 in Referral Credit per Referrer per calendar month, equivalent to 20 Qualified Referrals. Credits stack toward this monthly cap only, subject to Section 5.
7. Kraken Account and KYC Requirement
To receive and use Referral Credit, a Referrer must have a Breakout account in good standing and must satisfy Kraken’s geo-eligibility check at the time of credit issuance (see Section 8). A Referred Friend must complete KYC verification for a referral to qualify, plus any additional verification required by Kraken/POL or applicable law.
