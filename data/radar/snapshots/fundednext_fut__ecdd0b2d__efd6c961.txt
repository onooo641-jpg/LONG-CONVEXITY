4
Swap Fee
An interest fee you either pay or earn when you hold a position overnight or over the weekend.
Calculated on the interest rate difference between the two currencies in the pair. Swap fee is included in both DLL and MLL calculations. Triple-swap days vary by instrument and platform, check the symbol specs to see when they apply.

5
Overnight & Weekend Holding
Both overnight and weekend holding are allowed across every CFDs account, at every stage. Swap charges apply on positions held overnight or over the weekend.
All CFDs accounts, all stages : Overnight and weekend holding allowed.
Swap charges apply : Swap fees on overnight and weekend positions.
Triple swap days : ⁦3x⁩ swap is charged on Wednesday for Forex and Commodities, and on Friday for Indices, Crypto and Stock. A position held through Wednesday night, for example, accumulates three days of swap on Forex pairs.
