All-in price, not the sticker
The evaluation fee after the discount code, plus the activation fee some firms only show at checkout. The board prices every plan that way, so a cheap-looking evaluation with an activation fee behind it ranks where it belongs.

Drawdown type
End-of-day drawdown only moves at the close. Intraday trailing moves with every tick and can stop you out of a winning trade. Static never moves. It is the rule most traders misjudge, and it matters more than the profit target.
End-of-day drawdown Static drawdown No trailing drawdown EOD only on the board

Payout speed
Days to $ is how soon you can request the first payout after passing, and the payout cycle is how often after that. Some firms pay within a trading day and others hold you for weeks. Sort the board by Days to $ if speed matters.

Profit split and scaling
Most firms let you keep the bulk of the profit, and many hand over the whole first payout before the split starts. A scaling plan then adds contracts as the account grows. Together they decide what a payout is actually worth to you.

Evaluation or instant funding
An evaluation is cheaper to start and slower to pay. Instant funding skips the test for a higher price and tighter rules from day one. Neither is better in general. The board prices both, and the lists show which firms sell each.

Futures prop firm questions , answered.
Straight answers from the board data, not opinions.
Q 1 What is a futures prop firm?
+ − A futures prop firm funds traders who prove they can manage risk. You pay for an evaluation, hit its profit target, and the firm backs you with its capital for a share of the profit. The board compares every evaluation by its real cost, and what is a prop firm walks through how it works, step by step.
Q 2 Evaluation vs instant funding: what is the difference?
+ − An evaluation is a test you pass before you trade firm capital. Instant funding skips the test for a higher upfront price and usually tighter rules from day one. Which route is cheaper depends on those rules, so the board prices both side by side, activation fee included.
Q 3 How much does a futures prop firm account really cost?
+ − More than the sticker. The real number is the evaluation fee after the discount code, plus any activation fee , which some firms only reveal at checkout, plus resets if you fail. The board prices every account plan all-in with the code applied, so the number you compare is the number you pay.
Q 4 Which futures prop firm is cheapest right now?
+ − The board answers that live: sort it by all-in price and the cheapest account plan is the top row, with its discount code applied and any activation fee added. It changes as codes rotate, so sort it again before you buy. For the no-activation-fee firms alone, see that list .
Q 5 What prop firm discount codes are active right now?
+ − SOPF is the universal code and it works at most firms on the board. Every code we verify is listed on the promos page with its discount, its end date and the account plans it covers, and each firm's own page prices every account with the code applied. Codes rotate, so check the date.
Q 6 Which prop firms have no consistency rule?
+ − A consistency rule caps how much of your profit target can come from one day, so a single big session cannot pass you. The firms that skip it entirely are on the no consistency rule list , and the board's Consistency column shows the rule for every account plan so you can filter it out.
Q 7 Which futures prop firms have no activation fee?
+ − Several firms charge nothing between passing and trading the funded account. The rest charge a one-off or monthly activation fee . Every all-in price on the board already includes that fee, so the no-activation firms rank honestly against the cheaper-looking ones. The full set is on the no activation fee list .
Q 8 Which futures prop firms offer instant funding?
+ − Instant funding , also sold as straight to funded, skips the evaluation for a higher upfront price. The firms that sell it are on the instant funding list , cheapest first, and on the board each instant account sits next to its evaluation twin so you can see what skipping the test really costs.
