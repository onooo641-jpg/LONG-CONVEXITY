What Is Stock Trading With The Trading Pit?
You trade standardised, exchange-traded shares on global exchanges, during regular exchange hours. There is no leverage on stocks, and no overnight financing fees — the costs are the broker commission and exchange fees. Stocks trading suits intraday, day-trading strategies, as positions are not held overnight.
What Is The Stocks Challenge And How Does It Work?
The Stocks Challenge is our entry-level stock programme. You pay an entry fee and receive simulated trading capital to trade popular stocks. Reach the Profit Target while following the rules, and you move to the Earning Phase, where you can request rewards on the profit you generate. All trading takes place on demo accounts using simulated funds — there is no time limit on the Challenge.
