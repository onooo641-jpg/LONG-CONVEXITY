What is LucidMaxx
LucidMaxx is our exclusive eval-to-live daily payout plan reserved for our most consistent and profitable traders.
This plan is not available to the general public. It can only be purchased by traders who have earned LucidMaxx status through demonstrated performance.
LucidMaxx is built to offer a direct path to live capital with daily, uncapped payout potential.
Achieving LucidMaxx Status
LucidMaxx status is reserved for traders who demonstrate exceptional consistency and profitability.
Our quantitative risk team uses defined performance criteria to identify traders who qualify. Once a trader meets the required benchmarks:
They will be notified directly via email
They will be granted access to purchase the LucidMaxx evaluation
LucidMaxx status is earned, not requested.
LucidMaxx Benefits

Daily payouts

Up to 5 accounts
No DLL (No Daily Loss Limit)
Static live drawdown

Live starting balance, withdrawable from day 1

LucidMaxx Live
The Live account has a static drawdown.
The Live account starts with a balance, as per the table below, which is withdrawable from day 1.
The trader may be eligible to earn a repeatable bonus, per account, based on their Live performance. To earn the Live Bonus, a trader must meet 2 objectives:
Generate Live Profits above the starting balance, equal to the Profit target outlined below.
Complete 20 trading days
The profit target calculation is not negatively impacted by taking payouts
A trading day is defined as 1+ trade taken, generating +$1/-$1 P&L net of fees at the end of the trading session. We strongly encourage legitimate trading activity, and exploitation of this bonus system, via "contract flipping" or similar, may lead to further review.

Cashout
A full cashout option has been introduced for Maxxers who do not yet feel comfortable trading live capital. This cashout is subject to a 90/10 profit split. If no choice to cashout is made within 24 hours of receiving the move to live email, your accounts will automatically move to Live.
Cooldown
Traders who have been moved live may return to purchase a new evaluation after blowing a live account or requesting full cashout.
Standard cooldown period: 2 weeks
Traders who repeatedly blow live accounts due to reckless or "yolo" behavior may be subject to longer cooldowns at the discretion of the risk team
There is no cooldown upon breaching an account in the Pre-Live stage
LucidMaxx FAQs
Can I buy a LucidMaxx account directly?
No. LucidMaxx can only be accessed by traders who have been invited by the Lucid risk team based on their performance history. There is no public purchase option.
How long does it take to earn LucidMaxx status?
There is no set timeline. The risk team evaluates traders based on their overall track record, not a fixed number of payouts or days.
Does LucidMaxx use real capital upon passing?
Yes. LucidMaxx accounts trade live capital from the moment the evaluation and pre-live stages are passed.
Can I withdraw from my starting balance on day 1?
Yes.
What is the profit split on LucidMaxx live accounts?
90/10. You keep 90% from the first payout onward.
How many LucidMaxx accounts can I hold?
Up to 5 simultaneous accounts.
Can I purchase/trade LucidMaxx accounts whilst I have an active Live account?
You may trade and reset your existing LucidMaxx accounts alongside your Live account, however, you may not purchase new LucidMaxx accounts whilst you hold an active Live account.
