A few things to know
The boards, explained.
How are the Signature boards ranked? Fastest Payout ranks the time from purchase to first approved payout. Most Payouts in a Day ranks approved payouts on one account in one day, New York time. Both count Signature accounts only.
When do the boards update? Once a day. Today's approved payouts show after the daily update.
When can I request a payout? Payouts are on demand, paid at an 80% profit split. The minimum is $100 on the $5K account and $250 on the $10K and $20K. Payouts are subject to plan terms, eligibility, and review.
