What is E8 Pro?
This is our 1-Step challenge - designed exclusively for traders looking for freedom in daily payouts, static drawdown with NO consistency rules, or payout caps.
Available markets:
Classic Markets, Perpetual Futures.
​
​ The market can be selected during checkout, and it affect palet of available instruments and the structure of fees or functions of the trading platform. Other aspects, like drawdowns and overall trading rules, payout mechanism remain the same.
SimFi™ Challenge rules
8% Profit Target - Closed profit in your simulated trading account.
Unlimited Trading Days - There is no time limit but you need to place and close at least one trade each 60 days.
2% Daily Profit cap - In each day, only 2% counts toward the target or profit. You can make more than 2% in a day, but only 2% of the initial balance per day counts toward the profit target or profit.
For a $100,000 account, that’s $2,000 profit cap per day.
2.5% Daily Drawdown – Your account must not lose more than 2.5% of your initial balance in a single day. The limit you can not exceed is calculated from your starting balance.
8% Static drawdown – A fixed loss limit based on your initial balance. It never moves, except when a first payout is processed.
SimFI™ Performance rules
You will get to the SimFI™ Performance stage once you complete SimFi™ Challenge account by following all rules and reaching the profit target. Users who follow the guardrails and comply with rules and guidelines are eligible to receive up to 100 % of their trading performance and request a payout.
Unlimited Trading Days – There is no time limit but you need to place and close at least one trade every 60 days.
2% Daily Profit cap - In each day, only 2% counts toward the target or profit. You can make more than 2% in a day, but only 2% of the initial balance per day counts toward the profit target or profit.
​
For a $100,000 account, that’s $2,000 profit cap per day.
2.5% Daily Drawdown – Your account must not lose more than 2.5% of your initial balance in a single day. The limit you can not exceed is calculated from your starting balance.
8% Static drawdown – A fixed loss limit based on your initial balance. It never moves, except when a first payout is processed.
Criteria for the payout:
No caps
No consistency rules
No minimum trading days
Payout frequency: You can request every day!
Minimum profit for first and additional payouts: 1% of your initial balance
​
With a $100,000 account balance, you need to make at least $1,000.
Every payout is calculated in two steps: first, 50% of your total profits is set aside as your requestable amount. Then, depending on your chosen plan, you receive up 100% of that amount. The remaining portion stays in your account as a drawdown buffer, protecting you from violations after the Static Drawdown Loss level is moved to your initial balance level.

FAQ For E8 PRO
Does E8 Pro have daily payouts?
Payouts are available daily, with a minimum profit requirement of 1% - making it easy to request payouts regularly. The built-in 50% buffer mechanism ensures that requesting a payout will never put your account at risk of a drawdown violation, as half of your profits always remain in the account as protection.
Is there a consistency rule or minimum trading days?
No consistency rule or minimum profitable days. E8PRO v2 keeps the rules deliberately simple: hit the profit target, respect the static and daily drawdown, and request a payout any day once your profit reaches at least 1%.
If there is no consistency rule, can I request 50% of everything I make in one day?
Not quite. There is still a daily profit cap. For example, even if you make $15,000 on a $100,000 account in a single day, you won't be able to request 50% of that total. With a 2% daily cap, only $2,000 counts toward your profit, and the rest is forfeited at market rollover.
Can you pass the E8 Pro challenge in one day?
​
No - due to the daily profit cap, it is not possible, and the possible minimum is 4 days. However, you can enjoy freedom in the Performance stage without any consistency.
