Profit goal
Each payout cycle has a profit goal: the minimum profit before you can request. You must reach it again every cycle, and scalped profit doesn't count toward it. Once you reach it, you can request up to 50% of your profit for the cycle, up to the cap.

Other conditions
At least 10 trading days, each with at least $75 (25K), $150 (50K), $250 (100K) or $350 (150K) of profit.
Consistency: 20% for the 1st payout, 25% for the 2nd, 30% from the 3rd. No single day can be more than that share of your total profit.
The profit split is 80%.
