Ignite is a plan designed to help traders start fast: simple rules, flexible payout options, no recurring fees, and our lowest-cost instant funded account . It is perfect for traders who want a clean, affordable entry into futures with the ability to scale quickly. All plans are one-time fees with no monthly or activation charges.
Account sizes
Ignite is available in $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 and $150,000 .

15% consistency rule: no single day may represent more than 15% of your total profit.
No minimum trading days. You can reach your payout target in any number of days as long as you meet the consistency requirement.
End-of-day trailing drawdown. Your breach level is active from the moment you start: starting balance minus your max drawdown (e.g. $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000).
It trails upward as your account reaches new highs.
It moves up only at the end of the day, based on your closing balance. Intraday highs don’t move it.
It does not move down if your balance decreases.
The breach level is checked in real time. If your equity goes at or below it at any moment, the account is breached.
90% / 10% profit split. You keep 90% of your payout amount.
The 5% profit target resets after every payout cycle.
Why traders pick Ignite: the lowest-cost instant funded account, one-time fee, simple rules and payouts as soon as you hit 5% profit with the consistency rule met.
Daily Loss Limit (DLL)
If your DLL is hit, your account is paused for the rest of the trading day and resumes when markets reopen at 6:00 pm ET .
Overnight trading and EAs
Overnight trading and expert advisors or bots are not allowed .
All trades must be closed by 4:00 pm ET .
Minimum trade time is 10 seconds . See our prohibited trading practices for the details of the 10-second rule.
Payouts
First payout: available once you have achieved a 5% profit and met the 15% consistency rule . No minimum trading day requirement.
Subsequent payouts: each payout requires another 5% profit relative to the balance after your last payout.
You can request a payout as soon as you reach your consistency and profit target.
Minimum payout: $250 . Profit split: 90% to you , 10% stays with the firm.
See Payout Requirements for worked examples.
Drawdown lock
Once your profits reach the drawdown amount plus $100 , your trailing drawdown locks at $100 above your starting balance and no longer follows account growth. This gives more stability as you scale. The lock also activates after your first payout .

Transition to live trading
After three successful Ignite payouts from a single account, your trading performance is reviewed by our Risk Team.
The Risk Team may keep you in the sim-funded environment for further review, or transition you to a live account.
Top One Futures may transition you to live before your third payout at its discretion.
When moving to live, you begin with one live account . Any additional accounts are closed.

Common questions
Can I copy trade Ignite with other plan types? No. Copy trading is allowed only between Ignite accounts of the same size.
How is the consistency rule calculated? No single trading day should represent more than 15% of your total profit. This ensures steady, sustainable results before payouts. See the 15% Consistency Rule article .
