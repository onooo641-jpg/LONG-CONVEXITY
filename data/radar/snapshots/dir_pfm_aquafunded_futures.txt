Firm Rules
News Trading
Allowed for all evaluations and funded accounts
Micro Scalping
To maintain fair and sustainable trading conditions, AquaFutures requires that at least 50% of all closed trades remain open for longer than 10 seconds. In addition, at least 50% of total generated profits must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds. Traders are also expected to maintain an average risk-to-reward ratio above 0.3RR across their overall trading activity.
Expert Advisors, HFTs, Automation
The use of fully automated systems, unattended algorithms, trading bots, AI-driven execution software, latency arbitrage systems, or any form of self-operating execution mechanism is strictly prohibited across all AquaFutures account types. Semi-automated systems may only be used when the trader remains fully present during execution, actively manages positions, fully understands the strategy being used, and is not attempting to exploit simulated market behavior. AquaFutures also prohibits excessive trading frequency that resembles high-frequency trading behavior. Traders may not execute 100 or more trades within a single trading day.
Copy Trading
Copy Trading is allowed between your own accounts (including external accounts personally owned). Copying trades from or to another trader's account, using shared EA configurations, third-party copy services, or "Pass Your Challenge" services is not permitted. Violations result in account breach, profit forfeiture, and potential permanent ban.
Trading Hours
Daily session:
10:00 PM UTC – 8:10 PM UTC the following day (Sunday through Friday). All open trades are auto-closed at market close, this does not count as a violation.
Holiday hours may vary. Times may shift seasonally due to daylight savings.
Inactivity Rule
Evaluation
You are required to place at least one trade within 7 days to avoid your account being marked inactive. Failure to this, your account will be breached due to inactivity and no further action can be done on the account.
Funded
You are required to place at least one trade within 7 days to avoid your account being marked inactive. Failure to this, your account will be breached due to inactivity and no further action can be done on the account.
2% Price Limit Rule
Trading within 2% of a CME price limit is prohibited. This protects traders from market halts, extreme price swings, and major slippage.
Drawdown Lock Rule
The trailing drawdown locks at starting balance + $100 and no longer trails for the remainder of the account's lifetime.
Prohibited Strategies
Order Management, Execution Manipulation & Market Conduct, Gambling Behavior, Account Rolling & Reckless Risk Management
