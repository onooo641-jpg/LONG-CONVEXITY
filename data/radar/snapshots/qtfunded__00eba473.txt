Marketing Attribution & Analytics
We use our own first-party analytics and marketing-attribution tool to understand how visitors use our store and to measure which marketing touchpoints (ads, emails, referral links) lead to subscriptions or purchases. We operate this tool ourselves on our own infrastructure. It does not use third-party advertising pixels, and we do not sell or share your data with advertising networks such as Meta, Google or TikTok.
What it collects — depending on your cookie choices and location, the tool may record: a random first-party browser identifier (a “visitor ID”); the pages and products you view, the actions you take, and when; the campaign parameters in the link you arrived from (e.g. UTM parameters or click identifiers); the website or service that referred you; your approximate country, derived from your IP address; and subscription and purchase events, including order value and currency.
We store only the path of the pages you visit (e.g. /products/example) and an allow-listed set of campaign parameters — not full web addresses, form contents, payment details, or precise location. We do not use device fingerprinting.
