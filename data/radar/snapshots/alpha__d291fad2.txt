Written by Benjamin Chaffee
July 9, 2026
In an effort to implement trader feedback and diversify our offerings, Alpha Futures has launched the Alpha Direct Account. The Direct Account is available as of July 7th, 2026.
All Direct Accounts are one time fee, no monthly subscription.
​
​Benefits of Direct Account:
1.) No Evaluation, jump straight into a Qualified Account from day one
2.) 90% profit split on every withdrawal
3.) Pay yourself up to 4 times per month. Withdraw when you want as soon as your Profit Target and Consistency Rule are satisfied
4.) One time fee, no subscription payments
5.) No Scaling Plan on Direct Qualified Accounts
​
Users that select the Direct Account can skip the Evaluation stage and trade in a Qualified Account straight away. Full payout policy can be reviewed here , but in order to eligible for a withdrawal request, user must satisfy the Withdrawal Profit Target, and 20% Consistency Rule .
​
Withdrawal Profit Targets are the profit needed to earn in between withdrawal requests. After each withdrawal, your profit target resets and you must earn fresh profit from new trades to reach the next target. Leftover profit from the previous cycle does not carry over.
