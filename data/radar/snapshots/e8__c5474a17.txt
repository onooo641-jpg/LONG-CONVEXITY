Last Updated: [25.11.2026]
Article I: General Provisions
These Terms and Conditions (the “Agreement”) govern the relationship between the participant of this Affiliate Program (the “Affiliate”) and E8 Funding LLC , a Texas limited liability company (the “Provider”).
This Agreement supersedes and replaces all prior affiliate program agreements, including but not limited to the previous Forex Affiliate Program , Futures Affiliate Program and the version of this Agreement dated 25.11.2025.
The Global Affiliate Program unifies all affiliate operations under a single structure across Forex, Futures, and Crypto markets , providing Affiliates with one centralized affiliate account, one referral link, and one or more discount codes under the unified system. One commission structure applies across all products.
Participation in the Global Affiliate Program is by invitation only . Persons without an invitation may apply through the Provider’s affiliate application form or join the Provider’s Friends program, which is governed by its own terms.
Article II: Definitions and Interpretation
For purposes of this Agreement:
(a) Affiliate: A natural or legal person who enters into this Agreement with the Provider.
(b) Affiliate Account: The account created by the Provider to track Affiliate commissions, conversions, and sales.
(c) Affiliate Link: A unique referral link assigned to the Affiliate that identifies customers and connects sales to the Affiliate Account.
(d) Discount Code: A code assigned to the Affiliate which generates commission on any order or reset purchased using that code.
(e) Master Discount Code Configuration: A unified system determining the default discount value (5%) and its dynamic adjustment during promotional campaigns (e.g., No Activation Fees , 20% Discount , 10% Additional Balance ).
(f) Conversion: A completed sale or reset made using the Affiliate’s Discount Code.
(g) Reset: A paid retry or reactivation of an account or challenge purchased using the Affiliate’s Discount Code.
(h) Unique User: A first-time customer purchasing from the Provider using an Affiliate’s Discount Code.
(i) First Order: The first order a Unique User places with the Provider, including every account or product purchased in that order.
(j) Affiliate Backend System: The Provider’s proprietary system that calculates commissions, tracks conversions, and manages Affiliate dashboards.
(k) Invitation: A single-use link issued by the Provider’s business development team or by an existing Affiliate, tied to the invitee’s email address, through which a new Affiliate joins the program.
(l) Tier: The Affiliate’s level in the program: Affiliate, Top Affiliate or Ambassador, as set out in Article VI.
(m) Introductory Period: The first ninety (90) days after an Affiliate joins the program. For Affiliates who joined before 25.11.2026, the Introductory Period starts on 25.11.2026.
(n) Qualification Threshold: At least USD $5,000 in revenue and at least five (5) Unique Users generated by the Affiliate’s Discount Codes over the last three (3) full calendar months.
(o) Team Commission: The additional earnings described in Article VII, paid to an Affiliate on the commissions of the Affiliates in their Team.
(p) Team: The Affiliates an Affiliate invited directly and the Affiliates those Affiliates invited.
(q) Flight Deck: The official affiliate resource portal containing approved promotional materials and program documentation.
(r) Main Website: www.e8markets.com

Article IV: Commissions, Attribution and Payout Conditions
Commissions are earned only when a customer makes a purchase or reset using the Affiliate’s assigned Discount Code.
Default Discount: 5% (adjustable under the Master Discount Code Configuration).
Commissions apply exclusively to sales generated by the Affiliate’s Discount Code or direct Affiliate Link.
Commission rates and limits are those of the Affiliate’s Tier at the time of the order, as set out in Article VI.
The Provider reserves the right to review and withhold commissions for suspicious, fraudulent, or self-referral transactions.
Commissions on orders that are refunded or charged back are reversed in proportion to the refunded amount, together with any Team Commission paid on them.
# Attribution
Opening an Affiliate Link stores the Affiliate’s Discount Code in the customer’s browser for twenty (20) days and applies it at checkout.
Attribution is by last click . The code present on the order at checkout determines the Affiliate who earns the commission.
If an order carries an Affiliate’s Discount Code, that Affiliate earns the commission and no Friends program reward is paid on that order. If the customer opens a Friends program share link after an Affiliate Link, the stored Affiliate Discount Code is cleared.
Each order is rewarded once, either as an Affiliate commission or as a Friends program reward.
# Payout Conditions and Limitations
Minimum Payout Threshold: USD $200.
Eligibility Period: Payouts become available no sooner than 28 days after either (i) Affiliate registration or (ii) the most recent payout confirmation.
Frequency: Affiliates may qualify for weekly payouts , subject to meeting the eligibility criteria below.
Activity Requirement: Between payouts, the Affiliate must have earned commissions from at least three (3) unique users .
The Provider reserves the right to audit and validate sales activity before approving any payout.
Commissions and Team Commissions are processed according to the Provider’s standard payout schedule once eligibility requirements are met.

Article VI: Tiers and Commission Structure
The Global Affiliate Program consists of three Tiers. Every new Affiliate joins at the Affiliate Tier.
# Affiliate
Joined through an Invitation from the Provider’s business development team or from an existing Affiliate.
Commission during the Introductory Period:
85% of a Unique User’s First Order, with the commission capped at USD $300 per First Order
10% on subsequent sales or resets
Commission after the Introductory Period:
If the Affiliate has met the Qualification Threshold at the end of the Introductory Period, the Affiliate moves to the Top Affiliate Tier.
Otherwise: 20% on a Unique User’s First Order and 10% on subsequent sales or resets.
An Affiliate who meets the Qualification Threshold at a monthly check after the Introductory Period moves to the Top Affiliate Tier.
# Top Affiliate
Earned automatically by meeting the Qualification Threshold.
Commission:
85% of a Unique User’s First Order, with the commission capped at USD $300 per First Order
10% on subsequent sales or resets
Only Top Affiliates and Ambassadors may create giveaway codes. Giveaway amounts are set by the Provider.
The Qualification Threshold is checked on the 1st of each month . A Top Affiliate who no longer meets it moves back to the Affiliate Tier at 20% on First Orders and 10% on subsequent sales or resets. A Top Affiliate is not moved back at the first monthly check after their promotion.
# Ambassador
By individual agreement with the Provider only (for example, price-match partners and key opinion leaders).
Commission rates, discount limits and other terms are set in the Affiliate’s individual agreement and shown in the Affiliate Dashboard.
Ambassador terms are reviewed by the Provider at least every ninety (90) days. Ambassadors are not moved between Tiers automatically.
Affiliates who held individually agreed terms before 25.11.2026 keep those terms at the Ambassador Tier until the Provider reviews them.
General Notes:
Conversions include both Purchases and Resets made using the Affiliate’s Discount Code.
The Affiliate’s current Tier, its rates, the end of the Introductory Period and progress toward the Qualification Threshold are shown in the Affiliate Dashboard.
Revenue and Unique Users for the Qualification Threshold count the Affiliate’s own commissioned orders only, net of refunds and chargebacks. Team Commissions do not count.
All Affiliates operate under the Master Discount Code Configuration , ensuring that discounts automatically adjust to the best available global offer.
Additional Discount Codes may be requested through the Affiliate Dashboard or the Affiliate’s business development representative and are subject to the Provider’s approval and the limits of the Affiliate’s Tier.
Article VII: Team Commission
An Affiliate may invite new Affiliates from the Affiliate Dashboard. Invited Affiliates join at the Affiliate Tier.
The inviting Affiliate earns a Team Commission, paid by the Provider and not deducted from the invited Affiliate’s commission:
1% of the commission earned by each Affiliate they invited directly, and
0.5% of the commission earned by each Affiliate invited by those Affiliates.
No Team Commission is paid beyond these two levels.
The Team Commission is earned for as long as the invited Affiliate earns commission. No commission on an order means no Team Commission on that order.
No Team Commission is paid where the inviting and invited Affiliate are the same person or share an account, including matching identity verification. Such Invitations are refused.
No Team Commission is paid to a suspended or removed Affiliate. In that case the second-level Team Commission is still paid to the next eligible Affiliate above them.
Team Commissions appear as a separate line in the Affiliate’s payout history and are paid with the Affiliate’s regular payout.
