At My Funded Futures , we are pleased to announce our strategic partnership with Volumetrica Trading Platforms. Volumetrica is a sophisticated trading platform designed with a focus on volume-based trading tools and services.
Recognizing the demand among our traders for a professional-grade platform in the proprietary trading space, we have identified Volumetrica as an ideal solution.
This platform is both robust and user-friendly, catering to the needs of both novice and experienced traders.
What Data Feed Does Volumetrica Use?
Volumetrica Trading Platforms, including desktop applications such as VolSys and VolBook, utilize dxFeed as their primary data source. The web-based version of Volumetrica also relies on dxFeed for its real-time data feed, ensuring consistency across all platforms.

Why Choose Volumetrica and dxFeed?
Our commitment to providing superior trading solutions led us to partner with dxFeed, a reliable data provider that ensures seamless execution with minimal disruption. We recognize that many traders depend on advanced tools such as footprint charts, time and sales, and other key indicators to make informed trading decisions.
By integrating Volumetrica, we are delivering a top-tier platform that meets the high standards of our trading community.

Can I Trade on a Mac?
Absolutely. The web-based version of the Volumetrica platform is fully compatible with Mac devices and operates smoothly on Safari.
Please Note: The desktop versions of Volumetrica platforms, such as VolSys and VolBook, are designed for Windows. However, they can be run on a Mac using Parallels VM.
