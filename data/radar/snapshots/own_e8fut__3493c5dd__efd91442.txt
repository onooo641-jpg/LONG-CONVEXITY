How to log into the Tradovate trading platform
August 20, 2026
​ 1. Locate your account credentials
After you purchase your challenge, you will be able to locate your account credentials in your dashboard.
2. Log in to the trading platform
Select the Trading platform and insert your login credentials and access the trading platform.
​
(Keep in mind that login credentials to our dashboard E8X is different from the login to the Tradovate trading platform.)
Tradovate WebTrader
Tradovate Windows Desktop
Tradovate Mac Dekstop
Tradovate Android Mobile
​ Tradovate Iphone Mobile
Always select Simulations as a Trading mode
3. Start trading
You are ready to go!
​
Before you start, make sure you have selected the proper account on which you want to place a trade. You can also check this guide on how to use the WebTrader Tradovate.
​
Having login issues with Tradovate?
Make sure you are using the copy from the clipboard function
​
2. If you want to use Tradingview, you need to first log in to the Tradovate platform and accept all documents; otherwise, you won't be able to connect the account to the Tradingview platform.
3. You are not allowed to change your username inside the Tradovate platform , because you won't be able to use credentials from the dashboard, which can cause login issues.
If you have accidentally changed your username in the Tradovate platform, you need to change it back.
​ This can be done in your profile under Tradovate Settings
​
The Username must be changed to the Login number that can be found in your dashboard under account credentials
