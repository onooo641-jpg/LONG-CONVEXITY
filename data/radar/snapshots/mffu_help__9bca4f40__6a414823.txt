ATAS Download
Click on the Downloads button from your MFFU dashboard and select ATAS .
Once you select ATAS, you will be directed to the ATAS download page. You must register with ATAS to obtain the platform license. Make sure to use accurate information, as ATAS will email you your password for platform login.
On the download page, click Get ATAS for Windows and follow the installation instructions.
Once the download is complete, install the ATAS software.
Get Funded to Trade on ATAS
ATAS Login and MFFU Connection
After the installation is complete, launch ATAS.
Enter your login credentials, which were provided by ATAS via e-mail during the registration process , and click Connect.
Once logged in, you'll arrive at your ATAS dashboard.
Click Connections from the ATAS dashboard.
From the Connections window, click Add .
In the " Data Provider " pop-up window, make sure to select dxFeed prop , then click Next .
Next, you will need to add your MFFU connection. Enter your MFFU credentials, which can be found on your MFFU dashboard , and then click Finish .
After adding your MyFundedFutures connection, return to the Connections window and click Connect .
When you are successfully connected to the server, the state of your connection will be highlighted in green "Connected" status, along with your MFFU accounts displayed in the accounts window.
