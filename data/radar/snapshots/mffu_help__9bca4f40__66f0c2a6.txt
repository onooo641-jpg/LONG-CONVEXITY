Navigating the world of trading platforms can be daunting, but at MFFU, we aim to make the process as seamless as possible. Here's a step-by-step guide to connecting to our supported platforms:
​
TradingView, Tradovate, and NinjaTrader
Connecting to these platforms is straightforward:
Access Tradovate : Head over to the Tradovate website at https://trader.tradovate.com and sign in using your credentials.
Sign the Agreement : Upon logging in, you'll be prompted to sign an agreement for live market data access. This is a crucial step to ensure you receive real-time market data.
Activate Live Data : Once the agreement is signed, your trading account will automatically update, granting you access to live market data. This ensures you're always trading with the most recent market information.
​
Once you are connected with the platform of your choice , follow these instructions to access live market data.
Visit the Tradovate website at https://trader.tradovate.com and log in to your account.
As soon as you log in, the first thing you will see is the necessary agreement for live market data access.
After successfully signing the agreement, your trading account will be updated to include live market data, allowing you to stay up-to-date with real-time market movements and execute your trades with confidence.
​
This process ensures that you have the necessary permissions and access to harness the full potential of these platforms for your trading endeavors.
