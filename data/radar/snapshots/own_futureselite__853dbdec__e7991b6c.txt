Using cards
Where cards can be used and how many times.
October 13, 2026
Open your Inventory, choose a card and press Use card . Cards that act on an account ask which account first; the apply screen only lists accounts the card can be used on. Read the card's scope first so you pick the right one.
Scope on the card
Where it works
Evaluation
Evaluation accounts only
Funded Sim
Funded simulated accounts only
Any Account
An evaluation or a funded simulated account
New Account
Opens a fresh challenge
Checkout
Your next purchase
Cards don't expire and can't be sold, traded or transferred.
Rule-changing cards, such as the Minimum Trading Days remover or the MLL Drawdown Buffer, work once per account.
Cards with several uses are spread across different accounts, one each.
If a card doesn't fit an account, nothing is lost: the card stays in your inventory.
One discount card per purchase, on top of the current dashboard code.
