Prime drawdown and Daily Loss Limit
The End of Day Max Loss Limit and the soft-breach Daily Loss Limit.
October 18, 2026
Table of contents
End of Day Max Loss Limit
The most your account can ever lose: $1,000 (25K), $2,000 (50K), $3,000 (100K) or $4,500 (150K). It is checked on your end-of-day balance. On a 50K Prime it starts at $48,000.
If an end-of-day balance closes at or below the limit, it is a hard breach : the account is closed permanently.
Daily Loss Limit
Daily loss limit: on Elite only if you chose it at checkout; always on for Prime, Ultra and Oracle; Nitro and Instant have none.
The most you can lose in one trading day. The level is measured from the higher of your balance and equity at the previous daily close.

Hitting it is a soft breach : if your equity reaches it, trading is locked for 24 hours from that moment; the account stays open.
Contracts
No scaling plan: your full allowance applies from day one in both stages. One mini equals ten micros, and you can mix them as long as the total never exceeds your mini limit. On a 50K, 2 minis + 20 micros = 4 minis.
