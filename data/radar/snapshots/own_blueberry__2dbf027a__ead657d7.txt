The Onboarding Process
You hit the profit target and followed the rules. Congratulations on passing your evaluation! Here is how to claim your funded status.
​
The Steps:
Close All Trades: Ensure all positions are closed and your balance is above the profit target.
System Review: Our risk team automatically reviews passed accounts. This typically takes 24–48 hours (business days).
KYC Verification: Look for an email from our verification partner Veriff . You must complete your ID check (Selfie + ID).
The Contract: Once verified, you will receive a digital Trader Agreement . Sign this to accept the terms of the funded account.
Credentials: After the contract is signed, your new Funded Account credentials will be emailed to you.
​
Note : You do not need to cancel your monthly subscription manually; Your subscription will end once you activate the account
