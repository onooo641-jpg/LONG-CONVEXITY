Do I need to be verified (KYC) to request a payout?
Yes. We use Veriff for secure, instant identity verification.
November 24, 2025
Table of contents
KYC Verification with Veriff
To ensure the security of our funds and yours, we comply with strict AML (Anti-Money Laundering) policies. We cannot release a single cent to unverified accounts.
We partner with Veriff to make this process fast and secure.
When to verify:
We recommend completing KYC as soon as you pass your evaluation . Do not wait until your payout date to start the process.
How to verify:
Log In to Your Account: Access your Blueberry Futures trader dashboard.
Navigate to ' Account ': Find the ' KYC ' tab on the Superior menu.
Click the verification link in your dashboard when it becomes available.
Follow the Veriff on-screen prompts.
You will need to scan your Government ID (Passport, Driver's License, or National ID) using your phone or webcam.
You will be asked to take a quick Selfie to confirm it’s really you.
How long does it take?
Thanks to Veriff, verification is usually instant . However, if a manual review is required, it may take up to 24-48 hours.
💡 Tips for success:
Ensure you are in a well-lit room.
Avoid glare on your ID card.
Make sure your ID is not expired.
Make sure you receive no assistance in the KYC process
