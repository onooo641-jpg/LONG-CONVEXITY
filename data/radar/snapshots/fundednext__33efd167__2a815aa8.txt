Does FundedNext offer a Scale-Up plan?
Does FundedNext offer a Scale-Up plan?
September 3, 2026
For Stellar Challenges Purchased or Reset Before January 12, 2026
Traders with Stellar Challenges purchased or reset before January 12, 2026 will continue to follow the previous Scale-Up plan criteria, even if they pass the Challenge and receive their FundedNext Account after January 12, 2026.
For Stellar Challenges Purchased or Reset After January 12, 2026
Traders with Stellar Challenges purchased or reset after January 12, 2026 will follow the new Scale-Up plan.
FundedNext provides a Scale-Up plan for traders who consistently demonstrate success and follow the trading rules.
The Scale-Up plan presents traders with an opportunity to expand their trading activity by potentially receiving a simulated capital allocation of up to $4 million. Skilled and disciplined traders seeking to elevate their trading endeavors can benefit from FundedNext's Scale-Up plan. The eligibility criteria for the Evaluation, Express, Stellar 1-Step, Stellar 2-Step, or Stellar Lite Accounts remain consistent to simplify the process. The trader must ensure that all trades are closed and should contact support immediately after completing their trading cycle before initiating any new trades.
Review Process & Eligibility Criteria:
To be considered for scaling up, traders must meet the following eligibility criteria in a FundedNext Account over a four-month period:
Achieve a minimum accumulated growth of 10% in four consecutive months.
Receive at least two Performance Reward within these successive four months.
The last trading cycle should end in profit.
FundedNext Accounts (Evaluation, Express, Stellar or Stellar Lite) undergo a review every four months. Traders who meet the eligibility criteria are eligible for a 40% increase in the account balance, up to a maximum of 4 million.
Based on the trader's performance over the four consecutive months and if all other criteria are met, FundedNext will review the trader's account and potentially Scale the account size up by 40%. So, with the 40% Scale-Up, the new balance is $140,000.
Therefore, considering the new account balance:
The daily loss limit is 5% (4% in Stellar Lite Accounts and 3% in Stellar 1-Step Accounts), which amounts to $7,000 ($5,600 in Stellar Lite and $4,200 in Stellar 1-Step).
The maximum loss limit is 10% (8% in Stellar Lite Accounts and 6% in Stellar 1-Step Accounts), which equates to $14,000 ($11,200 in Stellar Lite and $8,400 in Stellar 1-Step).
The new scale-up target for the next four months is a 10% disbursed amount growth of $140,000.
Scaling up with FundedNext is a straightforward process that opens doors to enhanced opportunities for traders.
What if a trader managed to reach 10% of their disbursed amount for scaling up but was unable to make a profit in the last trading cycle?
For instance, let's consider a trader who began trading on January 1st. After four consecutive months (January, February, March, and April), the trader managed to achieve a 10% disbursed amount growth by the end of April. However, during the last trading cycle, the trader was unable to end in profit. As the trader was unable to end his last trading cycle in profit, the trader will not be eligible for the Scale-Up plan. In such a scenario, the trader's account will be evaluated again in May, following the completion of their trading cycle (February, March, April, and May). Essentially, if the trader meets all the criteria during the most recent four-month period, they will be eligible for the Scale-Up plan.
