How much does an Account Reset cost? (Evaluation & Funded)
Hit a drawdown limit? Here is how to restart your evaluation or funded account instantly for a discounted fee.
December 20, 2025
Table of contents
Sometimes the market moves against you. It happens to the best of us. If you’ve violated a rule or hit your drawdown limit, you don't have to pay full price for a brand new challenge. You can use an Account Reset to wipe the slate clean, whether you are in the evaluation phase or already funded.
The Reset Perks:
Instant: No waiting period. You are back in the game immediately.
Cheaper: significantly lower cost than a new evaluation.
Same Account: Keep your existing login credentials.

How to Reset:
Log in to your Trader Dashboard.
Navigate to your " Challenges ".
Click the "Reset Account" button.
Note: A reset restores your starting balance. It does not change your phase or merge with other accounts.
