FAQ
How do I create and verify my account?
To create and verify your account, sign up at app.hata.io/register and log in. Click Get Started to begin, then choose to verify using your computer or mobile. Provide your personal details and follow the steps on screen to get a verified account.
If you already have a verified Hata account, click here to apply for a Hata Global account.
How does Hata keep my funds secure?
Hata is regulated by the Labuan Financial Services Authority, ensuring full compliance and transparency. Customer funds are held 1:1, meaning they are never used for any other purpose. All fiat funds are safeguarded by a licensed trustee and kept completely separate from company accounts.
How do I make a deposit in Hata?
To make a deposit, click Deposit. Make a bank transfer to the provided account, then upload your receipt with the required details and submit the amount and reference code. Deposits usually reflect within 1–4 working days.
