I'm not an American/US Citizen, can I still get Funded?
If you are not an American citizen you can still get funded
Written by Francis
March 7, 2025
Yes, of course you can.
After successfully passing the Trader Career Path ® / Gauntlet Mini™ and receiving your funding offer, you will be required to fill out a W-8BEN form and identify yourself as the holder of a financial account in the US. The form requires your Foreign Tax Identifying Number (FTIN), which is issued to you by the tax jurisdiction of your residence. This is very important so you can be correctly engaged according to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and take advantage of any potential tax benefits if applicable. For details on taxation, we recommend you contact your tax advisor.
The W-8BEN form can be downloaded and completed here:
https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw8ben.pdf
It's a very simple form to fill out and must be submitted along with relevant formal identification. You can do this for free for yourself.
​
Citizens of the following countries are unable to get funded owing to sanctions or other reasons:
Afghanistan, Albania, Azerbaijan, Burma (Myanmar), Central African Republic, Cuba, Gibraltar, Iran, Iraq, Kenya, Libya, Mali, Nauru, Nigeria, North Korea, Pakistan, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Turkmenistan, Ukraine, Venezuela, Yemen.
If you are a user from a country that has recently been added to the sanctioned list, you will not be able to continue or reset your evaluation with Earn2Trade. Additionally, purchases or resets made from these sanctioned countries will not be refunded starting from May 15. Any attempts to circumvent this policy by providing false information regarding your country of origin or address will result in an immediate ban and deletion from our services without a refund.
Notes:
Our data feed provider does not list Palestine as an available country option. Our list of countries is updated accordingly. We kindly ask users from Palestine to select Israel as their region during signup so that we can connect them to their data feed.
