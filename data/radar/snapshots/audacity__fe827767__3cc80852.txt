Hari Prakash Ramasamy 's Trading Journey
"Success in trading isn't about taking more trades—it's about taking the right ones."
For Hari Prakash Ramasamy , trading has never been about chasing quick profits. Over the past 13 years , he has built a trading career based on patience, discipline, and consistency—qualities that continue to define his approach today.
As a dedicated swing trader, Hari focuses on identifying high-probability opportunities rather than trading every market movement. His philosophy is simple: quality always beats quantity.

A Trading Strategy Built on Conviction
Hari specializes in swing trading across Forex, Commodities, Metals, and Soft Commodities , holding positions from several days to multiple weeks.
Instead of relying on lower-timeframe noise, he combines:
Supply and Demand analysis
Commitment of Traders (COT) Reports
Seasonality
Market Valuations
Price Action
This combination helps him identify high-conviction trading opportunities while avoiding unnecessary market exposure.
"I would rather take two or three quality trades each month than twenty average ones."
Discipline Over Emotion
Like many successful traders, Hari believes psychology is just as important as strategy.
Early in his journey, he faced challenges including perfectionism and skepticism from those who viewed trading as gambling. Rather than letting outside opinions influence him, he focused on improving his process and trusting his own analysis.
Today, every trade follows predefined rules and a structured trading plan.
Risk Management Comes First
One principle has remained unchanged throughout Hari's career:
Protect capital before chasing profits.
He risks no more than 2% of his trading capital per position , ensuring that one losing trade never jeopardizes long-term performance.
Rather than increasing risk to grow faster, he believes consistent traders should scale their capital while maintaining disciplined position sizing.
This professional mindset has helped him navigate changing market conditions over more than a decade.
Finding Opportunities Through Market Structure
Hari doesn't rely on traditional support and resistance alone.
Instead, he focuses on:
Supply and Demand Zones
Institutional Order Flow
Market "Trap Zones"
Reversal Price Action
Higher Timeframe Structure
Combined with COT data and market fundamentals, this approach helps him anticipate potential trend reversals and continuation opportunities before they become obvious to most market participants.
Why Swing Trading Works for Him
Hari believes traders should choose a style that matches their personality—not one that's popular on social media.
For him, swing trading provides:
More time to analyse markets
Less emotional pressure
Better decision-making
Higher-quality trade setups
Improved work-life balance
He often recommends swing trading to newer traders because it encourages patience and reduces impulsive decision-making.

Transparent trading rules

For Hari, having a prop firm that rewards consistency allows him to focus on what matters most—executing his strategy with confidence.
Advice for Aspiring Traders
Hari's message to new traders is straightforward:
Master one trading strategy before looking for another.
Never risk more than you can afford to lose.
Focus on consistency rather than quick profits.
Choose a trading style that fits your personality.
Learn from experienced traders, but don't follow hype or social media trends blindly.
His journey demonstrates that sustainable trading success isn't built overnight—it's the result of disciplined execution, continuous learning, and effective risk management.
