Do you restrict any countries from taking part in Blueberry Futures?
July 9, 2026
Yes. To comply with international laws, financial regulations, and internal risk controls, Blueberry Futures restricts access from the following countries and regions :
Australia
Cuba
Iran
North Korea (DPRK)
Syria
Pakistan
Somalia
Russia
Belarus
Venezuela
Myanmar (Burma)
Sudan
Zimbabwe
Crimea, Donetsk (DNR), and Luhansk (LNR) regions of Ukraine
Zaporizhzhia and Kherson (Russia-occupied areas of Ukraine)
Traders from these jurisdictions are not permitted to sign up, purchase evaluations, or access funded accounts . Use of VPNs, VPSs, or any location-masking tools to bypass these restrictions is strictly prohibited and may result in account termination and forfeiture of any profits .
If you're unsure about your eligibility, please contact our support team before registering.
