Part II: General Trading Rules
The following rules apply to all QT | FUNDED programmes unless otherwise stated. By participating in any evaluation or funded programme, you agree to comply with these Terms at all times.
1.1 Exposure
Definition
Exposure represents the maximum potential loss across all open positions at any given time.
Exposure is calculated using the greater of:
The maximum potential loss to the position’s stop-loss level; or
The current floating (unrealised) loss.
Your total account exposure is the combined exposure across all open positions.
Exposure Calculation
Exposure Per Trade — The greater of:
Maximum Stop-Loss Risk; or
Current Floating Loss.
Total Exposure — The sum of the exposure across all open positions.
IMPORTANT INFORMATION
If no stop loss has been placed, or if the current floating loss exceeds the predefined stop-loss risk, exposure will automatically be calculated using the current floating loss. Failure to implement appropriate stop-loss protection may also constitute a breach of the All-or-Nothing Trading Rule.
1.2 High-Impact News Trading
High-impact economic announcements may create exceptional market volatility, widened spreads, increased slippage and unpredictable market conditions.
To encourage disciplined trading and effective risk management, trading activity is restricted during designated high-impact news events.
Restricted Trading Window
Trading is prohibited during the following period:
Five (5) minutes before a restricted news event.
Five (5) minutes after a restricted news event.
This creates a total restricted trading window of ten (10) minutes surrounding the scheduled announcement.
During the Restricted Window
The following actions are not permitted:
Opening new market positions.
Closing existing market positions.
The following actions remain permitted:
Modifying stop-loss orders.
Modifying take-profit orders.
Cancelling pending orders.
1.3 Floating Loss Rule
The Floating Loss Rule is designed to ensure traders maintain disciplined risk management whilst positions remain open.
HARD LIMIT
At no time may your combined floating loss exceed 2% of your account balance. This limit is monitored continuously while trades remain open.
Exceeding the maximum permitted floating loss constitutes a breach of these Terms, regardless of whether the position subsequently recovers or is eventually closed in profit.
Definition
Floating Loss (also referred to as Unrealised Profit & Loss) is the current loss displayed on open positions based on live market prices before those positions have been closed.
As market prices fluctuate, your floating loss will increase or decrease accordingly.
Example
Account Balance: $100,000
Maximum Floating Loss: 2% ($2,000)
Open Positions:
Trade 1: -$800
Trade 2: -$700
Trade 3: -$600
Total Floating Loss: -$2,100
As the combined floating loss exceeds the permitted limit of $2,000, this would constitute a breach of the Floating Loss Rule.
1.4 All-or-Nothing Trading
QT | FUNDED is designed to identify disciplined traders who demonstrate responsible and consistent risk management.
Trading behaviour that exposes an account to excessive or unsustainable levels of risk is strictly prohibited.
Examples of prohibited behaviour include, but are not limited to:
Trading without appropriate stop-loss protection.
Holding positions through major economic news events without suitable risk controls.
Risking more than 75% of the permitted daily drawdown limit across open positions.
Using an excessive portion of the available margin.
Demonstrating reckless, inconsistent or otherwise irresponsible risk management practices.
Such behaviour significantly increases the probability of substantial losses and is inconsistent with the objectives of QT | FUNDED’s evaluation process.
1.5 Reverse Trading
Reverse Trading refers to holding opposing positions on the same financial instrument across multiple trading accounts.
This practice may be used to artificially hedge exposure or circumvent programme rules and is therefore prohibited.
The Rule
Traders must not hold opposing positions on the same financial instrument across separate accounts where:
Opposing positions remain open for longer than two (2) minutes; or
Opposing positions occur more than three (3) individual times, regardless of how long each position remains open.
Example
Opening a Buy position on one account whilst simultaneously opening a Sell position on another account in the same financial instrument may constitute Reverse Trading where it exceeds the limits outlined above.
Any activity designed to artificially offset risk, manipulate account performance or circumvent the rules of any QT | FUNDED programme is strictly prohibited.

6. Instant Funded Accounts
Programme Overview
Instant Funded Accounts provide immediate access to a funded trading environment without the requirement to complete a traditional evaluation.
All traders are expected to demonstrate responsible risk management and comply with all General Trading Rules.
Minimum Trading Requirement
A minimum of five (5) separate trading days must be completed before a withdrawal request may be submitted.
Daily Drawdown
The Daily Drawdown is fixed at 3% of the original account balance.
This limit does not increase following profitable trading and does not decrease following losses.
Maximum Drawdown
The Maximum Drawdown is calculated using a 6% Trailing Drawdown.
The trailing drawdown follows the highest recorded account balance or floating equity until the account reaches the original starting balance.
Following an approved withdrawal, the trailing drawdown becomes fixed at the original starting balance and will no longer trail future profits.
Consistency Requirement
No individual trading day may account for more than 25% of the trader’s total accumulated profit at the time a withdrawal request is submitted.
