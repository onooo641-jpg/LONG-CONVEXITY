Can I share or transfer my account?
May 5, 2025
No, you cannot share or transfer your account with Blueberry Futures.
Each account is strictly personal and must only be used by the individual who registered for it. Account sharing, trading on behalf of others, or entering into third-party arrangements is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification and forfeiture of any profits or payouts.
This rule helps ensure fair use of the platform, protects data integrity, and maintains a level playing field for all traders.
