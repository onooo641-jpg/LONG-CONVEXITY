How to pay with Cryptocurrency
April 8, 2024
We know that paying with cryptocurrencies can be confusing for some users, so we have put together a short and simple guide on how to buy an account without having to worry about something going wrong.
First, select the cryptocurrency that you want to pay with (you can pay from any cryptocurrency wallet or exchange you want).
​
Now you can either use the QR code for the payment or copy the address and send the displayed amount there.
Please note that you must select the correct network when paying with cryptocurrency.
(this is only an example, do not use the QR code from this picture)
A new unique address is generated for each order so that we can identify which order is associated with your payment. Therefore, please do not save any addresses of wallets to which you have sent coins in your last order, as we would then be unable to identify your payment and provide you with your account.
Be also aware of internet scammers. Our team will never contact you directly via social media and will never send you the link for crypto payment. Never use links from someone else, and never modify your invoice/ID number.
