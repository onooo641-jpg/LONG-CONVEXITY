S2F payouts: eligibility, profit targets per cycle and max per request.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
S2F accounts pay out 100% of simulated profit. Requests start at $500 , in $50 increments.
Eligibility
10 Qualifying Days since your last payout or purchase. The day you request doesn't count.
The cycle's profit target is reached, counting only profit made since your last payout (or since purchase).
Your best day is no more than 20% of the cycle's profit.
A Payout Method is set up (Plane or DT Wallet).

Example (50K): your first payout needs $3,500 profit (balance $53,500); you can then request up to $2,000.
Requesting a payout
On your Dashboard, click Request Payout , choose the account and amount, and submit. Place at least one trade afterwards so the payout shows on your charts.
If you're called to live: your simulated accounts are closed. Excess simulated balances are generally forfeited, but they may be used as performance rewards in live, subject to the Risk Team's discretion. If you are not comfortable with this, do not build large excess simulated balances.
