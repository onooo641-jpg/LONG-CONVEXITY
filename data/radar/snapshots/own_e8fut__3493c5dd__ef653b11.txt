How can I change my trading platform?
Are you able to request platform change on Futures?
Updated over 3 weeks ago
At this moment, we offer only one trading platform for the Futures market. If you are not satisfied with the Tradovate platform , we are unfortunately unable to change your trading platform or offer a different solution. You can still connect your Tradovate account to the Tradingview option for trading, or you can request a refund if you're eligible for it.
