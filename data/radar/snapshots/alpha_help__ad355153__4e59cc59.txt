Integrating your Alpha Futures Account with TradingView
B
Written by Benjamin Chaffee
September 30, 2026
Alpha Futures is excited to offer TradingView ​with our partners at Plus500.
To use this integration, you must have an Evaluation, or Qualified Account utilizing TradingView
You MUST sign your Non-Professional Agreement before your account will receive real-time data or be able to trade on TradingView.
In order to sign your Non-Professional Agreement, log in to AlphaTrader using the username and password provided in your dashboard in Account Information.
You can launch AlphaTrader directly from your user dashboard with the "Launch AlphaTrader" button. You may also choose to sign in directly using this link . ​
Once you have launched AlphaTrader, you will be prompted to sign in with the credentials provided in your “Account Info”.
Once logged in, you MUST sign your Non-Professional Agreement before your account to be able to trade on AlphaTrader . Be sure to select the 'Simulation' when prompted to select a Trading Mode.
Now, you will need to sign in to TradingView.com with your personal TradingView account credentials. If you do not have a username with TradingView you will need to first create an account to use the integration.
Please select Plus500US under the listed brokers. Be sure to select “demo” when asked to connect.
Add “AlphaTrader” as the firm and input the username and password under your account info page
At this point, you are successfully connected and can enjoy trading your Alpha Futures Account(s) on TradingView through AlphaTrader integration.
