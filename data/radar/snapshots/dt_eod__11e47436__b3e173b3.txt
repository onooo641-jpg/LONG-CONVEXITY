How the S2F end-of-day trailing drawdown works and where it stops, by size.
October 22, 2026
Copy for LLM
S2F accounts use an end-of-day trailing drawdown : the level only moves after each trading day closes, based on your closing balance. Your balance must stay above it at all times, including intraday.
How it works
It starts at your starting balance minus the max drawdown.
After a day closes at a new high, it moves up to that balance minus the max drawdown. It never moves down.
It stops at your starting balance.
