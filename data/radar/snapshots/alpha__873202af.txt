In order to take a Trading Evaluation, a monthly subscription is required.
​
Our Trading Evaluation is meant to help traders build their skills and habits as they hope to move into a Qualified Account. Those that pass will become Alpha Futures Qualified Traders. Our Qualified Traders are the talent considered for Alpha Prime .
​
In order to pass an Advanced Evaluation: there are two conditions to be met, the trader must reach (+ maintain) the profit target, and satisfy the 40% consistency rule .
There is only one way to fail on Evaluation Accounts, do not allow the Account Balance to hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit .
​
​ Benefits of Advanced Account:
1.) Largest withdrawal cap in industry of $15,000, per request, per account.
2.) Consistent Profit Target to Drawdown Ratios on all account sizes.
3.) No Daily Loss Guard on Evaluation or Qualified Accounts
4.) No Consistency Rule on Advanced Qualified Accounts
5.) No News Trading Rule or restrictions
6.) No Scaling Plan on Advanced Accounts. Get access to full contract limit at all times.
7.) 90% profit split from the start, no tiered system.
8.) Pay yourself up to 4 times per month. Withdraw when you want after 5 winning days of $200 or more, no assigned payout dates.
9.) Accounts Purchased after July 8, 2026 enjoy No Activation Fees
