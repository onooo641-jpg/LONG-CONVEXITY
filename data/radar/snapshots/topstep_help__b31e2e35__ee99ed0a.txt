The Starter Challenge is a 2-round account for Traders who have never spent money with Topstep. Pay $10 one time. Hit the Profit Target in the Challenge Round, then again in the Payout Round, and request a fixed $100 Cash Payout.
Who can buy it
Traders who have never spent money with Topstep.
Free accounts don’t count as a purchase.
Limit of 1 per Trader.

New to trading? A Profit Target is the amount of simulated profit you need to make to pass a round. A Maximum Loss Limit is the most your simulated account can lose before violating the account rules.
How does the Starter Challenge work?
Round 1: Challenge Round
Purchase your Starter Challenge for $10.
Open TopstepX and start trading in the Challenge Round.
Reach your $100 Profit Target without breaking the account rules to pass.
When you pass, trading automatically stops and your account displays "Passed."
Need another try? You get two free Resets in the Challenge Round. A Reset returns your account to its starting balance and clears its previous rule violations.
Round 2: Payout Round
Open your Topstep Dashboard and activate the Payout Round.
A new, separate trading account will be created. It can take up to 15 minutes to become available.
Trade in your new account and reach another $100 Profit Target while following the rules to pass the Challenge.
When you pass, trading automatically stops, and you can request your $100 cash payout through your Dashboard.
Important: There are no Resets in the Payout Round. The Starter Challenge account closes after the Payout.

Lots / Position Size / Contracts
Units of trade. All mean the same thing.

1 mini is a lot/contract. An account that can trade a maximum of 1/10 has a Maximum Position Size of 1 mini and 10 micros (hint: 1 mini = 10 micros). Learn more about what products you can trade here .
Mini
A Mini is a smaller futures contract that trades electronically. It’s a fraction of the size of a standard futures contract, so it takes less capital and margin to trade.
Examples of a mini you'll trade in TopstepX include E-mini S&P 500 (ES) and E-mini NASDAQ 100 (NQ).
​
Learn more about what products you can trade here !
Micro
A Micro is a mini-sized Mini. It tracks the same market at about 1/10 the size, so you get a lower cost to enter and more control over your position size. In TopstepX, 1 Mini = 10 Micros.
Examples of a micro you'll trade in TopstepX include Micro E-mini S&P (MES) and Micro E-mini NASDAQ 100 (MNQ).
​
Learn more about what products you can trade here !
Tick
The minimum price increment a contract can move.
For E-mini S&P 500 (ES), one tick is worth $12.50 per contract.
Slippage
Execution at a different price than expected. Common in fast markets. Only occurs with market orders.
You place a market order to buy 1 NQ at 21,000.00 and get filled at 21,000.50. That is 2 ticks of slippage, or $10.
Fat-Finger
Accidental order entry error

FAQs
Where do I find the Starter Challenge?
Eligible Traders see it on their Topstep Dashboard!
Can I use a promo code?
Yes. The Starter Challenge accepts any promo code.
How do Resets work?
A Reset returns your Challenge to its original starting balance — Account Balance and Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) all go back to day one. It's a second chance!
You get 2 free Resets in the Challenge Round.
There are no Resets in the Payout Round.
You apply a Reset from your Topstep Dashboard by going to the Accounts page, selecting the Challenge you’d like to Reset, and clicking the Reset button.
Does the Starter Challenge lead to a Live Funded Account®?
No. There’s no path to a Live Funded Account (LFA) through the Starter Challenge.
What happens if I want a refund?
Our standard Topstep Refund Policies apply.
Are there any other limitations I should know about?
In the Starter Challenge , products are capped at 1 micro contract. However, additional trading limitations apply due to high-risk product limitations. See details below.
Additional Restrictions : MCL, MGC, MBT, MET, MHG, SIL, HG, PL, and SI are 0.
During Tier 1 News Events: Only M6A, M6B, M6E, and MNG can be traded, at 1 lot each. MES, MNQ, MYM, and M2K move to 0.
Learn more about why here .
