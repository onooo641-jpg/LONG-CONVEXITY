What countries are restricted at BrightFunded?
September 15, 2026
At BrightFunded, we are committed to providing a smooth and equitable trading experience for our global community. However, due to international sanctions, regulatory requirements, compliance considerations, and other risk-related restrictions , our services may not be available in certain jurisdictions.
Who Is Affected?
Individuals residing in or holding nationality from the following countries are currently not permitted to purchase Challenges or register with BrightFunded.
Restricted Country List
Cuba
Iran
North Korea
Syria
Vietnam
Pakistan
Important: Traders from Pakistan who already have an active account may continue trading.
Why Are These Restrictions Necessary?
These restrictions are implemented based on applicable international sanctions, regulatory and compliance requirements, risk-management considerations, and BrightFunded’s internal policies .
​
They help us operate responsibly across different jurisdictions while maintaining a secure and reliable trading environment for our community.
Should you have any questions or concerns regarding these restrictions, our Support Team is available to assist you.
