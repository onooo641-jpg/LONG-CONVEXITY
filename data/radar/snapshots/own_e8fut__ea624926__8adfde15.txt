All accounts at E8 Markets are demo accounts within a simulated environment powered by real market data. This applies to all accounts, including SimFi ™ Challenge accounts (Phase-1) and SimFi ™ Performance accounts (After you reach the profit target in the Challenge stage).
​
Why is it simulated?
Think of our platform as a flight simulator for real trading: we provide the simulated capital and environment so you can train and refine a legitimate strategy without trading and risking your personal capital with brokers.
​
Your journey starts with the SimFi ™ Challenge account , where you demonstrate your ability to follow specific safety guardrails and operate within the guidelines.
​
After you demonstrate that and complete the profit target, you are moving to a SimFi ™ Performance account . This is the final stage, the cockpit. Even here, you remain in a simulated environment, but you become eligible to receive a payout based on your validated performance data.
