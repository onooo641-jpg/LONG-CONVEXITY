Refund & Cancellation Policy
Our refund policy, eligible exceptions, and how to get in touch.
July 13, 2026
Table of contents
All purchases are final and non-refundable once completed. Access to your account is granted immediately upon payment, and all sales are considered final from that point.
Filing chargebacks on non-refundable purchases may result in a permanent ban at Blueberry Funded.
Non-Refundable
Change of mind or reconsideration after purchase
Dissatisfaction with challenge rules or trading conditions
Failure to trade or use the account after purchase
Payments made via any method other than a direct debit or credit card transaction , including but not limited to: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other mobile wallets; PayPal; Skrill; UPI; cryptocurrency; bank transfers; and any other third-party or alternative payment method
Payments sent via an incorrect or unsupported crypto network
Partially completed challenges or breached accounts
Exceptions
For payments made by direct debit/credit card only (excluding mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, and all other alternative payment methods), a refund request will only be reviewed in the following situations:
Duplicate or accidental purchase - where the same order was charged more than once, supported by payment evidence
Service non-delivery - where Blueberry Futures is unable to provide account access after confirmed support attempts within our resolution timeframe
In all exception cases, our support team will first attempt to resolve the issue directly. A refund is only considered if the issue cannot be remedied.
How to Request a Refund
Refund requests are accepted only when they meet the eligible exceptions outlined above and are submitted within 14 days of purchase . To submit a request, reach out via email at [email protected] or through live chat with the following details:
Your order number
Your account ID
The email address used at checkout
Requests submitted outside the 14-day window or without the required details will not be processed.
Promotional Refunds
Any refund arrangements tied to a promotion or offer are governed by the specific terms of that promotion and are separate from this standard policy.
Cancelling Your Account
To cancel your account, shoot us an email at [email protected] .
After cancellation, all access to your account and Blueberry Futures services will be revoked. No refunds will be issued for previously paid fees.
Right to Termination
Blueberry Futures reserves the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates our General Terms & Conditions . That includes:
Deactivating accounts
Blocking access to our services
Forfeiting evaluation fees
Potential bans from future participation
