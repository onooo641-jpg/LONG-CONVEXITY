Rapid EOD 25k is built on the same foundation as the standard Rapid 25K plan. The key difference is that the Sim Funded stage trails end-of-day instead of intraday, and the evaluation consistency rule is lower. It keeps Rapid's 90/10 profit split.

1. Sim Funded Account Parameters
How the Max Loss works
Your Max Loss trails upward each time your account closes at a new end-of-day high.
It does not move intraday – only at the close of each trading session.
The distance between your account and the Max Loss Limit is always $1,000 while it's trailing.
Floor Lock at $100
Once your trailing Max Loss reaches +$100 , it locks there .
From that point on, it does not trail anymore .
You must always keep at least $100 in the account.
If your balance goes below $100 → the account is breached . ​
News trading
News trading is not allowed on this Sim Funded account. ​
Contract limits
You may trade up to 2 minis and/or 20 micros total.
Exceeding a total of 2 minis or its equivalent in micros (20 micros) can result in a breach of the trading account. ​
2. Payouts on Sim Funded (Rapid EOD 25k)
Payout requirements
Required Buffer: $1,100
You must first build at least $1,100 in realized profits in the account before requesting your first payout.
Buffer Unlock: Once the buffer clears, each payout after that unlocks once your net profit since your last payout reaches $250 . ​
Timing & frequency
Payout Frequency: Daily
Max Payout per Cycle: No cap
Amounts & split
Minimum Payout Request: $500
Profit Split:
90% to the trader
10% to MyFundedFutures
In summary, the Rapid EOD 25k Sim Funded stage requires:
Respecting the end-of-day trailing Max Loss, which locks at +$100, and
Clearing the $1,100 buffer for your first payout, then reaching $250 in net profit since your last payout to unlock each payout after that, under the 90/10 split.

For details on transitioning to a Live account, the Reserve Program, and Live account parameters, see Understanding Rapid Live . Live transition on Rapid EOD 25k Plan follows the same rules as standard Rapid 25K.
Frequently Asked Questions
Q. Are there any consistency rules in the Rapid EOD 25k evaluation?
A. Yes, there is a 30% consistency rule in the evaluation phase of the Rapid EOD 25k Plan, and it can be passed in as little as 4 days.
Q. Is there a Daily Loss Limit (DLL) on the Rapid EOD 25k Plan?
A. No, there is no daily loss limit in the evaluation or sim funded stages of the Rapid EOD 25k Plan.
Q. How is the drawdown different from the standard Rapid 25K Plan?
A. The standard Rapid Plan trails intraday at the sim-funded stage. Rapid EOD 25k trails end-of-day instead. Your Max Loss only moves based on where your account closes each session, not on intraday swings.
Q. Are there any buffer zones in the Rapid EOD 25k payout policy?
A. Yes, you cannot request your first payout until you clear the $1,100 buffer. After that, each additional payout unlocks once you've made $250 in net profit since your last payout.
Q. What's the minimum payout per request on the Rapid EOD 25k Plan?
A. The minimum payout amount on the Rapid EOD 25k Plan is $500.
Q. Are there any activation fees or monthly subscription costs on the Rapid EOD 25k Plan?
A. Our Rapid EOD 25k account does not have an activation fee or a monthly subscription cost.
Q. How do I transition to a Live account on Rapid EOD 25k?
A. See Understanding Rapid Live for transition triggers, Live account parameters, and the Reserve Program. Rapid EOD 25k follows the same rules as standard Rapid 25K.
Q. Can I trade on live and sim at the same time?
A. No, once you have a live account, you cannot trade on the sim and live environment simultaneously.
