How the S2L and live intraday trailing drawdown works and where it stops, by plan.
Updated over a month ago
Copy for LLM
S2L evaluations and live accounts use an intraday trailing drawdown : the level follows your highest balance in real time, including open profit, and never moves down. Touching it fails the account.
How it works
It starts at your starting balance minus the max drawdown.
Evaluation: it stops trailing once it reaches your profit-target balance.
Live account: it starts again at the full max drawdown and stops at your starting balance. That's also when live payouts unlock.
