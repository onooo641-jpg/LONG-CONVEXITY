How many accounts can I apply for at once?
What is the maximum allocation of E8 accounts I can apply for?
Updated this week
Table of contents
SimFi™ Challenge accounts (Phase 1):
All products - UNLIMITED
​ There is an unlimited allocation for accounts in phase 1
Maximum allocation for SimFI™ Performance accounts:
Max for E8 One = $500,000 allocation per market
Max for E8 Pro = $500,000 allocation per market
E8 Zero = 3 performance accounts
E8 Classic = $100,000 allocation
E8 Signatures Futures = 5 performance accounts with $50,000 size, or 3 performance accounts with size of $150,000 or $100,000 ( 5 is always maximum, meaning you can combine, for example, 3x $150,000 with 2x $50,000)
This means that in the Simfi™ Performance accounts, you can manage up to $3,900,000 in Simulated capital at the same time!
Note: These allocation limits apply per household . Multiple users within the same residence/same IP must not exceed this collective maximum.
Examples:
During the Challenge stage, there are no limits on how many accounts you can purchase. For example, you could buy a $400,000 E8 One account, a $100,000 E8 One account, and a $200,000 E8 One account.
​
If you pass all of them, since the maximum allocation limit on the Performance stage for E8 One is $500,000, you will be able to trade only with a $400,000 and a $100,000 account. The other $200,000 account will be kept as a reserve until your allocation on the Performance stage drops below the limit.
​
Similarly, for Signature models, you can purchase as many Challenge accounts as you like, regardless of account size. Note that you can only actively manage 5 performance accounts for each market in the Performance stage at any given time; any additional accounts will be kept in reserve.
Single User (E8X) Profile policy
Users are only allowed to have a single user profile, so all purchases of our challenges must be made through one registered user profile. All accounts must be traded solely by their respective owners.
Creating multiple user profiles under different emails or trading accounts from different users is a violation of our terms and conditions and may lead to the temporary or permanent suspension of your active accounts and user profile.
​
If you have unintentionally created an additional profile, please reach out to our support team at support@e8markets.com and we will assist in deleting the extra profile promptly.
