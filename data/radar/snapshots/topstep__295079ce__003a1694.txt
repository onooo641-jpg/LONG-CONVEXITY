The Basics
Topstep is always innovating — and Topstep Labs is where that shows up first. You've probably had ideas a prop firm should try. An account structure that made sense. A feature that could help. A program you wished existed. Labs is where those ideas get tested for real.
​
Not just concepts — actual experiments you can trade right now. Most Labs drops are limited; when it's gone, it's gone. Some Labs ideas graduate into the full Topstep experience. Others won't. But every drop teaches us something. This article tracks every Labs offering as it launches — full details and dates, all in one place.
​
👉 You can also visit topstep.com/labs to see what's live and sign up to stay in the know.
How Topstep Labs Works
We drop an experiment. A new Labs offering goes live as a limited drop.
You trade it. Real Traders, real data. This is how we find out if an idea actually works.
We see what works. Some experiments make it out of the lab and into the full Topstep program.

Daily Payout details:
After your first Payout, you must be net positive since your last Payout.

5 p.m. CT is the cutoff for a Payout request. Any request after 5 p.m. CT applies to the next trade day.

Frequently Asked Questions
$25K Static Trading Combine
What does "static drawdown" mean, and how is it different from trailing drawdown?
Topstep uses 2 types of drawdown. Here's how each works using the same scenario: a Trader starts with a $25,000 balance and a $1,000 Max Loss Limit, earns $1,000 in profit (balance hits $26,000), then loses $500 (balance ends at $25,500).
End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing (standard Topstep Trading Combines): The Max Loss Limit trails the end-of-day closing balance — not intraday peaks. Trader ends at $25,500, limit moves to $24,500. They start the next day with $1,000 of room.
​
Static (this Trading Combine): The Max Loss Limit never moves. It stays at $24,000 no matter what. With a $28,000 balance, the Trader has $4,000 of room — not $3,000.

What does "static drawdown" mean, and how is it different from trailing drawdown?
Here's how each works using the same $150K scenario: a Trader starts with a $150,000 balance and a $6,000 Maximum Loss Limit , earns $6,000 in profit (balance hits $156,000), then loses $3,000 (balance ends at $153,000).
End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing (standard Topstep Trading Combines): The Maximum Loss Limit trails the end-of-day closing balance, not intraday peaks. Trader ends at $153,000, limit moves to $147,000. They start the next day with $6,000 of room.
Static (this Trading Combine): The Maximum Loss Limit never moves. It stays at $144,000 no matter what. The Trader starts the next day with $9,000 of room. Grow the balance to $168,000 and that room is $24,000.
