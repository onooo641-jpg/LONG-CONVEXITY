Payout conditions, cushion and caps.

Payout conditions
6 trading days, each with at least $200 (25K), $250 (50K), $500 (100K) or $750 (150K) of net profit.
After the payout your balance stays above the starting balance plus the cushion.
A request between the minimum and the cap.

Example
50K at $54,000: $54,000 minus $50,000 minus the $1,500 cushion leaves $2,500, the 1st payout cap.
After a payout
Trading days restart from zero. You can request again after at least 1 day. Profit split: 100%.
