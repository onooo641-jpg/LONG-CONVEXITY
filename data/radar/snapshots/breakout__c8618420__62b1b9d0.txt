Breakout After Hours: Crypto’s Ugly Weekend and What’s Next
Bitcoin hit a four-month low near $62,000, Nvidia, the Fed and Anthropic's IPO all land inside 48 hours, and Breakout got a new look.
November 18, 2026
·
2 min read
Breakout After Hours, week of November 17, 2026: Bitcoin hit a four-month low near $62,000, Nvidia, the Fed and Anthropic’s IPO all land inside 48 hours, and Breakout has a new look.
Well… that wasn’t a relaxing weekend.
Bitcoin fell to roughly $62,000 early Sunday, November 15, a four-month low and within a few thousand dollars of the year’s bottom. About $2 billion in positions got liquidated in 24 hours, 87% of it longs, and more than 312,000 traders got wiped out. On the way down, Bitcoin sliced through its 200-week moving average, historically the floor in bear markets. As of November 17, it was down about 25% since October 21 and roughly 49% below its all-time high.
The supporting cast isn’t helping.
Spot Bitcoin ETFs have bled nearly $3 billion since October 30, and stablecoin supply shrank $14.2 billion in 30 days, the fastest contraction since June 2022. Fewer dollars on the sidelines means fewer dip buyers. And MARA, the biggest public miner, disclosed it sold 13,597 BTC after September at an average of about $81,000. That didn’t cause the weekend, but with Bitcoin stuck in the low $60,000s, the miner that sold at $81,000 is quietly the smartest money in the room.
The Week That Could Change the Mood
Last week’s After Hours covered the Nvidia CEO scandal. By Friday, November 13, Jensen Huang had stepped down and the stock was down nearly 13% on the week. Nvidia was due to report earnings right after the November 17 edition went out, its first report without him, with Wall Street looking for roughly $109 billion in revenue. Wednesday, November 18, brings the Fed’s October minutes, with a December hike already locked in. And Anthropic is expected to price its IPO on November 18 at $70 to $78 a share before trading Thursday, November 19, on the Nasdaq as ANT.
Nvidia, the Fed, and a trillion-dollar IPO, all inside 48 hours. Buckle up.
